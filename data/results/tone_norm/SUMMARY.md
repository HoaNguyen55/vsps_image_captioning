# Tone-Mark Normalisation & CJK Sensitivity: Phase 2 Side-by-Side Rescoring Summary

- **Date**: 2026-10-14
- **Pre-registration**: `manuscript/prereg/2026-10-13_tone_norm.md` (`C-027`)
- **Normalisation**: `rescap.chair.norm_tone(text)` (canonicalizes Kiểu mới `òa/óa/ỏa/õa/ọa`, `òe/óe/ỏe/õe/ọe`, `ùy/úy/ủy/ũy/ụy` to Kiểu cũ before substring matching in `objects_in()` and `vi_objects()`).
- **Fallback**: `raw_tone=True` or `RESCAP_RAW_TONE=1` reproduces all pre-fix numbers to `< 1e-9`.

## 1. Key Findings & Mechanism

1. **100% Invariance of KTVIC-Finetuned SFT Models (`p1`, `fuse2`, `tags_fuse2`, `e5`, `p1assist`, `p1mask`, `mr5assist`)**:
   - Because 95.3% of `'toà nhà'` mentions in `ktvic/train_data.json` are written in Kiểu cũ (`toà nhà`), every model fine-tuned on KTVIC (`p1-gold`, `p1_s43`, `p1_s44`, `p1assist_s42/43/44`, `fuse2_s42/43/44`, `tags_fuse2_s42`, `e5_s42`) already outputs Kiểu cũ spelling and has **0 changed captions (`0.00` delta across all metrics)** on `ktvic/test_data.json`.
2. **Where Changes Occur on KTVIC (`30 / 73` systems)**:
   - Un-finetuned or base/VCD/VSPS/DPO systems (`zeroshot-detailed`: 67 caps, `vcd-detailed`: 58 caps, `tags_zeroshot-detailed`: 50 caps, `main_s42/43/44-detailed`: 6–8 caps, `v3_s42-detailed`: 7 caps, `vsps_clean-detailed`: 6 caps, `zeroshot-short`: 5 caps, `vsps-detailed`: 5 caps, `vsps_base-detailed`: 5 caps, `vsps_neutral-detailed`: 5 caps, `p2-noverif4090-detailed`: 5 caps, `a1-detailed`: 5 caps, `a2-detailed`: 4 caps, `vcd-short`: 4 caps, `zeroshot-two`: 4 caps, `roundA-detailed`: 3 caps, `e5dpo_s42-detailed/short`: 1 cap) emit Kiểu mới `'tòa nhà'`.
   - Under `raw_tone=True`, `'tòa nhà'` failed to match `'toà nhà'` and fell through to the shorter substring `'nhà'` (a distinct canonical class with `_HYPER['toà nhà'] = 'nhà'`). On `zeroshot-detailed` (67 captions), 54 captions swapped `'nhà' -> 'toà nhà'` and 13 captions that already contained `'nhà'` gained `'toà nhà'` as an additional class, shifting `zeroshot-detailed` `F1` `40.96 -> 41.24 (+0.28)`, `F1_spec` `41.95 -> 42.23 (+0.28)`, `CHAIR_i` `65.07% -> 65.03% (-0.04pp)`, `CHAIR_s` `98.03% -> 98.03% (0.00pp)`.
3. **100% Metric Invariance on UIT-ViIC (`n = 231`) and COCO Shards (`coco5000`, `coco5000_vcd`, `coco_transfer`)**:
   - All 6 UIT-ViIC systems have **0 changed captions** under M2 (`vi_objects` `v2`) and **0.00 metric delta** under both M1 and M2.
   - All 9 COCO shard groups (`coco5000`, `coco5000_vcd`, `coco_transfer`) have **0 changed captions (`0.00` delta across all metrics)** under M2 (`vi_objects` `v2`).
4. **OpenViIC Second-Panel & Two-Gold Audit (`second_panel.json`, `two_gold_audit.json`)**:
   - In `ktvic_openviic_refs.json`, 693 / 19,355 train captions and 71 / 2,992 test captions normalize `'tòa nhà' -> 'toà nhà'`. Because OpenViIC gold sets gain specific `'toà nhà'` (replacing generic `'nhà'`), SFT models (`p1`, `fuse2`) achieve higher precision (`P_I` `+0.34..+0.67`, `false_I` `-0.007..-0.015`) against the two-gold panel.
