## Rulings
1. Science, tier 1. The design is sound for a dev-explore: closed rule-generated candidates (no lexicon, no gold), a same-model grey-image text-only baseline T, a harm guard, a pre-declared finite grid computed from saved numbers, a cross-fit row, and an explicit statement that the selected M* is optimistic. KILL is computed on the optimistic M*, so a KILL is robust; a GO only buys a tier-2 prereg on C. That is the three-tier method (PI-1029n) applied correctly.
2. Power. The draft's power note is wrong in the safe direction. The paired SE of dEM is driven by correctness flips, not by changed answers: at the SESOI with the harm guard (about 29 good, 7 bad flips) SE ~ sqrt(36)/2,210 ~ 0.3 points before a mild image-cluster inflation (~3 QA per image). A true +1.0 then clears CI_lo > 0 with high probability. Fix the text (W7); the rule stands.
3. Known biases (disclose, not blocking): summed unnormalised log-likelihood favours variants with fewer tokens (T shares the bias, so the image margin is like-for-like; alpha = 1 cancels it); the base keeps its own placement while candidates use old style (a likelihood nuisance only; EM is placement-free); independent per-syllable choice applied jointly is never scored jointly (§9 names it). All three push toward "no change", i.e. toward KILL.
4. Leakage. No gold and no C image reach M2; C's L1-a preds travel inside the pinned pred files but are predictions, not gold, and rerank reads E ids only. But C's gold WAS seen in L1-a (ceilings, the old-style placement majority, §2.5). The tier-2 wording must be "C was not used for any L1-b selection", never "C was never seen" (W9).
5. Non-Qwen requirement (CC-165 r5). Kept as P3/NO_VERDICT. As written it cannot work: gemma always fails check_snapshot (W1), and gemma failing an assessability test that needs gold is only seen at score, after which no fallback data exist (W2).
6. Gates. CC-165 r4 is NOT overruled here: no L1-b GPU step until G1 and G3 close. Exact closure (W3): G3 ViTextVQA leg = PI-1029l (HF card, CC BY-NC 3.0, click-to-accept) + PI-1029q (PI: datasets public, use freely); I accept these two LOG ids as the written-terms record for dev research.
