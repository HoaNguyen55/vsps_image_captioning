## Rulings
1. Ranking unchanged: A-1 first. Tier 1 GO-with-fixes at 90% after W2' and W5'.
2. W2 is amended, not overruled silently. CC-170 W2's synonym clause is narrowed for a reason: WordNet-style synonyms ("passenger vehicle", "can", "set", "goggle box") are rare in captions and narratives, and dropping bus/toilet/microwave for them costs power without reducing the label-vs-narrative bias the clause targeted. What does bias that contrast is any ADE class the matcher sends into V without the label rung counting it (e.g. minibike -> bicycle, stool -> chair). So the binding rule becomes W2' below: name-level consistency in BOTH directions, enforced by code from the pinned objectInfo150 before any label or narrative is counted; synonym-level conflicts are reported descriptively. This supersedes CC-170 W2 where they differ.
3. W8 is acceptable as non-blocking for the writer, but the coordinator must add the ledger line to manuscript/directions/PORTFOLIO_3Y_2026-10-29.md §2.3 BEFORE or IN the finalisation gatekeeper commit (CC-170 r1: "now, not at tier 3"). It is a precondition of finalisation, not of tier 3.
