==========================================================================================================================
0. BOTTOM LINE
==========================================================================================================================
- Statistics of the annotators ALONE cannot produce b = 0.75. Model the references as Zipf/Heaps-type naming propensities:
  beta-binomial fit to the 5-annotator incidence data, plus Chao2 for unseen classes. With ANY monotone ranking of those
  objects (ideal ranker, pseudo-annotator union, logit noise at any temperature, constant hallucination share), the model
  gives b = 0.87-0.96. Calibrated to P1's precision, it predicts P = 75.4-76.8 at fuse2-detailed's count; observed 67.95.
- What works is a two-factor decomposition, measured on gold built from h = 1..5 test references:
    P_sys(M; h) = s1 (M/M1)^-D * P_pool(M; h),  so  b_sys(h) = b_pool(h) - D.
  - P_pool(M; h) is the precision of a pool of k fresh humans against h others. It is computed exactly from the
    k-annotator accumulation curve U(k): TP = U(k) + U(h) - U(k+h), with Chao2 extrapolation where k + h > 5.
  - Two system constants, s1 = 0.892 and D = 0.194, fit all 10 system precisions (P1 and fuse2-det x h = 1..5) with
    RMSE 0.78 P.
  - Fitting on h <= 4 only predicts b(h=5) = 0.756, against the pre-registered 0.7468.
- WHY 0.75: 1 - b = 0.253 = 0.053 (reference saturation, the Heaps/Good-Turing part, 21%) + 0.200 (the system, 79%).
  - The system part is D, the rate at which the share of a system's mentions that are not "human-nameable" grows with
    count.
  - Humans have D = 0: their gold-hit rate is flat over mention position (86.6/83.5/84.9/85.5). Detailed systems decay
    as r^-0.25.
- What a frontier-beating method must change: only s(M), the in-population share. P_pool is fixed by the references.
  - Re-ranking by the model's own evidence (vote share, calibrated q, count plans, longer targets) leaves s(M) unchanged,
    hence a zero residual. This explains UPE, PLAN-F1 and the ladder runs.
  - Halving D is worth about +3.9 P / +3.5 F1 at detailed counts. Raising s1 from 0.89 to 0.95 is worth about +5.1 P /
    +3.2 F1 at P1's count.
- Value: a reference-count-invariant object precision, s = P_sys / P_pool. It gives two numbers per system (s1, D),
  comparable across datasets with different numbers of references. No prior work found derives or decomposes this curve.

1. PROBLEM AND HYPOTHESIS
==========================================================================================================================
Elasticity identity (exact). b(M) = dlnTP/dlnM = P_marg(M) / P(M). A constant b means the gold-hit rate of the r-th
  marginal mention decays as r^-(1-b) = r^-0.253. Between the anchors, marginal P = (2.143-1.400)/(3.154-1.783) = 54.2%
  against an average of about 73%.
Model (salience-ordered naming, two populations):
  - Each image has human-nameable objects with naming propensity pi, heavy-tailed. Measured: Q_c = classes named by
    exactly c of 5 annotators = 1.28/1.01/0.65/0.39/0.38 per image (c = 1..5); Chao2 Q0 = 0.65 unseen nameable classes
    per image; U(k) = 1.743/2.577/3.101/3.460/3.717 (test).
  - The gold is the union of h annotators.
  - A system lists objects in decreasing evidence. A share s(M) of its mentions falls in the nameable population and
    behaves like a pooled human (hit rate P_pool(M; h)). The rest (hallucinated, or real but never named in KTVIC style)
    misses the gold.
  - Hence P_sys = s(M) P_pool(M; h). Power-law share: s = s1 (M/M1)^-D.
Annotator-side identities (exact, no model):
  - P_1vh-pool = 1 - [U(k+h) - U(h)] / U(k).
  - Good-Turing form: P_1v4 = 1 - Q1/(5 U1) = 85.25, vs the measured 85.33 (human_parity.json; the gap is the matcher
    version).
Falsifiable predictions:
  H1 (gold-size invariance): D and s1 do not depend on h. Kill: for any frontier system pair, max_h D - min_h D > 0.06
     with a CI excluding 0, or the 2-parameter RMSE exceeds 1.5 P.
  H2 (panel extension): with OpenViIC added to the gold, b_sys rises, and D' < D by the share of "real but
     KTVIC-unnameable" mentions.
  H3 (position law): any new detailed-mode frontier run has a position decay alpha_pos in [0.21, 0.29], while human
     captions stay flat (alpha < 0.05).
  H4 (methods): a method that moves only the count, or re-ranks within an image by the model's own evidence, keeps
     (s1, D) within seed noise. Only a signal outside the model's evidence can move s.

2. CLOSEST PRIOR WORK (verified this round by web search unless marked)
==========================================================================================================================
1. Spain & Perona, "Measuring and Predicting Object Importance", IJCV 91 (2011); ECCV 2008 precursor "Some Objects Are
   More Equal Than Others". Importance = P(object named first), with naming order modelled as draws from an urn.
   - NEW here: we model the SYSTEM's naming order against an incomplete multi-annotator gold, and derive the frontier
     exponent and its gold-size dependence. Overlap: moderate (the ordered-naming premise).
2. Berg et al., "Understanding and Predicting Importance in Images", CVPR 2012 (DOI 10.1109/CVPR.2012.6248100). Predicts
   what gets described from composition, semantic and context factors.
   - NEW: a frontier theory; their importance model is a candidate source of the nameability prior (pilot P-E).
     Overlap: low.
3. Zhou et al., LURE, arXiv 2310.00754 (ICLR 2024). Hallucinated objects appear later in the generated text (position
   factor).
   - Feng et al., arXiv 2406.12663 (2024): object size shrinks along a caption; they question "more detail = more
     hallucination".
   - arXiv 2507.12455 (2025, title seen in search, details UNVERIFIED): hallucinations rise later in captions.
   - NEW: a quantitative law (alpha_pos ~ 1 - b), and a decomposition into a reference part and a system part.
     Overlap: moderate on the empirical position effect.
4. Kalai & Vempala, "Calibrated Language Models Must Hallucinate", arXiv 2311.14648 (STOC 2024). The Good-Turing singleton
   rate lower-bounds hallucination.
   - NEW: Good-Turing/Chao applied to the REFERENCE process. Human precision equals 1 - singleton rate exactly, and it
     sets b_pool. Overlap: low (different object).
5. Chao et al., "Rarefaction and extrapolation with Hill numbers", Ecol. Monogr. 84(1):45-67 (2014), DOI
   10.1890/13-0133.1. Incidence rarefaction and extrapolation. "Reliable up to about double the sample": Colwell et al.
   2012, UNVERIFIED.
   - NEW: used to compute a reference-size-dependent object frontier. Overlap: tool only.
6. Misra et al., arXiv 1512.06974 (CVPR 2016), reporting bias (verified in R8 T3); Rohrbach et al., arXiv 1809.02156
   (EMNLP 2018), CHAIR (verified, search hit). Conceptual root of the "nameable share" and the metric being modelled.
7. Menon et al., ICML 2015: AUC is invariant to label corruption. Explains why monotone recalibration and count methods
   stay on the curve (NOT re-verified this round).
   Davis & Goadrich, ICML 2006, the PR/ROC relation: the frontier is a PR curve in count coordinates (NOT re-verified).
Novelty scan (6 web searches: Heaps law captions/annotators; precision-count power law; hallucination vs length theory).
  - No paper found that fits or derives TP ~ M^b for object mentions.
  - No paper found that predicts the precision-count curve from annotator accumulation statistics.
  - No paper found that splits it into a reference part and a system part.
  - The nearest is Sekai2, arXiv 2608.09449, which fits Heaps' law (exponent about 0.43) to annotation prompts: vocabulary
    only, not objects.
Ratings: decomposition and invariant precision MODERATE-HIGH; position law LOW-MODERATE; "why 0.75" as a whole MODERATE.

6. RISKS AND FIT WITH PD-2
==========================================================================================================================
Risks:
- D has been measured on ONE system pair. Its h-invariance has a slight upward drift (0.166 -> 0.200) and needs B.
- b_pool(5) rests on Chao2, a lower bound, under violated exchangeability. The h <= 2 values are exact, so the
  decomposition survives, but the 21% / 79% split could shift to about 15% / 85%.
- P_pool is evaluated at the total count M, not at the in-population count s*M. A refinement changes D by about 0.01-0.02.
- s(M) is descriptive. Splitting it into hallucination vs unnameable needs C and D.
- The power law is a local summary. The models give slowly varying elasticities. Do not extrapolate beyond M of about
  3.3; the selffuse residuals are an s1 loss, not curvature.
Fit with PD-2: the EVALUATION/METHODOLOGY pillar.
- It explains frontier invariance and the 0.75 value.
- It turns the C10 R2 "k-annotator human frontier" into the b_pool term of the same equation.
- It gives a reference-count-invariant precision s = P/P_pool usable on KTVIC, UIT-ViIC and COCO.
- It states the exact target a method must hit (raise s1 or lower D) and why count-only methods cannot.
