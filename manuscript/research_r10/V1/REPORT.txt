0. BOTTOM LINE
======================================================================================================
- Idea: a v3 extractor for the 113-entry matcher (rescap.chair.objects_in). It parses classifiers by
  reading the existing NOUN_CLASSIFIER agreement table in reverse: `cây cầu` -> bridge, not tree+bridge.
  It adds a frequency-mined table of compound exceptions (`cửa hàng` is not a door), a negation scope, and
  it merges classifier-split classes (`toà nhà`/`nhà`, `con đường`/`đường`).
- Size: changes 17.4% of KTVIC test references and 34.9% of test gold sets. P1 short F1 rises by +1.34
  (3-seed mean); zero-shot by +1.9 to +2.0; human 1-vs-4 F1 by +1.13.
- No PD-2 conclusion moves. Paired contrasts change by <= 0.54 F1. P1 stays on the pre-registered frontier
  (|residual| <= 0.01 TP/img). The P1-to-human gap moves by 0.24.
- Validated with existing human labels only, no new gold:
  * COCO instance labels: v2 pulls 3,806 `quả` ("fruit") mentions from UIT-ViIC human captions; they are
    supported 0.2% of the time, and v3 removes 99.4% of them.
  * KTVIC: a classifier-only `cây` is confirmed as a tree by another annotator 6.6% of the time, vs 77.8%
    for the trees v3 keeps.
- Value: a must-do measurement-validity fix, a robustness section for the evaluation pillar, and a small
  releasable resource. NOT a headline novelty (novelty 2/5). Keep v2 as the pre-registered primary metric and
  report v3 as a pre-registered sensitivity analysis.

1. PROBLEM AND HYPOTHESIS
======================================================================================================
Problem. Every KTVIC object number goes through rescap.chair.objects_in: Table IV (scripts/object_prf.py),
CHAIR-vi, the count frontier (scripts/prereg_ladder_check.py L158/L166), UPE-MBR (scripts/upe_mbr.py L96-100)
and PLAN-F1 (scripts/plan_f1.py L43-66). It also builds TRAINING targets (build_gold_sft.py, build_dpo_data.py,
e5_select.py; plan_f1.py comment "D10: every training list equals objects_in(target)").
objects_in is a greedy 3/2/1-syllable longest match over a stop-listed lexicon. Running it confirms:
  (a) Classifier read as a noun: `một cây cầu` -> [cây, cầu]; `quả bóng` -> [quả, bóng].
      `cây` and `quả` are not in the _NOT_OBJECTS stop list, although con/cái/chiếc/bức/tấm are.
  (b) Compound truncation, head is not the referent:
      `cửa hàng` -> [cửa] (door); `hoa quả` -> [hoa, quả]; `cầu thang` -> bridge; `bàn tay` -> table;
      `chiếc xe hơi` -> [xe], never `ô tô` (the chair.py docstring claims they are one object).
  (c) Classifier-split classes: `toà nhà`, `căn phòng`, `bức tranh`, `con đường` are lexicon keys separate from
      `nhà`, `phòng`, `tranh`, `đường`. CHAIR merges them via canonical(); object_prf.py and the frontier tool
      do NOT, while upe_mbr.py does. One lexicon, three definitions of "same object" (CHAIR / Table IV + frontier / UPE).
  (d) Negation: `không có người nào` -> [người].
  (e) Tone variants: already fixed by matcher v2 (data/results/tone_norm/SUMMARY.json; +0.2 to +0.5 F1,
      zero-shot and legacy rows only).
H1. The errors are frequent in references and outputs, so absolute object P/R/F1 is biased.
H2. They are mostly common-mode: system contrasts, count-matched endpoints and the frontier are robust.
H3. A rule-based classifier-aware extractor agrees better with independent human labels.
Falsifiable predictions, to pre-register before the confirmatory run (§4):
  F-1 P1 short 3-seed mean F1 rises by +1.0 to +1.8; detailed by +1.2 to +2.0.
  F-2 Every paired contrast in the paper keeps its sign and CI-excludes-0 status; |change| <= 0.6 F1.
  F-3 UPE vs same-count COUNT control: per-seed ΔF1 stays within ±0.3 of +0.07/+0.03/+0.17 (kill 2 still
      fires). PLAN-F1 vs raw-vote control stays within ±0.5 of +0.08.
  F-4 P1 response-only residuals vs the v2-fitted pre-registered frontier stay within ±0.03 TP/img.
  F-5 UIT-ViIC x COCO: v2 `quả` instance support < 2%, and v3 removes > 95% of those mentions.
  F-6 KTVIC: classifier-only `cây` cross-annotator confirmation < 15%; kept-tree confirmation > 60%.
  If F-2, F-3 or F-4 fails, the paper must report extractor sensitivity. That is a finding, not a kill.

2. CLOSEST PRIOR WORK (V = verified by web search this session; U = unverified detail)
======================================================================================================
[1] Rohrbach et al., "Object Hallucination in Image Captioning", EMNLP 2018, arXiv:1809.02156 (V). CHAIR:
    English COCO-80 lexicon with synonyms and double words (`hot dog`). New here: Vietnamese classifier
    agreement used for parsing; frequency-mined compound exceptions; validation on human instance labels.
    Novelty vs [1]: LOW-MODERATE (same family: lexicon + rules).
[2] Petryk et al., "ALOHa", NAACL 2024 short, ACL 2024.naacl-short.30 (V; arXiv:2404.02904 U). LLM object
    extraction plus Hungarian matching, English. New here: deterministic Vietnamese extractor with no LLM in
    the measurement path (project rule: AI labels are not evidence). MODERATE.
[3] Ben-Kish et al., "Mitigating Open-Vocabulary Caption Hallucinations" (MOCHa/OpenCHAIR), arXiv:2312.03631 (V).
    Open-vocabulary benchmark built with an LLM and a T2I model, English. MODERATE.
[4] Wada, Kaneda, Sugiura, "JaSPICE", CoNLL 2023, arXiv:2311.04192 / 2023.conll-1.28 (V). Closest non-English
    analogue: a parser-based (dependency + predicate-argument) scene-graph metric for Japanese captions.
    New here: object-hallucination P/R/F1 and Vietnamese classifier semantics. MODERATE.
[5] Vu et al., "VnCoreNLP", NAACL 2018 demos, arXiv:1801.01331 (V). Nguyen & Nguyen, "PhoNLP", NAACL 2021
    demos, arXiv:2101.01476 (V). VLSP tagset with Nc = classifier (V via arXiv:2206.06992 and BKTreebank
    arXiv:1710.05519; VTB = ACL W09-3035, title U). They segment and tag Nc but have no visual-class mapping.
    Pilot (§3.5): Nc cannot tell `cây cầu` (bridge) from `cây dừa` (coconut TREE). MODERATE (application layer).
[6] Qu et al., arXiv:2408.00550 (V): multilingual POPE built by translating probes. CLAIM, arXiv:2506.11073 /
    ACL 2025 long.640 (V listing): multilingual object-hallucination mitigation. Neither extracts objects from
    free-form non-English captions. MODERATE.
[7] Classifier semantics:
    - Peinelt et al., "ClassifierGuesser", IJCNLP 2017 demos, ACL I17-3011 (V): Chinese classifier PREDICTION.
    - Dao 2012, NSM study of Vietnamese con/cái, ALS proceedings pp. 58-74 (V listing).
    - Tran, CLA 2019 (V listing): `cây` = long/tree-like, `quả` = round/fruit-like.
    New here: agreement used in the PARSING direction, as an extraction signal. MODERATE.
[8] KTVIC arXiv:2401.08100 (V); UIT-ViIC arXiv:2002.00175 (V listing); UIT-OpenViIC (arXiv id U). None reports
    CHAIR-style object extraction. A search for Vietnamese CHAIR / object hallucination in captioning found
    nothing (one search: U).
Overall: LOW-MODERATE as science, MODERATE as a resource. No prior Vietnamese object-mention extractor with an
existing-label validation protocol was found.

3.5 Tools and coverage (coverage.json; pyvi is installed, VnCoreNLP is not, locally)
  pyvi POS tags `cây` as Nc in BOTH `cây cầu` and `cây dừa`; it mis-tags `toà` (in `toà nhà`) as V and gives
  ô_tô/V in `xe ô tô`. Segmentation gets cửa_hàng, cầu_thang, quần_áo right. Design: segment + Nc +
  semantic-head rule; POS alone does not work.
  Lexicon coverage of classifier-marked NPs (Nc then N): KTVIC test refs 63.9% (n=2169); P1 short 73.6% (n=451);
  zero-shot detailed 45.8% (n=2511, of which 705 are the meta phrase `bức ảnh`).
  Top references outside the lexicon: chiếc đèn lồng 50, bức tường 49, con phố 42, bức tượng 35, tấm bia 33,
  chiếc xuồng 29, tấm bảng hiệu 14, đứa bé 13, cậu bé 9.
  Several are plain synonyms of existing classes, a RECALL gap: con phố -> đường, chiếc xuồng -> thuyền,
  tấm bảng hiệu -> biển hiệu, đứa bé/cậu bé -> trẻ em/bé trai.
  Mining classifier-headed NPs (Nc + N) gives a principled, frequency-ranked list for extending the lexicon.

6. RISKS AND FIT WITH PD-2
======================================================================================================
Risks:
  - Ad-hoc appearance. Mitigations: the agreement rule reuses the project's own NOUN_CLASSIFIER; compounds are
    frequency-mined; every rule is validated on human labels (3.4); the tables get human sign-off (Step 0).
  - Forking paths. The primary metric must not change after pre-registration, so v3 is a sensitivity analysis.
  - Test contamination. Discovery saw test references; re-derive the tables on train (3.6).
  - Small effect. About +1.3 F1 common-mode on P1: credibility, not a new result.
  - Coverage is ~64% of classifier-marked NPs, so recall stays lexicon-bounded. Extending the lexicon would
    move the frontier; keep that as a separate sensitivity analysis, or out of this paper.
Fit with PD-2: strengthens the EVALUATION/METHODOLOGY pillar:
  (i)   matched-count conclusions are shown to be robust to the extractor;
  (ii)  it fixes the three-way "same object" inconsistency (CHAIR / Table IV + frontier / UPE);
  (iii) a reusable Vietnamese object-mention extractor with a validation protocol built only on existing
        human labels.
  Paper footprint: one paragraph plus one table in the measurement section; the resource goes in the supplement.
