0. BOTTOM LINE
- The idea: score every caption system on two count-matched axes. rho_A is reference typicality: % TP above the count
  frontier against KTVIC's own 5 refs. rho_I is independent agreement: the same against the OpenViIC panel on the same
  555 images. Each frontier is log TP = a + b log M, fitted on the 5 pre-registered anchors.
- The map has 73 systems and two regimes. (a) Across trained systems the axes are LOCKED: all 36 KTVIC-fine-tuned
  systems (every current-pipeline method) sit at (0, 0); no gap CI excludes 0. All 37 zero-shot, training-free and
  legacy FAIR-pipeline systems sit at rho_A -55 but rho_I only -21. Reaching the KTVIC style buys typicality about 3x
  faster than independent agreement (median ratio 0.35; zero-shot -> P1: +35.2 vs +9.1). (b) Under per-image selection that reads references, the axes DECOUPLE. On dev300 at fixed count,
  picking by 2 KTVIC refs gains +6.5 F1 on 2 other held-out KTVIC refs but +1.2 on 2 panel captions (kappa 0.19);
  the CIDEr F4 result is +44.7 vs +0.07.
- Single summary: yes for systems, no for selection. rho_I (the count-matched panel residual) is the defensible scalar.
  Any method that reads references per image (MBR, reranking, RL reward) must also report a Goodhart index
  G = 1 - d(rho_I)/d(rho_A_heldout). Raw-F1 Pareto fronts are count fronts.
- Novelty: MODERATE. The "in-distribution vs independent-label" plane exists already (ImageNet ReaL, effective
  robustness, proxy-vs-gold reward overoptimisation). New here: two independent human caption panels on the same images,
  count-matched residuals per axis, and the locked-vs-decoupled two-regime result. Value: a general evaluation protocol
  for the PD-2 methods pillar, at about 0 GPU-h for the core and about 2 GPU-h for the confirmatory curve.

1. PROBLEM AND HYPOTHESIS
Setup. A caption names an object set m. Gold A = classes the dataset's own refs name (team-A salience). Gold I = classes
an independent team names (OpenViIC on KTVIC; COCO-80 instances on COCO / UIT-ViIC). P_g = micro precision,
R_g = per-image recall (object_prf.py). M = classes per caption. TP_g = P_g * M.
Model. A mention c is named by panel g with probability t_c * s_g(c): t_c is true presence and s_g is the panel's
salience. Training on A teaches a system s_A. It raises P_A at fixed truth, and raises P_I only through
corr(s_A, s_I). Count M moves both axes along fixed frontiers (frontier invariance, prereg 2026-10-12).
H1 (locked population). For systems trained on the same refs, (rho_A, rho_I) lie on one line through (0, 0). Methods move
   M, not the residuals.
   FALSIFIED IF a fine-tuned method has a rho_I - rho_A CI that excludes 0, or rho_I > 0 with its CI excluding 0.
H2 (style axis). Off-family systems (zero-shot, legacy, independent humans) pay more on A than on I: rho_I/rho_A < 0.5.
   FALSIFIED IF the median ratio is >= 0.7.
H3 (decoupled selection). Per-image selection on A refs transfers to held-out A refs far more than to I.
   kappa = d(F1_I)/d(F1_A_heldout) at matched count <= 0.35 (object) and <= 0.10 (CIDEr).
   FALSIFIED IF object kappa >= 0.7. In that case the axes are one, and a 1-D report suffices.
H4 (RL prediction, filed before 35_e's test look). A KTVIC-CIDEr-reward RL run raises KTVIC CIDEr. Its count-matched
   rho_I stays in [-1.5, +1.5], and its panel-CIDEr gain is <= 0.25x its KTVIC-CIDEr gain.
   FALSIFIED IF rho_I > +1.5 with the CI excluding 0.

PROPOSED SUMMARY, AND WHERE NONE EXISTS
- Systems (population regime): one scalar, rho_I with its CI, always shown beside rho_A on the 2-D frontier plot.
  Why it is defensible:
  (i) count is removed;
  (ii) among current-pipeline systems rho_A and rho_I are collinear (r 0.81, 0/36 gaps), so the choice of axis does
      not change rankings;
  (iii) where they diverge (off-style systems), A penalises atypical but true naming: OpenViIC humans score
       rho_A -26.6;
  (iv) the training signal cannot fit rho_I directly.
- Selection, MBR or RL that reads refs per image (instance regime): NO scalar. Report (d rho_A_held, d rho_I) and
  G = 1 - d rho_I / d rho_A_held. Per-image preferences of the two panels are near-orthogonal (kappa 0.19 object,
  about 0.002 CIDEr), so any weighted sum is a value choice. Dominance (both >= 0) is the only assumption-free claim.
- Rejected:
  - raw F1 on either axis: count-driven, corr(log M, F1_I) 0.84;
  - union-gold F1: it mixes the axes and weights them by panel size (OpenViIC names 6.0 classes per image vs
    KTVIC 3.7);
  - KTVIC CIDEr: Goodhart-prone (F4).

6. RISKS AND FIT WITH PD-2
Risks.
- The I axis is another team's salience, not truth. It is conservative by 11-46 points on unsupported mentions
  (3.6), and it penalises verbose but true captions. Mitigation: the COCO three-tier check (P2).
- Only one independent panel exists on KTVIC, and its guideline differs. rho_I may encode OpenViIC style. Symmetric
  check: OpenViIC humans score rho_A -26.6 on KTVIC.
- The locked result (H1) could be a power issue (SD about 2%), not a law. Report a TOST equivalence test on the
  gap with a pre-set margin of +-5% TP.
- Some reviewers may see "no method moves off the frontier" as a negative result. Frame it as a protocol result:
  it explains why KTVIC metric gains do not reflect content gains.
Fit. This strengthens the EVALUATION/METHODOLOGY pillar of PD-2 directly. It formalises the "count-matched primary
endpoint" as the rho_I axis and adds rho_A plus the Goodhart index for selection and RL methods. It also turns the
F4 anecdote into a measured curve. No new data, no annotation; OpenViIC is used for evaluation only.

7. SCORES (1-5)
Novelty 3: the plane and the Goodhart curve have close analogues (ReaL, effective robustness, RM overoptimisation).
  The two-panel, count-matched, two-regime form for captioning is not found in prior work (section 2).
Feasibility 5: the core is done on CPU in under a minute. The confirmatory curve is about 2 GPU-h.
Impact 4: it gives every PD-2 claim an axis label and makes the RL and MBR results interpretable. It is general to any
  dataset with two caption sources.
Risk 2: the main threat is the reading of the I axis as truth. The COCO tier tests it directly.
