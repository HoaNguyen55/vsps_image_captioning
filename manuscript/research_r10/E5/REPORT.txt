0. BOTTOM LINE
- Idea: report each system as k*, the number of pooled same-guideline annotators whose object coverage
  equals the system's. Coverage means the share of a fresh, held-out annotator's mentioned classes that
  are covered. The fold-averaged "k pooled vs 1 held-out" recall is exactly the incidence-based
  Good-Turing sample coverage of Chao & Jost. So k* gets standard extrapolation past the 5-ref panel
  (Chao-Jost; beta-binomial) and image-bootstrap CIs.
- Pilot on KTVIC test, k* [95% CI]:
  zero-shot short 0.61 [0.56,0.67]; zero-shot detailed 1.32 [1.19,1.48];
  P1 short 0.89 [0.83,0.94], P1 detailed 0.90 [0.84,0.96];
  fuse2 short 1.02 [0.96,1.12]; fuse2 detailed 1.75 [1.63,1.88].
  At a matched class count, every system covers less than a human pool: P1 -5.3, fuse2-det -15.1,
  zero-shot-det -35.9 coverage pts.
- Stability: with gold size h = 1..4 held-out refs, k* ranks are identical (Kendall tau 1.00), while
  F1 ranks flip (tau 0.35). k* is human-like flat for KTVIC-trained short systems (drift <= 0.03, CI
  spans 0) and drifts for detailed and zero-shot outputs (+0.20, +0.42): a salience diagnostic.
  k* is NOT portable across guidelines: in KTVIC units on OpenViIC gold, k* is 1.3-6.1x higher
  (falsified, tau 0.60).
- Novelty is LOW-MODERATE. "Rater equivalence" (Resnick et al., arXiv:2106.01254) already defines the
  smallest number of raters matching a classifier. What is new is set-valued coverage,
  species-accumulation extrapolation, the matched-count human frontier and the gold-size drift test.
  Value: an interpretable, reference-count-robust unit for the PD-2 evaluation pillar. It is not a method.

1. PROBLEM AND HYPOTHESES
Object P/R/F1 against caption-derived gold depends on how many references form the gold. Systems and
humans are then compared on different footings (1v4, 2v3, all 5 refs, OpenViIC, COCO). Proposal: express
coverage in human units.
Definition (protocol h): a fresh panel H of h annotators gives the gold G_H. A pool K of k annotators
drawn from the other 5-h gives the human comparator.
  R_h(X) = sum_i sum_(H,K) |X & G_H| / sum |G_H|   (ratio of sums over images and all folds)
  k*_h(sys) = R_h^-1(R_h(sys)) on the human pool curve.
- h=1 is incidence sample coverage: 1 - sum_c (Y_c/U) C(5-Y_c,k)/C(5-1,k), where Y_c is the number of
  annotators naming class c. kstar.py _check asserts this equals the fold average.
- Continuous k and k > 5:
  (a) Chao-Jost: log(1-C) is linear between integer k, then 1-C(k) = (Q1/U) g^(k-T+1) with
      g = (T-1)Q1/((T-1)Q1+2Q2).
  (b) Zero-truncated beta-binomial, p_c ~ Beta(a,b), fitted by ML to Q_1..Q_5:
      R_h(k) = [1 - E(1-p)^h - E(1-p)^k + E(1-p)^(h+k)] / [1 - E(1-p)^h]   (closed form, any real k, h).
- Count side:
  k_m = the pool size naming as many classes as the system (m(k): 1.74, 2.58, 3.10, 3.46, 3.72);
  dC = R_1(sys) - R_1(pool of k_m) is the human-frontier residual at matched count.
Predictions:
  P1  The 2-parameter beta-binomial (BB) reproduces every empirical human point (h=1..4) within 1 pt.
      HELD: max |dev| 0.81.
  P2  Within a guideline, k*_h is invariant to h for systems trained on that guideline
      (|k*_4 - k*_1| <= 0.1, CI incl. 0), while F1 changes. HELD for P1/fuse2-short/MBR.
      FAILED for detailed and zero-shot.
  P3  k* in KTVIC units is the same on KTVIC gold and OpenViIC gold (ratio in [0.8, 1.25]).
      FALSIFIED: 1.27-6.09.
  P4  No current system reaches the human pool at matched count (dC < 0). HELD for all 11.

6. RISKS AND FIT
 Risks:
 - Novelty is the main risk. Reviewers may read k* as relabeled recall plus rater equivalence. Lead with
   the matched-count human frontier and the gold-size invariance (tau 1.00 vs 0.35), not the unit.
 - Coverage-only. k* ignores precision, so always print k_m and dC next to it. Zero-shot detailed shows
   why: k* 1.32, but k_m > 10.
 - Annotator heterogeneity: the BB models class heterogeneity, not annotator heterogeneity (fold 5).
   Salience creates positive dependence, so the extrapolated asymptote is optimistic. Cap at 2T.
 - Guideline-bound (P3 falsified). The 113-class lexicon also hides off-lexicon objects.
 Fit with PD-2: strengthens the evaluation/methodology pillar.
 - (a) A human-anchored unit for every object claim: "P1 = 0.9 annotators, fuse2-det = 1.75".
 - (b) A non-fitted human frontier at matched count, which no system reaches (-5 to -15 pts).
   It complements the pre-registered system frontier.
 - (c) A reference-count robustness result: F1 rankings flip with gold size, k* rankings do not.
 - (d) A salience-drift diagnostic for detailed outputs.
 It does not solve "no METHOD survives count control".
