R10 / V2: ViPOPE+, a Vietnamese object-existence benchmark built from existing human labels  (2026-10-14)
Folder: research_r10/V2/. Pilot code: pilot_v2.py, pilot_v2b.py. Outputs: pilot_v2.json, pilot_v2b.json (aggregates only).

0. BOTTOM LINE
- Idea: Vietnamese probes with human-label positives; negatives only where labels are exhaustive or verified (COCO, XM3600+OIV7);
  KTVIC negatives aggregate-only, random mode, gated and panel-filtered; Vietnamese lexical negatives with an English control;
  and a joint per-object "seen but not said" analysis at matched FPR and matched object count.
- New: the multilingual POPEs we checked are translations (MHR, CLAIM; MHR's 13 languages exclude Vietnamese). None found has
  language-native lexical negatives or audits how contaminated caption-derived negatives are.
- Pilot lesson (UIT-ViIC, COCO truth): 0/5-mention popular/adversarial negatives are 33.7% present, still 18-19% after a second
  panel; capture-recapture underestimates this 3-6x; a salience gate cuts it to <=0.4%.
- Value: an evaluation pillar for PD-2 and a test of whether omissions are count-driven. Not a method win (optional S5 only).

1. PROBLEM AND HYPOTHESES
Translated POPE (data/results/pope_vi/) inherits three things:
- English-centric negatives;
- COCO label errors (RePOPE);
- no link between what a model affirms and what it says.
KTVIC has no instance labels, and its 5 references are incomplete (one annotator vs four: R 46.90, data/results/human_parity.json).
So "not mentioned" is not "absent".
H1 (label noise; pilot supports it): negatives from 0/5 caption mentions are contaminated in proportion to how often the class sits
   unmentioned in the background. Popular and adversarial modes are unusable without exhaustive labels. Random mode plus a salience
   gate is usable at <=0.5%.
   Falsified if: UIT-ViIC popular 0/5 contamination < 5%. Observed: 33.7%.
H2 (Vietnamese lexical negatives): for zero-shot Qwen2.5-VL-7B asked in Vietnamese, these items raise the FP rate by >= 3 points
   over matched non-lexical negatives (same image, same co-occurrence decile):
   - V1, shared head (lò vi sóng -> lò nướng, bình nước -> bình hoa, chậu cây -> chậu rửa, xe tải -> xe buýt);
   - V2, diacritic minimal pairs (voi/vòi, lò/lọ, ghe/ghế, diều/điều);
   - V3, classifier sense (cây vợt/cây dù -> cây cảnh; quả bóng -> quả cam/táo).
   The rise must shrink by >= 50% when the same items are asked in English. That English contrast separates lexical confusion from
   visual similarity, as in zebra/horse.
   Falsified if: the difference-in-differences CI includes 0 for all 3 systems.
H3 (gap): joint P(said | seen at 5% FPR, present) rises with object size (S < M < L).
   At matched object count, the model's said-set agrees with human said-sets about as well as humans agree with each other. If so,
   omissions are budget-driven, which is consistent with frontier invariance.
   Falsified if model-vs-human agreement at matched count is lower than human-vs-human, CI excluding 0. That would mean selection
   headroom exists, and it triggers Stage 5.
H4 (culture, exploratory): absent culture-typical classes (xe máy, nón/mũ, trâu, chợ, xích lô) draw more FP on Vietnam-locale XM3600
   images than on other locales, beyond the same contrast for control classes. Low power: 53 Vietnam-locale images carry OIV7 labels.

2. CLOSEST PRIOR WORK
V = verified in this session (arXiv page or search result); M = ID from memory, Gemini must verify.
- POPE, Li et al., EMNLP 2023, arXiv 2305.10355 [M; the RUCAIBox repo is pinned in scripts/pope_vi.py].
  English COCO yes/no probes with random/popular/adversarial negatives from instance labels.
  New here: Vietnamese-native negatives, an audit of caption-derived negatives, and the joint gap. Novelty vs it: moderate.
- RePOPE, Neuhaus & Hein 2025, arXiv 2504.15707 [V]. Re-annotates POPE and shows that label errors reorder models.
  New here: no new annotation; contamination is estimated from existing panels and ground-truth proxies. Moderate.
- AMBER, Wang et al. 2023, arXiv 2311.07397 [V]. Generative and discriminative tasks on the same images, with a coverage metric.
  English. It is closest for (c).
  New here: a per-object JOINT conditional P(said | seen) by class and size, at matched FPR and count, against human omission.
  Moderate.
- MME, Fu et al. 2023, arXiv 2306.13394 [M]. Its existence subtask is tiny and English/Chinese. Low overlap. High novelty vs it.
- MHR, Qu et al. 2024, arXiv 2408.00550 [V]. POPE translated by GPT-3.5 into 12 languages (ru de zh ja fr es pt uk bg tr ar ko).
  No Vietnamese; translated negatives. New here: Vietnamese, native lexical negatives. Moderate-high.
- CLAIM, Ye et al., ACL 2025, arXiv 2506.11073 [V for the paper; language list UNVERIFIED]. Translated POPE and MME
  hallucination subsets; a method paper. Moderate-high.
- MHaluBench / UNIHD, Chen et al., ACL 2024, arXiv 2402.03190 [V]. A meta-benchmark for hallucination detectors, English.
  It is not a multilingual POPE. Low overlap.
- THRONE, Kaul et al., CVPR 2024, arXiv 2405.05256 [V]. Free-form (Type I) and yes/no (Type II) hallucination diverge.
  It compares benchmarks, not per-object joint measures. Moderate.
- PatchGate, Ko et al., arXiv 2608.21819 (Aug 2026) [V abstract only]. A "verbalization gap" between an intrinsic object set and
  the objects mentioned, measured with AMBER coverage and CHAIR, in English.
  This is the closest to (c). If its full text has per-object probe-vs-caption analysis by size, (c) novelty drops to low.
- HOPE, Xie et al. 2025, arXiv 2508.06530 [V]. CLIP-chosen content-aware distractors, English.
  Ours are lexical and orthographic distractors with a cross-lingual control. Moderate-high.
- NOPE, Lovenia et al., ALVR 2023/24, arXiv 2310.05338 [V]. LLM-generated negative-pronoun VQA. Low overlap.
- HallE-Control, Zhai et al., arXiv 2310.01779 [V]. CCEval: VQA benchmarks understate caption hallucination. Moderate for (c).
- DSC2025 ViHallu, Nguyen et al., arXiv 2601.04711 [V]. Vietnamese hallucination detection, TEXT ONLY. It is not visual.
- Data sources [M]: XM3600 (Thapliyal et al., EMNLP 2022, arXiv 2205.12522); Open Images V7 verified image-level labels
  (Kuznetsova et al., IJCV 2020, arXiv 1811.00982); UIT-ViIC (arXiv 2002.00175); KTVIC (arXiv 2401.08100).
Overall novelty: MODERATE.
- No single piece is unprecedented.
- The combination is not in any source we found: Vietnamese, exhaustive or verified negatives, a ground-truth audit of
  caption-derived negatives, lexical negatives with an English control, and a joint gap at matched count.
- One web search found no Vietnamese visual object-hallucination benchmark. Make no "first" claim.

3. EVIDENCE SO FAR (CPU; pilot_v2.py 66 s, pilot_v2b.py ~60 s; 38 MB RAM; nice 19, 1 thread)
A. UIT-ViIC: 3,846 COCO images, 5 Vietnamese and 5 English refs each.
   Truth: data/uitviic/instance_gold.json. Matchers: vi_objects v2 and en_classes (scripts/score_coco_probe.py).
   - Positives named by >=1 of 5 vi refs: 99.1% present (>=2: 99.4%). Ref-union positives are sound.
   - Negatives from 0/5 vi mentions, 3 per image per mode (n = 11,538 each). % truly present:
       mode          0/5      0/5 + EN panel 0/5   Chapman estimate   salience gate   gate + panel
       random        2.34     1.40                 0.23               0.20            0.06
       popular      33.73    17.91                 5.42               0.39            0.13
       adversarial  33.74    19.24                 5.47               0.19            0.10
   - Capture-recapture underestimates 3-6x, because misses are correlated: background objects are missed by both panels.
   - The gate is q_c = (1-p_c)^5 <= 0.02, with p_c the zero-truncated binomial MLE.
     It keeps 8 of 80 classes: bear, bed, cat, dog, elephant, horse, kite, person. So it is clean but much less hard.
   - q_c is NOT calibrated: predicted P(0/5 | present) is ~0, but the true value is 0.08-0.33 for the kept animals.
     The gate works because unmentioned prevalence is low, so use it as a ranking rule, not as a probability.
   - Human "said" rates (>=1 of 5 refs | present) show heavy human omission of background classes, so any model gap must be
     referenced to a human baseline:
     person 99.0, sports ball 56.4, tennis racket 41.8, baseball bat 31.3, glove 6.4, bottle 4.4, car 1.9, chair 1.7, bench 0.8,
     handbag 0.0.
   - Southern 'banh' (ball) is missing from the lexicon, but appears in only 76 of 19,256 captions, so its effect is minor.
B. KTVIC test: 555 images with the OpenViIC panel (data/ktvic_openviic_refs.json, 5.39 captions per image), 87 lexicon classes.
   Aggregates only; no caption text was written.
   - Share of 0/5 negatives that the independent panel names: random 4.62%, popular 32.37%, adversarial 34.11%.
   - (image, class) pairs named: refs 2,513, panel 3,981, both 1,929.
   - Chapman residual after the panel filter: 1.97 / 5.54 / 5.90%. Given A's 3-6x underestimate, the plausible true residual is
     about 6-35%.
   - The gate keeps 11 classes, but the panel still names 4.56 / 9.25 / 8.77% of what passes, because person-attribute classes
     (giày, quần, cô gái, học sinh) get through.
   => KTVIC negatives are not usable at item level. KTVIC is used for positives and for the gap analysis; its negatives appear in
      aggregate only, with bounds.
C. Joint seen and said: POPE-vi probe replies x COCO-2500 captions, on the 32 overlapping images (83 present pairs).
   P(said | probe yes, present):
   - zero-shot: short 37.5, detailed 79.7;
   - P1 s43: short 40.5, detailed 36.5;
   - fuse2 s42: short 42.4, detailed 51.5.
   Said-but-not-seen: 0-1 of 83. So correct caption mentions are a subset of probe-yes answers.
   This is a joint estimate where R9 C7 used a ratio of marginals from different image sets, and it supports R9's "verbalization,
   not sight" reading. n is tiny (CI about ±12 points). Stage 2 scales it up.
D. Lexicon (data/coco_probe/coco80_vi.json):
   - 22 shared first syllables: xe x6 classes, máy x5, bánh x5, ván x4, ghế x3, bàn x3, ly/cốc, bình, chậu, lò, bồn, tàu, gấu,
     ngựa, đồng, quả/trái, cây, con, cái, chiếc, ô.
   - Diacritic minimal pairs across classes: voi/vòi, lò/lọ, ghe/ghế, diều/điều, cừu/cứu, bóng/bông, ván/vằn, tủ/từ, bành/bánh.
   - Items available on COCO-2500 plus UIT-ViIC (6,350 images, exhaustive COCO labels):
     V1: 1,895 items on 1,498 images;
     V2: 1,338 items (chair->boat alone 858; cap per pair);
     V3: 13,714 items (subsample);
     H (bench/couch -> chair, where the Vietnamese hypernym is ambiguous): 443, kept only as a separate diagnostic.
E. XM3600 + OIV7 (data/xm3600/manifest.json): 2,119 images with a verified label on 68 mapped Vietnamese classes.
   - Pairs: 4,453 verified present and 8,276 verified absent.
   - Vietnam-locale images: 100, of which 53 have OIV7 labels.
F. Existing POPE-vi yes-ratio: P1 42.7 vs zero-shot 36.0 (vi, random; data/results/pope_vi/scores.txt).
   Raw "yes" is therefore biased across systems, so "seen" must be defined at a matched FPR.

4. PILOT DESIGN FOR GEMINI
Build (CPU, under 5 min): new script scripts/vipope_build.py, plus a --items flag for scripts/pope_vi.py.
- Output: items.jsonl rows {set, image, class, prompt, label, mode, pair_of, size_bucket, ref_k}.
- Prompt: pope_vi's "Trong ảnh có {term} không? Trả lời có hoặc không." with the first coco80_vi term
  (or the colliding form for V2).
- The --items flag asks arbitrary items and records first-token log P(có) and log P(không) (and yes/no for English) next to the
  greedy answer. One forward pass per item.
Sets:
- COCO: COCO-2500 (data/coco_probe/instance_gold.json; sizes from instances_val2014 areas: S < 32², M, L > 96²,
  by largest instance).
  - Positives: all present classes.
  - POPE-recipe random/popular/adversarial negatives from instance labels.
  - V1/V2/V3 items, capped at 60 per pair, each with a matched control: same image, an absent class with no lexical overlap,
    in the same co-occurrence decile as the pair's present class.
  - Diacritic-stripped copies (with and without classifier) of all V2 items, their controls, and 500 random items.
  - On the 32 POPE images, use RePOPE labels.
- XM3600: every verified pair (labels flagged as machine-proposed but human-verified), with the VN-locale flag.
- KTVIC test:
  - positives = ref-union classes, with k-of-5 recorded;
  - negatives = random mode only: 0/5 refs AND 0 panel mentions AND gated classes, excluding person-attribute classes;
  - aggregate only, never released per item.
Stages (GPU-h assume 0.35 s/item on a 3090; Gemini should VERIFY from the original pope_vi logs):
- S1, H2: V1/V2/V3, controls and stripped variants, in vi and en.
  About 6.3k items per system per language. 3 systems (zero-shot, P1 s43, fuse2 s42) x vi, plus en for zero-shot and P1:
  about 3.1 GPU-h.
- S2, H3: COCO-2500 positives (~7.2k) plus 1 random negative per image (2.5k, used to set each system's 5%-FPR threshold).
  Captions already exist (data/results/coco_transfer, coco5000). 3 systems: about 2.8 GPU-h.
- S3, H4 and the verified split: XM3600, 12.7k items per system, 2 systems: about 2.5 GPU-h.
  Add about 1 GPU-h for captions if job 39_xm3600_eval has not produced them.
- S4, KTVIC aggregate: about 4.2k items per system, 2 systems: about 0.8 GPU-h.
- S5, optional method: probe-ranked object selection on KTVIC dev300 (300 x 87 classes).
  About 2.5 GPU-h, or about 0.5 GPU-h if the image KV-prefix is reused.
- Core total S1-S4: about 9-10 GPU-h. CPU total: under 0.5 h.
Scoring and endpoints:
- PRIMARY-H2: paired, image-clustered bootstrap (B = 2000) of FP(V) - FP(matched control), and the DiD (vi - en),
  per system and per V type.
- PRIMARY-H3:
  - joint P(said | seen@5% FPR, present) per size bucket;
  - per-image said-set agreement with each human ref, at matched object count. Compare it with leave-one-out human-vs-human
    agreement. Matched count is the PD-2 rule: compare to top-k_i at the model's own k_i.
- PD-2 count control (S5): probe-rank top-k_i vs raw-sample-vote-share top-k_i at the SAME k_i; paired ΔF1 on dev300 only.
  This counts as a model-generated signal inside a method, and is flagged as such.
- KTVIC FP: FP_true = (FP_obs - c*R)/(1 - c), with c bounded by [panel-detected = 0, UIT-ViIC-calibrated upper bound].
  Report the interval.
Pre-registration before any look (manuscript/prereg/), with frozen items.jsonl hash, matcher v2, thresholds and the kill rules.
- K1: DiD < 1.0 point, or CI includes 0, for all systems -> drop the Vietnamese-negative claim and report it as null.
- K2: diacritic-stripped answer-flip rate < 2% -> drop that arm.
- K3: S < M < L fails for >= 2 of 3 systems, or all CIs are wider than ±5 points -> descriptive only.
- K4: KTVIC corrected-FP interval wider than 10 points -> report "not estimable".
- K5: S5 ΔF1 < +0.5, or CI includes 0, on dev300 -> stop. Test split is never used.

6. RISKS AND FIT WITH PD-2
Risks:
- Qwen2.5-VL may simply not confuse diacritics or heads, so K1 fires. A null result is still a clean finding.
- Visual similarity confounds V1 (zebra/horse, teddy bear/bear). The English DiD addresses it, but not perfectly, because English
  "teddy bear" also contains "bear".
- COCO label errors: RePOPE relabelled 139 of POPE's 1,500 'yes' questions, and the 'no' error rate is 0.3-2.6%.
  These errors remain outside the POPE-500 images.
- OIV7 negatives are sparse, and their candidates were proposed by a machine, which adds a selection bias. The labels themselves
  are human-verified.
- The salience gate makes KTVIC negatives easy, so KTVIC hallucination numbers are weak by design.
- Low power for H4.
- Novelty is moderate. PatchGate (2026), AMBER and THRONE cover parts of (c), and MHR and CLAIM cover multilingual POPE.
Fit with PD-2: this strengthens the evaluation and methodology pillar.
(i) It gives a general, quantified warning for every benchmark that derives negatives from captions, a problem common in
    low-resource languages, with a ground-truth audit and a failed capture-recapture fix.
(ii) It gives a Vietnamese probe resource whose labels come only from humans.
(iii) H3 tests directly whether omissions are count-driven. If they are, that explains why UPE and PLAN-F1 collapse to count shifts.
     If not, S5 is the one cheap method test at matched count.

7. SCORES (1-5)
- Novelty 3: the combination is new, but each piece has neighbours (MHR, CLAIM, AMBER, THRONE, PatchGate, HOPE).
- Feasibility on 1x3090: 5. The data is local, the build is CPU-only, and the core is about 9-10 GPU-h.
- Expected impact 3: a solid evaluation section and a reusable lesson on negative contamination, but not a method that beats the
  frontier unless S5 survives.
- Risk 2 (low): the main risk is a null lexical effect, which is still reportable. The KTVIC side is already de-risked by the pilot.
