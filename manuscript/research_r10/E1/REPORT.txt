R10 / E1 -- COUNT-CONTROLLED RE-EVALUATION OF PUBLISHED (ENGLISH) HALLUCINATION-REDUCTION METHODS
2026-10-14. Agent E1. CPU only: one pilot, nice 19, 1 thread, 170 MB RSS, 3.3 min wall. No VLM loaded, no labels made.
Files (all in research_r10/E1/): e1_chair_count.py (CHAIR re-implementation + count controls), e1_results.json/.log,
e1_decomposition.txt, dl/ (downloaded public outputs and COCO annotations; untrusted data, never on sys.path).

==================================================================================================================
0. BOTTOM LINE
==================================================================================================================
- Idea: re-score PUBLISHED captions of English hallucination methods at matched per-image object count. Controls:
  (a) MCP = matched-prefix: the first k_i distinct COCO classes of each caption, k_i = min(method_i, baseline_i);
  (b) TRF = the baseline's own truncation frontier at the method's mean count.
  Together they split a CHAIR gain into: mean-count shift + per-image count allocation + better object choice.
- Pilot on released per-caption outputs (OPERA repo: 4 LVLMs; Less-is-More repo: VCD, OPERA, OPERA-fast, LiM-EOS):
  - 12 of 15 method-vs-own-baseline gains are significant in raw class precision. Only 3 of 15 survive MCP.
  - One gain reverses: OPERA-Shikra vs beam5, -1.23 [-2.37, -0.08].
  - Mean dP falls from +3.4 (raw) to +0.6 at matched count: about 80% of the published precision gain is count.
  - Examples: OPERA/LLaVA-1.5 +0.07 [-0.93, +1.21]; VCD/LLaVA-1.5 +0.06 [-0.42, +0.53]; LiM-EOS/LLaVA-1.5-7B
    +7.9 raw -> +1.08 [-0.14, +2.25].
- Why it is new: HallE-Control/CCEval balances MEAN length through prompting, and says it does not enforce it.
  SumGD and others only report recall next to CHAIR. No verified work re-scores other groups' released outputs at
  per-image matched count with paired CIs, or gives this three-part split.
- Value: our KTVIC finding "no method survives count control" turns into a general, citable fact about the field.
  Phase A is CPU-only (done for 2 repos); the Phase B GPU extension takes about 15 GPU-h.

==================================================================================================================
1. PROBLEM AND HYPOTHESES (falsifiable)
==================================================================================================================
Problem:
- CHAIR is precision-type: naming fewer objects lowers it for free (early mentions are more precise). Papers report
  CHAIR, sometimes recall or AMBER Cover next to it, but never AT matched count.
H1 (decoding methods: VCD, OPERA, M3ID, ICD, DoLa, HALC, PAI ...):
- Of pairs with raw CHAIR_i gain >= 1 point, >= 2/3 have |dP_MCP| < 1.0 or a CI covering 0. Falsified if > 1/3 keep
  a Holm-significant dP_MCP >= +1.0.
H2 (stopping or length methods: LiM-EOS, "Less is More"-style data filtering, EOS bias):
- Gain = mean-count shift + per-image allocation: dP_TRF > 0, dP_MCP ~ 0. Falsified if Holm-significant
  dP_MCP >= +1.0 on >= 2 of 3 base models.
H3 (preference or RL training: RLHF-V, RLAIF-V, CHAIR-DPO, HA-DPO, POVID):
- They change object CHOICE, so median dP_MCP(training) > median dP_MCP(decoding). A genuine prediction; can fail.
H4: the base frontier TP = a * M^b has b in [0.5, 0.8] for English long captions (KTVIC 0.7468). Placebo: two
sampling seeds of one model give |dP_MCP| < 0.5.

- Source: SumGD Table 7 (arXiv HTML), LLaVA-1.5-7B, CHAIR_S/CHAIR_I/Recall at 64/128/256/512 tokens.
- Count proxy: M ~ R / (1 - CHAIR_i); TP ~ R.

- dCHAIR_i vs greedy at the same budget, then at matched count:
  - M3ID: -1.0 to -3.2 -> -0.3 to -0.9 (70-90% of its gain is count);
  - OPERA: about 0 -> about 0 (+-0.8);

- No CIs here. CHAIR_i is mention-weighted (r1: class precision 73.3 vs 1 - CHAIR_i 84.7, from repeated correct

PRE-REGISTRATION (write before any Phase-B look)
- Primary endpoint:
  - dP_MCP = class-level precision of the first k_i* = min(k_method, k_base) distinct COCO classes, method minus its
    own published baseline;
  - paired bootstrap over images, 2,000 resamples, Holm over all listed pairs;
  - SURVIVES iff Holm p < 0.05 AND dP_MCP >= +1.0 (SESOI).
- Secondary:
  - dP_TRF (uniform truncation at matched mean count), and the 3-part decomposition;
  - dF1 at matched count, which equals dP_MCP by construction;
  - b of the base frontier.
- Exploratory: aggregate-table screen (3d) for papers without outputs.
- Kill rules:
  - K1 fidelity: our CHAIR differs from published by > 1.0 (CHAIR_i) or > 1.5 (CHAIR_s) on more than 20% of
    reference rows -> fix the matcher before any confirmatory look.
  - K2 placebo: |dP_MCP| > 1.0 or CI excludes 0 for the seed placebo -> the control is miscalibrated; stop.
  - K3 power: positive control dP_MCP < +3 -> the test is underpowered; enlarge to 2,000 images (the COCO-2500
    manifest exists).
  - K4 thesis: if more than 1/3 of the pre-listed decoding pairs SURVIVE, drop the "published gains are count shifts"
    headline and report "count control is necessary and changes the ranking".
Decision: the paper writes up the result whichever way it falls; both outcomes support the count-matched endpoint.

6. RISKS AND FIT WITH PD-2
==================================================================================================================
Risks:
- Prefix truncation is the natural count knob for released text, but it is still one choice.
  - B1 EOS-bias points and the confidence-ranked control (B-only) test whether conclusions depend on it.
  - Truncation breaks fluency, so the count-matched analysis is for object metrics only, never CIDEr.
- Weak or degenerate baselines inflate survivors. Example: InstructBLIP greedy has CHAIR_i 23.0 vs 15.3 for beam5
  (repetition loops). Report every method against both greedy and its own base decoder.
- Small corpus (2 repos, 4+4 models); groups that release outputs may differ. Shared baselines make pairs
  dependent: Holm, per-pair reporting, no pooled p-value.
- CHAIR's 80-class vocabulary and synonym quirks; our regex/vocabulary matcher still reproduces published numbers
  within 0.6.
- Reviewer reply: "obviously shorter is more precise". The answer is the decomposition: the field reports the raw
  number, and the per-image allocation share is a real but different contribution.
Fit with PD-2: this strengthens the EVALUATION / METHODOLOGY pillar.
- The count-matched primary endpoint is shown to be general (English, COCO, 8 LVLMs), not a KTVIC quirk.
- It reframes "no method survives count control" as the field's norm.
- One section (and one figure: raw vs MCP per method) fits the Q2 paper. A standalone short paper is possible if
  Phase A2/B add 10+ methods.
- It costs nothing from the KTVIC GPU queue unless Phase B runs.
