1. PROBLEM AND HYPOTHESIS
Vietnamese spaces separate syllables, not words, so every n-gram metric depends on a chain of choices:
segmenter, tone placement, case, punctuation, CIDEr-D settings, IDF source and reference panel. Papers
rarely report these choices (KTVIC Table 3 mixed them by accident).
H1 (level): the choices move absolute CIDEr by more than published between-method gaps (GRIT gap = 12.2).
  Falsified if every protocol in the stress set moves P1-assist by < 5 CIDEr.  -> NOT falsified (+16.1).
H2 (within-family stability): among systems with the same output style, the ranking is stable. Pass if
  tau_FT >= 0.9 and 0 significant reversals under every stress protocol.  -> HOLDS (min tau 0.905).
H3 (interaction): the effects depend on the system (punctuation, case, length), so cross-family deltas are
  protocol artefacts. Falsified if the |delta| of a cross-family gap varies < 20% across the stress set.
  -> NOT falsified (MBR gap 18.7..63.8; FT/zero-shot ratio 4.0x..6.6x).
H4 (panel): a reference panel with another guideline reorders the FT cluster. Falsified if
  tau_FT(OpenViIC refs) >= 0.8.  -> NOT falsified (-0.05). Still needs the capped-refs check (sec. 5, item 6).

2. CLOSEST PRIOR WORK (IDs checked by web search on 2026-10-14 unless marked)
a) Post 2018, "A Call for Clarity in Reporting BLEU Scores", WMT18, arXiv:1804.08771 / W18-6319.
   Reference tokenisation moves BLEU by up to 1.8; introduces sacreBLEU signatures. New here: captioning
   metrics (CIDEr-D), Vietnamese segmentation, rank stability with bootstrap, reference-panel effect.
b) Grusky 2023, "Rogue Scores", ACL 2023, DOI 10.18653/v1/2023.acl-long.107 (no arXiv found). ROUGE
   configurations go unreported or are buggy across 2,000+ papers. New: captioning, Vietnamese, CIDEr.
c) Marie, Fujita, Rubino 2021, "Scientific Credibility of MT Research", ACL 2021, arXiv:2106.15195.
   Meta-evaluation of 769 MT papers: BLEU deltas without significance tests. New: measures the effect
   instead of surveying practice.
d) Li, Zong, Ng 2011, "Automatic Evaluation of Chinese Translation Output: Word-Level or Character-
   Level?", ACL 2011, P11-2028. Character-level metrics correlate better with humans (MT, Chinese). The
   closest analogue for segmented languages. New: captioning, Vietnamese, and rank and significance
   stability rather than correlation.
e) dos Santos, Colombini, Avila 2021, "CIDEr-R", W-NUT@EMNLP 2021, arXiv:2109.13701. CIDEr-D's sigma=6
   length penalty is ill-suited to non-COCO length distributions (Portuguese). New: here sigma is one
   factor among many, and the punctuation/max-IDF mechanism is quantified.
f) Freitag, Grangier, Caswell 2020, "BLEU might be Guilty but References are not Innocent", EMNLP 2020,
   arXiv:2004.06063. References drive metric validity. New: an independent second human panel
   (OpenViIC) reorders a captioning leaderboard.
g) Chen et al. 2015, "Microsoft COCO Captions: Data Collection and Evaluation Server", arXiv:1504.00325.
   PTB tokenisation and punctuation REMOVAL; the de facto English card. New: the Vietnamese pipeline here
   keeps "." as a token, which differs from COCO.
h) Vedantam et al. 2015, CIDEr, arXiv:1411.5726. Defines the TF-IDF consensus. IDF from the scored refs
   is the source of the max-IDF penalty on unseen tokens.
i) Context only, protocols NOT verified: STAIR Captions arXiv:1705.00823 (Japanese), COCO-CN
   arXiv:1805.08661, AI Challenger arXiv:1711.06475 (Chinese). I could not confirm their MeCab/jieba
   evaluation tokenisation. UNVERIFIED: Gehrmann, Clark, Sellam 2022 survey, arXiv:2202.06935 (search
   failed).
Searches for "Vietnamese captioning protocol sensitivity" returned nothing relevant. Searches also

PROTOCOL CARD (one line per scored table; signature modelled on sacreBLEU):
  data=ktvic-test|N=558|ids=sha1:<..>|refs=5,field=caption,panel=KTVIC|norm=NFC+tone:vnc|case=lc|
  punct=token(.)|seg=VnCoreNLP-1.2/RDR(hyp+ref)|cider=D,n4,s6,df=test-refs|bleu=closest|rouge=b1.2|
  meteor=1.5,-l en|impl=pycocoevalcap-1.2|scale=x100|ckpt-select=dev300|vocab=train-only
ROBUST REPORTING RULE:
 R1 Compare a number only with numbers that carry an identical signature. Otherwise re-score the other
    system's released predictions (as done for KTVIC T3), or do not compare.
 R2 Claim "A > B" only if all three hold:
      - the P0 paired-bootstrap 95% CI excludes 0;
      - the sign holds under every protocol in S;
      - the OpenViIC-panel delta is reported next to it, whatever its sign.
 R3 Give each headline delta its protocol range [min, max] over S.
 R4 Main table at word level (P0). Syllable level goes in the appendix with its own signature.
    Never mix the two.

  the T3 correction, the max-IDF punctuation mechanism and the panel test are what make it new; keep
  claims modest.
- The P12 panel uses variable ref counts (2-20) and a different guideline. Treat it as a style-robustness
  check, not ground truth (K2).
- The MBR punctuation finding cuts against nothing, but it changes how UPE/MBR CIDEr costs should be
  described. Tell the owners of those sections.
- Policy: OpenViIC is used only as an evaluation panel; scores are aggregate; no caption text is written
  (U-002 respected).
Fit with PD-2: this directly strengthens the evaluation/methodology pillar (the main contribution).
- Section "Evaluation protocol": card, signature, lint, R1-R4.
- Appendix table: 26 systems x 15 protocols.
- Erratum-style footnote on KTVIC T3.
- It also supports the paper's reference-style thesis: CIDEr on KTVIC rewards KTVIC style, and the
  OpenViIC panel flattens FT differences.
