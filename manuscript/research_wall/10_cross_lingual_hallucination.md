# 10. Cross-lingual hallucination and faithfulness outside English (agent 10, 2026-10-10)

Scope: hallucination and faithfulness work on captioning and VLMs in Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Thai, Indonesian, Indic languages, Arabic and multilingual settings; translated caption datasets and their artefacts. Items already covered in `../novelty/09_multilingual_lowresource.md` and `../novelty/05_vietnamese_captioning.md` are not repeated: MHR/TOMM 2026, CLAIM, CCHall, MELLA, PALO, SHEEP, SHROOM-Visions, Multi-PixMo/Multi-POPE, KOFFVQA, ThaiOCRBench, HalDec-Bench, Multilingual FActScore, Poly-FEVER, LIBRA, GAIC, VisualRoBERTa, VELVET, LaVy, Vista, Med-StepBench and ViHallu.

**Fact check on how VSPS uses COCO.** The brief says "COCO-2014 in Vietnamese". The repo does **not** translate the COCO captions. VSPS and zero-shot generate Vietnamese captions for COCO images. Objects are then mapped to the 80 COCO classes through the frozen dictionary `data/coco_probe/coco80_vi.json`, and gold comes from the English `instances_val2014` annotations. This dictionary has 232 surface forms. Its `_note` still says "BẢN NHÁP" (draft), and 8 entries are flagged `_rui_ro` (risky): `bò` is also a verb, `chén`/`dĩa` mean different things in northern and southern Vietnamese, bare `cam` is also a colour, and `con chuột` can be the animal or the computer mouse. So the translation artefacts reviewers will ask about are (i) lexicon mapping error in the CHAIR-vi/COCO metric, and (ii) translationese in the backbone's Vietnamese, which `rescap/pipeline/realize.py` and `rescap/vi/classifier.py` already target. They are not artefacts of translated training captions. The paper text should say this in one sentence (§IV, the COCO paragraph), or reviewers will assume translated COCO captions.

## (b) Overlap risks (max 5)
1. **DEJIMA (#6), medium.** Language-specific, grounding-constrained caption supervision for Japanese, with human-rated factuality. Defence: VSPS uses typed propositions, a 3-state dual-query VLM verdict, neutralization and span traceability, and it measures hallucination (CHAIR, gender). DEJIMA uses detector evidence plus LLM rewriting and has no hallucination evaluation.
2. **Judge-corrector (#5), low–medium on C2.** A three-way verdict whose middle class means "needs the image to decide", routed to repair instead of deletion. Defence: it judges translation correctness, not visual propositions, and its repair is regeneration, not specificity reduction.
3. **M²CQA/CFHR (#1), low.** The "affirmative + counterfactual" probe resembles VSPS's dual query. Defence: M²CQA is an evaluation metric; VSPS uses the dual query to *build* SFT targets. Cite it so the dual-query design is not presented as new by itself.
4. **"First Vietnamese" claim, low.** MM-JudgeBench (#3) includes Vietnamese, but only for judging. No paper found in this sweep measures generative object/gender hallucination in Vietnamese captioning. The qualified claim in `novelty/05` still holds.
5. **Reviewer misreading "COCO in Vietnamese" as translated COCO.** This is not a paper overlap but the most likely reviewer question in this angle. Fix it with one sentence plus Action 1.

## (c) Must-compare baselines / must-cite (max 5)
1. **Lupascu et al., Information Fusion 2026 (#19).** Q1 journal survey that names hallucination in low-resource LMMs as open and already covers Vintern-1B.
2. **M²CQA, Findings ACL 2026 (#1).** Most recent evidence that VLM hallucination rises outside English, and the closest metric analogue to the dual query.
3. **Hinck et al. 2024 (#2) + a language-fidelity column.** Cheap, and pre-empts "does SFT break Vietnamese output?"
4. **STAIR Captions (#10) + COCO-CN (#9) + Kaleidoscope (#7).** Canonical evidence that native > translated. Justifies KTVIC-first evaluation and the translation-free COCO probe.
5. **Optional zero-shot multilingual-LVLM row: Centurio (#12) or Pangea (#13).** Only if the Q2 reviewer is likely to ask "why not a multilingual model?"; MHR and CLAIM remain the multilingual-hallucination baselines (already planned).

## (d) Actionable improvements, ranked by expected gain / cost

1. **Audit the COCO dictionary and add an English-pivot CHAIR cross-check.** Cost: about 0.5–1 GPU-h. Risk: low.
   - Repo changes:
     - `data/coco_probe/coco80_vi.json`: finish the review the `_note` asks for (remove "BẢN NHÁP"; decide the 8 `_rui_ro` entries).
     - `scripts/score_coco_probe.py`: add `--pivot en`. It translates each Vietnamese caption to English with the backbone itself (Qwen2.5-VL-7B in text mode) or NLLB, then scores standard Rohrbach CHAIR with the English synonym list. Report Spearman/agreement between the dictionary-based and the pivot CHAIR on COCO5000.
   - Effect: no change to recall or precision. It removes the reviewer objection "your Vietnamese CHAIR is an artefact of a hand-made dictionary". If the two agree (likely, given 232 surface forms), COCO becomes a strong cross-lingual result.
   - Runtime: about 10K captions × 2 systems of text-only MT, under 1 h on an RTX 3090.
   - Risk detail: MT can add or drop objects. Mitigate by checking a 100-caption sample by hand (JAMMEval-style).
2. **Zero-shot language-gap table: same COCO5000 images, English vs Vietnamese prompt.** Cost: about 4–6 GPU-h. Risk: low.
   - Repo change: `scripts/coco_probe.py` gets a `--lang en` prompt variant; English CHAIR via the standard COCO synonyms.
   - Effect: quantifies how much more zero-shot Qwen2.5-VL hallucinates and misses objects in Vietnamese than in English, the MHR/M²CQA claim tested on Vietnamese, with no new training. If VSPS closes part of that gap, it is a new cross-lingual result for the journal version.
   - Runtime: about 5,000 detailed greedy captions; estimate ≈3–4 s/image → ~5 h.
   - Risk: none for the paper. The worst case is "no gap", which still informs the framing.
3. **Language-fidelity metric (no GPU).**
   - Repo change: a 30-line `scripts/language_fidelity.py`, or a flag in `scripts/evaluate.py`, reusing the `out_of_script`/CJK regexes from `scripts/clean_supervision.py`. It reports the % of captions with English/CJK spans and the % with code-switching, for zero-shot, unverified SFT and VSPS on KTVIC and COCO.
   - Effect: shows that SFT on Vietnamese-native rendered targets does not degrade, and probably improves, output-language faithfulness (Hinck et al.).
   - Cost and risk: 0 GPU, near-zero risk.
4. **CFHR-style verifier calibration, then a stricter SUPPORTED rule for relations (targets verifier precision, 63.1%).**
   - Repo change: from the stored K=5 affirmative/negated answers (`scripts/audit_props.py`, `scripts/relation_audit.py`), compute P(neg = yes \| aff = yes) per type. Against the human audit (`scripts/machine_vs_human.py`), test whether "aff ≥ k/5 AND neg ≤ m/5" with type-specific (k, m) raises SUPPORTED precision. Relations are the obvious target, where humans rejected 42.9% of machine-SUPPORTED items.
   - Effect: higher verifier precision, and it answers reviewer 2's relation inconsistency. It may lower recall slightly, because more relations get demoted to UNCERTAIN.
   - Cost: offline, 0 GPU for the analysis. Re-rendering plus one QLoRA epoch is about 1 day if adopted.
   - Risk: medium (recall trade-off).
5. **Cross-lingual entity proposal, to raise proposal recall (a hypothesis; check cheaply first).**
   - Diagnose first: `scripts/recall_funnel.py` already shows where recall is lost. Run it first. Act only if the proposal stage (not the verifier or the 9-proposition budget) is the main loss.
   - The change: `rescap/pipeline/generate.py` `PROMPT_ENTITIES` adds a second, English entity-listing pass. Its nouns are mapped to Vietnamese via `rescap/vi/lexicon` (not free MT) and then verified in Vietnamese as usual.
   - Effect: English prompting usually lists more objects (per MHR/M²CQA). Since every added entity is still verified, precision should hold, and recall of SFT targets could rise by a few points.
   - Cost and risk: re-running stage 1 is a large share of the 88 GPU-h. Pilot on 300 images (~7 GPU-h). Medium risk: it conflicts with the design note "Vietnamese-native surface forms (H5)". Keep the English pass for proposals only, never for surface text.
6. **Multilingual CLIPScore column (#8).** Optional.
   - What: a reference-free "is it still informative" signal for VSPS vs zero-shot; minutes of GPU time.
   - Risk: CLIPScore favours longer captions, so report it with caption length. The checkpoint may not be public; check first.
