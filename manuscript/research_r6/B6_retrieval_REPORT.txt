0. BOTTOM LINE
==========================================================================================================================
(1) Retrieval alone is far below the fine-tuned VLM on KTVIC. With CLIP ViT-B/32 retrieval over KTVIC train
    (near-duplicates removed), the nearest neighbour's caption scores CIDEr 61.6 [55.0, 68.3]. The dev-selected kNN
    CIDEr-D consensus caption (k = 30, similarity-weighted) scores CIDEr 90.9 [83.4, 98.9], B-4 29.7, METEOR 32.5,
    ROUGE-L 52.9, and CHAIR_i 37.1. P1 s42 scores CIDEr 149.8 and CHAIR_i 19.2, so the consensus is -58.9 [-68.0, -49.2]
    CIDEr below it, with about twice its CHAIR_i. Retrieval reaches 62% of P1's CIDEr on KTVIC. On UIT-ViIC (sports COCO
    images) the same recipe reaches CIDEr 127.4, where retrieval is much stronger.
(2) The consensus step is annotator-style TYPICALITY. Against the independent UIT-OpenViIC panel of the same images, the
    kNN consensus keeps 48.8% of P1's score, against 62.5% on the KTVIC references. The gap is +13.7 points [+8.1, +19.2].
    A single neighbour caption shows no gap (-0.2 [-4.4, +4.2]). This agrees with B3 (retrieved evidence: +3.2 KTVIC
    CIDEr, ~0 on the panel) and with R5's "precision measures typicality".
(3) Retrieval does hold content that P1 misses, beyond chance, but it is buried in noise:
    - The 10 nearest train images' references contain 50.3% [45.1, 55.5] of the consensus trigrams (>= 3 of 5 refs)
      that P1 s42 misses. Random train images contain 20.3%.
    - They contain 47.7% of the RARE consensus words (train DF <= 20 images). P1 names 31.0%; random images contain 0.6%.
    - But only 4.3% of the neighbours' trigrams occur in the test references. Copying them doubles CHAIR_i.
(4) Little of that content can be turned into CIDEr on top of P1, even with oracle help:
    - Picking per image between P1 and the kNN consensus, with references {0,1} choosing and the non-adjacent {3,4}
      scoring, gains only +1.8 [-3.7, +7.1] (s42), +3.7 [-1.6, +9.0] (s43) and +2.2 [-3.4, +7.8] (s44).
    - Adding retrieved captions to the greedy + 6-sample pool under the same held-out selection gives +1.6 [-0.7, +4.1]
      (P1 s43) and +0.5 [-2.0, +3.0] (fuse2).
    - Retrieval anchored on P1's own caption (generate-then-retrieve) gives a better consensus, CIDEr 118-122, but it is
      still -25.5 [-30.8, -20.2] below P1 s43.
    - "Conventional" phrasing (every 4-gram seen in train refs) predicts CIDEr among T = 0.8 samples: +29.3 [22.4, 37.0]
      at a fixed object set. But conventional samples do NOT beat a novel-phrased GREEDY caption: -3.7 [-23.8, +13.7]
      (P1 s43, 62 images) and +1.8 [-16.0, +21.2] (fuse2, 71 images). Phrase-prior decoding (kNN-LM / train-n-gram
      constraints) therefore has little headroom over greedy.
(5) Literature agrees that retrieval gains shrink as the captioner gets stronger:
    - SmallCap: +7 to +8 CIDEr with 7M trainable parameters.
    - Sarto et al. 2024: +2 to +3 after XE but only +1.1 to +1.4 after SCST.
    - Fei 2021 kNN memory: +2.5 to +4.6 on 2019-era captioners.
    - In-prompt retrieved captions, keywords and concepts are established: SmallCap, Li et al. ACL 2024, DualCap 2025,
      CONCAP 2025, EVCap 2024.
    - Retrieved captions as MBR evidence are 2015 work (m-RNN, Devlin et al.), already owned by B3/B4.
    - The closest RL use is Re3Cap (EMNLP 2026), which uses retrieval guidance during RL and no retrieval at inference.
(6) VERDICT: retrieval augmentation is NOT a breakthrough lever for KTVIC with a 7B SFT'd VLM. Expect about +0 to +3 CIDEr
    from it, mostly typicality, and no proposal here clears the +8 bar on its own. Two cheap, killable designs are proposed
    (section 5), each with an early stop:
    - P-B6-1 RMH, Retrieved Mention Hints: a ~1.5 GPU-h inference-only probe first. Hints come from neighbours' human
      panels and replace the detector tags in the same prompt slot. Measured: they hold a P1-missed consensus content
      word on 15-16% of test images, against 1.3-1.4% for Grounding-DINO tags.
    - P-B6-2 RHX-RLOO: the same hints as train-time exploration hints inside B1's CIDEr-D RL, distilled into a
      retrieval-free policy. It upgrades B1's tag-hinted EHX-RLOO and only makes sense if B1's RL runs.
    Do NOT spend GPU on raw-caption RA-SFT, kNN-LM or train-phrase constrained decoding (section 6).

2.4 What P1 already does (pilot_overlap.py, pilot_diag.py, pilot_within*.py)
- Verbatim copies of a train caption: 21.5% of P1 s42 outputs, 20.6% s43, 19.9% s44.
  - They are generic templates: 77 distinct captions for s42, 63 of 120 found in several train images; the most copied
    is "there are many people appearing in the market". Only 9/120 come from the CLIP top-1 neighbour and 34/120 from
    the top 10; median rank 39-45.
  - Copied outputs score 184.8 CIDEr against 140.2 for the rest. That is a selection effect: P1 copies on typical
    images.
  P1 is already an implicit, parametric template retriever, and it does better than explicit CLIP retrieval.
- Phrasing is already conventional: 99.7 / 97.9 / 94.7 / 88.3% of P1's 1/2/3/4-grams occur in train refs. 41-42% of
  captions contain an unseen 4-gram; they score 117-128 against 160-165 for fully conventional ones (between images,
  confounded).
- Within image (greedy + 6 T=0.8 samples):
  - Conventional candidates beat novel ones by +38.0 [31.3, 44.7] (P1 s43) and +33.0 [25.8, 40.5] (fuse2).
  - At the SAME object set: +29.3 [22.4, 37.0] and +28.0 [18.9, 37.3]; on refs {3,4} only, +27.1 and +18.7.
  - But where the GREEDY caption is novel, conventional same-object samples do not beat it: -3.7 [-23.8, +13.7]
    (n=62) and +1.8 [-16.0, +21.2] (n=71). A test-blind "use a conventional sample instead" rule moves the corpus by
    -0.2 [-2.3, +2.0] / +0.3 [-2.0, +2.9].
  => The conventionality premium is sampling noise in T=0.8 samples, not a fixable phrasing defect of greedy decoding.

Retrieval-augmented captioners and their measured retrieval gain
- SmallCap. Ramos, Martins, Elliott, Kementchedjhieva. CVPR 2023. arXiv 2209.15323. FT.
  - Frozen CLIP ViT-B/32 + GPT-2; 7M trainable cross-attention parameters.
  - k=4 captions retrieved with CLIP RN50x64 image-to-text over COCO train captions.
  - COCO test CIDEr 119.7. Without retrieval (OPT decoders) COCO val 113.4 / 112.6, vs 120.8 with: +7 to +8.
  - The gap shrinks as trainable capacity grows (Fig. 6).
- Li, Li, Ramos, Tang, Elliott. Understanding Retrieval Robustness for Retrieval-Augmented Image Captioning. ACL 2024.
  arXiv 2406.02265. FT.
  - Copying of "majority tokens" (present in > half of the retrieved captions): 86.5% copy rate in a 2-good/1-bad
    setting.
  - Random retrieval drops CIDEr by up to 50%.
  - Sample-k from top-7 during training: in-domain about equal (119.2-119.3 vs 120.1); NoCaps +2.3 to +3.2.
  - No LVLMs tested.
- Sarto, Cornia, Baraldi, Nicolosi, Cucchiara. Towards Retrieval-Augmented Architectures for Image Captioning. ACM TOMM
  2024. arXiv 2405.13127. FT.
  - kNN memory, k=10.
  - COCO: no-memory 121.6 -> 123.7 / 124.5 after XE; after SCST 135.3 -> 136.4-136.7, i.e. only +1.1 to +1.4.
  - The query image's own captions are excluded during training. No near-duplicate filtering, no copying analysis.
  - Earlier version: Sarto et al., CBMI 2022, arXiv 2207.13162.
- Fei. Memory-Augmented Image Captioning. AAAI 2021, 35(2):1317-1324. FT (text).
  - kNN-LM for captioning: (context vector, next word) memory, k=512, T=100, lambda=0.3.

5. PROPOSALS (at most 2; neither clears the +8 bar alone; both have early kills)
==========================================================================================================================
P-B6-1. RMH: Retrieved Mention Hints (neighbours' human-panel vocabulary in the tag slot), inference probe -> hint-dropout SFT
Mechanism
- Retriever.
  - open_clip ViT-B-32-quickgelu (OpenAI) image embeddings.
  - Datastore: KTVIC train for test; train minus the 300 dev images while tuning.
  - Exclude the query and every image at cos > 0.95 (86 train images have such a twin). k = 10.
- Hint words: RDRSegmenter words of the neighbours' 5 refs with train DF <= 5%, scored sum_j cos_j^4 * [w in panel_j] *
  idf(w); keep the top m.
- Prompt: the repo's existing tag template, " Các đối tượng có thể có trong ảnh: w1, w2, ...", underscores removed. Only
  the hint SOURCE changes from the GDINO-tags runs.
- Stage A, no training. Decode the existing adapters (P1 s42/s43/s44, fuse2 s42) on KTVIC test with m = 5, k = 10,
  fixed now, before any run. Compare with the plain and tag preds already in data/results.
  - Also one arm with retrieved OR tags (m = 5 plus tags).
  - Pooled paired bootstrap across the 3 P1 seeds. Effect-size estimate only; no setting is picked on test.
- Stage B, only if Stage A passes.
  - SFT with hints and the same target as the round's chosen recipe (P1 first caption, or B2's MR5). Hint dropout 0.3
    keeps a hint-free mode.
  - 20% counterfactual hints (another random train image's hints, target unchanged) teach the model to check hints
    against the image (RetRobust/RAFT-style negatives).
  - Tune m in {3, 5, 8} and the inference mode on dev, retrain on full train, 3 seeds.
  - UIT-ViIC replication with retrieval over UIT-ViIC train, where retrieval is much stronger (2.7).
New element
- Retrieval supplies mention-worthiness evidence (words human annotators used for visually similar train images), not
  presence evidence.
- It is tested head-to-head against the detector tags in the identical prompt slot, plus counterfactual-hint training.
- Measured (2.6): the hints hold a P1-missed consensus content word on 15-16% of test images, against 1.3-1.4% for GDINO
  tags. Precision is lower (24% vs 39%), which is why the counterfactual-hint training is there.
Closest prior and delta
- DualCap 2510.24813 (keywords from captions of similar scenes, lightweight GPT-2 captioner).
- CONCAP 2507.20411 (retrieved captions + concepts; +10 CIDEr on COCO-35L at 111M trainable parameters).
- IFCap 2409.18046, EVCap 2311.15879. The repo's GDINO-tags-in-prompt baseline: +1.1 / +2.5 CIDEr.
- Delta: a QLoRA 7B LVLM in Vietnamese; hints drawn from neighbours' human reference panels; the source-controlled
  comparison with detector tags; counterfactual hints. Novelty is low.
Pilot
- Stage A, about 1.5 GPU-h: 4 decodes x 558 short captions at 4-bit on a 3090, about 0.35 GPU-h each. Hints are
  computed on CPU from this report's code.
Expected gain (KTVIC short, word level)
- Stage A: +0 to +2 CIDEr. Basis: tags gave +1.1 / +2.5; the hints are richer but noisier.
- Stage B: +1 to +3 CIDEr over the matched no-hint recipe; B-4 +0 to +1; CHAIR_i -1 to +1.5 (noisy-hint risk).
- Independent panel: about 0 to +1.
GPU-h: about 23 in total.
- Stage A: 1.5.
- Dev arm: 3 SFT + 0.6 sweeps.
- 3 seeds x 3.2.
- Test decodes: 1.2.
- UIT-ViIC: 3 x 2.2.
- Hint prompts add about 25 tokens.
Kill criterion
- Stage A: pooled 3-seed paired dCIDEr < +1.0, or 95% lower bound <= 0, or dCHAIR_i > +1.0 -> do not train.
- Stage B: dev dCIDEr-D (hint SFT vs matched no-hint SFT) < +2.0 or lower bound <= 0 -> stop.
Synthetic training data: no. The hints are human captions of OTHER train images and the targets are human references.
No detector, no AI labels.
Confidence: low.

P-B6-2. RHX-RLOO: Retrieval-Hinted eXploration for CIDEr-D RL, distilled into a retrieval-free captioner
Mechanism
- B1's CN-RLOO: word-level, EOS-aware CIDEr-D reward; copy-neutral advantage mask; RLOO; KL to ref; train minus dev.
  k = 8 rollouts per image:
  - 4 from the plain short prompt x;
  - 4 from x_h = x + the RMH hint line of P-B6-1, with the image itself and its cos > 0.95 twins excluded from its
    neighbours.
- One pooled RLOO baseline over the 8 samples.
- The plain policy is updated on all 8: hinted samples off-policy with weight min(2, pi_theta(c|x) / pi_old(c|x_h)), as
  in B1-P2.
- Inference uses the plain prompt: no retrieval and no extra model at test time.
- Why retrieval hints and not B1-P2's detector tags:
  - B4 shows tags are presence evidence, misaligned with what the references name.
  - Section 2.6 shows the retrieved hints carry ~10x more of the consensus content P1 misses.
New element
- Human-panel words of visual neighbours as train-time exploration hints for captioning RL.
- What hinted rollouts find is transferred to a hint-free policy by importance-weighted policy gradient inside a pooled
  hinted+plain group baseline.
Closest prior and delta
- Re3Cap 2608.21305: retrieval-derived keep/add/remove guidance for refining rollouts, a retrieval-free policy, dense
  captions, CLIP/CIM rewards.
- QuestA 2507.13266, LUFFY 2504.14945, ADHint 2512.13095; B1-P2 (detector tags).
- Delta: lexical mention-worthiness hints from other images' human captions, not the image's own gold and not a
  refinement instruction; reward is reference CIDEr-D on short Vietnamese captions. Novelty is moderate-low.
Pilot (requires B1's P1_dev SFT and its CN-RLOO G1 arm)
- G0r, about 0.6 GPU-h: 4 plain + 4 hinted samples on the 300 dev images. On CPU: hinted best-of-4 minus plain
  best-of-4 under disjoint-reference selection ({0,1} choose -> {3,4} score).
- G1r, about 2.5 GPU-h: a 1,000-image RL arm matched to CN-RLOO G1 (same seed and beta).
Expected gain: +1 to +3 CIDEr over CN-RLOO at plain-prompt inference (B1 expects CN-RLOO itself at +5 to +9), so about +6
to +12 over the P1 mean if additive. CHAIR_i -1 to +1.
- Weak CPU support: retrieved neighbour CAPTIONS added to the sample pool gave only +1.6 [-0.7, +4.1] (2.5). Hinted
  rollouts describe the image itself and should do better, but this is untested.
GPU-h: about 37 if run in full (B1-P2's budget). About +5 GPU-h over CN-RLOO for G0r/G1r and hinted prefill.
Kill criterion
- G0r: hinted minus plain best-of-4 < +2 CIDEr-D, or lower bound <= 0 -> stop before RL.
- G1r: plain-prompt dev greedy < CN-RLOO G1 + 1.0 at matched updates -> stop.
Synthetic training data: no detector and no AI labels. The model-generated text is the hinted RL rollouts, scored against
human references (RL rollouts are an allowed method component). No generated text becomes an SFT target.
Confidence: low.

==========================================================================================================================
6. NOT RECOMMENDED (evidence-based)
==========================================================================================================================
- Raw-caption RA-SFT (retrieved neighbour captions in the prompt):
  - Retrieved captions are poor candidates: +1.2 / -3.7 held-out (2.5).
  - Copying them doubles CHAIR_i (37-48 vs 18-19).
  - Gains in the literature shrink to about +1 after CIDEr optimisation (Sarto 2024).
  - If the synthesis still wants it as a diverse committee member for B3's MBR, use dedup-aware sample-k retrieval
    (Li et al. 2024) and expect +0 to +2.
- kNN-LM "panel memory" and train-n-gram constrained decoding: conventional phrasing does not beat a novel greedy caption
  at fixed content (2.4). P1 already writes 88% train-seen 4-grams and copies templates on 20% of images. B2's MR5 covers
  the multi-annotator part parametrically.
- Retrieval-pseudo-reference rewards on unlabelled images: the reward would be typicality only. Neighbour content is
  hallucination-prone (consensus CHAIR_i 37 vs 18).
- Test-time training on neighbours (2305.18466 style): per-image fine-tuning cost for what is, at most, the +3 typicality
  that MBR evidence already gives (B3/B4).

==========================================================================================================================
7. CAVEATS
==========================================================================================================================
- All KTVIC test numbers are estimates on test. Only the kNN setting was selected, and on train dev.
- The within-image and exploration analyses use only 6 T=0.8 samples per image for P1 s43 and fuse2 s42. The
  greedy-vs-conventional comparison covers 62-71 images, so its CIs are wide.
- Retrieval uses CLIP ViT-B/32 only. A Vietnamese text-side retriever (ViCLIP-OT) or a larger CLIP could raise retrieval
  quality. The hybrid caption-query retriever already gains +38 consensus CIDEr over image-only, and is still 25 below
  P1.
- UIT-ViIC k = 30 was at the edge of the grid. Cross-paper UIT-ViIC comparisons depend on tokenisation.
- The hint "content word" filter (train DF <= 5%) is a fixed, untuned rule. It excludes the frequent objects that tags
  cover, which is why the tag recall on it is low.
