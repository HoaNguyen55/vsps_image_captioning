- Full text: TLC-L 2305.07021 (sec. 3.3.2 veto rule, Table 4; PDF text saved by the P-B4-2 verifier, re-read here);
  MBR-CBDT 2509.12677 (sec. 4.2 image captioning; text saved by the P-B4-1 verifier).
Closest prior, component by component:
 1. The veto. TLC-L (Petryk et al., arXiv 2305.07021). A learned confidence on object words, with labels taken from
    reference captions. Starting from the top beam, it REJECTS any candidate with an object whose confidence is
    below gamma (gamma set on validation). OFA-Large: CHAIR_i 1.78 -> 1.17, CIDEr 144.4 -> 141.8. CT-Veto's
    "fewest nouns with p < 0.5, ties -> utility" is a soft variant of the same rule.
    SelfVal (arXiv 2601.22451) validates the objects in SAMPLED candidate captions, then selects or aggregates.
 2. The utility U. MBR-CBDT (Deguchi & Nagata, EMNLP 2025, arXiv 2509.12677). Self-MBR is interpolated with the true
    references of retrieved similar training items, and image captioning (MSCOCO, nocaps) is evaluated.
    m-RNN (1412.6632) and Devlin et al. (1505.04467) rerank hypotheses by consensus with kNN train captions.
 3. The novel feature kA = the share of the 30 CLIP-nearest train images whose references name the object. This is
    nonparametric kNN label transfer of annotator tags:
    - JEC (Makadia et al., ECCV 2008); TagProp (Guillaumin et al., ICCV 2009); 2PKNN (Verma & Jawahar, ECCV 2012).
    LLM-era captioning that uses neighbour or memory object evidence:
    - EVCap (2311.15879): object names retrieved by image similarity from a visual-name memory, given to the LLM.
    - MeaCap (2403.03715): retrieve-then-filter of key concepts from a caption memory, to cut hallucination.
    - ARA (2408.00555): CLIP-retrieved image-caption pairs against LVLM hallucination.
    - NES (2511.08909): filters hallucination-prone entities out of retrieved captions.
    Learned hallucination detectors with external priors:
    - HaloProbe (2604.06165): a non-contextual prior (position, repetition, occurrence).
    - MetaToken (2405.19186): meta-classifier on caption features.
    - LURE (2310.00754): co-occurrence, uncertainty and position factors.
    No paper found uses the neighbour NAMING RATE as a feature of a candidate-level mention critic, so this is not
    scooped verbatim. But it is one logistic feature added to a known critic, inside a known veto and a known
    utility. B7's own words: "neither is a breakthrough alone".
Remaining delta: Vietnamese / KTVIC setting; the contextual rate replaces the class prior (the fitted weight on the
prior collapses from 0.45 to 0.06 once kA is in; kA weight 0.44, vote 2.73). No step beyond prior art.
