Closest prior work, and what each already does
- ECO, NICE @ CVPR 2024 (arXiv 2405.01028):
  - picks, per image, the caption with the highest CIDEr-derived consensus against all other candidates in a pool
    "generated by different models" (~60 per image, ~30 after filtering);
  - consensus alone CIDEr 202.89; with CLIP 218.47.
  This is committee CIDEr consensus selection in captioning.
- Eikema & Aziz, EMNLP 2022 (arXiv 2108.04718): MBR approximations that "decouple the cost of exploration from the cost of
  robust estimation of expected utility". Mode-seeking hypotheses plus sampled evidence. This scoops "greedy outputs as
  hypotheses, T=0.8 samples as evidence only". Kamigaito et al. 2024 (2410.15021) and Fernandes et al. 2022
  (2205.00978) also cover it.
- UA-MBR, ICLR 2025 (arXiv 2503.05318): MBR over an ensemble or posterior; "performance improves with prediction
  diversity".
- Farinhas et al. 2023 (arXiv 2310.11430): hypothesis ensembling across LLMs, prompts and sampling, with MBR the most
  effective rule.
- HESM, BioNLP 2023 (arXiv 2306.05317): token-level ensembles of diverse fine-tuned models, then MBR.
- JHU IWSLT 2025: MBR ensembling of systems fine-tuned under different data settings.
- Devlin et al. 2015 (1505.04467) and m-RNN section 8 (1412.6632): CIDEr consensus reranking in captioning.
- IC3, EMNLP 2023 (2302.01328): committee-consensus captioning.
- ATED (2510.18321): token-level LVLM ensembles reduce hallucination. This covers the CHAIR_i side.
- Adjudicated Captioning (2607.28986, 2026): consensus-distilled beam reranking, +9.6 CIDEr on COCO zero-shot. Related.

No paper was found that does exactly this:
- a committee of LoRA VLM captioners trained under different supervision policies;
- any MBR or ensemble result on KTVIC or UIT-ViIC (Vietnamese).
The first delta is measured above at <= ~2 CIDEr with CIs spanning 0. The second is an application, not a method.
