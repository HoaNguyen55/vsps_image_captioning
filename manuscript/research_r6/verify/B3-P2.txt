VERDICT: KILL.
  Novelty   INCREMENTAL. It is MBR finetuning (Finkelstein et al., ICLR 2024) applied to captioning with an ensemble
            teacher. The core loop (committee teacher -> pseudo-captions on unlabeled images -> student) is Self-
            Distillation for Few-Shot Image Captioning (WACV 2021). The SFT-on-MBR-outputs arm is an explicit
            baseline in C-GRPO (arXiv 2602.03102, 2026), and C-GRPO's RL variant beats it. In this repo, it is
            round-2 A8 D1 (VTF self-distillation on the hidden-label OpenViIC pool, killed by V5) with a CIDEr
            selector. The proposal itself says "incremental".
  Gain      Realistic: 0 to +3 CIDEr over the P1 3-seed mean (point estimate about +1), inside seed noise (SD 3.0-3.4
            per run). The closest literature analogue is a student that already has human supervision. There,
            self-MBR adds +0.54 COMET with beam, and the greedy student (60.93) does not beat the reference-finetuned
            model (60.95). Self-training at 130 CIDEr adds +0.2 (UCM).
            Even the proposal's own +3 to +6 is below R6's +8 bar, so P2 cannot be the breakthrough method. At best
            it is a cost reduction for B3-P1.
  Gate      The go/no-go rule (dev-300, one run per arm, +3) passes a NULL effect 30% of the time. It passes a true
            +4.5 only 62% of the time. It is also biased toward the student, because the teacher adapters were
            trained on the dev images.
  Cost      Fits one RTX 3090. 35 GPU-h is about right for a 3-seed, KTVIC-only run (about 36 by measured
            throughput). A claim that meets CONTEXT_R6 (UIT-ViIC replication; enough seeds for a +3 to +6 effect)
            needs about 60-95 GPU-h.
  Dominated The non-synthetic MR5 run (multiref; B2 estimates +3 to +9) is already training on n3. The CIDEr-RL track
            (CN-RLOO) is already running on n2, and C-GRPO shows RL amortises MBR better than SFT on MBR outputs.

2. GAIN REALISM (lens 3)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2a. Where the teacher's gain sits (verify_p2.json, gain_by_P1_seed_agreement). Images are grouped by how many distinct
    greedy captions the 3 P1 seeds give:
      all 3 identical (162 images): mean gain +2.0, 5.7% of the total gain
      2 distinct      (240 images): mean gain +8.9, 37.1%
      3 distinct      (156 images): mean gain +21.2, 57.2%
    => The gain is mostly variance reduction on images where the seeds disagree.
       A single student is one more seed. It keeps this gain only to the extent that sequence-level ensemble
       distillation transfers "pick the consensus" to unseen high-uncertainty images. Measured transfer at strong,
       already-supervised baselines is small (2c).
2b. Pseudo-target quality (what the student would imitate):
    - Against KTVIC refs 3-5 (non-adjacent to caption #1; B2's chain finding), as CIDEr-D on test images:
        human caption #1   150.0
        teacher4 pick      132.2   (-17.8 [-28.2, -7.3] vs caption #1)
        P1 greedy          121.8-126.9
        fuse2              126.7
      As KTVIC-metric targets, the pseudo-captions sit between P1's own outputs and the human target that P1
      already trains on.
    - Against the independent panel: caption #1 48.72, teacher4 47.92, P1 mean 44.92. The pseudo-captions are close
      to human quality there.
    So the extra rows dilute the human target on the primary metric. They can only help through more images.
2c. Literature effect sizes (full text read unless noted). Students that already have human supervision:
    - Finkelstein et al. 2309.10966, Table 2 (en-de, COMET20):
        base beam 57.79 -> MBR decoding 59.35 (+1.56)
        beam-finetuned 57.08 (-0.71); MBR-finetuned on the 30k finetuning sources 58.02 (+0.23)
        self-MBR from BASE on 200k new sources 59.94 (+2.15). This is the number P2 cites, but its student has no
        human supervision.
        reference-finetuned 60.95; self-MBR from REF 61.49 (+0.54); self-beam from REF 60.06 (-0.89)
      Table 4: the from-ref MBR-finetuned student decoded GREEDY scores 60.93, below 60.95.
      P2 decodes greedy and mixes human rows, so its analogue is "from ref": about +0.5 at beam, about 0 at greedy.
      Their own explanation: self-teacher samples "do not closely approximate the true reference distribution".
    - C-GRPO 2602.03102, Table 1 (6 WMT24 settings, Mistral-7B/Llama-3-8B):
        base 0.616; random-reward GRPO 0.638; SFT on MBR outputs 0.672; MBR decoding 0.711; C-GRPO 0.719 / 0.748
      SFT recovers 59% of the MBR gain from an UNtuned base (much more headroom than P1). Consensus RL matches or
      exceeds MBR.
    - UCM 2201.02010, Table IV (COCO): self-training on 300k unlabeled images gives +1.6 CIDEr under XE
      (117.8 -> 119.4) and +0.2 after CIDEr optimisation (129.9 -> 130.1).
    - LightCap 2212.08985: ensemble KD from 3 differently initialised teachers (with ensemble heads) gives +1.7 CIDEr
      at about 125.
    - BLIP CapFilt (2201.12086, per round-2 A8): +1.9 COCO CIDEr from bootstrapped data.
    => Expect 0 to +3 KTVIC CIDEr (point about +1), 0 to +1.5 panel CIDEr, CHAIR_i about unchanged.
       The success bar is +8; P2's own target is +3 to +6.
2d. In-repo precedents:
    - E5 continued fuse2 for one epoch on 1,458 gold-selected DETAILED-prompt samples of SEEN images plus gold_fuse2.
      Short CIDEr went 143.9 -> 137.9; with DPO, 123.1.
      Different prompt, selector and image pool, so E5 is weak evidence against P2. P2's "fixes E5" claim is plausible
      but untested.
    - Round-2 A8 D1 (the same OpenViIC hidden-label pool, VTF selector) was killed by V5 because the inference gain to
      distil was about 0 against greedy. P2's CIDEr inference gain is real (1b), so that specific reason does not
      carry over.
    - B2 stand-in: CIDEr-MBR consensus targets chosen among HUMAN refs do not beat caption #1 (-2.2 n.s. at target
      level; -10.5 vs multiref in the generalisation stand-in).
2e. Metric gaming in what the student would learn (verify_p2.json, diagnostics; 558 test images):
                       words  distinct-2  "có"-initial  verbatim train ref  4-grams in train refs  repeated word
      human caption#1  10.27   0.283        63.4%          50 (9.0%)            73.7%                 19.2%
      P1 s42           10.49   0.177        95.9%         120                   88.4%                 22.6%
      teacher4         10.51   0.181        96.6%         113 (20.3%)           89.0%                 23.7%
    - No length hacking.
    - No more templating or copying than P1 itself.
    - BUT the pseudo-targets are far more templated than the human targets they would be mixed with: 96.6% vs 63.4%
      "có"-initial, distinct-2 0.18 vs 0.28. A ratio-1.0 student is pushed further toward the template. That is
      KTVIC typicality, which the panel does not reward.
2f. Leakage:
    - The teacher rule is clean (1b).
    - Pool: B2-2 found about 3 FREE images that are likely crops of test images (SSCD 0.70-0.88). CLIP >= 0.93 may
      miss crops: the KTVIC train/test audit found 3-4 same-photo crops between 0.90 and 0.95.
    - DEV GATE BIAS: the 4 teacher adapters were trained on FULL KTVIC train, including the dev-300 images (caption #1
      and fused targets). Their pseudo-captions of similar OpenViIC scenes can carry dev-caption phrasing into the
      student. The P1-on-train-minus-dev baseline never saw it. The bias favours the student, with unknown size; the
      gate margin is only +3.

3. GATE AND POWER (lens 3)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Single-run null on 300 images: built from 4 same-recipe legacy-mask P1 runs (s42, s43, s44, p1mask_s42; full-test
  pair differences 0.2 to 6.1), with 2,000 random 300-image test subsets.
    SD of a single-run difference                 4.77 CIDEr
    P(diff >= +3 | no effect)                     30%
    threshold for a 5% false pass                 +7.6
    P(pass) for a true effect of +3 / +4.5 / +6   50% / 62% / 74%
- Panel criterion ("panel CIDEr rises"):
    panel seed SD over the 4 runs 0.77; single-run null SD on 300 images 1.34; 5% threshold +2.2
  A student keeping 30-60% of the teacher's +3.0 panel gain (+0.9 to +1.8) is undetectable with one run, and a null
  "rises" half the time.
- Final claim:
  - With 3 seeds per arm, a between-seed test detects about +12.7 (CONTEXT).
  - +4.5 at 80% power needs 7-9 seeds per arm (between-seed SD 3.0-3.4).
  - A paired image bootstrap pooled over seeds ignores the dominant seed term. For example, P1 s42 - s43 =
    +5.88 [+0.89, +10.90] (seedci.py), for the same recipe.
  - The proposal does not say which test it will use.

Closest prior and exactly what it already does:
- Finkelstein et al., MBR and QE Finetuning, ICLR 2024, arXiv 2309.10966 (full text).
  MBR-decode unlabeled sources with a self or external teacher, finetune the student on the MBR outputs, decode
  greedy/beam at 1x cost. It covers from-base and from-ref students. This IS P2's mechanism.
- C-GRPO, arXiv 2602.03102 (2026; full text).
  "Distills MBR decoding into training" so that inference is a single pass. It includes "SFT (MBR decoding)" =
  P2's arm as a baseline, and shows consensus-utility RL beats it.
- Wang et al., Don't Throw Away Data, arXiv 2407.10456: MBR-selected teacher outputs as sequence-KD targets (MBR-n).
- Chasing COMET, arXiv 2405.11937: finetuning on MBR-decoded outputs for domain adaptation and low-resource MT.
- Better Instruction-Following Through MBR, arXiv 2410.02902: self-training on MBR outputs makes greedy match MBR.
- NewsPaLM MBR/QE dataset, arXiv 2408.06537 (title only): MBR-generated training data beats web-crawled data.
- Chen, Jiang, Zhao, Self-Distillation for Few-Shot Image Captioning, WACV 2021 (abstract; full text 403).
  An ENSEMBLE of base captioners is the teacher. It writes confidence-weighted pseudo-captions for unpaired images and
  the students train on them. This is the committee-teacher pseudo-captioning loop for captioning.
- LightCap, arXiv 2212.08985 (full text): ensemble KD from 3 differently initialised captioning teachers, +1.7 CIDEr.
- Cockatiel, arXiv 2503.09279: select among several models' synthetic captions with a scorer, train on them, then
  distil (video captioning).
- UCM, arXiv 2201.02010 (full text): teacher pseudo-captions on 300k unlabeled images plus labeled data, then a student.
- BLIP CapFilt (2201.12086). Devlin et al. 2015 and ECO 2405.01028: CIDEr-consensus caption selection.
- In repo: round-2 A8 D1 (manuscript/research_r2/A8_REPORT_final.txt). Same OpenViIC hidden-label pool, a self
  committee/vote selector and a student; killed in R5-00 section 6.

Not found: this exact instance, i.e. a supervision-policy LoRA committee with CIDEr-D MBR and samples as evidence only,
distilled into a VLM captioner, in Vietnamese, audited on an independent human panel.
The delta is an instance of known parts, not a new mechanism. Novelty is incremental, and the proposal itself says so.
