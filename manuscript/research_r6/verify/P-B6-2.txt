What prior work already does:
1. Re3Cap. Jia et al., EMNLP 2026, arXiv 2608.21305 (FULL TEXT). This is the closest prior, and it covers the whole
   transfer mechanism.
   - Retrieval: image-to-image over a caption corpus (OpenCLIP ViT-H/14, top-K).
   - CRS: a k-core over the neighbours' sentences keeps "semantic elements that consistently appear across visually
     similar images" and "suggests LVLMs to incorporate these elements".
   - CQA: flags hallucinations and omissions.
   - The guidance goes into the rollout prompt.
   - Sec. 3.3: "Re3Cap removes the initial caption and guidance used in the rollout stage from the policy input during
     policy optimization. This creates an off-policy optimization setting ... we decouple the proximal policy from the
     behavior policy" (decoupled-PPO importance ratios).
   - Inference: "the optimized policy is conditioned solely on the image, eliminating the need for retrieval ... at
     inference time."
   - Setting: Qwen2.5-VL-7B is one of the backbones; GRPO; rewards CLIP / SC / CIM; dense captions (COCO-LN500,
     DOCCI500); object, attribute and relation F1/QA; no CIDEr.
   B6 summarised Re3Cap as "keep/add/remove guidance for refining rollouts". The off-policy correction to an image-only
   policy, which is P-B6-2's claimed transfer step, is in Re3Cap's sec. 3.3.
2. Guide-GRPO. Nath et al., arXiv 2506.13923 (FULL TEXT).
   - One rollout group holds plain rollouts plus guided rollouts (Eq. 6).
   - Per-token ratio pi_theta(o_t | x, o_<t) / pi_old(o_t | x_h, o_<t), "to optimize the policy for contexts in which
     the hints are no longer present". That is P-B6-2's pooled 4 + 4 group with weight pi_theta(c|x) / pi_old(c|x_h).
   - Ablation: "Always" guidance, which is P-B6-2's choice (4 of 8 rollouts are always hinted), "significantly impairs
     results" compared with guiding only when all plain rollouts fail.
   - PPO clipping on guided rows "causes training divergence at approximately 50 steps".
   - It needs a policy-reshaping factor (App. E).
3. Off-Context GRPO. arXiv 2607.19313 (FULL TEXT).
   - Guided ("off-context") rollouts, importance-corrected per token to the unguided objective; unbiased.
   - Theorem 2: "a guided success retains credit only to the extent that the model could have produced it without the
     guidance".
   - +3.8 absolute on hard math, where unguided groups have zero reward.
4. Beacon. arXiv 2607.28595 (VLM agentic reasoning). Hint-guided capability expansion on all-wrong groups. Hinted
   responses are importance-weighted and the hint is removed, so the hint-free policy learns from them.
5. Same family:
   - SAGE 2602.03143 (privileged hints, reward unchanged, h = empty at test)
   - POPE 2601.18779
   - EDGE-OPD 2605.23493 (guided rollouts inject privileged-context behaviour)
   - PTD-PO 2606.07000 (multimodal privileged hints; the student is optimised in the hint-free context)
   - HINT/AAPO 2510.09388
6. The hint content itself (neighbour-caption words in an LVLM prompt): TUNA 2406.10839, DualCap 2510.24813,
   CONCAP 2507.20411, ViECap 2307.16525 (see the P-B6-1 verifier).
Residual delta:
- The reward is word-level, EOS-aware CIDEr-D, with CN-RLOO's copy mask.
- The hints are top-5 neighbour words in one prompt line, rather than k-core refinement guidance on an initial caption.
- Vietnamese short captions.
This is a reward / format / language swap of Re3Cap + Guide. No retrieval-hinted SCST or CIDEr RL was found, so "first
CIDEr-D instance" is the only claim left. That is an application, not a method step beyond prior art.

LENS 2 - FEASIBILITY ON ONE RTX 3090: feasible, tight, not cheap; new off-policy code with a documented failure mode
=========================================================================================================================
- What HEAD scripts/train_rl.py does not support:
  - one prompt only (Policy.text = the short prompt);
  - pg_loss(lp, lp.detach(), ...) fixes the ratio at 1;
  - generation log-probs are kept only in the dry run.
- What P-B6-2 must add:
  (a) A train-image retrieval datastore. B6's CLIP features exist only in a scratchpad. Twins at cos > 0.95 are
      excluded (2.2% of train images); the pool is train minus dev while tuning.
  (b) A hint builder.
  (c) A second generate call on x_h with output_logits.
  (d) The off-policy ratio and its truncation.
  (e) An IS-aware KL term: k3 on hinted rows does not estimate KL(pi(.|x) || ref).
  (f) The C3 log-prob-gap guard, re-validated on the x_h path.
- Memory:
  - Hinted responses are scored under the plain prompt, so its KV cache serves all 8 rows. The training graph is the
    same as CN-RLOO at k = 8. The B1-P1 verifier estimates 18-22 GB reserved against the 22 GB guard.
  - Measured now on n2: the G0 sampling job alone (k = 8, T = 1, greedy batch 8, one adapter, no gradients) holds
    19.0 / 24.6 GB.
  - Generation logits for 4 x 40 steps x 152K vocab add ~0.1 GB. Fits at k = 8, tightly; k = 16 does not.
- Time: two generate calls (4 + 4) and an extra ~625-token prefill cost ~+10-20% over CN-RLOO. That is ~5.5-9 s per
  image, or 8-13.5 GPU-h per seed for 1.5 epochs.
- GPU-h. The proposal says 37. The plan as written:
    G0r                               0.3-0.6
    G1r                               2-3
    3 seeds x (8-13.5)                24-40
    test decodes                      0.5
    UIT-ViIC (own datastore)          6-10
    total                             about 35-55 (central 45)
  - A paper-grade +1 to +3 increment needs 5+ seeds per arm or a pooled paired bootstrap, plus COCO (the method is
    language-agnostic): about 100-130 GPU-h.
  - All of it comes ON TOP of CN-RLOO (~75 GPU-h, B1-P1 verifier). CN-RLOO must exist and pass its own gate first.
    Its G0 finished on n2 during this check; no G1 exists yet.
- Implementation risk: medium-high.
  - Guide's divergence with PPO-clipped guided rows applies: train_rl.py clips at 0.2.
  - Sequence-level min(2, w) is not the stable recipe of either Guide (reshaping) or OC-GRPO (per token, bounded
    correction).
  - A ~25-token prompt change makes the weights heavy-tailed (OC-GRPO App. E: variance grows with guidance length).
    The hinted half can have an effective sample size well below 4, giving the plain policy less signal than CN-RLOO's
    k = 8 at the same compute.

3c. Why the transfer cannot be larger (analytic; OC-GRPO Thm 2, Guide Lemma 1).
- With q = pi_old(.|x_h) and p = pi(.|x), E_{c~q}[min(2, p/q) A grad log p] = sum_c min(2q(c), p(c)) A(c) grad log p(c).
  Each caption's weight is <= p(c), its on-policy weight.
- So content found under the hint is credited AT MOST at its plain-policy probability. Truncation only removes mass
  that the plain policy prefers.
- The only gain left is variance reduction for rare content. That matters when groups collapse; here 0% do, and 64.5%
  of the useful words are already sampled.
- Uptake dilemma, from the only measured hint-in-prompt decodes (GDINO tags, KTVIC test):
  - The extra prompt line changed 275 / 277 of 558 greedy captions.
  - It inserted a tag word in only 6-8% (P1 s43: 34-43 of 558) and 2% (fuse2: 10-13).
  - Low uptake: hinted rollouts are mostly prompt perturbation, with little new content.
  - High uptake: their IS weights shrink back to the plain probability.
- G0r tests the BEHAVIOUR policy (hinted best-of-4). Importance correction does not pass that quality to the plain
  policy, so even a G0r pass would not predict a plain-prompt gain.

3d. Neither gate can resolve the claimed effect.
- G0r (hinted best-of-4 minus plain best-of-4, pick {0,1} -> score {3,4}; pass needs >= +2 and LB > 0):
  - Null per-image SD 92.5, so SE 5.34 at 300 images.
  - An observed mean >= +10.5 is needed to pass.
  - P(pass) = 2.5% at a true 0, 5.6% at +2, 15.3% at +5, 46.5% at +10. Empirical null pass: 2.5% over 200 random 4/4
    splits.
- G1r (">= CN-RLOO G1 + 1.0"; check_g1gate.py):
  - P1-family greedy policies differ per image with SD 48-69, so SE 2.8-4.0 at 300 images.
  - A null arm clears +1.0 with P = 0.36-0.40 at one checkpoint, and more often with best-checkpoint selection.

3e. Effect size against noise and literature.
- The proposal claims +1 to +3 over CN-RLOO.
- Noise: single-seed SD ~3.4, MDE 2.5-3.8. A 3-seed t-test detects ~+12.7. Pooled paired SE is ~1.4-1.7.
- Literature:
  - Hint and guidance RL gains come from sparse, zero-signal regimes: Guide +3.4 pp, OC-GRPO +3.8 pp, SAGE +1.2.
  - Re3Cap reports dense-caption F1/QA with CLIP/CIM rewards; no CIDEr.
  - Retrieval added on top of SCST at >= 135 CIDEr: +1.1 to +1.4 (Sarto et al. 2024, arXiv 2405.13127).
- Realistic: -1 to +1 CIDEr over CN-RLOO; B-4 about 0; CHAIR_i 0 to +1.5 (a rise is possible). CN-RLOO alone is +2 to +6
  (B1-P1 verifier), so the total stays well below the +8 bar.

3f. Gaming and leakage.
- No test leakage: hints are used only in training, inference uses the plain prompt, test is never read, and dev is
  outside the pool while tuning.
- Within train: 4.2% of images keep a near-twin at cos 0.90-0.95. Its captions can carry the image's own reference
  content into the hint.
- Typicality:
  - The hints are KTVIC-annotator vocabulary.
  - B6's panel check shows retrieval-consensus gains are typicality: share gap +13.7 [+8.1, +19.2].
  - 76% of hint words are named by no reference, and samples that say only wrong hint words score -10.9 [-23.7, +1.9].
  - Learning to emit unverified hint vocabulary at hint-free inference (the "privilege illusion", DOPD 2606.30626)
    would raise CHAIR.
- Repetition, padding and truncation: word-level, EOS-aware CIDEr-D punishes repetition (-40) and padding (-60), and
  EOS closes the truncation loophole. Hinted rollouts may raise TEMPLATE copying (P1 already copies a train caption
  verbatim on ~20% of test images, B6). The copy mask checks only the image's own references.
- Synthetic data: none. The hints are human captions of other train images. The rollouts are allowed RL samples.
