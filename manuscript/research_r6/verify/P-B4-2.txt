P-B4-2 PM-Probe (hidden-state panel-mention probe as the veto critic of EV-MBR)
Adversarial verification, round R6, 2026-10-13. Work dir: research_r6/verify/pb42/ (code, logs, literature).
CPU only (nice 15, <= 4 threads, B = 2000). Repo untouched. HEAD d0c349c copies of evaluate.py, object_prf.py,
score_ci.py, score_coco_probe.py, rescap/, data/coco_probe/ are in pb42/head. Word-level RDRSegmenter (VnCoreNLP-1.2,
local OpenJDK 21) runs locally. The P-B4-1 verifier's independent re-implementation (evmbr_check.py, own CIDEr-D
asserted equal to pycocoevalcap) is reused as a module. Every KTVIC number below comes from TEST pools (greedy + 6 T0.8
samples of P1 s43 and fuse2 s42), so each one is an EFFECT-SIZE ESTIMATE, not a result.

VERDICT: KILL.
(1) The claimed novel element (a learned confidence on the captioner's own states at object-word positions, trained
on held-out images with labels taken from existing references and used as a candidate-level veto, reported with CIDEr)
was published as TLC-L (Petryk et al., arXiv 2305.07021, 2023).
(2) The simulation behind the expected gain reproduces exactly, but it does not survive matched operating points.
The label-free vote veto that P-B4-1 already has matches the simulated AUROC-0.81 probe.
(3) The 0.85 gate is very likely out of reach. A perfect reader of the model's own sampling distribution extrapolates
to about 0.79. An independent 5-person human panel describing the same image, stacked on vote + prior, reaches only
0.834 / 0.809.
(4) Whatever extra CHAIR_i reduction a KTVIC-mention critic buys is mostly KTVIC-panel naming. Only 25-43% of it
survives an independent panel, against 53-88% for the label-free veto.
(5) It has no CIDEr upside, and CIDEr is the user's primary metric.

Searches (2026-10-13; logs in pb42/lit):
- arXiv API: 23 keyword queries (arxiv_1-4, 5-9, 10-15, 16-20, 21-23.txt). Topics: probes / hidden states for object
  hallucination; probes plus reranking or best-of-N; captioning with internal states; rejection sampling with detectors;
  semantic-entropy probes; RL reward with probes; token-level confidence for captions; caption reranking with
  confidence; Vietnamese captioning (KTVIC, UIT-OpenViIC); content selection and mention; MBR with verifiers;
  self-consistency against hallucination; hallucination-detection classifiers; verifier best-of-N; salient-object
  mention; caption QE; confidence estimation.
- arXiv id_list lookups for 25 ids (ids_1-4.txt).
- Semantic Scholar: 1 query succeeded (s2_1-2.txt). 5 others returned HTTP 429 after retries.
- OpenAlex: HTTP 429 on all 4 queries (quota exhausted, retry-after 21,961 s). Logged in openalex_1-4.txt.
- Full text read: 2305.07021 (PDF, sections 3.2-4.4 and Table 4); 2603.05465 HALP (HTML, Table 1); 2604.06165
  HaloProbe (HTML: detection table, ablation, mitigation); 2502.16842 (HTML: method).
Closest prior:
 1. TLC-L. Petryk, Whitehead, Gonzalez, Darrell, Rohrbach, Rohrbach, "Simple Token-Level Confidence Improves Caption
    Correctness", arXiv 2305.07021 (2023). Full text read. It has every element P-B4-2 claims as new:
    - a learned confidence estimator g on the FINE-TUNED captioner's own encoder and decoder features (the decoder
      state just before the vocabulary projection, i.e. a hidden state), at each token of a teacher-forced candidate;
    - trained on a HELD-OUT split (the captioner's val set), "a better estimate of the captioning model's performance
      on test data" (the same reason for P-B4-2's cross-fitting);
    - labels from EXISTING REFERENCE CAPTIONS, not presence: a token is correct if it matches a reference token at that
      step given the reference prefix;
    - used as a CANDIDATE-LEVEL VETO: among B = 25 beams, reject any whose COCO-object tokens have confidence < gamma,
      with gamma chosen on val, and fall back to likelihood order;
    - reported with CIDEr/SPICE as well as CHAIR. Table 4, OFA-Large: CHAIR_s/CHAIR_i/CIDEr 2.79/1.78/144.4 ->
      1.74/1.17/141.8. OFA-Base: 3.78/2.39/142.9 -> 2.05/1.48/137.5. At baselines >= 140 CIDEr the learned veto cuts
      CHAIR_i by 34-38% (relative) and COSTS 2.6-5.4 CIDEr. Training the captioner on the held-out data instead
      (Standard-Aug) gives 153.3 CIDEr (+8.9).
    This contradicts the proposal's novelty sentence ("prior probes ... target presence ... and are scored on CHAIR
    only").
 2. HaloProbe, 2604.06165. Token-level posterior combining internal signals (attention, logits) with a learned prior
    on external statistics. AUROC 93.5 on LLaVA-1.5 (internal-only 92.1; external-only 81.7). Used to score
    candidates in hallucination-aware beam search and to remove hallucinated objects after decoding. Also run on
    Qwen3-VL and InternVL3.5. Reports CHAIR and F1, no CIDEr.
 3. Sun et al., 2502.16842. Token-level hallucination classifier on hidden representations from parallel with-image
    and without-image passes. Labels come from an automated object-hallucination pipeline. Drives a decoding strategy
    that controls the hallucination rate in image captioning.
 4. SelfVal, 2601.22451: validates objects in sampled candidate captions, then selects or aggregates captions (the
    veto-over-samples form). Petryk et al. 2305.07021 and B4's own "cheap critic" are the reference-label versions.
 5. HALP, 2603.05465: Qwen2.5-VL-7B probe AUROC is 0.787 with visual-only features (VF), 0.668 with VT and 0.915 with
    query-token states (QT). B4's "~0.79 for Qwen2.5-VL-7B" quotes the weakest family. The label there is VQA
    hallucination, not panel mention.
 6. Context. Obeso et al. 2509.03531 (entity-token linear probes beat semantic entropy, AUC 0.90 vs 0.71; long-form
    LLM output). Semantic Entropy Probes 2406.15927 (probes approximate sampling-based uncertainty). Lookback Lens
    2407.07071 (linear classifier guides candidate selection at decode time). Quality-aware decoding 2205.00978 (MBR
    plus learned-QE reranking: the "MBR x learned critic" structure). MetaToken 2405.19186. TruthPrInt 2503.10602.
    In-house E2 already used "named by any reference" as the probe label.
 7. Vietnamese: KTVIC 2401.08100 and UIT-OpenViIC 2305.04166. No Vietnamese probe or veto work was found.
Remaining delta, all incremental:
- the label is "any of 5 refs names the noun anywhere" (bag of nouns) rather than TLC's prefix-conditional token match;
- the veto sits inside MBR with retrieved pseudo-references rather than likelihood-ranked beams;
- PCA-64 + logistic regression on layer 20-28 states of a 7B LVLM, with cross-fitted half adapters;
- Vietnamese.
None of these is a step beyond the prior art.

==========================================================================================================================
LENS 2 -- FEASIBILITY on one RTX 3090: yes (not the blocker); ~4.4 GPU-h, medium implementation risk
==========================================================================================================================
- Extraction. train_rl.token_logprobs (HEAD) already teacher-forces k candidates through the 4-bit LoRA policy on a
  shared ~600-token prompt KV cache. Adding output_hidden_states to the continuation pass gives every layer:
  29 x 7 x 40 x 3584 x 2 B ~ 58 MB per image. Peak memory stays well under 24 GB (4-bit 7B ~5.5 GB).
- GPU-h, using the repo's measured unit costs:
    two P1 half-adapters (~1,735 images each; 6,938 s per full-train P1 SFT)          ~1.8
    greedy + 6 samples on 1,500 cross-fitted train images (1.4 + 3.6 s per image)       ~2.1  (B4 counted samples only)
    teacher-forced states for ~1,800 dev/xfit pools and 3 x 558 test pools              ~0.5
  Total ~4.4 GPU-h. A further ~1.5 h of dev300 and s42/s44 test pools is shared with P-B4-1. Reusing the queued fuse2
  xfit halves (jobs 60-63) brings it to ~2.6, at the price of a fuse2-vs-P1 candidate and feature mismatch.
- Risks:
  (a) aligning lexicon nouns (multi-syllable, canonicalised by objects_in/canonical) to byte-level BPE offsets;
  (b) feature shift: the probe is fitted on half-adapter or p1dev states but applied to full-train seed adapters.
      Either read every candidate through one fixed critic adapter, or show the transfer on dev;
  (c) labels are per (image, noun) while states are per occurrence;
  (d) the train set is ~7k (image, noun) keys: enough for PCA-64 + logistic regression with image-grouped CV.

LENS 3 -- GAIN REALISM: pilot reproduces exactly; the expected gain does not survive scrutiny
==========================================================================================================================
3a. Reproduction (pb42/probe_sim.py -> out/probe_sim.log). B4's pilot8 numbers match exactly (CIDEr / CHAIR_i):
      P1 s43:    PE-MBR 151.4 / 17.0; cheap veto 152.3 / 15.5; sim 0.81 150.0 / 14.9; sim 0.90 151.1 / 11.4
      fuse2 s42: PE-MBR 149.8 / 20.9; cheap veto 149.9 / 19.6; sim 0.81 150.0 / 18.6; sim 0.90 151.8 / 14.6
    Oracle veto: 156.3 / 3.0 and 158.7 / 6.6.
    Cheap critic: cross-fitted AUROC 0.782 / 0.765; vote+prior 0.775 / 0.762; tags add +0.007. All confirmed.

3b. Matched operating points: the veto threshold tau is a CIDEr-vs-CHAIR knob, and the simulation compared critics at
    different points.
    P1 s43 (CIDEr / CHAIR_i / object R):
      label-free vote veto (no labels, no probe):
        tau 0.3: 151.1 / 14.4 / R 41.0
        tau 0.5: 147.4 / 12.8 / R 38.5
      sim 0.81 at tau 0.5: 150.0 / 14.9   -> DOMINATED by the label-free vote veto at tau 0.3
    Lowest CHAIR_i each critic family reaches with CIDEr >= PE-MBR - 1 (thresholds picked on test for every family):
                            vote-only   cheap (other pool)   sim 0.81   sim 0.85   sim 0.90
      P1 s43                  14.4           14.9               15.6       13.4       10.5
      fuse2 s42               19.6           19.4               17.2       14.5       12.7
    Gain over the label-free knob: on P1 s43, nothing at AUROC 0.81 and -1.0 at 0.85, even under B4's
    independent-noise assumption. fuse2 gains more because its extra objects are consistent across samples
    (see 3e for what that gain is).

3c. Is the 0.85 gate reachable? Very unlikely (my estimate P <= 0.1-0.15; B4 said ~0.3).
    - Self-distribution ceiling (K-extrapolation). AUROC of the vote computed from K' of the 6 samples, on a fixed key
      set:
        K' = 1 .. 6   P1: 0.637  0.690  0.718  0.737  0.751  0.763
        fit a + b/K'  P1: K' -> inf 0.794 (vote + prior 0.795); fuse2: 0.772 (0.778)
      A probe that reads the model's own mention distribution perfectly gets about 0.79 / 0.78. To reach 0.85 it must
      carry information that never reaches the model's own outputs.
    - Independent human ceiling (pb42/panel_ceiling.py -> out/panel_ceiling.log). Use the count (0-5) of the 5
      OpenViIC captions of the SAME test image that name the noun. These are humans who saw the image:
                                      P1      fuse2
        count alone                  0.776    0.750
        vote + prior + count         0.834    0.805-0.809
      Five independent human descriptions of the image do not lift the critic to 0.85. The probe would have to know
      more about the KTVIC panel's naming than a second human panel does. That knowledge is KTVIC naming convention,
      and the SFT model's vote and the train prior already carry it.
    - Caveats on the literature figures:
      - E2's 0.807 was on Stage-1 proposition objects, base model, where the class prior alone reached 0.710. On the
        captioner's own candidate nouns the prior reaches only 0.608 / 0.620, so the task is harder.
      - HaloProbe 93.5 and HALP 0.915 are truth labels on detailed captions or VQA, where position and question-level
        cues help.
      - TLC-L's labels are reference-derived like ours, and its veto lowers CIDEr.

3d. A realistic probe has noise correlated with the vote. Combined critic = cross-fitted logistic(vote, prior, z),
    z = D*y + rho*vote_noise + sqrt(1 - rho^2)*e, veto at tau 0.5, mean of 3 seeds:
                                             combined AUROC          P1 CIDEr / CHAIR_i
      probe 0.75, rho 0.0 / 0.3 / 0.6       0.849 / 0.820 / 0.797    151.1 / 13.6 | 150.7 / 14.6 | 151.3 / 15.5
      probe 0.80, rho 0.0 / 0.3 / 0.6       0.874 / 0.845 / 0.822    150.7 / 12.3 | 151.1 / 13.9 | 150.9 / 14.7
      probe 0.85, rho 0.6                   0.856                    151.1 / 13.7
    Against the label-free vote veto (151.1 / 14.4) at the same CIDEr:
      combined 0.845-0.856 -> CHAIR_i -0.5 to -0.7
      combined ~0.875      -> -2.0
      combined 0.90        -> -3.0 to -3.6
    The proposal's "-1.5 to -3.5 on top of P-B4-1" needs a combined AUROC of about 0.87-0.90, above the human-panel
    ceiling in 3c. Even at a combined 0.85 the P1 gain is -0.5 to -1.0. That is below the per-seed paired CI
    half-width for CHAIR_i (~1.2-1.6; ~0.8-0.9 pooled over 3 seeds).

3e. What the extra CHAIR_i reduction measures. The probe's label IS CHAIR-vi's gold rule: _gold_objects of the same
    5 KTVIC refs. Noun precision gain vs PE-MBR, 555 images, KTVIC refs vs the INDEPENDENT OpenViIC panel:
                                    KTVIC panel   OpenViIC panel   share that survives
      P1 s43
        vote tau 0.3                    +2.6          +2.1               81%
        vote tau 0.5                    +4.2          +3.7               88%
        sim 0.85                        +2.8          +0.7               25%   (OpenViIC CI includes 0)
        sim 0.90                        +4.9          +1.8               37%
        oracle                         +14.1          +4.5               32%
      fuse2 s42
        vote tau 0.5                    +3.4          +1.8               53%
        sim 0.85                        +4.9          +1.9               39%
        sim 0.90                        +7.5          +3.2               43%
    A critic that predicts KTVIC mention lowers CHAIR-vi mostly by matching the panel's naming (typicality, which is
    the paper's own verified finding 1), not by removing false objects. On P1 s43 the label-free veto at tau 0.5
    raises independent-panel precision MORE (+3.7) than the simulated 0.90 probe (+1.8). Caveat: the simulated probe
    is y + noise, so it also "knows" the test refs' idiosyncrasies; a real probe would transfer somewhat better, but it
    cannot beat the 3c ceiling.

3f. Metrics, gaming, leakage.
    - CIDEr: at tau 0.5 the simulated probe changes CIDEr vs PE-MBR by -1.4 to +2.0 (every CI spans 0). There is no
      CIDEr upside, and CIDEr is the user's primary metric. In the literature at >= 140 CIDEr (TLC-L, Table 4), a
      learned veto costs 2.6-5.4 CIDEr.
    - Mention count: the veto lowers mentions/cap from 1.77 to 1.62-1.71, part of the classic CHAIR lever.
    - The proposed RL reward-shaping use would optimise the evaluation's own gold definition (Goodhart): no.
    - Leakage: the design uses train refs only. The pilot uses test labels by construction (simulation, cross-fitted
      critic), so it can only estimate effect sizes.

3g. "The only B4 route to the CHAIR Pareto bar" is refuted. On P1 s43 the label-free vote veto at tau 0.3 already
    gives CIDEr 151.1 / CHAIR_i 14.4 (-4.0 vs the 3-seed mean 18.4; single seed, threshold picked on test). Projected
    to 3 seeds, it is about -3.3 from each seed's greedy, i.e. ~15.1. A probe at combined 0.85 would add -0.5 to -1.0,
    so the bar stays borderline even if the gate passes, and most of the gain would be panel typicality.
