Closest prior (full text read where marked):
 1. Deguchi & Nagata, "Case-Based Decision-Theoretic Decoding with Quality Memories", arXiv 2509.12677, EMNLP 2025 main
    (DOI 10.18653/v1/2025.emnlp-main.1710). FULL TEXT READ. It estimates the expected utility of each hypothesis from the
    TRUE references of retrieved similar training examples (kNN on the input; memorised hypotheses scored against their
    true reference, weighted by input and output similarity) and proposes MBR-CBDT = (1-lambda) MBR + lambda CBDT,
    min-max normalised, lambda = 0.5. It is evaluated on image captioning: MSCOCO (memory = COCO train) and nocaps (memory =
    Localized Narratives), BLIP-2 with 256 epsilon samples, DINOv2 image similarity, BERTScore utility. MBR -> MBR-CBDT on
    COCO: BLEU 25.6 -> 26.0, chrF 43.2 -> 44.4, BERTScore 68.4 -> 68.6 (no CIDEr reported). This is the EV-MBR typicality
    half (self-MBR + retrieved true references of similar training items, equal weight) in all but implementation detail.
 2. m-RNN, Mao et al. arXiv 1412.6632: rerank the model's hypotheses by mean CIDEr/BLEU vs captions of the k nearest train
    images (COCO val CIDEr 84.2 -> 93.8). Devlin et al. 1505.04467: kNN consensus caption. EV-MBR's "retrieved refs as
    pseudo-references" scoring IS this rule; mixing it with self-MBR is the MBR-CBDT interpolation.
 3. NICE 2024 ECO, arXiv 2405.01028: caption re-ranking with ensembled CLIP + consensus score (LVLM era, CIDEr-ranked).
 4. SelfVal, arXiv 2601.22451: validates objects in SAMPLED candidate captions, then caption selection or aggregation
    (presence-based). The EV veto is the same family; its critic is in practice a vote-share threshold (logistic weight on
    vote 3.1-3.6, prior 0.33-0.44), i.e. object-level sample consensus, and its "mention-worthiness" label is exactly
    CHAIR-vi's reference-derived gold.
 5. MBR for captioning: model-based MBR 2311.05263, AMBR 2401.02749 (both with captioning experiments).
Remaining delta: (a) retrieval keyed on the greedy CAPTION text (TF-IDF) instead of the image -- a useful engineering
finding (B4: CLIP 1-NN unstable), not a method; (b) CIDEr-D utility and Vietnamese LVLM setting; (c) veto threshold on
sample vote share. None is a step beyond prior art; the B4 author's own rating ("not a breakthrough on its own") stands.

3d. Leakage. Selection uses no test refs: self-MBR DF over the 558 candidate pools (transductive but label-free -> use a

3e. Seed noise and K. Per-adapter gains +5.9 to +7.9 (paired CI_low +3.0 to +4.4) exceed the seed SD 3.4, but both pools come

    (+-2.5). K scaling (greedy + 2/4/6 samples): self-MBR 140.7/144.7/146.5 (P1), 141.5/143.9/145.8 (fuse2) is still rising
    ~+1.8 per 2 samples; the retrieval increment at equal K shrinks on P1 (5.0 -> 3.9 -> 3.3) and is flat on fuse2 (3.9 ->
    3.8 -> 4.1). B4's criterion (b) "EV(K=6) >= self-MBR(K=24) + 2" is at real risk.
3f. Literature at >= 130 CIDEr (COCO Karpathy test, PDFs read): ensembles of 2-4 models add +2.0 to +3.3 (M2 Transformer
    1912.08226: 131.2 -> 133.2 (2) -> 134.5 (4); AoANet 1908.06954: 129.8 -> 132.0 (4)); MBR-CBDT over MBR on COCO +0.4 BLEU.
    EV-MBR's +6 to +8 is 2-3x larger. This is plausible here because the KTVIC panel is formulaic and K is small, not
    because the method is stronger. Transfer proxy (out/transfer_proxy.log, human refs only, equal ~3.7k-image index): a

    41.2% of UIT-ViIC test refs occur verbatim among its train+val refs (KTVIC 6.5%, COCO 0.9%) -> the typicality lever
    probably transfers (it may be even larger on UIT-ViIC, which makes copy-rate reporting mandatory there).
3g. Stacking: "stacks with B1 RL / B2 MR5" is untested; a CIDEr-D-RL policy concentrates its samples, which typically
    shrinks reranking headroom. Must be measured, not assumed.
