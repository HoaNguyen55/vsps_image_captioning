P-B4-1  EV-MBR: evidence-factorised MBR decoding (panel typicality x mention-worthiness veto)
  Mechanism (decode-time, one adapter, one 3090, no new training of the captioner):
   1. Candidates: greedy + K = 6 short samples from the adapter (temperature chosen on dev from {0.5, 0.8}).
   2. Typicality evidence: retrieve the k train images whose 5 refs are closest (TF-IDF, word uni+bigrams) to the
      greedy caption; their 5k refs are pseudo-references (k chosen on dev from {10, 30}); optional presence gate.
   3. Score = CIDEr-D vs the other candidates (self-consensus) + CIDEr-D vs the retrieved refs, equal weight.
   4. Mention-worthiness veto: a logistic critic gives p(noun named by the panel) for each candidate noun from [vote share
      among the K+1 candidates, KTVIC-train leave-one-out naming prior, (GDINO tag status)]; fit on the dev split's
      candidates with their EXISTING 5 references as labels. Keep the candidates with the fewest nouns of p < 0.5; take
      the best score among them.
  Novel element: the Bayes decision is factorised by evidence type -- panel typicality comes from the SAME panel's
   captions of retrieved images mixed into the MBR pseudo-reference distribution, object choice is vetoed by a critic of
   PANEL MENTION-WORTHINESS (not presence). The project's diagnostics motivate both halves (sections 2, 4) and the
   pilots show presence evidence is dispensable (8c).
  Closest prior and delta: m-RNN 1412.6632 / Devlin 1505.04467 rerank by kNN captions alone (pilot: alone +3.1 to +4.4;
   mixed with self-MBR +6.0 to +7.9); MBR / MBMBR 2311.05263 use self-samples only (pilot: +2.3 to +2.6); SelfVal
   2601.22451 and HaloProbe 2604.06165 veto or rescore by PRESENCE-hallucination signals and report no CIDEr.
  Pilot (test, ESTIMATES): P1 s43 CIDEr 143.9 -> 151.1-152.3 (+7.2 [+3.1, +11.9] with the critic fitted on another
   adapter; +8.4 [+4.7, +12.6] cross-fitted), CHAIR_i -2.2 to -2.7, BLEU-4 +0.8, METEOR +0.4, ROUGE-L +0.7, object P +2.2
   at equal R, independent-panel P +1.4 [+0.1, +2.8]; fuse2 s42 +5.9 to +6.2 CIDEr, CHAIR_i -1.4 to -2.4.
  Dev protocol (no test tuning): dev = random.Random(0).sample(sorted(train_ids), 300) (B2 wrote it to
   B2_targets_data/out/dev300_train_ids.txt); P1-dev adapter trained on the other 3,469 train images; retrieval index =
   train minus dev; choose T, k, gate on/off and fit the critic on dev; then freeze and run on test with the 3 full-train
   P1 seeds. Required control on dev: self-MBR with 24 samples (is it just "more pseudo-references"?).
  GPU-h (one RTX 3090; measured unit costs: P1 SFT 6,938 s; greedy short 1.3-1.5 s/image; 6 short samples ~3.6 s/image;
   GDINO <= 0.58 s/image): P1-dev adapter 1.8 + dev greedy and samples at 2 temperatures 0.7 + 24-sample control 0.9 +
   test samples for s42 and s44 (s43 exists) 1.1 + UIT-ViIC test samples, 3 seeds 0.7 (+ the UIT-ViIC P1 seeds that the
   generalisation requirement needs anyway) + GDINO on dev/UIT-ViIC test 0.1 = ~5.3 GPU-h. COCO-Karpathy replication
   (language-agnostic; 2,500-image subset, 1 seed) +2.5. Inference cost: 7 decodes per image (~4.9 s vs 1.4 s greedy).
  Expected gain: +5 to +8 CIDEr over each seed's greedy (3-seed mean ~151-153 vs 145.8), CHAIR_i -1.5 to -3, BLEU-4 /
   METEOR / ROUGE-L +0.4 to +1. P(+8 over the 3-seed mean, pooled paired bootstrap over 3 x 558 pairs) ~0.25 alone;
   it stacks with any adapter (B1 RL, B2 MR5), which is the realistic path to the +8 bar.
  Kill criterion: on dev, EV-MBR must beat greedy by >= +4 CIDEr with paired CI_low > 0 and CHAIR_i <= greedy, and beat
   the 24-sample self-MBR control by >= +2 CIDEr; drop the retrieval term if it adds < +1.5 over self-MBR on dev, drop the
   veto if the critic's dev AUROC < 0.72. On test, stop if the mean gain over the 3 greedy seeds is < +4 CIDEr.
  Synthetic training data: none for the captioner. The critic (4-5 weights) is fitted on model-generated candidate nouns
   labelled by existing human references. Overlap: the self-MBR half is B3's baseline; merge with B3 if both are chosen.
  Confidence: medium.

P-B4-2  PM-Probe: a hidden-state PANEL-MENTION probe as the veto critic (internal evidence), the CHAIR Pareto route
  Mechanism: teacher-force each candidate caption through the captioner with the image; take the hidden state (layers
   20-28; E2 found 24 best) at the last token of each lexicon noun; a PCA-64 + logistic probe predicts "named by any of
   the 5 references" (existing human labels). Train it on candidates of UNSEEN train images (the 300 dev images plus
   750 + 750 cross-fitted images: half-A adapter on half-B images and vice versa, as in the queued xfit jobs 60-62) so
   the labels are not memorised; add the noun's token log-probability (HaloProbe-style internal statistic) and the
   cheap features of P-B4-1. Use the calibrated output as the veto critic of P-B4-1 (optionally also as a reward-shaping
   veto for B1's RL).
  Novel element: the probe's target is panel mention-worthiness at the noun's position INSIDE the candidate caption,
   learned from existing references, and it is used as a candidate-level MBR veto; prior probes (HaloProbe, HALP,
   TruthPrInt, 2508.04567) target presence on detailed captions and are scored on CHAIR only.
  Closest prior: E2 (in-house, question-format, AUROC 0.807); HaloProbe 2604.06165 (Bayesian internal x external
   posterior, beam scoring); HALP 2603.05465 (Qwen2.5-VL-7B ~0.79 visual-only).
  Pilot (CPU simulation on test candidates, ESTIMATES): with P-B4-1's pools, a critic of AUROC 0.81 gives CHAIR_i 14.9
   (P1 s43) / 18.6 (fuse2) at CIDEr 150.0; AUROC 0.90 gives 11.4 / 14.6 at CIDEr 151.1 / 151.8; the cheap critic sits
   at 0.76-0.78 (15.0-15.5 / 19.6-20.0). Simulated noise is independent of the cheap features, so the real increment
   of a probe will be smaller than the simulation at the same AUROC.
  GPU-h: two P1 half-adapters 1.9 (0 if the queued fuse2 xfit halves are reused, at the price of an adapter mismatch)
   + 6 short samples on 1,500 unseen train images 1.5 + teacher-forced hidden states for ~1,800 x 7 candidates 0.3 +
   test 3 seeds 0.3 = ~2.1-4.0 GPU-h. GDINO tags for the 1,500 train images (if the tag feature is kept) 0.25.
  Expected gain: if the combined critic reaches out-of-fold AUROC >= 0.85 on dev candidate nouns: CHAIR_i a further
   -1.5 to -3.5 on top of P-B4-1 (3-seed mean ~13.5-14.5 vs 18.4) at CIDEr within +-1 of P-B4-1, i.e. the Pareto bar;
   P(AUROC >= 0.85) ~0.3 (HALP's Qwen2.5-VL-7B figure and E2's 0.807 are below it). Below 0.85 the gain is <= 1 point.
  Kill criterion: dev out-of-fold AUROC of (probe + cheap features) < 0.85, or < +0.05 over the cheap critic -> stop
   after the 2-4 GPU-h gate; on test, CHAIR_i not >= 1.5 below P-B4-1 with CIDEr >= P-B4-1 - 1 -> drop.
  Synthetic training data: none for the captioner (probe inputs are model states of model-generated candidates; labels
   are existing human references). Confidence: low-medium.

Not proposed (considered; reasons in section 9): tag-SFT (the orchestrator's first suggestion), retrieval-augmented SFT

10. RISKS, CAVEATS, OPEN QUESTIONS
==========================================================================================================================
- All KTVIC numbers are test-set effect-size estimates from ONE seed's sample pool per adapter. P1 s43 is a low seed
  (143.9 vs s42's 149.8); gains may shrink on s42 (regression to the mean). Samples for s42/s44 cost 1.1 GPU-h.
- The cross-fitted critic of pilot 8 was fitted on test labels (other folds); 8b/8c fit it on the other adapter's pool
  (same test images, different model) and keep the gain. A dev-fitted critic is the real test.
- Retrieval keyed on the greedy caption is a two-pass procedure; it uses only model output and train data (no leakage).
  15 test images have a train near-duplicate; the 543-image numbers match the 558-image ones.
- Simulated-probe numbers assume probe noise independent of the cheap features: optimistic.
- Winner's curse: the same-reference oracles (objorc 152.5-164.3, CIDEr oracles 167-201) are NOT achievable; only the
  independent-panel and simulated rows are realistic ceilings.
- Power: per-seed paired CI half-width ~ +-4 CIDEr; pooling 3 seeds' image-level paired differences gives ~ +-2.5.
  A between-seed t-test (3 per arm) detects only ~ +12.7 (R5-00), so EV-MBR claims must use the pooled paired bootstrap.
- CHAIR-vi uses reference gold: part of the CHAIR_i and precision gain is typicality; about 60% of the precision gain
  holds against the independent OpenViIC panel (section 5 D2). Faithfulness wording should use the two-panel numbers.
- Overlap with B3 (decoding): the self-MBR half and the candidate pool are B3's levers; B4 contributes the retrieved
  panel references, the presence/typicality analysis and the mention-worthiness veto.
