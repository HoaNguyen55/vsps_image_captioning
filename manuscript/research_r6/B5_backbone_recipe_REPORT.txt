B5_backbone_recipe -- strongest open backbone and QLoRA recipe on one RTX 3090 (R6 lever B5)
Agent B5, 12 Oct 2026. CPU only (local, nice 15, <=4 threads); n2/n3 used read-only (scp copies of logs, data and
one prediction file; nothing written there, GPUs and /root/queue untouched). Repo read-only. Scoring with a copy of
HEAD's evaluator code (head/), RDRSegmenter word level, x100. No new labels; AI-silver not used.
Every number computed on KTVIC test below is an EFFECT-SIZE ESTIMATE, not a tuned result: nothing was selected on test.

=====================================================================================================================
0. BOTTOM LINE
=====================================================================================================================
1. Nothing in this lever is a breakthrough. Backbone and recipe changes together are worth about +2 to +5 CIDEr
   (central about +3) over the P1 seed mean. That is useful on top of whatever method wins R6, but below the +8 bar. Novelty: none
   (engineering).
2. The most reliable recipe gain is SEED ENSEMBLING, deployed as an exact delta-space LoRA soup.
   - CPU proxy: MBR over 4 existing P1 replicates gives CIDEr 149.1 against a seed mean of 146.3:
     +2.79 [+0.62, +5.15].
   - The other metrics move the same way: B-4 +0.9, ROUGE-L +0.6, METEOR +0.2, CHAIR_i -0.3.
   - With K=3 the gain is +2.3 on average over the four 3-of-4 subsets (range +1.1 to +2.8).
   - Literature agrees: averaging the deltas of 3 seed LoRAs beats the individual seeds by +0.4 to +3.7 points (vanilla-LoRA rows)
     (SeedLoRA, ICML 2025).
   - It costs no extra training, because the paper needs >= 3 seeds per arm anyway.
   - Measured on the existing adapters:
     - P1 seed deltas have cosine 0.29-0.31 (SeedLoRA's same-task range), with uncorrelated random A inits.
     - So the soup must be formed in DELTA space ("cat"); factor averaging is invalid.
     - It keeps the shared component in full and shrinks the seed-specific part to 58 %.
3. BACKBONE. The strongest open <=10B VLM that fits QLoRA on one 3090 in Oct 2026 is Qwen/Qwen3.5-9B
   (27 Feb 2026, Apache-2.0, 201 languages, natively multimodal, hybrid Gated-DeltaNet).
   - Estimated QLoRA SFT peak: about 15.4 GB at a 512-token cap and about 16.2 GB at 1,024 (+-1.5 GB).
   - The expected KTVIC gain after SFT is small, 0 to +2 CIDEr:
     - In PaliGemma 1 -> 2 a newer LM of the same size gave no fine-tuned captioning gain (COCO -0.6, COCO-35L -1.9).
     - Only LM size helped: 3B -> 10B gave +1.4 to +2.4.
     - LoRA-tuned Qwen3.5-9B beats Qwen3-VL-8B by only +0.6 / +1.2 OA on CDVQA (arXiv 2604.18429).
   - Fallback: Qwen3-VL-8B, an easier port (standard attention, transformers >= 4.57).
4. Vietnamese tokenization is NOT a differentiator. Every modern tokenizer needs 1.03-1.19 tokens per syllable on
   KTVIC captions, i.e. 12.4-14.5 tokens per test caption.
   - Qwen3.5's 248K vocabulary is the most efficient: 12.43 [12.35, 12.51] vs 13.72 for Qwen2.5/Qwen3 (-9.4 %).
   - MiniCPM-V 4 (MiniCPM4 tokenizer) is the outlier at 3.48 tokens per syllable (42 tokens per caption):
     exclude it.
5. Vision tokens are not a first-order lever on KTVIC.
   - The 512-token cap already covers native resolution for 281/558 test images.
   - Raising the cap to 1,024 raises the mean from 417 to 592 tokens.
   - Qwen3.x uses 32-px tokens: at the same 512 cap it sees 20 % more pixels (0.39 vs 0.33 MP) with fewer tokens
     (382 vs 417).
   - InternVL's default tiling (max 12 tiles + thumbnail) costs 2,134 tokens per image, 5x Qwen2.5-VL's, for no
     captioning reason.
6. The legacy loss-mask bug does not matter for P1 (predicted, then confirmed by the finished clean pair). It is
   predicted to matter for fuse2:
   - In P1 rows, 69 % of supervised tokens were the instruction and assistant header. The response was 31 %
     (exact, Qwen2.5-VL tokenizer).
   - transformers 4.51.3 Qwen2.5-VL computes a per-row MEAN loss (no num_items_in_batch).
   - For P1 the bug mainly slowed response learning:
     - The fixed instruction is memorised within about 40 steps (logged loss 4.25 -> 0.59 by step 50).
     - Adam's step is suppressed by 27 % at step 50, falling to 6 % at step 470.
     - At matched steps of epoch 1, response token accuracy is 3-6 points lower than under the fix (P7).
     - Predicted |dCIDEr| <= 4, direction unknown: inside the single-seed MDE.
   - In fuse2 the bug weighted detailed-row responses 1.91x relative to short-row responses. The fix removes that
     weighting, so fuse2-short should become more P1-like. That is a real difference-in-differences (DiD), not
     seed noise.
   - Clean test (p1assist_s42 vs p1mask_s42: same seed, LoRA init and data order) finished during this round:
     - CIDEr 148.66 vs 147.67, i.e. +0.99 [-3.59, +5.43]. CHAIR_i +0.26 [-1.19, +1.67]. B-4 +0.95.
     - Every pre-registered bound held (P10).
     - So for P1 the fix is metric-neutral. For fuse2 it is untested and predicted to matter.
7. Do not run, each <= +1 or harmful: DoRA / rsLoRA / PiSSA / LoRA+ / rank sweeps, NEFTune, R-Drop, and vision-tower
   LoRA.
   - NEFTune makes outputs about 2.8x longer and lowers BLEU/ROUGE against references, which is fatal under CIDEr-D's
     length penalty.
   - DoRA on COCO captions: +0.6 CIDEr.
8. Headroom diagnostic: on KTVIC test a held-out human caption scores CIDEr-D 181.5 [173.7, 189.0] against the other
   4 references.
   - P1 seeds score 143.7-149.8 against the same 4 references.
   - The headroom (32-38 points) is in matching the annotators' phrasing, not in recognition. Consensus levers
     (ensembles, multi-reference targets, CIDEr-RL) can reach it; a better visual backbone mostly cannot.
   - Caveat: KTVIC references were typed by one person from 3 participants and are dependent, which inflates this
     ceiling.

2. BACKBONE SURVEY (verified on the HF API, 12 Oct 2026; MP = megapixels seen per image)
=====================================================================================================================
Column key:
- tok/cap: tokens per KTVIC test caption [95 % CI].
- vis tokens: mean vision tokens per KTVIC test image at a ~512 / ~1,024 budget.
- W4: estimated 4-bit resident weights (NF4 + double-quant; embeddings, lm_head and norms in bf16).
- peak: estimated QLoRA SFT peak at bs 1 with gradient checkpointing, +-1.5 GB. It is anchored on the MEASURED
  Qwen2.5-VL-7B peak of 11.7-12.7 GB (nvidia-smi, n2 p1assist and n3 mr5assist, 12 Oct). Adjustments: dW4,
  logits (seq x vocab x ~14 B), LoRA + Adam (16 B per parameter), activations.

   - Vietnamese: Qwen2.5 LM is multilingual; MTVQA (9 languages incl. vi) 29.2.
   - Captioning: no public COCO/XM3600 numbers. KTVIC zero-shot CIDEr 29.0; P1 SFT 146.3 (4 replicates, SD 3.0).

What published evidence says about a backbone swap AFTER task fine-tuning (closest to our setting):
- PaliGemma 1 -> 2: same 3B size, newer Gemma LM, same transfer protocol. Model cards, arXiv 2407.07726 and
  2412.03555.
  - COCOcap: 141.9 -> 141.3 at 224 px, 144.6 -> 143.4 at 448 px.
  - COCO-35L avg34: 115.8 -> 113.9 and 118.0 -> 115.8.
  - XM3600: 42.4 -> 42.8 and 42.9 -> 43.2.
  - Net: no gain from a newer same-size LM. LM size 3B -> 10B: +2.4 COCOcap, +1.9 COCO-35L, +1.7 XM3600.
  - Resolution 224 -> 448 (4x tokens): +1.2 to +2.1.
- LoRA SFT on CDVQA (arXiv 2604.18429), Qwen3.5-9B vs Qwen3-VL-8B: 74.05 vs 73.47 (test1), 69.44 vs 68.29 (test2).
  Sizes 0.8B -> 9B do not order monotonically.
- Centurio (arXiv 2501.05122), zero-shot XM3600 multilingual CIDEr: Qwen2-VL-7B 24.6, InternVL2.5-8B 15.7,
  Centurio-Qwen 34.4.
  - Chat VLMs are weak multilingual captioners zero-shot. In-language SFT is what fixes it, which we already do
    (zero-shot 29 -> 146).
- Reading: after SFT on 3.7K images the backbone generation matters little; size and in-language data matter.
  Expect 0 to +2 CIDEr from Qwen3.5-9B, not +8.

4. RECIPE LEVERS: literature, expected effect on KTVIC short-mode CIDEr, verdict
=====================================================================================================================
Current recipe:
- QLoRA NF4 with double-quant; LoRA r16, alpha 32, dropout 0.05, on all 7 LM projections. The vision tower is
  excluded on purpose (its LoRA got no gradient).
- lr 1e-4, linear decay, no warm-up, 2 epochs; batch 1 x accumulation 8 (942 steps for P1); 512-token cap; legacy
  mask in every finished run.

(a) LoRA rank / alpha / rsLoRA / DoRA / PiSSA / LoRA+.
    - QLoRA (arXiv 2305.14314): "r is unrelated to final performance if LoRA is used on all layers".
    - "LoRA Without Regret" (Thinking Machines blog, 29 Sep 2025, read):
      - LoRA equals full fine-tuning on small and medium SFT data.
      - Use all layers, especially the MLP.
      - The optimal LR is about 10x the full-FT LR and nearly rank-independent under 1/r scaling (alpha 32).
      - Our 1e-4 at r16 is in range.
    - DoRA (arXiv 2402.09353, HTML read): VL-BART COCO captioning 115.3 -> 115.9 (+0.6); LLaVA-1.5 average
      66.9 -> 67.6.
    - Expected effect: <= +1, far below the single-seed CIDEr MDE of about 14-16 (A6 power analysis).
      VERDICT: do not run (agrees with A6).
(b) Vision-tower LoRA / projector (merger) tuning.
    - Code fact: the 192 vision LoRA tensors never received a gradient under the current checkpointing setup
      (train_stage2 comment). Enabling them needs input-grad hooks on pixel_values or unfrozen merger weights.
    - The prior is weak. KTVIC scenes are in-distribution for web-pretrained encoders, and the CIDEr headroom is in
      phrasing (P4), not perception.
    - VERDICT: not a metric lever. Run only if a perception-error analysis shows misrecognition (B4 owns evidence).
(c) Epochs / LR / warm-up / early stopping on a dev split.
    - The loss drops 0.074 nats per token at the epoch boundary (A6), so epoch 2 is mostly memorisation.
    - The direction of the CIDEr effect is unknown: less memorisation can mean more typical captions, or less
      KTVIC style.
    - Free check: the epoch-1 checkpoints (checkpoint-472) of p1mask_s42 and p1assist_s42 exist on n2; 0.23 GPU-h per
      mode.
    - Any epoch choice for a claim must be made on a 300-image dev split of TRAIN (context rule).
    - VERDICT: fold into proposal B5-1 as within-run trajectory averaging (cheap). Do not sweep LR.
(d) NEFTune (arXiv 2310.05914, HTML read).
    - LLaMA-2-7B on Alpaca: AlpacaEval answers grow from 60.5 to 169.4 whitespace tokens.
    - ROUGE-L and BLEU against ground-truth answers DROP. The stated mechanism is less fitting of the dataset's
      "formatting, exact wording, and text length".
    - That is exactly what CIDEr-D and BLEU reward on KTVIC (12-word references, Gaussian length penalty with
      sigma 6).
    - VERDICT: predicted harmful. Do not run.
(e) R-Drop (arXiv 2106.14448, abstract): NMT, summarisation, NLU, LM and image classification; no captioning.
    - Twice the compute per step. Only LoRA dropout (0.05) is stochastic in our model, so the consistency signal is
      weak.
    - VERDICT: <= +1, not worth 2x GPU.
(f) Weight averaging.
    - Model soups (arXiv 2203.05482, ICML 2022): averaging fine-tuned weights approaches ensembling "without
      incurring any additional inference or memory costs".
    - SeedLoRA (Liu et al., ICML 2025, PMLR 267:38384, full text read): seeds trained on the SAME task.
      - Cosine similarity between seed deltas is about 0.28-0.30, against about 0 across tasks.
      - Plain "Model Soup" of 3 seed deltas vs single seeds:
        - LLaMA2-7B GSM8K 66.6 vs 63.8-64.1.
        - Mistral-7B GSM8K 79.1 vs 75.4-76.3.
        - MATH 28.5 vs 24.8-25.9.
      - Their two-stage fusion adds a little more: 68.9 / 80.7 / 28.8.
    - Caveats:
      - Averaging the FACTORS of adapters with different random inits fails: AdapterSoup (arXiv 2302.07027)
        recommends a shared init.
      - CoTo (arXiv 2506.05713) reports a vanilla-LoRA midpoint at about 39 % vs about 79 % on commonsense, and its
        footnote separates factor "weight fusion" (B1+B2)(A1+A2) from the delta "model ensemble" B1A1 + B2A2.
      - So average in DELTA space. PEFT add_weighted_adapter(combination_type="cat") is exact; "linear" averages
        factors and is wrong across seeds.
    - Expected effect: +2 to +3 CIDEr over the seed mean (P2 proxy), plus lower seed variance (SeedLoRA vanilla-LoRA soups: +0.4 to +3.7 over single seeds).
    - VERDICT: PROPOSAL B5-1.
(g) Image resolution / vision-token budget.
    - P3: 1,024 changes only 114-277 test images. PaliGemma 4x tokens gave +1.2 to +2.1 on COCO.
    - Literature cited in A6 (FastV, SparseVLM, ICLR 2025 token studies): everyday scenes tolerate token cuts.
    - Expected effect: <= +1. VERDICT: keep job 73 optional. If the backbone moves to Qwen3.x, keep the 512 cap; it
      already sees 20 % more pixels.
(h) Backbone. Section 2. VERDICT: PROPOSAL B5-2 (low confidence, 0 to +2), or use it only as the second-backbone
    replication.
(i) Loss mask. Section 5. VERDICT: adopt --loss-mask assistant for every new run. This removes the length-dependent
    row reweighting, a hidden hyper-parameter, rather than chasing a metric gain.

=====================================================================================================================
5. THE LEGACY LOSS-MASK BUG: WHAT IT DID AND THE EXPECTED EFFECT OF THE FIX
=====================================================================================================================
Facts:
- Every finished run used the legacy mask. The code measured the prompt length before the image placeholder was
  expanded.
- So the 29-token instruction and the 5-token assistant header were supervised:
  - 69 % of supervised tokens in P1 and multiref rows;
  - 68 % in fuse2 short rows;
  - 39 % in fuse2 detailed rows (P5).
- In transformers 4.51.3 each row's loss is a per-row mean and rows are weighted equally (P5, code-verified).
  Three mechanisms follow:
  (1) Gradient dilution. Response gradients are scaled by about 0.31 (short rows) or 0.61 (detailed rows).
      - A constant scale is cancelled by Adam, and by clipping when the norm exceeds 1.
      - A scale that DIFFERS between rows is not cancelled. In fuse2, legacy effectively weighted detailed-row
        responses 1.91x relative to short-row responses, against the fix.
  (2) A memorisation transient. Steps 1-40 are spent learning the fixed instruction (logged loss 4.25 -> 0.59 by
      step 50). The early raw gradient is 5.4x the late one. After clipping, Adam's second moment stays inflated,
      cutting the effective step by about 27 % -> 6 % over epoch 1 (P7).
  (3) Instruction tokens keep a share of every update. At matched steps the legacy run fits the response worse:
      token accuracy 0.67-0.69 vs 0.73 (P7).
Literature:
- Prompt-loss weight matters mainly for SHORT completions. PLW = 0 is often worst; the optimum is about 0.24; effects
  are < 2 points on short-generation benchmarks (Huerta-Enochian & Ko, EMNLP 2024, arXiv 2401.13586).
- Loss over instructions helps with long instructions and short outputs on little data, through less overfitting
  (Shi et al., NeurIPS 2024, arXiv 2405.14394).
- Both studies use DIVERSE instructions. Ours is a single fixed string, so its "regularising" content is near zero
  once memorised. What remains is mechanisms (1)-(3).
Predictions (written before p1assist_s42's test numbers existed; P10 has the outcome, and all P1 bounds held):
- P1, assistant vs legacy, same seed:
  - The fix trains the response faster, with a higher effective step and no instruction share, so the model fits
    and memorises the training captions more.
  - Short-mode CIDEr changes by |d| <= 4: the direction is not predictable, and it sits inside the 1-seed MDE of
    about 14-16.
  - B-4 and ROUGE-L by <= 1.5. Mean caption length by <= 0.5 words. CHAIR_i by <= 2.5 (single-seed MDE 2.7).
  - The R5-00 §8 rule then lands in "keep old runs, disclose" or the middle zone, not "rerun everything", for P1.
- fuse2, assistant vs legacy (no clean pair has run; 50_b was killed because it was still legacy):
  - The fix up-weights short rows relative to detailed rows (1:1 instead of 1:1.91).
  - Predicted direction: fuse2-short becomes more P1-like (fewer mentions per caption, higher precision, higher
    B-4/CIDEr). fuse2-detailed changes little in content and may get slightly shorter.
  - This is the mechanism behind a possible nonzero DiD in R5-00 §8.
  - The mr5assist_s42 run on n3 (multiref, assistant) is homogeneous short rows, like P1, so the reweighting does
    not apply there.
Recommendation: use --loss-mask assistant for every new R6 run (it removes a hidden, length-dependent row weighting).
- For the paper, report fuse2's legacy runs as "length-weighted" training and give the reweighting factor (1.91).
- Do not expect a metric gain from the fix itself.

6. PROPOSALS (at most 2; ranked by expected metric gain x confidence / cost; novelty is low for both)
=====================================================================================================================
B5-1  Seed soup: deploy the exact delta-space average of K >= 3 seed adapters (plus within-run trajectory averaging)
  Mechanism:
  - Train the final recipe with K seeds, as the 3-seed claims require anyway.
  - For every adapted layer, form dW_soup = (1/K) * sum_k (alpha/r) * B_k A_k. With PEFT this is
    add_weighted_adapter(names, [1/K]*K, combination_type="cat"): exact, rank K*r, works on the 4-bit base. Never use
    "linear", which averages the factors and is wrong across random inits.
  - Optional: within each run, average the epoch-1 and epoch-2 deltas first (2-point LAWA/SWA). It is free: the
    checkpoints already exist.
  - Optional upgrade: SeedLoRA two-stage fusion (TIES-style robust/conflict split + SVD subspace fusion). Report the
    uniform soup as primary.
  - Measured on the existing adapters (P8):
    - Seed deltas have cosine 0.30 and independent A inits, so "cat" is mandatory.
    - The uniform soup has 0.73x the member delta norm.
  - A norm-preserving variant (weights 1/(K*0.73)) is a hyper-parameter: choose it on the 300-image dev split,
    never on test.
  - Fallbacks if weight space misbehaves:
    - Logit-ensemble decoding over the K adapters: one base, K adapter passes per token, about 0.7 GPU-h per mode on
      558 images.
    - MBR over the K greedy captions: the measured +2.8.
  Why it should raise the key metrics:
  - KTVIC CIDEr variance is template variance. Same-recipe seeds differ by 6.1 CIDEr; all 4 write the same caption
    on only 26 % of images.
  - CIDEr-D rewards the modal phrasing of the references (P4). A consensus over seeds lands on that mode more often
    (P2).
  Novel element:
  - None methodologically (model soups 2022; SeedLoRA 2025).
  - The only new content: showing that seed consensus collapses CIDEr's template lottery in small-data VLM
    captioning, and using the soup to shrink seed variance. That shrinks the MDE of every later comparison.
  Closest prior: SeedLoRA (Liu et al., ICML 2025, PMLR 267:38384-38396); Model soups (Wortsman et al., ICML 2022,
    arXiv 2203.05482); AdapterSoup (arXiv 2302.07027).
  Pilot (0.5 GPU-h; pre-register before running):
  - CPU merge already done, ready to copy to n2 and evaluate:
    - adapters/soup_p1x3_uniform: rank 48, alpha 96, = (1/3)(p1_s43 + p1_s44 + p1mask_s42) in delta space.
    - adapters/traj_p1mask_s42_ep1ep2: rank 32, the epoch-1 + epoch-2 average.
    - Built by make_soup.py (numpy only). Its built-in assert checks soup(x) == sum_k w_k delta_k(x) on random
      probes; it passed.
  - Run, e.g.: python scripts/evaluate.py --model qwen2.5-vl-7b --adapter <dir> --split test_data.json --prompt short
    --name soup_p1x3-short. Then the same for detailed.
  - Evaluate the uniform delta soup in short mode, plus detailed mode, on KTVIC test: 0.25 GPU-h per mode.
  - Pre-registered comparison: soup minus the mean of its members, paired image bootstrap B = 5000.
  - The same pilot on epoch-1 + epoch-2 trajectory averages costs 0.23 GPU-h per run.
  Expected gain (vs the mean of its own seeds; P2 proxy + SeedLoRA):
  - CIDEr +1.5 to +3 (central +2.3 for K = 3). B-4 +0.5 to +1.0. ROUGE-L +0.3 to +0.6. METEOR +0.2 to +0.4.
  - CHAIR_i about -0.3 (+-1).
  - Between-soup SD expected well below the single-seed 3.0. The overlapping 3-of-4 MBR subsets span 147.9-149.8
    vs 143.7-149.8 for single seeds.
  - It stacks with any method, because it acts on seed variance, not on targets.
  GPU-h: 0.5 for the pilot. In production 0 extra training, plus 0.25 GPU-h per soup per mode. If two independent
    soups are wanted for a variance estimate, add 3 seeds: about 7.2 GPU-h for the P1 recipe, 13.4 for fuse2.
  Kill criterion:
  - On the pilot, uniform delta soup CIDEr < member mean + 1.0, OR CHAIR_i > member mean + 1.0.
  - If killed, switch to logit-ensemble decoding. If that also fails (< mean + 1.0), drop the lever.
  Synthetic training data: no.  Confidence: medium.
