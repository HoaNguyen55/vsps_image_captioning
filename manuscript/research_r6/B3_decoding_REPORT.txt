0. BOTTOM LINE
=====================================================================================================================
1. The "MBR at T=0.8 drops CIDEr to ~97" finding (R5-00 §2b) came from the OBJECT-CONSENSUS utility, not from sampling.
   CIDEr-D MBR over the same six T=0.8 samples gives 139.4 (P1 s43) / 141.1 (fuse2), i.e. +42.6 [37.0, 48.5] and
   +43.1 [37.6, 48.6] CIDEr over the object-consensus pick (96.7 / 98.0), which chose long, object-heavy samples
   (12.0 words, 2.13 objects/caption vs 10.5 / 1.77). R5-00 was right that T=0.8 samples are poor CANDIDATES (a random
   one scores 117.7), but they are good EVIDENCE.
2. Committee CIDEr-D MBR, using greedy captions as hypotheses and T=0.8 samples as evidence only, is the largest
   zero-training metric lever measured in this project:
     - 4 adapters (P1 s42/s43/s44 + fuse2 s42; no tags, no E5), 4 greedy captions + 12 samples:
       CIDEr 156.2, +10.3 [6.9, 14.0] over the P1 3-seed mean; +6.4 [2.1, 10.8] over the best seed (s42).
       Also B-4 +1.8, METEOR +1.1, ROUGE-L +1.6, and CHAIR_i -1.0 [-2.3, +0.1], so CHAIR_i is not worse.
     - The task's 8 members + 12 samples: CIDEr 157.5, +11.7 [7.5, 16.0]; +11.0 [6.6, 15.5] even without P1 s42.
   This meets the R6 bar (+8 CIDEr over the P1 mean, CHAIR_i not worse) as an estimate, at K-fold inference cost and
   no training. The CIs come from a paired image bootstrap pooled across the 3 P1 seeds (B = 2000).
3. Most of the gain is real quality, not typicality. Scored against the INDEPENDENT UIT-OpenViIC panel of the same
   images, the 4-adapter committee + samples gains +3.0 [1.6, 4.5] CIDEr and the 8-member one +3.5 [1.9, 5.3].
   Relative to the P1 mean that is +6.7% and +7.8%, against +7.1% and +8.0% on the KTVIC references, while CHAIR_i
   against the panel is unchanged.
   Adding human captions of retrieved TRAIN images as extra evidence (m-RNN-style, 2015) adds a further +3.2 KTVIC
   CIDEr (160.7, +14.9 [10.4, 19.2]) but ~0 on the independent panel (-0.3 at k = 5; -0.8 to +0.7 over k = 1..20).
   That part is KTVIC annotator-style typicality, the same effect as R5's "precision measures typicality" finding.
4. Committee MBR with samples moves OFF the P1<->fuse2 trade-off. Compared with the per-image P1/fuse2 mixture that
   names the same number of objects, CHAIR_i is -2.3 [-3.4, -1.2] (4 adapters) and -3.0 [-4.2, -1.8] (8 members).
   V5's object-F1 committee (+2 P vs the union gold) and the detector tags also leave it. Here the test is CHAIR_i,
   and it holds for the CIDEr-D rule too.
5. Novelty is the weak point. CIDEr consensus reranking against retrieved training captions is 2015 work (m-RNN §8:
   +9.4/+9.8 CIDEr on COCO; Devlin et al. 2015). Committee/ensemble MBR is covered by UA-MBR (ICLR 2025) and
   Multi-prompt MBR (EMNLP 2024). Separate hypothesis and pseudo-reference sets are covered by Kamigaito et al. 2024.
   What is new here is narrow:
     - the committee is drawn from the paper's own supervision-policy ladder;
     - samples serve as evidence only;
     - the gain is decomposed into transferable quality (independent panel) and typicality (retrieval).
   The highest-value use of B3 is as the decoding stage of whatever training method R6 picks, plus a distillation
   design that removes the K-fold inference cost.
6. Length/EOS calibration has no headroom: P1 averages 10.42-10.49 words against 10.68 for the references (SD 1.66 vs
   1.68), and only 34 of 558 images are more than 3 words from their mean reference length. Beam search, LoRA seed soups
   and logit ensembles need a GPU; they are folded into P1's 8 GPU-h pilot as side arms.

- Scorers: HEAD's scripts/evaluate.py and rescap/ (exported with git archive HEAD into headsrc/), pycocoevalcap 1.2
  (BLEU closest-ref, METEOR 1.5 jar "-l en -norm", ROUGE-L, CIDEr-D sigma 6), rescap.chair CHAIR (gold drawn from the

- Checks, all passed:
  (a) RDRSegmenter(caption).lower() equals KTVIC's own segment_caption on 18,845/18,845 train and 2,790/2,790 test captions.
  (b) For all 10 stored systems, per-image re-scoring reproduces data/results/<sys>-short.json to 1e-6 on B-4, METEOR,
      ROUGE-L, CIDEr and CHAIR_i (asserted in code).
  (c) The CIDEr-D replica used for selection equals pycocoevalcap per image (max |diff| 8.9e-16).
  (d) METEOR is re-aggregated per bootstrap draw from the jar's per-segment statistics. This reproduces the jar's
      corpus score exactly once the jar's rule is applied: a perfect-match segment adds no chunk to the aggregate.
      Without that rule the aggregate is off by 0.026.
  (e) HEAD's evaluate.py copy re-scored three MBR outputs end to end and matched: cmbr_all8|ev+12smp gives
      B-4 46.9 / METEOR 40.2 / ROUGE-L 65.7 / CIDEr 157.5 / CHAIR_i 17.1.
- Statistics: paired bootstrap over images, B = 2000, numpy seed 42.
  - BLEU-4 and METEOR are recomputed per draw from summed sufficient statistics; CIDEr and ROUGE-L are per-image means;
    CHAIR_i is the ratio of summed counts.
  - "vs P1 mean" averages the three seeds' corpus metric on the same draw, i.e. a paired image-level bootstrap pooled
    across seeds, as CONTEXT_R6 allows for a +8 claim. It does not capture between-committee seed variance; the GPU
    pilot adds 3 seed-disjoint committees for that.
- Selection rule (fixed before any result was seen): per image, the candidate h maximising the mean over the pool
  (minus h itself) of CIDEr-D(h, e).
  - Settings: sigma 6, clipped tf-idf; IDF from the 3,769 KTVIC TRAIN reference sets; ties go to the smallest string.
  - Candidates are the members' greedy captions. Evidence is the other members' greedy captions plus, where stated,
    the 6 short-prompt samples per image (T = 0.8, top_p 0.95) of p1_s43 and fuse2_s42 (n2:/root/ncs-data/samples,
    copied read-only).
  - IDF from the test candidate pool instead of the train refs changes nothing (153.6 vs 153.6).
- Retrieval block (pilot 2; k = 5 and lambda = 1 fixed before results): the 5 train images whose 5 references best
  match the committee's own captions (TF-IDF uni+bigram cosine; no test refs), or the CLIP ViT-B/32 nearest train image
  (data/dedup, images only). Utility = model-evidence mean + lambda x mean CIDEr-D vs the retrieved human captions.
- Data used: the 8 short-prompt prediction files named by the task, the 2 object-consensus MBR files, the 2 sample files,
  KTVIC refs, and the UIT-OpenViIC panel of 555 test images (data/ktvic_openviic_refs.json, read only for scoring;
  no panel caption is printed or put in any report or committed file; segmented copies exist only in the scratch
  cache cache/seg.json). No AI-made label was used.

Levers that need a GPU (not piloted; expectations only)
- Beam search vs greedy: no beam outputs exist on n2/n3 (checked).
  m-RNN reranked 10-best beam hypotheses. Borgeaud & Emerson 2020 report range voting on the beam beating beam search
  at large beams: COCO BLEU-4 28.23 at k = 100 vs 27.16 for the best beam. Neither reports a CIDEr comparison usable here.
  Beam N-best is proposed as extra hypotheses in P1's pilot (0.4 GPU-h).
- LoRA seed soup: average the three P1 seeds' updates (Delta W = B A) exactly by concatenating them into one rank-48
  adapter with weights 1/3 (PEFT add_weighted_adapter, combination_type "cat"; LoRA Soups / CAT, arXiv 2410.13025).
  This runs at 1x inference. Expected +0 to +3 CIDEr.
  Model soups (2203.05482) and SeedLoRA (ICML 2025) report gains from fusing same-task seeds, but nothing for
  captioning or CIDEr.
- Logit ensemble of the 3 seeds: 3x forward passes. Prior work: LoRA ensembles (2310.00035, probability averaging,
  QA tasks); ATED (2510.18321, token-level LVLM ensembles against hallucination).
  The committee MBR above is a sequence-level ensemble at the same decode count. Whether token-level averaging beats it
  is the question the side arm answers.
- Length/EOS calibration: dropped (section 0, point 6).

Full text read
- Mao et al., m-RNN, ICLR 2015, arXiv 1412.6632, §8 "Nearest neighbor as reference".
  Reranks 10-best beam hypotheses by mean CIDEr against the captions of the k = 60 nearest training images (top
  m = 125). CIDEr goes 0.842 -> 0.938 on val (+9.4) and 0.819 -> 0.917 on the COCO test server (+9.8). Oracle 1.272.
  This scoops "retrieved human captions as MBR evidence".
- Borgeaud & Emerson 2020, WNGT, arXiv 1908.06288 (ACL 2020.ngt-1.11). Range voting (MBR with a similarity) over beam
  candidates for a Show-and-Tell captioner on COCO and for MT. BLEU-1/BLEU-4 only, no CIDEr; human preference
  106 vs 73.
- Daheim, Meister, Moellenhoff, Gurevych, UA-MBR, ICLR 2025, arXiv 2503.05318.
  - MBR with an expectation over a weight posterior (IVON, deep ensembles, snapshots).
  - Sequence-level estimators pool hypotheses across members (Eq. 9) or sum utilities per member (Eq. 10).
  - Tasks: MT, summarisation and data-to-text; no captioning. IWSLT17 BLEU 19.93 -> 21.24 with a deep ensemble.
  - "More successful when the ensembled models are diverse."
  This is the closest prior for committee MBR.
- Deguchi & Nagata, CBDT, EMNLP 2025, arXiv 2509.12677, DOI 10.18653/v1/2025.emnlp-main.1710.
  - Estimates expected utility from retrieved domain examples (DINOv2 image similarity for captioning).
  - BLIP-2, 256 epsilon samples, BERTScore utility. MSCOCO BLEU: MAP 17.5, MBR 25.6, MBR+CBDT 26.0.
  This is retrieval-augmented MBR for captioning in 2025.
- Jeong et al., ECO, CVPR 2024 NICE workshop, arXiv 2405.01028.
  - A CIDEr-derived consensus score over a ~60-caption pool, where the references are the other pool captions,
    combined with a CLIP-model ensemble.
  - CIDEr: consensus alone 202.89, combined 218.47.
- Jinnai et al., Model-Based MBR, ICML 2024, arXiv 2311.05263.
  - BLIP-2 captioning on COCO and nocaps, BLEU only; epsilon 0.02, BERTScore utility.
  - COCO MBR BLEU 27.12 -> 33.24 as |H| goes 4 -> 64.
- Finkelstein et al., MBR and QE Finetuning, ICLR 2024, arXiv 2309.10966 (en->de, COMET20).
  - MBR teacher +1.56 over base beam.
  - Self-MBR finetuning on 200k new monolingual sources: +2.15, beating its own teacher.
  - The Phase-1 self-MBR student gained only +0.23, below its teacher.
  - Stronger external teacher: +5.72.
  This is the basis for P2.
- Wang et al., LoRA ensembles, arXiv 2310.00035. Five LoRA members differing in adapter init, probability averaging;

4. PROPOSALS (at most 2; ranked by expected metric gain x novelty x cost)
=====================================================================================================================
P1. LadderMBR: a supervision-policy committee with CIDEr-D MBR and samples as evidence only. Inference only.

Mechanism
- Members are the adapters the paper already trains or has queued for the frontier analysis:
  - P1 and fuse2 at s42/s43/s44 (all six adapters exist; fuse2 s43/s44 on n3);
  - the target-policy ladder (cons, unf2, rnd2, multiref, p1ep4) as it lands.
- Each member decodes one greedy short caption (the hypothesis) and 6 samples (T 0.8, top_p 0.95; evidence only).
- Per image, pick the hypothesis with the highest mean CIDEr-D (sigma 6, IDF from KTVIC train refs) against every other
  member's greedy caption and all samples. No test refs, no weights, ties go to the smallest string.
- Report two variants:
  - "LadderMBR", the main claim;
  - "LadderMBR+R", which adds the m-RNN retrieval block (k = 5, lambda = 1) only for the maximum-metric table, labelled
    as typicality.
- Freeze this rule now. Any decoding change (e.g. epsilon 0.02 or N = 16) is chosen on UIT-ViIC val with UIT-ViIC
  adapters, never on KTVIC test.

Novel element (narrow)
- The diversity source is supervision policy at controlled points of the measured precision vs object-count frontier,
  not seeds, prompts or architectures.
- Samples are restricted to evidence; this is measured to matter. Added as evidence they lift a committee by +2.7 to
  +7.1 CIDEr. As candidates, MBR over the samples scores 2.8 (fuse2) and 4.5 (P1 s43) below the adapter's own greedy
  caption, and a random sample scores 26 below.
- The gain is decomposed with an independent second human panel into transferable quality (+7% relative on both panels)
  and typicality (retrieval: +2% KTVIC, ~0 on the panel).
- The committee moves off the trade-off chord (CHAIR_i -2.3 at matched object count).

Closest prior
- UA-MBR (2503.05318): ensemble MBR with diversity helping; MT and summarisation, no captioning, no policy diversity.
- Multi-prompt MBR (2407.15343).
- Kamigaito et al. 2024 (2410.15021): decoupled samplers.
- m-RNN §8 (1412.6632) and ECO (2405.01028): CIDEr consensus in captioning.
- Delta: policy-ladder committee, evidence-only samples, panel decomposition.

GPU pilot (about 8 GPU-h, inference only, one 3090)
(i)   fuse2 s43/s44 on KTVIC test: greedy + 6 samples (1.3 GPU-h).
(ii)  6 samples for P1 s42 and s44 (0.9).
(iii) 16 epsilon samples for P1 s43, to check evidence quality (0.9).
(iv)  Side arms (1.3): P1 seed soup via rank-48 CAT, 1 greedy run; 3-seed logit ensemble greedy; beam-5 N-best for P1
      s42 as extra hypotheses.
(v)   UIT-ViIC test with the uitviic_p1/fuse2 adapters (1.6).
(vi)  A 1,000-image COCO Karpathy subset with the English adapters (2.4).
Primary endpoint: the 6-adapter committee {P1, fuse2} x {42, 43, 44}, compared with the P1 3-seed mean.
Replication: 3 seed-disjoint committees {P1_s, fuse2_s} + own samples, each vs P1_s and vs the P1 mean; paired image
bootstrap, B = 2000. Also report the same committees on the OpenViIC panel and the chord test.

Expected gain
- KTVIC: +8 to +12 CIDEr over the P1 mean. Pilot estimates: 4 adapters +10.3 [6.9, 14.0]; 8 members +11.7 [7.5, 16.0].
  Also B-4 +1.8, METEOR +1.1, ROUGE-L +1.6, CHAIR_i -1.0 to -1.3.
- Independent panel: +3 CIDEr (+7% relative).
- LadderMBR+R: a further +3 KTVIC CIDEr (pilot 160.7). Seed-disjoint 2-adapter committees: about +5.5.

Kill criterion
- On the new committee with fresh samples, the frozen rule gives < +6 CIDEr over the P1 mean on KTVIC test, or
  CHAIR_i > P1 mean + 1.0, or the panel-gain CI contains 0: downgrade to an analysis appendix.
- Drop a side arm (soup or logit ensemble) if it gains < +2 CIDEr over the P1 mean.

Synthetic training data: none. Risk: K-fold inference cost (report decodes per image).
Confidence: high for the metric gain; novelty low to moderate.

P2. Committee-MBR distillation on unlabeled in-domain images, giving one adapter at 1x inference.
FLAGGED: SYNTHETIC TRAINING DATA (model-written captions as targets). The user may veto.

Mechanism
- Run P1's frozen LadderMBR rule on about 4,000 of the ~8,770 UIT-OpenViIC images that are NOT KTVIC images.
  - Use images only, never OpenViIC captions, so the second panel stays independent.
  - Drop every image paired to a KTVIC train or test image in data/ktvic_openviic_refs.json "_pairs", and any CLIP
    near-duplicate (cos >= 0.93) of a KTVIC test or dev image.
- Keep the MBR-selected caption per image.
- Fine-tune one P1-recipe QLoRA adapter on the KTVIC train caption #1 plus these pseudo-captions.
  - The mix ratio is chosen from {0.5, 1.0} on the KTVIC dev-300 split, i.e. random.Random(0).sample(sorted(train_ids),
    300), held out while tuning.
  - Then retrain on full train for 3 seeds.
- Decode greedy.
- This fixes E5's stated failure, where self-RFT on SEEN images with an object-F1 utility caused memorisation and
  short-mode collapse:
  - new, unseen images;
  - a cross-policy teacher;
  - a CIDEr-consensus pick, which pilots show does not drift in length (10.5 words).

Novel element: a captioning VLM distilled from a supervision-policy committee's CIDEr-MBR decisions on unlabeled
in-domain images, audited on an independent human panel.

Closest prior
- MBR finetuning (2309.10966): self-MBR on 200k new sources gave +2.15 COMET vs the teacher's +1.56; the Phase-1
  self-MBR student gained +0.23.
- Sequence-level KD (1606.07947); BLIP CapFilt (2201.12086).
- Delta: committee-of-policies teacher, CIDEr consensus, captioning, panel audit.

GPU (about 35 GPU-h total)
- Teacher on 4k images: 4 adapters greedy + 2 adapters x 6 samples, about 9.3.
- Dev arm: student at 2 ratios + P1 baseline on train-minus-dev, about 11.7.
- Final: 3 seeds on full train + decoding, about 15.
- Go/no-go point after about 17 GPU-h (teacher + one student + baseline).

Expected gain: +3 to +6 CIDEr over the P1 mean at 1x inference (30-60% of the committee's +10), CHAIR_i <= P1.
Kill criterion: on dev-300, student minus P1 (trained on train-minus-dev) < +3 CIDEr, or CHAIR_i worse by > 1, or the
OpenViIC-panel CIDEr does not rise: stop.
Confidence: low (E5's history; MT evidence is positive only with new inputs).

Not proposed
- A single adapter conditioned on a policy token, as a "self-committee". Too close to the round-1 CoverageDial kill,
  and diversity may collapse.
- A learned reranker (Fernandes-style tuned reranking). It needs cross-fitted committees on dev, and it is prior art.
- Lattice/n-gram MBR decoding to synthesise new captions. Engineering-heavy and close to the killed phrase-graft fusion.

5. CAVEATS
=====================================================================================================================
- Every KTVIC number is a test-set estimate.
  - Pilot-1 variants and the retrieval k = 5 / lambda = 1 were fixed before seeing results.
  - The 4-adapter committee (pilot 4) was picked after pilots 1-3, as the natural "no tags, no E5" subset.
  - About 60 variants were scored in total (including oracles and the post-hoc grid), so treat single CIs as
    optimistic. Table E is post-hoc.
- The committee members share training data (KTVIC train) and backbone.
- The tags members need Grounding DINO at inference. E5 and E5-DPO cost extra training.
- Samples come from one sampling run (T 0.8, top_p 0.95), from two adapters only.
- CHAIR uses reference-derived gold, an upper bound that also measures typicality. The OpenViIC panel captions are
  longer (17.5 vs 12.1 words), so panel CIDEr values are about 3x lower; compare relative gains.
- METEOR follows the repo convention (jar "-l en -norm" on segmented Vietnamese).
- No UIT-ViIC or COCO committee outputs exist yet, so generalisation is untested (P1 pilot, items v and vi).
- Leakage: the dedup-543 subset gives the same gains (+11.1, +14.4). Retrieval draws only on KTVIC train references.
