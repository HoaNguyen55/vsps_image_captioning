2. THEORY: A TWO-PANEL MODEL OF REFERENCES AND WHAT IT PRESCRIBES
==========================================================================================================================
Model. For image x and class o, let d_o(x) be a latent describability.
  - Panel p's caption j mentions o with probability h_p(d_o): a panel-specific monotone selection link. KTVIC names 3.7
    classes per image over 5 captions; OpenViIC names 6.0 over 5.4.
  - Surface form follows panel style.
  - Captions inside a panel are dependent: the KTVIC paraphrase chain.
  - In CIDEr's tf-idf space (B7 T1 centroid identity): phi(r_pj) = a(x) + s_p + b_p(x) + e_pj, with
    content a(x), panel style s_p, panel-image selection b_p(x) shared by ALL of panel p's captions of x, and
    caption noise e (AR(1) along the chain).
Prop 1 (non-identifiability).
  - From panel K alone, a(x) and b_K(x) are confounded.
  - Every subset, reweighting, minimum or variance penalty over K's references keeps b_K with weight 1.
  - So n-gram robustifications cannot remove the panel-specific part; they only lose efficiency.
  - Prediction: no such reward beats plain CIDEr-D on an unseen panel. CONFIRMED (section 3a).
Prop 2 (unseen-panel Bayes rule).
  - Let panel O have n_O captions and expected class count E|G_O|, and let q_o = P(o in G_O) = 1 - E(1 - h_O(d_o))^n_O.
  - The expected object F1 of caption c is ~ 2 sum_{o in M(c)} q_o / (|M(c)| + E|G_O|) (ratio of expectations).
  - The model's samples estimate h_K(d_o) by the vote share v_o of the other candidates. With h_O >= h_K,
    q_o ~ 1 - (1 - v_o)^m, where m is about n_O.
  - Since E|G_O| (~6) >> |M| (~1.8), the rule SEEKS RECALL WITH EVIDENCE: a supported class adds ~ 2q/(|M| + E|G|);
    an unsupported class only enlarges the denominator.
  - The same expected F1, with q from the references and a PU credit for unnamed classes (KTVIC names only ~44.5% of
    mentionable classes), is the training-side reward UPR.
Prop 3 (frontier). A vote-blind increase in class count moves along the precision-count frontier, so P falls; an
  evidence-weighted increase moves off it. Tested with count-matched controls (3c).
Prop 4 (typicality blind spot). Classes added with evidence are mostly ones panel K did not name. So K-reference CHAIR_i
  rises while independent-panel P does not fall. Tested (3b, 3d).

M1  UPE-MBR: Unseen-Panel Expected-object-F1 MBR (decode time, training-free, reads no reference of the image).
  Rule. Candidates are the greedy decode plus k samples.
    - U(c) = selfMBR_CIDEr-D(c) + CIDEr-D(c, refs of the 10 train images retrieved by the greedy caption)
             + lam * 10 * 2 sum_{o in M(c)} q_o / (|M(c)| + E|G|);
    - q_o = 1 - (1 - v_o)^m, where v_o = share of the OTHER candidates naming class o;
    - keep greedy unless max U - U(greedy) > margin;
    - frozen: lam 2, m 5, margin 0.5, E|G| 6. Cost: k + 1 decodes, as PE-MBR.
  Falsifiable predictions (pre-register before any new decode):
    F1 KTVIC test, 3 P1-assist seeds; samples at T* in {0.5, 0.8} chosen on dev300 with the P1_dev adapter.
       - Pooled vs greedy: panel object F1 >= +2.5 with CI > 0, panel P >= -1.5, KTVIC CIDEr >= -3.
       - Vs the decode-time lexicon term at dev-matched cost: panel F1 >= +0.8 with CI > 0.
    F2 UIT-ViIC test + val (1,155 images), P1 UIT-ViIC adapter, 6 samples:
       - COCO-instance R >= +2.0 with CI > 0 and instance P >= -1.0 vs greedy;
       - vs the count-matched control: instance P >= +1.0 (the evidence effect on truth).
    F3 KTVIC-reference CHAIR_i does not fall (delta >= -0.5): the gain is invisible to panel-K metrics.
    Kill if any of these holds:
       - F1's panel-F1 CI contains 0;
       - the count-matched control equals UPE on panel P (CI contains 0) on 2 of 3 seeds;
       - F2's instance P falls by more than 1.

    Total ~3.5 GPU-h.

==========================================================================================================================
8. CAVEATS
==========================================================================================================================
- Dev candidates are T = 1.0 rollouts (72.9 to 79.6 CIDEr each).
  - The frozen config was chosen on dev halves. The cross-fitted numbers are the honest dev estimates.
  - Test pools are T = 0.8 samples of models that trained on all train images, so they are estimates only.
- Panel object metrics use the 113-class lexicon. "Confirmed by the panel" does not mean "present"; COCO truth exists
  only for human candidates (3f).
- The rule raises KTVIC-reference CHAIR_i (+0.7 dev, +1.6 / +2.4 test) and CHAIR_s (+4.0 dev).
  - This is consistent with R5-01: ~55% of KTVIC-reference-false mentions are confirmed by the panel.
  - It is still a cost on a headline metric of the paper.
- fuse2 (2.1+ classes per caption already) gets recall at a panel-precision cost, with no evidence effect (3c). The
  method suits short, precise captioners.
- Vote evidence is model-generated. It is used only inside the decision rule; it is never a label or an evaluation
  signal.
- One-step RL proxies bound one update, not a full RL run. Real RL can drift beyond the sample support.
