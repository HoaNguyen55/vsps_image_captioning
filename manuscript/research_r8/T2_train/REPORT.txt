- Clean pools (rewards read KTVIC refs, CLIP, lexicon or training labels only):
  - dev300: P1_dev greedy (evaluate.py, CIDEr 144.33) + 8 G0 rollouts at T = 1.0. Panel = OpenViIC "train" key;
    296/300 images.
  - train300: P1_dev on 300 of its own SFT images (seen), NF4 greedy + 8 rollouts at T = 1.0. Confirmation only.
  - Test pools P1 s43 and fuse2 s42: evaluate.py greedy + 6 samples at T = 0.8, top_p 0.95 (B4's files). Panel =
    "test" key, 555 images. FINAL REPORT ONLY; nothing was selected on them.
- One-step RL proxy (B1/S0 style):
  - Per image, pick argmax_k U_k + d * 1[k = greedy], with d in mean-SD units of U (a KL stand-in).
  - U is a sum of within-image z-scored terms.
  - KTVIC is reported in-sample (U reads the same refs) and CROSS-FIT: choose with refs {0,1}, score on {3,4}, and
    swapped. Non-adjacent halves, because of the paraphrase chain.
- SFT-target stand-in (B2's kNN consensus, but with CLIP ViT-B/32 image retrieval so the panel stays independent):
  - A query retrieves its k nearest training images (cos >= 0.93 near-duplicates dropped).
  - Output = consensus caption of the neighbours' TARGETS (max mean CIDEr-D vs the rest of the pool).
- Panel metrics: panel CIDEr-D (DF = the pool's panel sets; also without the length penalty, because panel captions
  are 17.8 syllables vs KTVIC's 12.7), panel object P/R/F1 (second_panel.py definitions), panel CHAIR_i.
- Not counted as truth: precision-only gains bought with a recall loss. R0 already shows them on dev
  (P +1.9 [+0.7, +3.3], F1 -0.5); this is the CHAIR knob.
- Reproduced exactly:
  - S0 dev: greedy 144.3; KTVIC-oracle best-of-9 +44.7 [+38.5, +51.7] on KTVIC, +0.07 [-3.66, +4.11] on the panel.
  - B4/K2 test pool: member CIDEr 143.9 / 117.7 ... (K2 self-check).
- Panel headroom inside the dev pool (panel-oracle, NOT a method): +24.9 [+21.4, +29.0] panel CIDEr-D at
  -25.5 [-37.5, -14.1] KTVIC. The two panels' preferences among samples are nearly orthogonal.
- Pre-registration: PREREG.txt, 20:30 UTC 12/10, with sha256 of the code.
  - Disclosure: an exploratory UIT-ViIC run printed UIT-ViIC TEST numbers vs first-caption targets before the

4. LITERATURE (sub-agent, 64 scoop queries; every ID checked via arXiv API, ACL Anthology, Crossref, S2 or the CVPR
   page; 4 unverifiable items dropped)
======================================================================================================================
- Self-consensus reward: close to scooped.
  - C-GRPO 2602.03102 (EMNLP 2026): mean utility vs its own group as the GRPO reward.
  - MBR-BoN 2404.01054 (NAACL 2025): exact form R + beta * mean U(y, y'), used for best-of-N and DPO.
  - Also TTRL 2504.16084, Semantic Voting 2509.23067.
  - Collapse is documented: SRT 2505.21444; CoVerRL 2603.17775 ("consensus trap"; reward accuracy ~95% -> ~80%).
  - In captioning, CIDEr-RL already shrinks vocabulary (Wang & Chan CVPR 2019; Luo & Shakhnarovich 2002.11848).
- Consensus objects: incremental.
  - SPIDEr 1612.00370 (SPICE scores against the union of the references).
  - VIFIDEL (ACL 2019) weights objects by how many references name them, in evaluation only.
  - The only new element is consensus over NON-ADJACENT references; no reward models reference dependence.
- CLIP-S reward: scooped.
  - Cho 2205.13115: CLIP-S alone gives COCO CIDEr 11.2; CIDEr + CLIP-S gives 124.6.
  - DiCO 2408.14547, Self-Cap 2408.16827, VLRM 2404.01911: hacking and length growth.
  - Multilingual CLIP has not been used as an RL reward; that is the only new element.
- Visual-PMI CIDEr: incremental vs DisCIDEr 2207.11118, CIDErBtw 2007.06877, M3ID 2403.14003. Kornblith 2307.16686:
  specificity guidance costs CIDEr 126.1 -> 78.6.
- Discriminative rewards: scooped (CIDErBtw, Show-Tell-Discriminate 1803.08314, Dai & Lin 1710.02534).
- Object-presence / detector rewards: incremental (ECOL-R EACL 2021, ViGoR 2402.06118, VES-RFT CVPR 2026,
  FGAIF 2404.05046). No reward built directly from COCO ground-truth instance labels was found.
- Vietnamese: only SCST on VieCap4H; no large-VLM RL.

5. WHY NOTHING BEATS THE LEXICON TERM (reading of sections 2-3)
======================================================================================================================
- Every truth gain found is OBJECT-level. Panel CIDEr-D moves with the sampling regime (T = 0.8 vs 1.0), not with
  the objective: no term changed panel CIDEr-D vs R0 with a CI excluding 0 in the right direction on the pooled test.
- Among object terms, the plain lexicon F1 (precision against all-ref support, recall of all ref objects) beats
  every refinement tried:
  - chain-aware consensus: F1 -1.6 on test;
  - adding CLIP-S: +0.0;
  - adding self-consensus: panel CIDEr-D -1.9.
- The KTVIC refs already hold the panel-transferable object information. Extra sources either repeat it (consensus),
  add style (self-consensus, typicality) or add visual-model preference that the panel does not share (CLIP-S on
  train300 and test).

7. PROPOSALS (at most 2). NEITHER meets the bar; listed for the judge as conditional options.
======================================================================================================================
P-T2-1  Instance-label-consistent SFT targets (COCO-image datasets only; UIT-ViIC)
  Mechanism: for each training image, the SFT target is the human caption with the highest COCO-80 F1 against that
    image's COCO instance labels. No model text, no detector, no new labels.
  Novel element vs closest prior: ECOL-R (EACL 2021) and ViGoR use detector labels as an RL reward plus image
    filtering. Here, HUMAN instance labels choose WHICH human caption is imitated. Incremental.
  Pilot (stand-in, test 231 images):
    - vs the lexicon target: English-caption F1 +2.6 [+2.0, +3.3] (k = 10), +1.1 [+0.6, +1.8] (k = 30);
      instance F1 +3.8 / +3.7.
    - vs first-caption targets: instance F1 +12.0 / +13.4, English-caption F1 +6.6 / +7.3, CIDEr -7.9 [-16.6, +1.2]
      and -11.5 [-23.6, +0.5]. Fails the <= 3 cost clause on test; meets it on val at k = 10 (+1.0 [-4.5, +6.4]).
  GPU: 1 seed vs uitviic_p1assist ~3.6 GPU-h (2 SFTs + eval).
  Kill:
    - val UIT-ViIC CIDEr cost > 3 vs P1-assist;
    - val English-caption F1 gain over a consensual-target SFT with CI including 0;
    - CHAIR_i on instance labels not lower.
P-T2-2  (Operational, not novel) LEX-guarded CN-RLOO, with the rollout temperature settled first
  Mechanism: CN-RLOO reward + the K2 lexicon F1 term (z-mix, weight 1). Run the R6 dev T = 0.8 sample job first to test
    whether F4 is a T = 1.0 artefact: R0's panel transfer is +0.92 (dev, T = 1.0) vs +5.27 (test, T = 0.8).
  Novel element: none beyond K2 and R6's T = 0.8 fallback. The new part is the evidence (pooled test, pre-registered
    comparator).
  Pilot (pooled test, vs R0): panel F1 +2.9 [+2.2, +3.6], CHAIR_i -1.6 [-2.7, -0.6], cross-fit -2.1 [-4.4, +0.2].
  GPU: 0.4 (dev T = 0.8 samples) + 2 x 3.5 (G1 with and without LEX) = ~7.4 GPU-h.
  Kill:
    - dev (evaluate.py greedy) panel F1 of LEX-RLOO minus plain RLOO with CI including 0;
    - KTVIC dev CIDEr cost > 3;
    - panel CIDEr-D of the RL policy minus P1_dev with CI including 0 at the chosen temperature
      (truth claim dropped, typicality label kept).
  Explicitly NOT proposed:
    - self-consensus reward: scooped, and it lowers panel CIDEr-D on test;
    - CLIP-S rewards: not reproduced on train300 or test;
    - chain-aware consensus objects: lose to LEX on test;
    - visual-PMI or typicality-subtracted CIDEr;
    - discriminative rewards.

======================================================================================================================
8. CAVEATS
======================================================================================================================
- One-step selection is not RL. Real RL generalises across images and can amplify hacks. Effect sizes are proxy
  sizes, not VLM deltas.
- The kNN stand-in imitates neighbours' human captions. Absolute CIDEr is 74-120. It ranks target policies; it does
  not size them.
- Dev (T = 1.0, P1_dev) and test (T = 0.8, P1 s43 / fuse2 s42) differ in temperature, model and split at once.
  train300 uses seen images (memorised refs).
- About 80 dev looks; that is why the confirmation (train300 + pooled test, Holm) is binding and dev CIs are not.
- Cross-fit KTVIC uses 2 + 2 refs; its scale is not the 5-ref scale.
- The K2 term is the 113-entry lexicon. K2 reports that it sees 42% of noun mentions; the panel object metric uses the
  same lexicon, which favours LEX somewhat.
- The UIT-ViIC test vs-pos0 numbers were printed before PREREG (disclosure in section 1).
