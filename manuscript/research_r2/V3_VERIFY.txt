KILLED as stated:
  * "typicality, NOT truth", "+0.0 instance precision", "singletons 98.1% present", "tau_P = 0.14" as general facts.
    They are artefacts of UIT-ViIC's image SELECTION, not of human truthfulness:
    sports ball is present in 100.0% of UIT-ViIC images and person in 97.5%. These two classes are 83.9% of all
    COCO-80 mentions; a class drawn from the mention mix is instance-present 87.6% of the time BY CHANCE.
  * On unhinted, unselected general COCO scenes (Karpathy test 5,000, English captions) the same leakage-free
    target audit gives fuse2-p1 dP_ref -7.67 [-8.06,-7.30] but dP_inst -4.00 [-4.30,-3.68] (truth share tau = 0.54
    [0.52,0.58], an upper bound), and singletons are only 59.2% instance-present (n = 2,356).
SURVIVES (and is stronger than the candidate on the paper's own data):
  * UIT-ViIC, classes other than the two selection classes: dP_ref -7.38 [-9.25,-5.60] vs dP_inst +0.63 [-0.46,+1.73];
    singletons 93.3% present (chance 30.9%). Not a hint artefact: unhinted English COCO captions on the SAME images
    give dP_ref -6.08 [-7.88,-4.40] vs dP_inst -0.38 [-1.40,+0.62].
  * KTVIC itself, full 113-noun lexicon, independent second panel (UIT-OpenViIC), existing labels only:
    - targets (KTVIC train, 3,724 images): fuse2-p1 dP -8.88 [-9.42,-8.34] vs held-out KTVIC refs,
      -4.74 [-5.21,-4.21] vs OpenViIC, -4.47 [-4.86,-4.08] vs union. Half of the cost is reporting typicality.
    - models vs their own fused references (KTVIC test, same 555 images, same gold = 2 KTVIC refs + OpenViIC):
      fuse2-detailed - P1: -4.87 / -5.44 / -5.98 (P1 seeds 42/43/44); fused-pair - first-caption HUMAN targets:
      -3.61 [-4.57,-2.67]; model-specific residual (difference-in-differences) -1.26 [-3.44,+0.84] /
      -1.83 [-3.99,+0.37] / -2.36 [-4.45,-0.28]. Short mode residual -0.68 / -1.25 / -1.78 (all n.s.).
Defensible headline (replaces the slogan): "Most of the precision that fusion costs against a dataset's own
references is a property of the references, not of the model: an independent human panel halves it, and the
fused human references themselves lose about as much as the model trained on them (residual ~1-2 points)."

(a) COCO category hints. Verified in Lam et al. 2020 (arXiv 2002.00175) sec. 3.1: the tool showed "categories hints
    for each image, displaying as friendly icon" (the image's COCO categories) and the English captions on request;
    sec. 3.1 also lets writers browse "the others'" captions (5 writers for the whole dataset), so captions of an
    image are not independent. Effect estimate with the unhinted control (English COCO captions, AMT, same 2,687
    images; Chen et al. 2015 sec. 2 lists no category display):
       non-selection classes   singletons present   fuse2 - p1 dP_ref      dP_inst
       Vietnamese (hinted)     93.3% (n=356)        -7.38 [-9.25,-5.60]    +0.63 [-0.46,+1.73]
       English (unhinted)      87.3% (n=741)        -6.08 [-7.88,-4.40]    -0.38 [-1.40,+0.62]
    Hints may add a few points of singleton presence (English also carries more matcher artefacts) but do not
    create the null. The real artefact is image selection (P1), not hints.

(c) Leakage / overlap (verified by id): UIT-ViIC train = Karpathy train 1,545 / restval 885 / val 132 / test 133;

(d) Target -> model. Target-level identity does not imply model-level identity (models add unsupported
    scene-default mentions: 0.45-0.57 vs 0.26 per caption). The DiD above is the right bridge: on KTVIC the model
    inherits its references' precision profile with a 1-2 point residual. For 59a/59b the registered G3 cannot fail
    (P3). Amended, pre-registered criterion (commit BEFORE scoring; sec. 4).

(e) Novelty (full texts read: Chen et al. 2015 1504.00325 sec. 4.2; Rohrbach et al. 2018 1809.02156 sec. 2.1;
    Feng, Tang, Zhang, Xu 2024 2406.12663; Neuhaus & Hein 2025 RePOPE 2504.15707; Tang et al. 2026 CCCaption
    2602.21655; Petryk et al. 2024 ALOHa 2404.02904; Lam et al. 2020 UIT-ViIC 2002.00175; abstract: Misra et al.
    2016 1512.06974; screened: Cheng et al. 2024 2411.05079, HalDec-Bench 2511.20515 -- no overlap).
    - Chen 2015 sec. 4.2 ASSUMES P(word | object absent) = 0 and derives human precision vs k references
      = pk/(pk+(1-p)^k): reference precision of human text depends only on mention propensity p and rises with k.
      This IS "reference precision measures typicality" as a modelling assumption (plus PHR).
    - CHAIR sec. 2.1: reference-only and segmentation-only golds both inflate hallucination ("frequently humans
      will not mention all objects"; "table" vs "dining table").
    - Misra 2016: "what's in the image" vs "what's worth saying" from human-centric labels.
    - Feng et al. 2024: >55% of CHAIR-flagged hallucinations (LLaVA-1.5, 500 COCO val) unjustified: rigid
      extraction + detail-poor human ground truth.
    - RePOPE: COCO-derived labels 9.3% wrong (+13.8% ambiguous) for "yes", 1.7% (+4.3%) for "no"; rankings shift.
    - CCCaption: asserts human references are incomplete/incorrect (motivation; no measurement).
    Closest prior: Chen 2015 (concept, untested) + CHAIR 2018 (two-gold for model outputs).
    Remaining delta (incremental, analysis-level): (i) measured with human-only evidence at the level of TRAINING
    TARGETS and annotator panels, leakage-free; (ii) the truth share of fusion's precision cost is domain-dependent
    (~0.1 sports scenes, <=0.54 general COCO) -- Chen's assumption holds for salient-object scenes, not generally;
    (iii) on Vietnamese KTVIC an independent-team panel + DiD shows the model inherits its references' precision
    profile. No prior found doing (i)-(iii); Vietnamese is first by default.

(4) amend G3 before 59a/59b. Would a Q1 reviewer accept it? As an analysis section supporting the fusion recipe:
yes. As the paper's principal contribution: no (incremental over Chen 2015 / CHAIR).
Remaining weaknesses to state: the second panel is another human panel, not truth (two panels cover ~84% of
mentionable classes); one fuse2 seed (s43/s44 queued -> two-level seed x image bootstrap); KTVIC's 5 captions may
come from one writer (R4-00), so "held-out annotator" = held-out caption.
