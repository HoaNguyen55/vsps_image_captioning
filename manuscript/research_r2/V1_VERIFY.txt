1.1 tilt.py self-checks pass. KTVIC coarse point estimates reproduce A4 exactly: P1-gold-short 80.2 -> 90.0,

1.2 Bug: wrong denominator (A4 ktvic.py and gof.py). mention_arrays drops every mention whose class no KTVIC test
    reference names ("if c not in cls_ix: continue"), from both numerator and denominator (check_drop.py).
    - P1 and fuse2: <= 0.6% of mentions, <= 0.4 P. Negligible.
    - zero-shot-detailed: 7.4-7.6% of mentions; P@K5 reads 34.2 instead of 31.6 (exact).
    - vsps-detailed: 5.8-6.3% of mentions; reads 45.7 instead of 42.9.
    Fixed in ifcr_panel2.py: those mentions are kept and get no hidden mass.

1.3 Bootstrap. The corrected estimator is biased downward in resamples (mean(boot) - point = -0.4 to -1.7; raw has
    no bias). The cause is the per-class cap hidden <= f0 plus the nonlinear fit.
    - Exact level: HUM KT 1, HUM KT 2 and HUM KT 1+2 have their point estimate OUTSIDE their own 95% percentile CI.
    - Use basic or BCa intervals (boot_audit.py).

1.4 Prop. 1's identification condition fails. 1-(1-p)^2 = 2p - p^2, so the union-2 weight is a linear combination
    of the single and consensus-2 weights. The computed components are exactly collinear within every class
    (sigma3/sigma1 = 1.3e-16; centred truncated profiles have rank 1; collinear.py).
    - The family is w(p) = a*p + b*p^2: ONE shape parameter per class, tied across classes only through
      class-specific normalisations.
    - lambda is not identified within a class. Readings such as "fuse2-short = consensus" or "fuse2-detailed =
      union" are unsupported. lambda jumps between runs: P1-short (0.13, 0.29, 0.58) vs (0.14, 0.00, 0.86).
    - The corrected precision itself is identified, under the model.

1.5 The KTVIC headline "fuse2-short - P1-short: -2.7 -> -1.6, no longer significant" does not hold (A4's npz,
    paired):
    a) It uses an estimate A4's own fit test rejects (fuse2-short pooled p = .005). By A4's rule that value is only
       a lower bracket.
    b) It is seed-fragile. With P1 seed 43 the corrected difference is -2.4 [-4.7, +0.1] (coarse; basic CI
       [-4.8, +0.0]). At exact level it is -2.7, basic CI [-5.7, -0.2], which is significant.
    c) The shift the correction makes is real: +1.08 [+0.46, +2.14] (coarse) and +0.69 [-0.04, +1.82] (exact).
       "Becomes n.s." mostly reflects added variance, on a raw gap that was barely significant to begin with.

    - The basis, the shrinkage, the "tilt" combination and the "flag if either p < .05" rule were chosen on these

=====================================================================================================================
2. ATTACKS (a)-(d)
=====================================================================================================================
(a) Dependence between captures. KTVIC fails here; COCO does not.

  Measurement: caption-index Jaccard of object sets (exch.py, band.py).
  - KTVIC has a lag-1 band:
    - test: adjacent 0.525 vs non-adjacent 0.404, difference +0.121 [+0.108, +0.134];
    - train: +0.090 [+0.084, +0.096];
    - lags 2-4 are flat at about 0.41.
  - COCO is flat (0.61-0.62). UIT-ViIC is nearly flat (0.43-0.47).
  - P1 is confirmed by caption index 0 (its SFT target) +0.087 [+0.049, +0.121] more than by the other indices.
    zero-shot shows +0.053, so part of P1's affinity is system-specific.
  - The captions are not exchangeable, and adjacent captions are positively dependent. Both break the
    hypergeometric/U-statistic components.

  KTVIC plasmode (plasmode.py).
  - Built from KTVIC's real (image, class) pairs, with salience drawn from the posterior given each pair's observed
    t (zero-truncated beta-binomial per class).
  - Synthetic references copy the previous caption with rho = 0.45. This reproduces the band ratio (1.28 vs 1.30)
    and, with eps = .05 extra naming on index 4, the per-index confirmation profile.
  - IFCR error vs truth (40 replicates, se 0.1-0.2):

      references                       single    union-2   single + affinity to ref 0
      independent                       -0.9      -1.8       0.0
      KTVIC band (rho = 0.45)           -9.2     -12.7      -7.5
      band + idiosyncratic index 4      -7.5     -10.4      -5.9

  - Averaging over all 10 K-subsets instead of the 5 cyclic ones improves this by only about 1.2.
  - Positive dependence makes held-out references look as if they confirm each other, so IFCR under-corrects.
    This is the classical list-dependence bias of capture-recapture and the conditional-dependence bias of
    latent-class models (Vacek 1985; Torrance-Rynard & Walter 1997; Albert & Dodd 2004).

(b) Leakage, and the "label-free" claim. See 1.6.
  - Ucal, tilt and ipw0 are NOT label-free; they use COCO / UIT-ViIC train instance labels. Only Ufit and U1 are.
  - The paper must quote Ufit's validation for KTVIC: 3.4 MAE on COCO VLMs, 5.8 on full captions.

(c) Identification and fit-test power (sim.py; COCO-calibrated world; truth known; A4's estimator and gof
    unchanged; 100 sims).
  - Size: systems that are in-family and independent of the references are flagged far above any nominal rate.

      system         flagged, n = 558 (100 reps)   flagged, n = 5000 (25 reps)
      single              44%                           44%
      union-2             54%                           80%
      consensus-2         23%                           56%

    The test treats components estimated from the same references as fixed. A4's humans passed only because their
    own captions are inside the components.
  - Power against salience-blind true mentions (Prop. 2's non-identified mass). A system that also names each
    present object with probability q:

      q       IFCR error n = 558   flagged     IFCR error n = 5000   flagged
      0.05         -3.7              48%            -3.3               64%
      0.10         -6.7              54%            -6.0               76%
      0.20        -11.7              69%           -11.0              100%

    At KTVIC scale the flag rate equals the in-family false-flag rate: no discrimination.
  - Union-3 (fusion of 3 captions) is outside the degree-2 family: -3.4 / -2.4.

(d) Naming. The corrected value depends on lexicon granularity (555 images, K = 3):
  - P1-short: 84.0 (exact) vs 89.6 (coarse); fuse2-detailed: 74.6 vs 79.4.
  - Under-correction against the second panel is larger at exact level (P1 -4.0 vs -2.9).
  - Naming variants act as capture-recapture misidentification ("tag loss"), which CR estimators assume away
    (Link et al., Biometrics 2010; from memory, not re-fetched).
  - "Corrected precision" is therefore defined only after a fixed naming merge, and that merge becomes part of the
    metric.

  precision.
- P2cal = raw + f0 * (OpenViIC-confirmed share / OpenViIC sensitivity). Sensitivity is measured on held-out KTVIC
  annotators' unreferenced mentions: 69.6% coarse, 63.2% exact.

  P1-gold-short         79.9   89.6   92.5    98.0    -2.9 [-5.5, -1.9]     ok

No paper applies "system as an extra capture occasion + imitator-family selection" to caption precision.
Closest priors:
- Latent-class / no-gold-standard test evaluation: Dawid & Skene 1979; Hui & Walter 1980; Vacek, Biometrics 1985
  (10.2307/2530967); Torrance-Rynard & Walter, Stat Med 1997; Albert & Dodd, Biometrics 2004
  (10.1111/j.0006-341x.2004.00187.x): dependence biases the estimates, and goodness-of-fit cannot detect it.
  2024 BMC MRM 10.1186/s12874-024-02432-x: detecting conditional-independence departures.
  IFCR is a one-sided (perfect-specificity references) special case. Its failure mode is exactly this
  literature's warning.
- Crowd species estimation: Trushkowsky et al. ICDE 2013 / CACM 2015 (10.1145/2845644); Chung et al. SIGMOD 2016
  (1507.05591); Chung, Krishnan, Kraska, VLDB 2017 (DQM, 1611.04878); crowdtesting (1805.03218); Luggen et al.
  ISWC 2019 (KG class completeness).
- PU performance recovery: Jain, White & Radivojac AAAI 2017 (class prior plus SCAR); Claesen 2015; PSP
  (Jain 2016); Schultheis KDD 2022.
- Capture-recapture for literature search: Kastner et al., J Clin Epidemiol 2009 (from memory, not re-fetched).
- VCap 2605.28023 is not a scoop: "hypergeometric" there describes an RL reward with a VLM adjudicator.
Delta: replicate-annotator U-statistic components and a system-specific selection family. This is real but small,
and its key assumption is refuted on the paper's main dataset.

=====================================================================================================================
5. REVIEWER
=====================================================================================================================
Strongest objection: the correction assumes five exchangeable, conditionally independent KTVIC captions.
- The data refute this (lag-1 band).
- An independent human panel shows IFCR under-corrects every model by 3-8 points (short / detailed) and up to 18
  (zero-shot).
- The fit test cannot see the failure.
- Where instance labels exist, direct measurement makes the estimator unnecessary.
Fix: drop IFCR as a contribution and use the two-panel evidence (existing human captions only).
