  - Constant captions (no image): "người" -> NAP precision 78.7% (exact 43.7, P_h 65.6), above zero-shot's 66.3 and

1.5 ATTACK (c): phi circularity and scene dependence  (phi_f30ke.out, code/phi_f30ke.py)
  - Circularity broken with EXISTING referent-level human labels: Flickr30k Entities (Plummer et al. 2015) links the NPs
    of an image's 5 captions to entity ids. Over 31,783 images, 1,119 head lemmas, 17,542 pairs: phi predicts "the two
    names usually denote the same entity" with AUROC 0.961 (coref>=0.5; 0.924 at >=0.3, 0.981 at >=0.7). Precision:
    phi>=0.5 -> 44% such pairs; phi>=0.9 -> 78%. On COCO, excluding CHAIR's person class (which merges man/woman/
    boy/girl into one 'synonym' class) raises A7's AUROC from 0.872 [0.846,0.899] to 0.938. So phi is validated as a
    referent-identity statistic; CHAIR's list understated it.
  - Scene dependence is real and is exactly what referent identity predicts (Flickr, phi / coref rate):
        man~person   1 people entity 0.99 / 1.00    >=3 people entities 0.46 / 0.54
        person~woman                 1.00 / 1.00                        0.40 / 0.42
        boy~child                    1.00 / 1.00                        0.77 / 0.53
    => người~đàn ông 0.80 (KTVIC) vs 0.28 (UIT-ViIC, players + crowds) is phi measuring how often the names co-refer,
    not noise. Implication: phi belongs in RECALL clustering (are these one referent?) and in admitting near-synonyms;
    it must NOT gate PRECISION credit for hypernyms (existence is entailed regardless of crowd size). A7 conflates the two.
  - Caveat: phi cannot tell a naming variant from an attribute disagreement. In Flickr images with ONE people entity,
    man~woman has phi 0.735 (annotators disagree on the gender of the one person). So phi must never license
    person-sibling credit; A7 correctly leaves sib_person uncredited.
  - Pending A7 kill criterion (UIT-ViIC naming-level mentions >= 95% instance-present): UIT-ViIC P1/fuse2 predictions do
    not exist yet (checked local, n2, n3). For person hypernyms the test is near-tautological (a COCO person exists
    whenever the other annotators name one); Flickr30k Entities is the informative label check and passes.

1.6 ATTACK (d): does NAP change any substantive conclusion?  (19 precision rules x 7 recall definitions)
  - Rankings: unchanged under every rule (P1 >= tags_fuse2 >= fuse2 >> zero-shot on P; fuse2 > P1 on R).
  - Fusion's recall gain: robust everywhere (short +5.5..+6.8, detailed +17.7..+18.8; all CIs exclude 0).
  - Fusion's precision cost: detailed robust (-6.9..-11.2, all significant); short significant only under hierarchy
    rules (P_h -2.9 [-5.5,-0.1], NAP -3.5 [-5.9,-0.9]), n.s. under exact or phi-only rules (-1.1..-2.7).
  - tags_fuse2 vs P1 precision: n.s. under every rule (-1.5..+1.0).
  - SFT vs zero-shot precision gap: sign robust (>= 13 points short, >= 36 detailed); magnitude rule-dependent.
  - Human parity: P1-short precision below the held-out human under every rule (-3.5..-10.6, all significant); fuse2
    recall = human (see 1.2 iii).
  - Shape of the shift: near-constant across fine-tuned systems (NAP - exact: +5.1..+6.5; differences between
    fine-tuned systems move <= 1.4), but NOT constant for zero-shot (+14.0 short) or humans (+2.8). So NAP is a
    near-constant offset for the paper's main P1-vs-fuse2 comparison and a real correction for zero-shot / human gaps.

  Component-by-component (closest prior -> exact delta):
  (1) Vietnamese extractor (tone, compounds, classifiers). Prior: CHAIR's released chair.py already hard-codes
      compound/qualifier rules (hot dog, teddy bear, baby bird -> bird). No Vietnamese CHAIR-type metric exists
      (KTVIC, UIT-ViIC, UIT-OpenViIC, ViTextCaps: none; ViHallu is text-only). Delta: a port plus an artefact audit.
      This is engineering, not a method; and V2 shows a list-free VnCoreNLP word-boundary matcher does most of it.
  (2) Precision crediting more general names: SCOOPED. HalCECE (Lymperaiou et al., 2025, arXiv 2503.00436, Sec. 4):
      WordNet-LCA edits; "underspecialized objects ... do not constitute hallucinations", over-specialisation does.
      VALOR-EVAL (Qiu et al., Findings ACL 2024, arXiv 2404.13874, Sec. 4): D_M (same meaning) and D_B ("broader
      conceptual matches", clothes for dress) both count for faithfulness. Hierarchical P/R (Kiritchenko et al. 2006;
      Taxonomy-Aware Evaluation of VLMs, CVPR 2025, arXiv 2504.05457). The repo's own CHAIR-vi gold expansion
      already does it.
  (3) phi: the only genuinely new element. Closest: Hodosh et al. (CoNLL 2010, W10-2920) cross-caption coreference over
      an image's captions, which "assume[s] that two words cannot be coreferent within a sentence" and groups NPs via
      WordNet (MUC F1 85.2); Young et al. TACL 2014 / Lai & Hockenmaier EACL 2017 denotational probabilities (no
      within-caption term); Berger & Ponti NAACL 2025 (arXiv 2409.16646): per-image name shares k_a/n from XM3600
      (Vietnamese included) via MT into WordNet, descriptive, no pairwise statistic; Cattuto et al. ISWC 2008
      (tags related when they share contexts but do not co-occur). Delta: an image-stratified observed/expected
      co-mention ratio, label-free, now validated against REFERENT gold (Flickr30k Entities AUROC 0.961, V2).
  (4) Recall per referent cluster: mechanism SCOOPED. The cluster rule (linked names never used together in one
      caption) is Hodosh 2010's within-sentence constraint plus WordNet grouping; "broader names earn precision, not
      recall" is VALOR-EVAL's Coverage = |D_M| / |F_G| and hierarchical hR. Delta: clusters induced from the 5
      references with phi instead of WordNet. Modest.
  (5) Naming agreement vs a held-out human: ManyNames v2 (Silberer, Zarriess, Westera, Boleda, COLING 2020) scores
      top name / adequate alternative / error (Bottom-Up 73.4 / 87.9); Ordonez et al. ICCV 2013 1-vs-2 human ceiling;
      Testoni et al. ACL 2024. Delta: modal name from free captions, scored per mention, 1-vs-4 ceiling. Small.
  No 2025-26 "naming-aware CHAIR" and no Vietnamese caption-hallucination metric found. Novelty claimable: (i) phi as
  a label-free referent-identity statistic validated on coreference gold; (ii) the measured Vietnamese decomposition
  and its effect on a held-out-human calibration. Must cite HalCECE, VALOR-EVAL, hierarchical P/R (CVPR 2025),
  Hodosh 2010, Young 2014, ManyNames v1/v2, Berger & Ponti 2025, and Chen et al. 2015 for the 1-vs-k human protocol.

3. REVIEWER (Q1: Pattern Recognition / Neurocomputing / TMM)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Accept as the paper's MAIN contribution? No. It changes no ranking, and on the paper's central P1-vs-fuse2
  comparison it is a near-constant offset. As a SECONDARY contribution it fits the story (referent-level evaluation
  from multiple partial references, calibrated against a held-out annotator), provided it is a priori, validated
  on referent gold, and reported as a band.

  STRONGEST OBJECTION: "Your metric's effects come from resources you hand-built while looking at the test
  references and the very outputs you score (90 compounds, 16 synonym sets, 7 near pairs, a parent map). Make them
  list-free and three of your claims move: fuse2's short-mode precision cost loses significance, fuse2's super-human
  recall disappears, and 'naming explains ~30% of SFT's hallucination reduction' spans 3-48%. And it changes no
  ranking -- it is a tuned re-scoring."
  MINIMAL FIX (existing data only; most of it already computed here):
   1. Freeze resources a priori and say where they come from: shipped syllable matcher plus an exception table of
      exocentric compounds attested in TRAIN references (A7's 66; identical numbers), with VnCoreNLP whole-word
      matching (the tool Sec. IV-C already names) as the list-free bound; the person tree completed in HYPERNYMS
      (8 entries, linguistically uncontroversial); synonyms from the shipped svp.matching.canonical; no root
      superordinates (đồ, vật, con vật, +quần áo, +giày dép, +tàu thuyền).
   2. Use phi only where it is the right construct: referent clusters for recall and admission of near-synonyms
      at tau = 0.9, justified by Flickr30k Entities coreference (AUROC 0.961; 78% co-referent at phi >= 0.9).
      Never use it to gate hypernym precision credit.
   3. Add an appendix sensitivity band: each claim under exact / CHAIR-vi / NAP / list-free, and claim only what
      holds across it (rankings, fusion's recall gain, fusion's detailed-mode precision cost, P1 below human
      precision, fuse2 = human recall).
   4. Add the vagueness stress test (hypernymised captions, constant captions) and the reporting rule that
      NAP-P always appears with R_ref and the 'above' share.
  SECOND OBJECTION: "CHAIR-vi in your FAIR paper had segmentation bugs and the text claims word segmentation."
  Disclose it, re-score, and show the detailed-mode conclusions survive (1.7). This is a credibility gain if
  pre-empted and a desk-reject risk if a reviewer finds it.
