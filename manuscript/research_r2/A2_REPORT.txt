0. ANSWER IN BRIEF
- The premise "most reference-false mentions are co-occurrence-prior objects that a prior-suppression method can remove"
  does not survive the pilot. On KTVIC and on COCO (human instance labels) a stronger co-occurrence prior marks a
  mention as MORE often correct, also within object class and beyond the first mention; no prior or annotator
  statistic separates truly-absent from present-but-unnamed mentions (AUROC 0.33-0.61, mostly ~0.5). Bound: dropping
  high-prior mentions buys dP <= 0.04 at any recall budget <= 2 points on all 5 KTVIC and all 4 COCO system/mode files;
  the best text-only filter (dropping LOW-prior mentions, i.e. the opposite) buys <= +0.6 (KTVIC short),
  <= +1.5 (KTVIC detailed), <= +2.1 (COCO) at dR >= -2 (thresholds tuned on test: optimistic).
- Where the ~10-point precision gap to one human annotator comes from (1v4 protocol, same held-out annotators):
  P1 excess 0.188 reference-false mentions/caption = naming 50% (person nouns: gender/age live in the Vietnamese
  noun) + background/part nouns 27% (áo, đường, cửa, cây, nhà ...) + countable things 24%. fuse2: 41% / 38% / 21%.
  Second-sentence and late-position errors are human-like: a second human caption also adds nothing the other
  annotators named in 58.4% [55.9, 60.9] of cases (fuse2 second sentence: 59.7% [55.9, 63.4]).
- COCO (zero-shot / old-VSPS Vietnamese captions, 5,000 images): 49.8-60.6% of reference-false mentions are
  instance-absent; 41.5-55.8% after excluding three classes whose Vietnamese terms are generic or polysemous
  (bàn = any table or bàn tay "hand"; ghế = any seat; ván trượt = any board).
- The separable part is NAMING: it is identifiable from the 5 references at training time, it is systematic
  (errors are as frequent where annotators agree as where they disagree), and fixing it to human level would lift
  1v4 precision 75.2 -> 79.4-80.4 (P1), 72.9 -> 77.6-79.0 (fuse2) at equal or higher recall.
  Method 1 (CNC) targets it. Method 2 (certified minimal pairs) is a low-confidence attempt at the existence part,
  where no existing signal separates and separability would have to be created by training.

2.2 Analysis findings on the premise
- For: LURE (hallucinated objects co-occur more, are less certain and later); PAI (39-72% of hallucinations reproduced
  without the image); M3ID (image dependence decays; ~27% of hallucinations in the last decile).
- Against using the prior to separate: 2605.21300 Fig. 2a (above); 2406.12663 (>55% of CHAIR-flagged hallucinations
  unjustified on manual check; later positions describe smaller objects); HDPO App. Tab. 6; M3ID over-compensation.
- Position: per-sentence hallucination rises from ~2-5% (sentences 1-2) to ~14-30% (5-6) in long descriptions (CGD,
  SENTINEL, SumGD); weak evidence for one- or two-sentence captions.
2.3 Gaps verified (agents' reading + my 12 targeted searches, Section 7)
- No paper uses multiple human references to decide which objects or names are negatives.
- No negative loss restricted to object / naming tokens (HALVA's phrase spans closest).
- No training method checks that its signal separates truly-absent from present-but-unmentioned objects with human
  presence labels; all report CHAIR on a caption+segmentation union where both count as errors.

4. METHODS (at most two)
M1. Consensus-Naming Contrast (CNC) - targets the naming share (41-50% of the gap to a human)
  Mechanism. During LoRA SFT on the usual target (P1 or fuse2 rows), at every token position where the target starts
  a person noun (and, as an extension, nouns of the other naming families: vehicles xe *, stall quầy/sạp hàng,
  synonyms in canonical()), add two terms computed from the logits the SFT forward already produces:
  (a) soft target: CE against the distribution of the first person noun across the image's 5 references (weight =
      share of annotators who named a person);
  (b) token-level unlikelihood -log(1 - p(n)) on person nouns of the same family that NO annotator used, only when
      >= 3 annotators agree on one noun (1,654 / 3,769 train images), at the first distinguishing token (trie over
      the lexicon). Vaguer names (hypernyms of a used noun, credited by P_h) are never penalised; images where
      annotators disagree get (a) only. Total: L = L_SFT + lambda_a L_a + lambda_b L_b.
  Why it targets the separable part: naming FPs are identifiable from the references at training time; they are
  systematic, not ambiguity (person-noun error rate 21.4% [15.9, 27.5] where >= 80% of annotators agree vs 14.9%
  [9.1, 21.5] where they disagree, P1); and the targets are not the cause (fuse2's consensus caption uses a person
  noun no other annotator used in only 2.1% of person images, P1's first caption 9.3%, yet both models err alike).
  Closest prior: Equalizer (Burns/Hendricks et al., ECCV 2018) - confusion/confident losses on English gender words
  using person masks. Bhargava & Forsyth 2019 - decoupled gender classifier. HALVA - phrase contrast against
  LLM-made alternatives. Delta: the per-image agreement of 5 human references decides when a gendered or aged noun
  is supported (no masks, classifiers or LLMs); covers gender, age and neutral naming where Vietnamese encodes them in
  the noun; token-level CE+UL inside generative LVLM SFT; no extra forward pass.
  Pilot: Sections 3.1, 3.2, 3.8 (bound +4.2 to +6.1 P in 1v4 at equal or higher R; 1,654 consensus train images).
  Cost: no extra forward/backward; fuse2-equivalent SFT ~3.6 GPU-h + KTVIC eval ~0.5 -> ~4 GPU-h per seed, 2 seeds 8.
  Kill criterion (pre-register; paired bootstrap B=5000 vs the same-seed baseline on KTVIC test): person-noun FP rate
  must fall >= 25% relative, exact P >= +1.5 with dR >= -0.5, P_h not lower, 1v4 naming excess down >= 1/3, and
  chair.gender_invention rate down; otherwise kill. Confidence: 55% that it passes.
M2. Certified minimal-pair object contrast (CMPC) - the only label-free lever left for existence errors
  Mechanism. Mine natural image pairs from the training set using the 5 references: object c named by >= 2
  annotators of image i (certified present), named by none of image j's annotators and among the top-k prior
  completions of j's named objects (certified absent and tempting), i and j sharing their most-named object (same
  context). Take i's reference prefix up to c's slot and add a token-level hinge on c's first distinguishing token:
  max(0, m + log p(c | I_j, prefix) - log p(c | I_i, prefix)). The text prefix is identical, so the language prior
  cancels and only visual evidence is trained; restricted to countable-thing classes (silence says little for
  background nouns such as áo, đường, cửa, cây).
  Why: Sections 3.6-3.7 show no existing signal (priors, annotator counts, the model's PMI) separates absent from
  present-but-unnamed mentions, so separability must be created by discriminative training on reliable pairs.
  Pilot: COCO purity (instance-verified, 17,629 test-image pairs, 73 classes): negative side absent 90.7% [90.2, 91.2],
  positive side present 80.0% [72.7, 86.5] (any class named by >= 2 annotators: 92.9%; by exactly 1: 59.2%).
  KTVIC train: 7,088 thing-class pairs over 26 classes (k=10); 94-96% of the models' thing-class unrelated FPs fall
  in classes with >= 30 pairs. Bound: small on KTVIC (thing excess 0.045-0.066 FP/caption; if ~half absent and all
  removed, +1.0 to +1.2 P in 1v4); the larger payoff, if any, is on COCO transfer / POPE-vi with instance gold.
  Closest prior: mDPO (same response, cropped image; random images worse), S-VCO (edited minimal pairs, sequence
  level), V-DPO (inpainted pairs), Dai & Lin NeurIPS 2017 / Luo et al. CVPR 2018 (other images as sequence negatives).
  Delta: natural pairs certified by human-reference agreement (no image editing), loss only on the differing object
  token with an identical prefix (exact prior cancellation), token-level inside SFT.
  Cost: one extra image forward per pair row on ~half the rows -> ~5.5 GPU-h per seed + KTVIC/COCO-2500 eval ~1.5
  -> ~7 GPU-h (one seed first).
  Kill criterion: on COCO-2500 transfer (instance gold, clean classes) CHAIR_i -2 points with R_inst within -1, KTVIC
  exact P >= +0.5 at dR >= -0.5, POPE-vi adversarial F1 >= +1; otherwise kill. Confidence: 20%.

5. KILLED IN THIS ROUND (by my own pilot)
- Prior-suppression training (unlikelihood on co-occurrence completions, PoE with a text-only expert, image-
  dependence reweighting): prior marks correct mentions; bound dP <= 0.04 at dR >= -2 (KTVIC and COCO); 2605.21300
  already shows the small CHAIR gains of the reweighting variant.
- Annotator-occupancy ("silence-is-absence") negatives: purity 49-74%, sign flips across systems.
- PMI-gated on-policy negatives: no separation in the partial PMI pilot (0.51 [0.36, 0.65]).
- Snowballing / stop-calibration methods: second-sentence errors are human-like; a corroboration stop target would
  cut continuation from 71.7% to 22.9%.
