2.6 How much true headroom is left? (v5_headroom.py, v5_headroom_cls.py)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Estimator: hallucinated mentions = rf - confirmed / s, where s is the panel's sensitivity to real but unmentioned
  objects.
- s is estimated from the held-out human's reference-false mentions: 59.4% [55.5, 63.0] globally. It is then
  class-adjusted to each system's own class mix: 61.5-64.8%, because system extras sit in classes the panel names more
  often.

Corrected P (class-adjusted):

|---|---|---|

| fuse2-short | 97.6 [95.0, 100] | — |

| fuse2-detailed | 95.5 [93.2, 98.2] | — |
| zero-shot short | 89.8 [86.0, 93.6] | — |
| FT10 MBR (short) | 99.1 [96.8, 100] | — |

Paired deltas:

| contrast | corrected dP |
|---|---|
| fuse2-short - P1-short | -0.7 [-3.0, +1.5] (fusion buys R +5.7 at no detectable true-P cost) |

The global (unadjusted) version is more optimistic: fuse2-short 98.9, fuse2-detailed 98.2.

Caveat (the strongest objection to (a)):
- The estimator assumes equal within-class detectability of system extras and human extras.

- Existing-data fix: validate the estimator where presence is known.

    two-panel corrected P of the queued transfer outputs (jobs 36/38/59).
