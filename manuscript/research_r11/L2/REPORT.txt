2. CLOSEST PRIOR WORK (ids checked on arXiv abs pages 2026-10-14 unless marked; G-A4 = also in Gemini's A4 check)
P1 Chen et al., "Microsoft COCO Captions: Data Collection and Evaluation Server", arXiv:1504.00325, §4.2.
   Human word precision against k refs from a mention propensity p: H_p = pk/(pk+(1-p)^k), H_r = p. Going from 4 refs
   to 1 lowers precision at the same recall; precision -> 1 as subjects -> infinity. Assumes subjects are independent
   given the object; COCO captions are "five independent" AMT captions. This is the human-side ancestor of b_pool and
   of E2's propensity. The R10 G2/G3/E2/E5 reports do not cite it (R2_03/R5 already list it as must-cite).
P2 Rohrbach et al., CHAIR, arXiv:1809.02156 (EMNLP 2018). Gold = COCO instance labels plus reference objects, to cut
   false hallucinations. It has no completeness index.
P3 Neuhaus & Hein, RePOPE, arXiv:2504.15707 (2025). They re-annotate all 500 POPE images. Errors concentrate in "Yes"
   (COCO-positive) questions, ambiguous pairs are dropped, and model rankings shift. This is label noise in a
   discriminative gold, not completeness of a free-form gold.
P4 Open-vocabulary CHAIR: OpenCHAIR/MOCHa (Ben-Kish et al., arXiv:2312.03631, gold from generative foundation models);
   ALOHa (Petryk et al., arXiv:2404.02904, NAACL 2024). AMBER (arXiv:2311.07397) is LLM-free with its own annotations
   (manual-annotation details from memory, VERIFY). All score against a fixed gold; none estimates what it misses.
P5 Dense golds: DOCCI (2404.19753, 15k images, ~136-word single descriptions); IIW (2405.02793, human-in-the-loop);
   DCI (2312.08578, mask-aligned). More complete, but single-writer, and none estimates residual incompleteness.
P6 SC-Captioner (2508.06125, ICCV 2025) refines CAPTURE "to alleviate its incomplete precision evaluation". An ad hoc
   fix; no estimator or law. Yan et al. (2406.17115) measure benchmark reliability and validity, not completeness.
P7 ReaL-style relabelling: ReaL (2006.07159); Vasudevan et al. (2205.04596: "nearly half of the supposed mistakes are not
   mistakes"); Northcutt et al. (2103.14749, NeurIPS 2021 D&B). For captions: ECCV Caption (2204.03359, ECCV 2022;
   x3.6 image-to-caption positives, rankings change) and CxC (2004.15020, EACL 2021). Retrieval or classification only.
P8 Resnick, Kong, Schoenebeck, Weninger, arXiv:2106.01254 v3 (2026-04-22), "Principled Evaluation with Human Labels:
   One Rater at a Time and Rater Equivalence" (G-A4). Defines the power curve over sampled panels and rater equivalence.
   App. G: the empirical curve reaches only panel sizes up to n-1, and parametric (Beta-mixture) synthetic extrapolation
   "works poorly in practice". It has no set-valued outputs and no Good-Turing/Chao.
P9 Trushkowsky, Kraska, Franklin, Sarkar, "Crowdsourced Enumeration Queries", ICDE 2013 best paper (arXiv 1202.2335,
   "Getting It All from the Crowd", per web search, VERIFY). Species estimators gauge how complete a crowd-enumerated set
   is; worker heterogeneity ("streakers") breaks them. This is the closest precedent for Chao over annotator lists.
P10 Incomplete judgments in IR: Buckley & Voorhees, SIGIR 2004, 25-32, doi 10.1145/1008992.1009000 (bpref); Keenan,
   Smeaton & Keogh, JASIST 52(7):570-574, 2001, doi 10.1002/asi.1096 (pool depth; G-A4).
P11 Missing-label-corrected metrics: Jain, White & Radivojac, arXiv:1702.00518 (AAAI 2017, PU-corrected P/R); Jain,
   Prabhu & Varma, KDD 2016, doi 10.1145/2939672.2939756 (propensity-scored P@k); Schultheis et al., arXiv:2207.13186
   (KDD 2022) criticise propensities. Ding, Botzer & Weninger (2106.07353, DADC 2022): post-hoc verification finds model
   predictions verified as often as the gold.
Not found (~12 web/abs queries; NOT a "first" claim): no system count frontier indexed by gold size; no capture-recapture
  or Chao coverage on caption references; no rater equivalence for set-valued caption content.
SCOOP RISK
C2 (b set by gold completeness; b_sys = b_pool - D): LOW-MEDIUM.
  - P1 already shows precision depends on k through propensity (the b_pool side), and P10 shows evaluation depends on
    judgment depth.
  - New: the system exponent indexed by k and instance gold, plus the reference/system split. Present b_pool as P1
    generalised to heterogeneous propensities.
  - On KTVIC, "k refs" means k captions by ONE writer (section 3, D3).
E2 (FP = sampling-unnamed / guideline-unnamed / hallucinated): MEDIUM.
  - "Many FPs are real unannotated objects" is established (P2, P3, P7, P11). The PU correction exists (P11).
  - New: the three-way split from capture-recapture plus an independent panel.
  - The sampling term comes from M_h/Chao on within-writer refs. Positive list dependence makes N_hat too low, so on
    KTVIC the sampling/guideline split is not identified. Report it as a bound.
E5 (k*): MEDIUM on the concept, LOW on set-coverage extrapolation.
  - P8 owns rater equivalence, and its one-rater-at-a-time protocol is E5's h=1.
  - App. G leaves E5's slot open (beyond-panel extrapolation) and rejects the parametric route: lead with Chao-Jost,
    and keep the beta-binomial as a check only.
  - P8 assumes raters are distinct people. KTVIC "k annotators" are captions by one writer, so a true annotator-
    equivalent exists only in OpenViIC units (independent students).

3. NEW DIRECTIONS (the three candidates evaluated, one added)
D3 first, because it conditions the others. Reference exchangeability audit. VERDICT: keep, as a measurement-validity
  result. The adjacency chain IS a dataset-construction artifact, and it is KTVIC-specific.
  Claim: KTVIC's adjacency excess comes from its documented single-writer protocol (P1 contrast: COCO = independent
    workers). A 3-statistic audit separates single-writer chains (KTVIC), sort artifacts (Flickr30k) and exchangeable
    panels (COCO, UIT-ViIC, OpenViIC):
      (i) the share of doubletons in adjacent pairs vs 0.40;
      (ii) the lag-1 minus lag-2..4 pair Jaccard;
      (iii) the slot-precision gradient.
    It must gate every Chao/Good-Turing estimate, k-subset curve and leave-one-out "human ceiling".

  Known for COCO/Flickr? COCO is exchangeable, as its paper states. Flickr30k shows an order (sort) effect, not a

        re-derived]), but agrees within 10% on COCO and UIT-ViIC;
    (b) KTVIC leave-one-out human P against equal-size adjacent-only vs non-adjacent-only gold differs by >= 2 P, and by
        < 0.5 P on COCO and UIT-ViIC.
  Test: extend scripts/adjacency_check.py on existing refs. Cost < 0.2 CPU-h, 0 GPU-h. Pillar C4; it also corrects the
    C2, C3 and E5 wording. Novelty 2, risk 1.
D1 Completeness-indexed reporting ("report b and C(k) next to every object metric"). VERDICT: keep, but only narrowed
  into a falsifiable claim-stability result. A reporting standard alone is novelty 1.
  Claim: a gold card (coverage C_hat(k), b, the D3 audit) predicts when an object-metric ordering flips between golds.
    The flips are carried by count differences.
  New: P1 and P10 show that completeness matters in principle. No caption or hallucination paper reports coverage or
    tests claim stability across gold completeness.
  Prediction:
    - raw object-P orderings flip between the least and most complete gold in >= 15% of system pairs;
    - >= 80% of the flips have |dM| >= 0.3 classes per caption;
    - at matched count (frontier residual), flips fall to < 5%.
    Systems: KTVIC 73 systems x all k-subsets; COCO-2500 9 x refs1..5/inst/inst+refs; UIT-ViIC 6.
  Test: the existing outputs of SP/research_r10/G2/g2_frontier.py, plus E5 kstar.py coverage. Cost 0.3 CPU-h. Pillar C2
    (+C1). Novelty 2, risk 2.
D2 Chao1/Chao2-corrected object precision. VERDICT: as a KTVIC metric it is already falsified (E2: Spearman 0.65 vs 0.91
  for raw), and the general correction belongs to P11 (PU, propensity). One clean test remains, on EXCHANGEABLE refs
  with an independent gold.
  Claim: with exchangeable refs (COCO, UIT-ViIC; D3), E2's M_h-corrected P* predicts precision against instance+refs
    gold better than raw ref-P. With within-writer refs (KTVIC) it does not.
  New: P11 needs a known prior or propensity; here the propensity comes from exchangeable references and is checked
    against instance labels. E2 tested only non-exchangeable refs.
  Prediction: on COCO-2500 (9 systems) and UIT-ViIC test (6 systems), in COCO-80 space,
    MAE(P*, P_inst+refs) <= 0.6 x MAE(P_refs, P_inst+refs), and Spearman(P*) >= Spearman(P_refs).
  Test: point E2's e2_pilot.py at data/datasets/{coco_en,uitviic} plus data/coco_probe/instance_gold.json. Cost 0.5 CPU-h.
    Pillar C2/C3. Novelty 3, risk 3: COCO-80 is small, and instance gold has RePOPE-type errors.
D4 (added; B2 x D gap 0 / 73.5, full map 1 / 329.9) Incomplete gold inside preference-pair construction.
  Claim: reference-labelled pairs ("rejected = names objects outside the refs") carry label noise that grows as the gold
    shrinks and correlates with count: the longer sample is rejected more often. DPO then learns brevity, a count shift
    that count-matched evaluation nulls.
  New: R-DPO (2403.19159) separates length; HA-DPO, POVID and HALVA build negatives by rewriting or injection. I found no
    paper that measures pair-label noise vs gold size (shallow search, VERIFY).
  Prediction: in M3-style pairs labelled with refs {0,2}, >= 20% of rejecting objects are named by the held-out refs
    {4} or by the OpenViIC panel (aggregates), and flipped pairs have larger dM (CI excludes 0).
  Test: the M3 builder on existing dev300 or train sample pools (VERIFY which pools exist). Cost 0.5 CPU-h. Pillar C1/C5
    (explains the DPO nulls). Novelty 3, risk 3.

6. WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY CONCLUSIONS (kill rules)
 D3: the KTVIC excess disappears under synonym-merged classes or the COCO-80 VI matcher (a matcher artifact). Or the KTVIC
     authors report several writers per image: then reframe it as an ordering effect and drop "single-writer".
 D1: unmatched-count ordering flips < 5% of pairs on both KTVIC and COCO. Then the gold card is one sentence, not a result.
 D2: on COCO or UIT-ViIC, the MAE ratio is > 0.8 or Spearman does not improve. Then drop P* everywhere and keep only Chao
     lower bounds.
 D4: < 10% of rejecting objects are named by held-out refs or the panel, or there is no dM correlation. Then drop D4.
 Scoop upgrades:
  - a paper that fits system precision against the number of references, or applies Chao/coverage to caption
    references: C2/E2 go to HIGH;
  - a rater-equivalence paper with coverage-based extrapolation for set-valued outputs: E5 goes to HIGH.
