0. BOTTOM LINE
- No verified paper uses multi-annotator mention frequency in LVLM hallucination/omission metrics or in preference
  optimisation. The closest is VIFIDEL (ACL 2019), which weights objects by reference mentions: one gold, no count control.
- Mechanism (new, supported): non-transfer between golds comes from GUIDELINE-ONLY mentions plus gold-size dilution (x0.69), not
  low-k gains. UPE gains shared objects (+0.016 of +0.017 TP/img), dropping panel-only objects (-0.008); 35_e's is control-side.
- Evaluation card (supported): P1 omits consensus objects (4/4 refs) at 25-28% vs 13-17% for a held-out annotator at near-equal
  count; 58.5-58.7% of its reference-"hallucinations" are panel-named, the same as a human's singletons (59.6-60.2%).
- Killed: class-level k/5 importance does not predict which FPs the panel confirms (AUC 0.52-0.57, CI includes 0.5 on test).

What the bridges are:
- A1/A2 x B3: one content bridge, M3ID (Favero et al., CVPR 2024, arXiv 2403.14003), a mutual-information DECODING
  method that cites Spain & Perona. The others (CLIPScore 2104.08718, a 2020 survey, Im2Text, PASCAL VOC) are generic.
- B3 x C1: PASCAL VOC and the DPM detector (generic references). B3 x F: a 2019 thesis on multi-label reduction.
  Conceptual bridge NOT in the map: Arroyo, Perona & Cole (arXiv 2305.15584) show that the positive label an annotator
  gives is not chosen at random (label bias). Mention-worthiness is that bias in caption golds.
- B1 x B3: CLIPScore, BISON, gender/racial-bias captioning papers, VIP and DIVE. They cite Berg 2012 as background; none
  scores omission or hallucination by k/n.
Caveat: the ratio is inflated by era. Seed citers are mostly 2013-2019 (Spain-Perona: 7 citers from 2023 on; Berg: 16), while
A1/A2/D are 2023+. Reading the 2019+ citers (S2 citer lists): only VIFIDEL (1907.09340) turns reference-mention frequency into
a caption metric. Gregorio, Ponti & Goldwater (CoNLL 2026) link salience to mention ORDER and grammatical role, not to
hallucination. The gap is real at the content level, but smaller than 5/80 suggests.

2. CLOSEST PRIOR WORK
Key: [C] = Crossref record via doi.org; [A] = arXiv abs page; [P] = paper text read today (arXiv HTML or ACL page).
Background (importance = what people name):
 1 Spain & Perona, "Measuring and Predicting Object Importance", IJCV 91:59-76, doi 10.1007/s11263-010-0376-0 [C]
   (published online 2010-08-27). Importance = probability an observer names the object; position and size predict it.
 2 Berg et al., "Understanding and predicting importance in images", CVPR 2012 pp.3562-3569, doi 10.1109/CVPR.2012.6248100 [C].
 3 Yun, Peng, Samaras, Zelinsky & Berg, CVPR 2013 pp.739-746, doi 10.1109/CVPR.2013.101 [C]. Gaze vs what is described.
 4 Jas & Parikh, "Image Specificity", arXiv 1502.04569 [A], CVPR 2015 (CVF page). Description consistency varies by image.
 5 Yatskar, Galley, Vanderwende & Zettlemoyer, "See No Evil, Say No Evil", *SEM 2014 pp.110-120, doi 10.3115/v1/S14-1015
   [ACL page]. Content selection from dense labels.
 6 Tavakoli, Shetty, Borji & Laaksonen, ICCV 2017 pp.2506-2515, doi 10.1109/ICCV.2017.272 [C], arXiv 1704.07434 [A].
   Salient objects are mentioned earlier. A saliency-boosted captioner gives no significant COCO gain (precedent for M2).
 7 Misra, Zitnick, Mitchell & Girshick, "Seeing through the Human Reporting Bias", CVPR 2016 pp.2930-2939,
   doi 10.1109/CVPR.2016.320 [C], arXiv 1512.06974 [A]. Separate presence and relevance heads. This is the conceptual
   precedent for "plausible-unmentioned vs absent", but it is used for training classifiers, not for evaluation.
Evaluation and omission metrics:
 8 VIFIDEL, Madhyastha, Wang & Specia, ACL 2019 pp.6539-6550, doi 10.18653/v1/P19-1654 [C], arXiv 1907.09340 [A]: takes into
   account "the relative importance of objects mentioned in human reference descriptions". This is the CLOSEST work. It has
   one gold, no count control, and no LVLM hallucination split.
 9 AMBER, arXiv 2311.07397 [A][P]. Cover = share of annotated objects mentioned, all objects weighted equally. The annotations
   include "all visible objects in the image, including background objects".
10 HallE-Control / CCEval, arXiv 2310.01779 [A][P]. Coverage = matched / all ground-truth objects, unweighted, GPT-4-assisted.
   It states "we do not strictly enforce consistent constraints (same coverage, average length, and average objects)".
11 CAPTURE, arXiv 2405.19092 [A][P]. Exact, synonym and soft matching, plus a stop list of abstract nouns. Objects are not
   weighted by salience; the F1 scores are weighted only by element type (5:5:2).
12 CompreCap, arXiv 2412.08614 [A][P]. Object coverage is unweighted. Area weights enter only the attribute score; the
   "tiny objects (<5% pixels)" filter is used only for its VQA part.
13 DeCapBench/DCScore + FeedQuill, arXiv 2503.07906 [A][P]. Unweighted P/R over information units; recall credits
   verified-correct unreferenced units. FeedQuill reward = precision + 0.5 x unit count: no importance, no omission check.
14 InfoMetIC, ACL 2023 pp.3171-3185, doi 10.18653/v1/2023.acl-long.178 [C]: reference-free "vision recall".
    CapMAS (Lee et al., arXiv 2412.15484) [A] reports factuality and coverage. Per Gemini A4, its 67.9->18.3 drop is
    FACTSCORE (reference-free), so it must NOT be cited as cross-gold evidence.
Preference optimisation and omission repair (B3 x D: nothing uses human mention frequency):
15 CHAIR-DPO, Compagnoni et al., arXiv 2508.20181 [A], BMVC 2025: CHAIR labels the winner of each pair. Abstract only:
   no importance or omission term. HSA-DPO, Xiao et al., arXiv 2404.14233 [A]: severity-aware AI feedback.
16 Omission: "Two Causes, Not One" arXiv 2509.00371 [A] (omission vs fabrication mechanisms). PatchGate arXiv 2608.21819 [A]
   (recovers visible objects that were left out, from model-internal evidence). ProCap arXiv 2607.21022 [A] (prominence-
   ranked object injection for VIDEO captions, using detector saliency rather than human mention statistics).
Cross-guideline and cross-language differences in content:
17 van Miltenburg, Elliott & Vossen, INLG 2017 pp.21-30, doi 10.18653/v1/W17-3503 [C], arXiv 1707.01736 [A]. Ye, Santy,
   Hwang, Zhang & Krishna, arXiv 2310.14356 [A] (CVPR 2025 per the map's DOI 10.1109/CVPR52734.2025.02762, not opened).
   Both: annotator groups (by language) differ in WHAT they describe. Gregorio et al., CoNLL 2026 pp.301-317,
   doi 10.18653/v1/2026.conll-main.18 [ACL page]: salience -> mention order and grammatical role.

D1 Two-gold transfer accounting at matched count (candidate 2; SUPPORTED in a refined form) -- pillar C3 (+C1)
 Claim:
 - Identity: dTP_K - dTP_P = d(KTVIC-only TP) - d(panel-only). Non-transfer is carried by GUIDELINE-ONLY mentions plus a
   gold-size dilution, not by gains on low-k objects.
 - UPE, test, 3 seeds:
   * dTP_K +0.017 [0.005, 0.029]: shared objects +0.016 [0.007, 0.027], KTVIC-only +0.001.
   * Where the gain comes from: panel-only dropped -0.008 [-0.017, +0.001], i.e. 42% of the FP cut; unconfirmed dropped
     -0.011 [-0.021, -0.002].

   * Dilution: first-order F1 per +1 TP/img is 42.8 (KTVIC) vs 29.5 (panel). Full TP transfer would give the panel
     +0.49 [0.14, 0.87]; observed +0.09.
   * The other pools fit: p1_s43 cuts mostly unconfirmed FPs (-0.039; panel-only -0.007) and transfers (+1.25 vs +1.30);
