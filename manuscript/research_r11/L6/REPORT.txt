==== 0. BOTTOM LINE ==============================================================================================================
- I found no verified work that builds preference pairs with equal object counts, or that re-scores DPO/RL-trained LVLMs at matched
  object count. In the map, A3 x D and A2 x A3 have 2 bridges each (15.7 and 19.5 expected); both are COCO and Visual Genome.
- The neighbourhood is crowded, though. HALVA's swaps are count-preserving by construction, RLAIF-V length-filters its pairs, and
  "Beyond Saying Less" (Sep 2026) names the shortcut. Scoop risk: C1 low; M3 medium (narrow the claim); M5 low (high as a method); M6 low.
- None of the 12 DPO/RL repos I scanned releases per-caption outputs, so a count-matched DPO leaderboard needs about 6 GPU-h of decoding.
- CPU pilot on released pairs: only 35-69% of pairs are count-matched. In HA-DPO, HSA-DPO and SENTINEL the chosen answer names fewer
  COCO classes in 82-90% of the unmatched pairs, often at nearly equal word length.

==== 2. CLOSEST PRIOR WORK (IDs verified on arXiv abs pages today; venue from the abs comment unless noted) ==========================
2a. Preference pairs (Q1). Distance to M3 = same-image pairs naming the SAME number of object classes, so a count reward cancels.
 [P1] R-DPO 2403.19159, Park/Rafailov/Ermon/Finn, Findings ACL 2024 pp. 4998-5017, DOI 10.18653/v1/2024.findings-acl.297 (Anthology).
      Text DPO; a loss-side length regulariser. FAR.
 [P2] SamPO 2406.10957, Lu et al., EMNLP 2024: down-sampled KL. LD-DPO 2409.06411, Liu et al.: length desensitisation. SimPO
      2405.14734 (NeurIPS 2024): length-normalised reward. All text-only and loss-side. FAR.
 [P3] Singhal et al. 2310.03716 (COLM 2024). DATA-side "length balancing" of reward-model pairs: it balances the direction of length
      differences in 10-token bins, not per pair. RM accuracy fell (WebGPT 61.5 -> 52.6). MEDIUM (data-side, but text and length).
 [P4] RLAIF-V 2405.17220 (no venue on the abs page). Same model, same input and decoding, different seeds ("deconfounded":
      style and word choice). It drops pairs whose preferred answer is too short, keeping the length gap under one word (sec. 2.2).
      Score = -(number of rejected claims); this count score beat a percentage score in human agreement (96.7 vs 83.3, App. C.1).
      So the pairs are length-matched on average but not count-matched, and the score itself favours fewer claims.
      CLOSEST DATA-SIDE PRECEDENT in LVLM DPO.
 [P5] HALVA/DPA 2405.18654, ICLR 2025. Off-policy one-for-one phrase swaps in GT-derived answers (Gemini Pro Vision + VG), so
      object swaps preserve the count. Phrase-level pairwise loss plus token KL (alpha 0.4). AMBER Cover 51.0 -> 53.0, CHAIR 7.8 -> 6.6,
      CHAIR length 100.6 -> 92.2 (7B). CLOSEST ON THE PAIR PROPERTY. Count is incidental: no count rule, no on-policy pool, no frontier test.
 [P6] mDPO 2406.11839, EMNLP 2024. The conditional pair is the SAME answer y_w with the original vs a <20%-cropped image. That pair is
      text-identical, so it is trivially count-invariant, but it teaches image reliance, not object choice. The standard term uses
      VLFeedback pairs. "May lead to a decrease in object coverage". MEDIUM.
 [P7] RLHF-V 2312.00849 (CVPR 2024): segment-level human corrections, which delete or replace, plus dense DPO. HSA-DPO 2404.14233:
      detect-then-rewrite plus severity weights. HA-DPO 2311.16839: "style-consistent" GPT rewrites. POVID 2402.11411 (no venue):
      GT vs GPT-4V-injected hallucinations. No count control in any. FAR.
 [P8] CHAIR-DPO 2508.20181, BMVC 2025 ("...via Object-aware Preference Optimization"). Of two T 0.7 samples the lower CHAIR_i wins;
      ties are discarded; no count control. AMBER CHAIR_i 7.6 -> 3.0 and Cover 51.7 -> 46.6 (beta 0.2). FAR: a rate winner rewards saying less.
 [P9] OPA-DPO 2501.09695, CVPR 2025. On-policy answer as rejected; GPT-4V minimal revision or GT as chosen; no count control.
      AMBER CHAIR 7.7 -> 2.2, Cover 51.6 -> 47.9 ("slightly conservative strategy"). MEDIUM-FAR (on-policy, but edits change the count).
 [P10] HII-DPO 2602.10425 (sec. 3.4) and SENTINEL 2507.12455: same-model pairs that share a prefix and diverge at one sentence.
      In HII-DPO the chosen answer omits the removed object, so the pairs are count-UNMATCHED by design. MEDIUM-FAR.
 [P11] HDPO 2411.10436 (Findings ACL 2025): rewrote over-long positives with GPT-4o "to mitigate length bias" [UNVERIFIED snippet].
2b. Evaluation at matched length or count (Q2; distance to C1)

      read (e6_mt15_verification.md sec. 5): per-model OLS of CHAIR on WORD length over up to 25 instructions; 6 base LVLMs, not

      This is the closest thing to a DPO comparison at matched coverage, and it is not matched.
 [E3] Own-method length checks: CAS 2605.27993 App. C "length-aligned CHAIR" (64-token truncation, CAS vs vanilla only);
      Sandhu et al. 2608.24966 (COLM 2026 workshop: fixed 64/128-token budgets, recall 0.78 -> 0.70); SumGD 2410.13321 (token budgets).
 [E4] DSCC 2608.12746. Re-ran VCD/OPERA under one protocol, UNMATCHED (89.5-104.9 words vs its 171.5), and calls 1 - CHAIR_I a

2e. Scoop risk per R10 item (Q4)
- C1 count-matched evaluation + CAR: LOW. The confound is widely acknowledged (LeHaCE, SumGD, CAS, Sandhu, DSCC, CheXalign,
  Beyond Saying Less), so it is not claimable. Not found anywhere: per-image object-count matching, a pre-registered count frontier,
  CAR, or re-scoring of other groups' released outputs. Watch item: A2 x D is the most crowded pair (2.2x), and a "fair comparison
  at matched recall" paper could appear at any time; re-search before submission.
  Caveat for the "count beats length" sub-claim (Gemini, e1_x1pb_verification.md sec. 3):
  - on English decoding pairs, count wins on variance (R2 0.488 vs 0.053; partial dR2 of length given count: mean 0.0015, max 0.005);
  - the signed mean alpha_len - alpha_cnt is only +0.186 P [-0.257, +0.579], so X1's P-B kill rule triggers on the signed mean;
  - the big verdict flip (MBR +2.60 vs -0.14) is a KTVIC density-changing case.
  The brief's "< 2.1% of F1 variance" is not in that file.
- M3 CM-DPO: MEDIUM. Why:
  - HALVA's count-preserving swaps (ICLR 2025), mDPO's text-identical conditional pairs and RLAIF-V's length filter cover parts
    of the idea, and 35-69% of public pairs are already count-matched by accident (2d);
  - M3 sec. 2 says "length control in DPO is loss-side". That is wrong: RLAIF-V, Singhal and HDPO all control length on the data side.
  Not found: an explicit equal-count pair rule with the cancellation lemma, a vote-matched control and a count-frontier test.
  Claim exactly that. Compare with HALVA and with Beyond Saying Less.
- M5 object budget: LOW as framed by M5 (the ranking/allocation/base decomposition and the frontier test). HIGH as a method:
  noun-count conditioning (2101.08000) and length conditioning (LaBERT, CaptionSmiths, 2005.14386) are published.
- M6 corpus allocation (knapsack): LOW. The rule itself is standard plug-in F-measure theory (Lipton 2014, Ye 2012, Koyejo 2015,
  Schultheis 2024 at-k), so it is not novel. Not found: a fixed total budget allocated across images for a caption object metric,
  or the point that per-image count control is blind to allocation. The map shows 0 bridges for A1 x F and A2 x F
  (137.7 and 61.1 expected; the F cluster may be undercounted).

       by >= 0.3 more than POVID/mDPO (|dk| <= 0.11).

==== 6. WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY CONCLUSION (kill rules) =============================================================================
- Scoop: a paper with equal-object-count preference pairs, or matched-count re-scoring of DPO models, would move M3 or C1 to HIGH.
  Cite it and reframe as replication or extension. Re-run the 2b/2a searches plus a Litmaps "cited by" sweep of RLAIF-V, HALVA,
  OPA-DPO and LeHaCE before submission.
- D1:
  - if the checkpoints' base CHAIR is more than 1.0 from the published values, fix the protocol before any look (fidelity, as E1-K1);
  - if dk-sign does not order dm (difference < 0.1 class), drop prediction (b);
  - if >= 1/2 of the checkpoints keep a Holm-significant dP_MCP >= +1.0, the headline becomes "training changes object choice",
    which still serves C1.
- D2: if RLAIF-V's chosen-fewer share among unmatched pairs is in [45%, 55%] AND D1 shows no dk -> dm link, drop "length control
  leaves a count channel" (keep the table as description).
- D3:
  - if dm(CM) < -0.10, the invariance does not protect in practice and M3's premise is weakened;
  - if dP_MCP(CM) - dP_MCP(random) has CI covering 0 with both arms keeping Cover, count matching is unnecessary on English data.
  Report either outcome as a pre-registered negative.
- Ratings: M3 drops to LOW only if HALVA's full method shows no count-balanced design intent (App. D.3 templates still unread).
  M6 rises if a corpus-level F budget paper for captioning or multi-label generation is found.
