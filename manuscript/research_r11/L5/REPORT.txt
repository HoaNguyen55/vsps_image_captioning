3. NEW DIRECTIONS INFERRED FROM THE GAP
D1. A punctuation-convention x IDF law for CIDEr-D, across languages.
 Claim: what a hypothesis-final punctuation token does to CIDEr-D is set by its document frequency in the references. A system
   whose punctuation convention differs from the panel's therefore gains or loses CIDEr predictably, in any language. Under
   keep-punctuation protocols (KTVIC P0), cross-family comparisons depend on that convention.
 New vs sec. 2: COCO, XM3600 and PaLI strip punctuation, which avoids the issue without measuring it. SacreEOS is about <Eos> in
   the SCST reward. ViTextCaps varies tokenisers, not conventions.
 Pilot already run (xm_punct_pilot.py; XM3600, 36 languages; caption 2 scored against caption 1; official tokens; 7.9 CPU-min,
 0.5 GB):
   - Spearman(reference final-punctuation rate, relative change) = 0.81; Spearman(IDF of the token, change) = -0.84.
   - 19 languages whose references rarely end in punctuation (rate < 0.10): -0.7% to -15.3%, median -7.7%.
   - he, te, en (rate > 0.80): +2.1% to +8.4%. vi: rate 0.38, change -1.2%.
   - Pre-registered: "<= -15% for every language with rate < 0.10" FAILED (2/19). "Rate > 0.80 -> >= -5%" HELD. "rho > 0.8" HELD.
   - So the sign is general; the size depends on the setup (KTVIC: -23%, 5 references).
 Prediction: the penalty deepens with more references. On KTVIC with k = 1..5 references it becomes monotonically more negative,
   reaching <= -20% at k = 5. On UIT-ViIC (98% of references end in " .") appending "." to the KTVIC-trained P1 outputs RAISES
   CIDEr.
 Test: KTVIC, UIT-ViIC and coco_en references plus data/results/*-short.preds.json and data/results/uitviic/*.preds.json; E4's
   Cider class; reference subsampling as in E4 P10/P11.
 Cost 0 GPU-h, ~0.5 CPU-h. Pillar C4, plus C5 (the MBR/UPE CIDEr "cost"). Novelty 2, risk 2.
D2. Corpus transfer of the Vietnamese object matcher: a per-corpus artifact audit.
 Claim: the share of CHAIR-vi "hallucinations" that are extractor artifacts depends on the corpus. UIT-ViIC's are role and child
   nouns; KTVIC's are cửa hàng and cây cầu. The share is large enough to reorder systems. So freeze the extractor from each
   corpus's TRAIN references and audit it with existing labels before making cross-corpus claims.
 New vs sec. 2: V1/V3 validated v3 on KTVIC only. Here KTVIC-derived v3 fixes 10 of 33 UIT-ViIC flags, and the v2 -> v3 change
   flips p1_s43 (transfer) vs zero-shot. No cross-corpus CHAIR audit was found in [1]-[11].
 Prediction: mine role and child nouns from UIT-ViIC train references, set 'đứa trẻ' = 'trẻ em', and freeze. Train-reference
   caption counts: cầu thủ 2,790; vận động viên 2,829; cậu bé 727; khán giả 326. Then:
   - uitviic_p1_s42-short: CHAIR_i <= 5.0%;
   - KTVIC P1: CHAIR_i moves by <= 0.3 pp;
   - the UIT-ViIC order (transfer < zero-shot) holds with a paired-bootstrap CI.
 Test: uitviic_chair_audit.py + run_v1_v3_unified_v3_audit.py on data/results/uitviic*/ and uitviic_ind_{test,val}/. Label check:
   data/uitviic/instance_gold.json (COCO person) supports role-noun mentions at >= 99%.
 Cost 0 GPU-h, < 0.5 CPU-h. Pillar C4, plus C1 (count-matched endpoints share this extractor). Novelty 3, risk 2.
D3. Classifier minimal pairs for object matchers and VLMs, in several languages.
 Claim: in classifier languages, standard tools segment numeral + classifier + noun inconsistently. XM3600's official zh tokens
   fuse "两只鸡" into one token but split "另一 只". Matchers and VLMs also confuse classifier senses (quả bóng is not a fruit; cây
   vợt is not a tree). Both bias object-level metrics for vi, zh, ja and th.
 New vs sec. 2: no classifier-aware CHAIR or probe was found for any language. V1 covers vi only.
 Predictions:
   (a) CPU: >= 10% of zh/ja numeral-classifier phrases in XM3600's official tokens are fused with their noun (~0% for vi
       syllables), and word-level vs character-level object recall then differs by >= 3 points;
   (b) GPU: V2's classifier-sense arm raises zero-shot Qwen2.5-VL FP by >= 3 points over matched controls, and the rise shrinks
       by >= 50% when asked in English.
 Test: XM3600 captions.jsonl (local, 36 languages, official tokens); data/coco_probe/coco80_vi.json; pope_vi.py --items (V2).
 Cost (a) 0.3 CPU-h; (b) ~1 GPU-h (a subset of V2 S1). Pillar C4. Novelty 3, risk 3.
D4. Packaging, not a novelty: a signature card for syllabic and unsegmented languages.
 Pilot, 1-vs-1 human CIDEr with punctuation stripped (whitespace / official / character tokens):
   zh 0.07 / 31.1 / 48.1; th 0.28 / 61.1 / 42.3; ko 28.6 / 59.6 / 91.9; vi 73.6 / 73.2 / 56.2; en 108.8 / 108.8 / 102.7.
 The card should name the segmenter, the unit, punctuation, case, IDF source and the number of references. Novelty 1 (cite [10]).
