# Paper A figure captions

Figures and captions sit outside the binding word count (PD-29 (b)). Every plotted value is read from number_manifest.tsv by `scripts/figures/paperA_figs.py`; `VALUES.json` lists each value with its manifest row, source file and key.

**Fig. 1.** The gold levels of this paper. Each caption is matched once and scored against nested reference golds (one, $k$ and all references) and against a label gold (COCO instance labels or human-verified Open Images labels), which is not nested in the references. The verbosity slope is the slope of F1 on $\ln M$ across the systems of a family under each gold (Section 3.6). The reference levels are ordered even if objects go missing at random (Section 4.2); the label-gold step is the informative one. Schematic, no data.

**Fig. 2.** Slope of mixed F1 (points) on $\ln M$, with 95% image-bootstrap intervals, under one random reference (circles), all references (squares; five, or the 2–3 of XM3600) and the label gold (triangles; Section 3.1), for each family of Table 6.1 and for the second-backbone sets of Section 6.3, where no all-references value is reported. Grades are those of Table 6.1; the UIT-ViIC-231 pass is not counted as support. On XM3600 images the label-gold F1 pairs verified precision with micro recall (Table 6.1). Open triangles: human-verified labels, verified precision (XM3600 images). Grey diamonds: the post hoc lower bound, with mentions OIV7 cannot verify counted as errors (Section 6.2; Supplementary S7); it carries no verdict.

**Fig. 3.** PASCAL-50S (registered; Section 6.4): slope of mixed F1 (points) on $\ln M$ over 14 runs of seven public LVLMs on the 997-image analysis set, with 95% image-bootstrap intervals (1,000 draws), for $k$ = 1, 2, 5, 10, 20 and 50 random references on the COCO-80 vocabulary (circles) and on the 20 VOC classes (V20, squares), and under the VOC labels (open triangle, V20). The dashed red segment marks the registered comparison 50 references < VOC labels, which failed (−2.29 [−5.15, +0.61]). Values are the registered no-prediction statistics P50-NS (Supplementary Table S7.P1); intervals hold the 14 runs fixed.

**Fig. 4.** COCO-5000 (exploratory; post hoc full-split extension); all intervals are post hoc 95% image-bootstrap intervals. (a) $\Lambda_k(M_0)$ with $\bar G(k) = U(k)$ for $k$ random references, for the label gold (COCO instance labels) and for the label gold with the five references (Supplementary S1 and S7). (b) Share of the rise in $\Lambda$ carried by the b-channel, from one to five references and from one reference to the label gold (Supplementary S1).
