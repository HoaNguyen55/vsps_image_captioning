Under the two controls that keep the order of mention, two of the twelve significant raw gains remain, and the mean gain falls from +3.41 to +0.62 and +0.65 points. One gain reverses: OPERA on Shikra has a raw precision gain over beam search of +3.47 [+0.75, +6.03] points and a significant loss at matched prefix, −1.23 [−2.42, −0.08]. The uniform random subset keeps 6 of 15 gains (mean +2.76) because it removes the count difference but not the order effect (assumption A2). The protocol's withdrawal rule (more than 5 of 15 gains significant at matched count) is not triggered under the order-preserving controls and is triggered under the random subset, so we draw no confirmatory conclusion. ## 6.3 Placebos and positive controls

A registered seed placebo (two sampling seeds of one model) admitted and selected the matched prefix (prediction HELD), but it was decoded in the leaderboard harness that failed its fidelity check (Section 6.4), so it calibrates the paired control only. The positive control is underpowered at 500 images on 2 of 8 baselines, and the pre-specified prediction that object count explains precision gains better than word length failed (+0.186 [−0.257, +0.579] points against a required +1.0) and is withdrawn (Supplementary S3).

Every number in this paper is produced by a script from a key of a stored result file and is listed, with that file's SHA-256, in a numbers manifest; each registration file carries its commit time, and Table 1 gives the status of every prediction (Section 3.6; Supplementary S4). The re-scoring code passed the fidelity check of Section 6.1 on the published CHAIR rows, and the XM3600 evaluator verified the registered hashes of its frozen inputs (Table 1, X-0). The code, matchers, registration files, manifest and score-only result files are deposited as a restricted record that reviewers can open through the editor (Data availability). The record is not public during review, and the code has not yet been run outside the authors' group.
