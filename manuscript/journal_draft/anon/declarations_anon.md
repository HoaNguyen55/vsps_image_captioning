# Declarations (anonymized for review; placed immediately before the references)

## Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the manuscript preparation process

During this work the authors used Claude (Anthropic) and Gemini (Google) to write and run analysis code, to draft and edit the manuscript text and to check references. The authors designed the study, directed and checked all analyses, reviewed and edited all text, and take full responsibility for the content.

## Ethics

No new human annotation was collected and no human subjects were involved; all human judgments are public datasets used under their licences (Section 11.2). No ethics approval was required: no new data from human participants were collected.

## Data and code availability

Code, pre-registration files, the numbers manifest and score-only result files are provided to reviewers as an anonymized supplementary archive; a public Zenodo DOI will be added on acceptance. Caption fields are removed from the result files for licensing, and 33 of the 677 manifest rows cite sources that could not be released; they are marked. Data files keep their sources' terms. The public datasets are available from their original sources; UIT-OpenViIC, KTVIC references and human-judgment ratings are not redistributed (Section 11.2).

## Related manuscript

A related manuscript on the validity of the Vietnamese captioning benchmark KTVIC is in preparation. It uses the same fine-tuned instrument. This paper's XM3600 results and its object-F1 slope results on UIT-ViIC appear only in this paper; the related manuscript reports other analyses of some of the same UIT-ViIC system outputs and cites this paper for the slopes. A copy is available to the editor on request.

The authors declare no competing interests.

This research received no external funding; all costs, including rented GPU time and AI-tool subscriptions, were borne by the authors' team.
