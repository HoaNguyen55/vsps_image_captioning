# Declarations (anonymized for review; placed immediately before the references)

## Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the manuscript preparation process

During this work the authors used Claude (Anthropic) and Gemini (Google) to write and run analysis code, to draft and edit the manuscript text and to check references. The authors designed the study, directed and checked all analyses, reviewed and edited all text, and take full responsibility for the content.

## Ethics

No new human annotation was collected and no human subjects were involved; all human judgments are public datasets used under their licences (Section 9.2). No ethics approval was required: no new data from human participants were collected.

## Data and code availability

Code, pre-registration files, the numbers manifest and score-only result files are provided to reviewers as an anonymized supplementary archive; a public Zenodo DOI will be added on acceptance. Caption fields are removed from the result files for licensing, and 29 of the 804 manifest rows cite sources that could not be released; they are marked. Data files keep their sources' terms. The public datasets are available from their original sources; UIT-OpenViIC, KTVIC references and human-judgment ratings are not redistributed (Section 9.2). UIT-ViIC (Lam et al., 2020) is likewise used only through aggregates and pointers, and no per-image UIT-ViIC panel is released; we have not received written permission from the UIT-ViIC authors and claim none.<!-- rev1 (PI-1029p item 5): UIT-ViIC authors have not given permission. --> The registered PASCAL-50S verdicts can be recomputed only by re-running the registered job from the pinned model snapshots, decoder and code; per-image outputs cannot be audited. A reviewer can check independently the scoring code, the decide files with their SHA-256, the commit times of the registrations and the numbers manifest; the PASCAL-50S per-image outputs and the rows marked as not releasable cannot be checked.<!-- rev2 (CC-181 plan T6): reproducibility sentence copied verbatim from declarations.md (rev1 items 7a, 15), no identifying detail -->
