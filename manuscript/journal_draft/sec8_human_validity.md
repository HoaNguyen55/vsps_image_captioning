## 7.2 Human span labels on COCO (registered secondary)

This test checks criterion validity against the human span labels of M-HalDetect (Gunjal et al., 2024; research use) on 3,419 COCO-2014 images and 13,997 records. Agreement between object precision and the human labels rises from one reference to all references (τ_b difference +0.057 [+0.043, +0.071], PASS) but not from all references to complete COCO-80 gold (−0.021 [−0.043, +0.002]); the second part is a **FAIL** under the registered rule "point ≤ 0" whose interval includes 0, so the registered outcome is not supported.
