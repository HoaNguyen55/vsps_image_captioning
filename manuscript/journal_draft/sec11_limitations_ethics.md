## 9.1 Threats to validity

Limitations are grouped by the validity type they threaten.<!-- pass 3 (2026-10-28): §10.1 reorganised as threats to validity (construct, internal, statistical conclusion, external); paragraph text unchanged except the labels and one multiplicity sentence (scorecard: evidence, writing) -->

**Scope of the confirmatory evidence.** In the XM3600 test two of five predictions failed; COCO-295 is a replication on discovery images, and the UIT-ViIC pass is not counted. Because the reference golds are nested, their ordering would also arise under random missingness, so the evidence supports the pattern, not the propensity model (Section 4.2). The registered PASCAL-50S replication failed at its label-gold step (−2.29 [−5.15, +0.61]; Section 6.4), whose premise, more complete VOC labels, was assumed at registration, and a post hoc count (ratio 0.83) is consistent with it not holding; so the ordering claim rests on XM3600 and its second backbone.<!-- src: .session_backup/2026-10-14/r12_verify/xm3600_full_verdict.json :: prereg_comparisons[16..20].in_interval_or_pass; manuscript/prereg/2026-10-18_never_committed_frozen_files.json :: files[2] (L1_COCO).in_any_git_commit = false; data/results/p50/p50_decide.json (sha256 0da4cab90fe9) :: analysis_set.P50-A4.parts[1].{point, ci95}; rev2 (CC-181 W8): full paragraph moved verbatim to Supplementary S10; the V20 slope comparison stays in Section 6.4 --><!-- rev2 (CC-181 W8): condensed; original paragraph moved verbatim to Supplementary S10 -->

**Construct validity: precision rule.** OIV7 verifies presence or absence only for its labelled classes, and it can verify between 43.4% and 69.7% of the mapped mentions of each scored system (Supplementary S7). **Statistical conclusion validity: system dependence and multiplicity.** The intervals hold the systems fixed. Post hoc, the XM3600 reference-gold signs rest on one verbose system (Section 6.5); on PASCAL-50S and on the capped second backbone the slope is in effect a short-versus-detailed contrast, and no PASCAL-50S system-level interval can be computed (Sections 6.3–6.4). Registered predictions carry uncorrected intervals (Section 3.6), so across Table 1 some verdicts are expected by chance.<!-- rev1 (items 1, 6); rev2 (CC-181 W8): truncation paragraph folded in here; both originals moved verbatim to Supplementary S10 --><!-- rev2 (CC-181 W8): condensed; original paragraph moved verbatim to Supplementary S10 -->

**External validity.** (A3) holds only within a family: the COCO-5000 family uses one backbone (Qwen2.5-VL-7B), the second backbone is from the same Qwen-VL family, the English LVLM outputs add seven backbones but only decoding variants, and the PASCAL-50S family is seven public English LVLMs. THumB and Composite hold short pre-LVLM COCO captions, and for long LVLM descriptions LongCap-Arena and DOCCI-Critique are both MIXED, with one or two matched classes per caption. On short COCO captions judges were near count-neutral, so the verbosity sign is a property of the metric and its gold, not of human preference.<!-- rev2 (CC-181 W8): condensed; original paragraph moved verbatim to Supplementary S10 --><!-- rev2 (CC-181 W8): condensed; original paragraph moved verbatim to Supplementary S10 -->

## 9.2 Data, licences and overlaps

**UIT-OpenViIC and UIT-ViIC.** Both are used only through aggregate statistics and pointers (image identifiers and file hashes); no caption, n-gram or per-image panel of either appears in this paper or its release (UIT-OpenViIC aggregates: Table 5.1 and Supplementary S5). The UIT-OpenViIC authors confirmed this use in writing; we have not received written permission from the UIT-ViIC authors (Lam et al., 2020).<!-- PI-1026az: written acceptance from the UIT-OpenViIC authors obtained by the PI; per-image panels still not released --><!-- rev2 (CC-181 W8): condensed; original paragraph moved verbatim to Supplementary S10 -->

## 9.3 Broader impact

Count-controlled reporting can make some published improvements look smaller; we do not aim to discredit specific methods, report the full specification curve, and recommend a pre-declared control against selective use.
