**Construct validity: precision rule.** Verified precision is the preferred estimand because OIV7 verifies presence or absence only for its labelled classes, so a mention outside them is unknown, not wrong; OIV7 can verify between 43.4% and 69.7% of the mapped mentions of each scored system (stored descriptive values, Supplementary S7).<!-- rev1 (items 9a, 9b): src data/results/n_f1/bracket_result.json (4796ed12) :: sets.{s75,xm600}.verifiable_share.*.point (min 43.37 xm3600_zeroshot-detailed, max 69.71 xm3600_p1_s43-detailed; manifest rows keyed; no new pass) --> The positive label-gold slope on XM3600 depends on the verified-precision rule: counting unverifiable mentions as errors leaves its interval including 0 on the second backbone's XM3600-600 outputs (post hoc; Section 6.2). **Construct validity: vocabulary and matchers.** Instance gold is limited to COCO-80, so "complete" gold is complete only for that vocabulary. The Vietnamese lexicon was not perturbed (Supplementary S2 changes the English matcher and the XM3600 label map only), so its effect on the Vietnamese-output results is untested.<!-- PD-29 (c): R1-M1 and R1-M4 kept disclosed as untested sensitivities -->

**Statistical conclusion validity: system dependence and multiplicity.** The intervals of Table 6.1 hold the systems fixed. Post hoc, the XM3600 reference-gold signs rest on one verbose system, while the ordering and the label-gold sign survive every drop (Section 6.5). Registered predictions carry uncorrected intervals (Section 3.6), so across Table 1 some verdicts are expected by chance; the claim rests on the ordering, which passed on both backbones and failed its label-gold step on PASCAL-50S. On PASCAL-50S the intervals hold the 14 runs fixed, the slope is in effect a short-versus-detailed contrast, and no system-level interval can be computed because the caption files were deleted (Section 6.4).<!-- pass 3: multiplicity threat stated; no new number --><!-- rev1 (items 1, 6) -->

**External validity: systems and human-judgment regime.** (A3) holds only within a family: the COCO-5000 family uses one backbone (Qwen2.5-VL-7B), the second backbone is from the same Qwen-VL family, and the English LVLM outputs add seven backbones but only decoding variants, and the PASCAL-50S family is seven public English LVLMs, each with a short and a detailed prompt. THumB and Composite hold short English COCO captions from pre-LVLM systems, and Composite has one crowd rater per item. For long LVLM descriptions, LongCap-Arena (registered) and DOCCI-Critique (exploratory) are both MIXED, and on both the COCO-80 matcher finds only one or two classes per caption. On short COCO captions judges were near count-neutral, so the verbosity sign is a property of the metric and its gold, not of human preference.

## 9.3 Broader impact

Count-controlled reporting can make some published improvements look smaller. We do not aim to discredit specific methods, and we report the full specification curve; a pre-declared control guards against selective use of the tools.
