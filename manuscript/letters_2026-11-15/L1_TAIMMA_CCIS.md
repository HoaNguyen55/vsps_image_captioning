**Subject:** TAIMMA 2027: questions from prospective authors on the CCIS proceedings, withdrawal and a later journal version

Dear [recipient 1: General Chairs of TAIMMA 2027],
dear [recipient 2: Springer contact for the CCIS series],

We are preparing a full paper (12–15 pages, LNCS format) for the third call of TAIMMA 2027 (deadline 2 December 2026). Before we submit, we would be grateful for your answers to four questions.

1. **Proceedings.** The conference site states that accepted papers will be published in Springer's CCIS series. Springer's list of forthcoming CCIS proceedings (dated 2 November 2026) names TAIMMA 2027 with the dates 27–29 March 2027. The conference has since moved to 16–18 March 2027. Does the volume remain approved for CCIS under the new dates, and is that approval final or "in principle"?

2. **Withdrawal.** We found no withdrawal or refund rule on the conference site. May authors withdraw an accepted paper before the camera-ready and author-registration deadline (10 January 2027), in particular if the publisher of the proceedings changes or if the authors do not sign the licence to publish?

3. **Later journal version.** If the paper is accepted, we plan to submit an extended version later to a journal of a different publisher. Springer's licence-to-publish form (Singapore) allows an expanded version that includes at least 30% new material, states the incremental change, and references the first publication in the volume. Will this form apply to the TAIMMA 2027 volume, and do its conditions hold when the journal belongs to another publisher?

4. **Timing.** Is there any restriction on when the extended version may be submitted to the journal, for example only after the proceedings have appeared?

As the last deadline is close, a reply by 1 December 2026 would help us greatly. Each of you may of course answer only the questions that concern you.

Thank you for your time, and for organising the conference.

Yours sincerely,
[corresponding author], on behalf of the authors
[affiliation]
Copy: [principal investigator], [co-author]
