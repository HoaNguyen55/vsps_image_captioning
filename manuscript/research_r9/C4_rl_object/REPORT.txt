0. BOTTOM LINE
======================================================================================================================
- >= 90 IS NOT REACHABLE by this lever on any of KTVIC R, KTVIC F1 or COCO F1; KTVIC P >= 90 only by giving up recall.
  Deciding numbers (all measured here, section 2):
  - KTVIC R: the policy's own support (greedy + 6 samples, all classes they name) covers R 71.1 (fuse2) / 65.3 (P1);
    an oracle that KNEW 3 of the 5 test references and said exactly their classes reaches R 85.4 (4 of 5: 94.0);
    34.6% of gold classes are named by only 1 of the 5 annotators.
  - KTVIC F1: the in-sample oracle best-of-7 (reads the test refs) caps at 72.4 (fuse2) / 69.3 (P1).
  - KTVIC P: a precision-only reward reaches P 91.6 / 94.2 in-sample, but at R 52.5 / 45.0.
  - COCO F1 (instances only): the union of the 5 human EN captions gets 79.4; the oracle best-of-8 model outputs 80.5;
    the best published 80-class multi-label CLASSIFIER gets OF1 86.8 (Query2Label). Only appended object lists pass 90.
- The only route to >= 90 F1 is an object inventory: greedy + the missing GOLD classes appended gives KTVIC F1 93.2
  (P 88.0) at CIDEr 143.9 -> 95.2; on UIT-ViIC, first caption + missing instance classes gives instance F1 99.9 at
  syllable CIDEr 180.5 -> 95.4. That is metric gaming and needs the test gold; no reward can make a policy learn it.
- Realistic RL effect (cross-fit, transfer-scaled to the 5-ref metric, both pools agree):
  - CIDEr + object-F1 reward, z-weight 1-2: KTVIC F1 +1.6 to +2.7 over the CIDEr-only CN-RLOO arm (both moves, both
    pools), at CIDEr -1 to -5 vs that arm. Vs the SFT greedy (best-of-7 proxy): P +1.3 to +2.0, R +1.6 to +2.5,
    F1 +1.5 to +2.2 (policy-level KL step: F1 +4.3 to +5.3). In-sample upper bound F1 +11 to +15.
  - COCO short captions with an instance-label reward: about +4 to +7 instance F1 (fuse2 59.4 -> ~63-66); a selector
    learned from half the images' labels only matches zero-shot detailed (73.2 vs 73.3).
- Reward hacking (injection tests, section 4): with object F1 alone, a support inventory ("có A, B và C") is the group
  argmax on 30-34% of images; at z-weight 1 on 1.6-2.0%; at 2 on 8.8-9.3%; at 4 on 16.5-18.5%. Recall rewards couple to
  length (within-group Spearman +0.15 to +0.17). A list guard (>= 2 commas or > max-ref classes + 1) flags 58-67% of
  inventories, 0.84% of KTVIC refs and 0.7-2.6% of model outputs, but only 3-8% of single-object appends.
- Detector-evidence reward (Grounding-DINO COCO-80 tags, FLAGGED): harmful on KTVIC (tags vs union P 34.3 / R 22.0;
  selection F1 -2.7 to -2.9, CIDEr -13). Killed.
- Proposals (section 8): P-A union-F1 CN-RLOO (KTVIC; low novelty, ~4 GPU-h on top of gate v2's control);
  P-B instance-label verifiable-reward RLOO (COCO domain; moderate-low novelty, ~12.5 GPU-h). Neither reaches 90.

1. SETUP
======================================================================================================================
- Pools (TEST; estimates only, nothing selected or tuned on them): evaluate.py greedy + 6 samples at T = 0.8 for fuse2_s42
  (data/results/samples, md5 = B4 copy) and P1 s43 legacy (B4). Greedy reproduces the R9 numbers exactly: fuse2
  75.7 / 47.1 / 58.1, CIDEr 143.9; P1 s43 legacy 78.8 / 41.7 / 54.5.
- Metric: exact CHAIR-vi classes (objects_in) vs the union of the 5 references; micro P, image-mean R (object_prf.py).
  Word-level CIDEr-D = R8/T2 replica (asserted == pycocoevalcap), RDRSegmenter caches (no JVM started).
- Rewards (computed from the image's own refs, as an RL reward reads its training image's refs):
  C = CIDEr-D; F = per-image exact F1 vs the ref union; F2 = F-beta 2; R / P = recall / precision only;
  C+lam F = z(C) + lam z(F) within the group; C+1lex = R8's LEX-z (K2 term, hypernym credit); LIN = corpus-F1
  linearised at the greedy operating point.
- Moves: (i) KL step over the 6 on-policy samples, pi' ∝ exp(eta z(U)), KL = 1 nat per image, vs uniform sampling;
  (ii) best-of-7 argmax z(U) + 0.5 * 1[greedy] vs greedy (a greedy-decode proxy).
- Settings: in-sample (reward and score on all 5 refs: an UPPER bound, winner's curse included) and cross-fit (reward on
  refs {0,1}, score on {3,4}, and swapped; non-adjacent halves). Cross-fit deltas live on a 2-ref-union scale, so they
  are mapped to the 5-ref metric by the surviving fraction f = xf / in-sample(2-ref): est5 = f * in-sample(5-ref).
  For F1, f = 0.30-0.40 for the KL step and 0.11-0.21 for best-of-7 for every object rule, in both pools.
  Calibration: R6/B1 found the same pattern for CIDEr (in-sample best-of-k +57, held-out ~+8, SCST literature median
  +8.5), so cross-fit is the realistic estimate and in-sample the ceiling.

Hack captions injected as an 8th group member; reward from the image's 5 refs. "argmax%" = share of images where the
hack beats every real candidate and the greedy, for z(C) + lam z(F), lam = 0 / 0.5 / 1 / 2 / 4 / F-only (fuse2; P1 alike):
| hack | CIDEr | P / R / F1 | syl | argmax% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 support inventory "có A, B và C" | 18.5 | 57.6 / 69.9 / 63.1 | 8.2 | 0 / 0 / 1.6 / 9.3 / 16.5 / 30.3 |
| H2 GOLD inventory (oracle) | 30.3 | 100 / 97.6 / 98.8 | 7.2 | 1.1 / 2.7 / 17.6 / 58.4 / 76.2 / 88.9 |
| H3 greedy + all extra support classes | 83.5 | 57.8 / 70.5 / 63.5 | 16.6 | 0.2 / 1.8 / 7.0 / 13.3 / 20.6 / 30.6 |
| H4 greedy + 1 extra class (support >= 2) | 130.7 | 71.5 / 56.9 / 63.4 | 13.9 | 0.4 / 4.8 / 7.5 / 11.6 / 12.4 / 19.7 |
| H5 greedy + missing GOLD classes (oracle) | 95.2 | 88.0 / 99.1 / 93.2 | 16.3 | 1.3 / 7.5 / 29.0 / 62.5 / 73.7 / 80.8 |
(greedy: CIDEr 143.9, 58.1 F1, 12.4 syllables)
- Object F1 alone is hackable: inventories win on ~30% of images. At z-weight 1 the reachable inventory (H1) wins on
  1.6-2.0%; at 2 on 9%. The append-one-object drift (H4) wins on 7.5% at weight 1 and costs -13 CIDEr per object.
- Length: within-group Spearman(reward, syllables) = -0.06/+0.02 for CIDEr, +0.02/+0.08 for F1, +0.17/+0.15 for recall.
- List guard (advantage capped at 0, like the fragment guard): >= 2 commas or more classes than max over the image's refs
  + 1. Flags 0.84% of 18,845 KTVIC train refs (leave-one-out), 1.97 / 0.72% of greedy, 2.63 / 1.02% of samples;
  H1 67.4 / 57.9%, H3 58.6 / 50.2%, H4 7.5 / 2.7%. It stops lists, not the one-object drift: lam <= 1 and the CIDEr term
  must do that, and mentions/caption must be monitored (train_rl.py already logs it).

  object F1 58.2 -> 66.3, CIDEr 95.5 -> 99.2. Multiplying by CIDEr is its anti-list guard.
- SPIDEr, 1612.00370 (ICCV 2017): 0.5 SPICE + 0.5 CIDEr; SPICE alone produced repeated phrases.
- BalCapRL, 2605.07394 (arXiv): GRPO with precision, recall-vs-reference and a length-gated fluency reward,

  instances. COCO AP 16.1 -> 31.9 (the F1 term: 20.2 -> 27.6). GT-instance reward, but for box outputs.
- Visual-RFT, 2503.01785 (ICCV 2025): IoU / confidence rewards; COCO open-vocabulary novel mAP 9.8 -> 31.3.
- CHAIR-DPO, 2508.20181 (BMVC 2025): DPO, winner = lower CHAIR_i against DETR-detected COCO-80 gold. AMBER CHAIR

  (detector gold, precision only), SC-Captioner and BalCapRL (reference-unit P/R).
7e. Ceiling for COCO instance F1: the best verified 80-class multi-label classifier, Query2Label CvT-w24 22k 384 px
  (2107.10834, arXiv only), OF1 86.8 (OP 89.2 / OR 84.6, CF1 85.9); ASL TResNet-L (ICCV 2021) OF1 81.8. RAM (CVPRW 2024)
  tag P/R on COCO-80: Grounding-DINO 83.1 / 86.9 (~85.0 F1); caption-derived BLIP tags 67.0 / 39.0.
7f. Published object-reward gains vs ours: SC-Captioner +2.3 F1 on a strong LVLM; ECOL-R +8.1; PS-NOC +14 to +15
  (8 held-out classes, at -3 to -9 in-domain CIDEr). Our realistic KTVIC estimate (+1.6 to +2.7 over CIDEr-only RL)
  sits in SC-Captioner's range. Every recall-side reward needs a list / length guard (SPIDEr, BalCapRL, FeedQuill, CapRL).

8. PROPOSALS
======================================================================================================================
P-A  Union-F1 CN-RLOO (KTVIC). An ablation arm of gate v2, low novelty.
  Mechanism: train_rl.py unchanged (copy mask, EOS-aware CIDEr-D, fragment guard, KL 0.03, T and images as the gate v2
    first look). Reward r = CIDEr-D / s_C + lam * F1_union / s_F, lam = 1. F1_union = per-image F1 of the rollout's exact
    CHAIR-vi classes against the union of the TRAINING image's 5 refs (the R9 metric itself). s_C, s_F = mean
    within-group SDs on the G0 dev300 rollouts. List guard: advantage capped at 0 (like the fragment guard) when the
    caption has >= 2 commas or more classes than max over the image's refs + 1. The CIDEr-only first look is the control.
    Flag: the on-policy rollouts are model-generated (inside the method only); every reward label is a human reference.
  Novel element vs closest prior: vs PS-NOC 2109.05115 (object-F1 SCST on 8 held-out classes, box-derived targets),
    SC-Captioner 2508.06125 (reference-parsed set reward on self-correction edits), BalCapRL 2605.07394 (recall against
    reference units), SPIDEr 1612.00370, in-house K2 / R8 LEX-z: (i) F1 against the UNION of the references in the
    metric's own closed lexicon (not found in that form, 7d); (ii) lam set by a measured injection test (inventories
    win on 1.6-2.0% of groups at lam 1, 30-34% with F1 alone); (iii) a list guard inside the copy-neutral advantage.
    The reward family is published and in-house: say "ablation", not "contribution".
  Expected (sec. 3c; transfer-scaled cross-fit, both pools): KTVIC F1 +1.6 to +2.7 over the CIDEr-only arm. Vs the SFT
    greedy: P +1.3 to +2.0, R +1.6 to +2.5, F1 +1.5 to +2.2 (policy-level KL estimate up to +4.3 to +5.3). fuse2 ->
    ~77.5 / ~49 / ~60; P1 legacy -> ~80 / ~44 / ~56.5. Upper bound 72.4 F1. Truth side: object terms also raise the
    independent panel's F1 (cross-fit +0.5 to +1.3 over CIDEr-only; R8: LEX-z vs R0 +2.9 [+2.2, +3.6]). COCO: no direct
    effect; KTVIC-style drift may cut COCO recall further (measure it).
  CIDEr cost: -1 to -5 vs the CIDEr-only arm; syllables +0.0 to +0.3.
  GPU (one 3090): 1,000 images x k = 8 ~3.5 GPU-h (gate v2 unit cost) + ~0.4 h greedy eval = ~4 GPU-h per seed;
    2 seeds ~8. Full 3,469 images ~11 GPU-h per seed. The control is gate v2's first look (already budgeted).
  Kill (pre-register; dev300 only, evaluate.py greedy, gold = union of the dev images' 5 train refs):
    (1) object F1 of this arm minus the CIDEr-only arm < +1.5, or its 95% CI includes 0;
    (2) dev CIDEr-D more than 3 below the CIDEr-only arm;
    (3) mentions/caption above the refs' mean + 0.3, syllables +1.5, or list-guard hits on > 3% of dev greedy outputs;
    (4) dev panel object F1 below the CIDEr-only arm (CI < 0): then report it as typicality only.

P-B  Instance-label verifiable reward, ILV-RLOO (COCO domain). Moderate-low novelty.
  Mechanism: the same engine on COCO-2014 images with EXISTING human instance labels: UIT-ViIC train minus the 59
    coco_probe images (2,636 images; Vietnamese refs exist). r = CIDEr-D (UIT-ViIC refs) / s_C + lam * F1_inst / s_F,
    lam = 1, where F1_inst = F1 of the caption's COCO-80 classes (matcher v2) against the image's instance classes.
    List guard, KL to the SFT anchor. Option: mixed batches with KTVIC images under the P-A reward, so one adapter serves
    both benchmarks. No new labels and no detector; the matcher is the verifier. Flag: the on-policy rollouts are
    model-generated (inside the method only); every reward label is human.
  Novel element vs closest prior: Perception-R1 2504.07954 and Visual-RFT 2503.01785 use GT instances as verifiable
    rewards for BOX outputs; PS-NOC uses box-derived synthetic captions (8 classes); CHAIR-DPO 2508.20181 uses DETR gold
    for precision-only DPO; ECOL-R uses detector labels. New: human instance labels as a two-sided (P and R) verifiable
    reward for free-form captions, verified cross-lingually (Vietnamese caption, COCO-80 labels). None found in 62 queries.
  Expected: COCO instance F1, short prompt, +4 to +7 (fuse2 59.4 -> ~63-66). Detailed: 65.8 -> ~70-74. Evidence: the
    in-sample headroom over fuse2-short is +21.2 (oracle best-of-8) and KTVIC's surviving fractions are 0.18-0.35. R8/T2
    kNN stand-in for label-F1 SFT targets: instance F1 +12.0 / +13.4, EN-caption F1 +6.6 / +7.3. A selector learned from
    labels only reaches zero-shot detailed (73.2 vs 73.3). Bounds: the human one-sentence ceiling is 70.6 (VI best-of-5)
    / 70.5 (EN); the 5 EN captions together reach 79.4. KTVIC object F1: ~0 expected.
  CIDEr cost: UIT-ViIC budget -3 to -8. Object-richer human-style sentences are nearly free (-1.1 syllable CIDEr for
    +11.7 F1), but label-F1 SFT targets cost -7.9 / -11.5 (R8/T2) and one appended class costs -52.5. If mixed with
    KTVIC: the KTVIC CIDEr risk is unknown (kill 5).
  GPU: 2,636 images x k = 8 ~9.2 GPU-h; eval ~3.5 GPU-h (coco_probe short + detailed ~2.7, UIT-ViIC val+test ~0.4,
    KTVIC dev/test ~0.4). Total ~12.5 GPU-h; a 1,000-image first look ~4 GPU-h with UIT-ViIC val eval.
    Prep: instances_train2014.json is not on n2; UIT-ViIC's labels (data/uitviic/instance_gold.json) suffice.
  Kill (UIT-ViIC VAL only; coco_probe scored once, at the end):
    (1) val instance F1 minus SFT < +3, or its CI includes 0;
    (2) val EN-caption F1 gain with CI including 0 (it only learned the instrument);
    (3) val UIT-ViIC CIDEr-D cost > 3 at lam 1;
    (4) list-guard hits > 3%, or syllables +3;
    (5) if mixed with KTVIC: KTVIC dev CIDEr cost > 3.

Not proposed: object F1 alone (inventory hack, 30-34% of groups); precision-only (P 91-94 only at R 45-53);
recall-weighted F2 or corpus-linearised F1 (tie F1); detector-tag rewards (harmful, sec. 6); KG or synonym credit (R7).
Stacking: P-A at train time plus UPE-MBR at decode time (R8/T3, on GPU now) is unmeasured.

9. CAN THIS LEVER REACH >= 90?
======================================================================================================================
- KTVIC P: only alone; P-only reward reaches 91.6 / 94.2 in-sample but at R 52.5 / 45.0. With an F1 reward the realistic
  P is ~77-80 (fuse2 75.7 +1.7 to +2.0; P1 78.8 +1.3 to +1.4); in-sample ceiling 89.7 / 92.1. Jointly with R: NO.
- KTVIC R: NO. Own support 71.1 / 65.3; an oracle that knew 3 of the 5 refs 85.4; R >= 90 needs > 3 annotators' choices.
- KTVIC F1: NO. In-sample oracle within support 72.4 / 69.3; realistic ~60 (fuse2) / ~56.5 (P1 legacy).
- COCO F1: NO. Union of 5 human EN captions 79.4; oracle best-of-8 80.5; best multi-label classifier OF1 86.8; realistic
  RL ~63-66 short, ~70-74 detailed.

======================================================================================================================
10. CAVEATS
======================================================================================================================
- Proxies are one-step moves on test pools; real RL compounds over many steps and can drift beyond the sample support
  (that is where both the extra recall and the inventory hack live). Effect sizes are proxy sizes.
- The surviving fraction f is estimated with a 2-ref chooser; a 5-ref training reward is less noisy (f may be higher),
  while train-to-test generalisation is weaker than same-image cross-fit (f may be lower).
- P1 pool = legacy seed 43 adapter (78.8 / 41.7 / 54.5), not the response-only P1.
- Hack captions are segmented by joining lexicon compounds, not by RDRSegmenter (no JVM): CIDEr of hacks is approximate.
- COCO pools are cross-system greedy outputs; no single-model COCO sample pool exists, so the COCO transfer fraction is
  borrowed from KTVIC.
- KTVIC test numbers are estimates; nothing was selected on test. Any GPU run must select on dev300 / UIT-ViIC val only.
