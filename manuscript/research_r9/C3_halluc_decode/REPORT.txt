==========================================================================================================================
0. VERDICT
==========================================================================================================================
Can the precision lever reach >= 90? KTVIC P: NO. KTVIC R and F1: NO. COCO F1: NO. The ceilings below are measured on our data.

(1) On KTVIC, precision is not a hallucination problem.
  - The OpenViIC panel confirms 56.5% [49.2, 63.6] of P1's KTVIC false positives as real (test, second_panel.json).
    On clean dev300 it confirms 56.6%.
  - A PERFECT, free remover of every mention neither human panel names (recall unchanged) gives:
    - P1_s43-short: P 78.7 -> 86.7 (test, from the published aggregates);
    - P1_dev: 74.0 -> 83.4 (dev300, measured).
  - Removing only P1's excess over a held-out human (4.1 points of mentions, same 1v4 gold) gives P 82.1.
  - One human caption scores P 85.3 against the other 4 KTVIC annotators.
(2) Error anatomy of P1-short's KTVIC false positives (21.2% of mentions):
  - 12.3 points are scene or stuff nouns no reference names (đường, quầy hàng, cửa, vỉa hè, sông ...);
  - 5.5 points are a different person noun from the one the references chose;
  - 1.2 points are other granularity mismatches;
  - 1.7 points are COCO-80 objects (the error class that CHAIR/POPE/AMBER methods target);
  - 0.6 points are a person when no reference names one.
  A Grounding-DINO verifier (Woodpecker/HALC style) has almost nothing to remove: 9 of 1,017 mentions, dP +0.0 [-0.2, +0.3].
(3) The best label-free veto (support among the model's own samples, AUROC 0.71-0.73 against KTVIC refs):
  - +1.8 P at F1 +0.0 (frozen rule, test);
  - +6.1 P at -3.0 F1 (majority rule).
  - Reaching KTVIC P >= 90 needs every sample to agree: P 90.5 at R 16.4, F1 27.8 (P1_s43); P 90.6 at R 21.9 (fuse2).
  - The F1-optimal veto (Lipton 2014: drop iff calibrated p < F/2 = 0.27-0.29) removes only the support-0 bin, about 3% of mentions.
(4) VCD as already run in the repo (zero-shot Qwen2.5-VL-7B) gives no KTVIC precision:
  - short: dP -1.4 [-4.0, +1.2], dR -4.9, dF1 -4.3, CIDEr 29.0 -> 26.1;
  - detailed: dP +0.3 [-0.7, +1.4].
  On COCO it buys precision with recall: detailed P +1.5 [+0.5, +2.4], R -4.0, F1 -1.9 (instance gold).
(5) COCO (instance gold):
  - P is already 89.5 (P1-short) / 90.9 (fuse2-short).
  - 56-62% of the remaining false positives are two furniture classes (bàn -> dining table, ghế -> chair). This is lexical
    scope and label gaps, not hallucination.
  - A perfect hallucination remover gives P 95.9-96.6 and F1 +1.3 to +2.0.
  - F1 at P = 100 would still be only 61.4 (P1-short), 68.6 (fuse2-detailed) or 80.6 (zero-shot detailed).
    Recall (44-67) decides COCO F1, not precision.
(6) Literature on Qwen2.5-VL-7B: training-free methods cut CHAIR_i by 0-2.8 points (relative 0-30%).
  - They often cost recall or AMBER Cover: OPERA derived R 58.2 -> 48.5; PAI Cover -6.3; ICT Cover -2.1.
  - Sample-validate pipelines cut more (SelfVal CHAIR_i 9.9 -> 4.1) but lose CHAIR-F1 (-1.7).
  - On short-caption models the CIDEr cost is real: VCD NoCaps CIDEr 103.9 -> 69.0; LURE COCO CIDEr 138.4 -> 0.0.
  Applied to our setting, these methods can at best touch the 1.7 + (unknown share of 12.3) points above.
Recommendation: do not spend GPU on hallucination decoding for the R9 targets.
- If a recall lever is adopted (UPE-MBR, CC-ADD, fusion), add proposal A as a free precision-repair stage. It reuses the
  UPE-MBR sample jobs.
- Proposal B only matters if COCO precision is itself a claimed result.

==========================================================================================================================
4. FEASIBILITY FOR OUR 4-bit QLoRA Qwen2.5-VL-7B, VIETNAMESE OUTPUT
==========================================================================================================================
- Logit-contrast methods (VCD, ICD, M3ID, MaskCD amateur):
  - These run today in the scripts/baselines.py step loop (two KV-cached streams, M-RoPE-safe), at 2-2.4x cost.
  - Our run and third-party Qwen2.5-VL tables say about 0. Rethinking-CD (research_wall A20) says greedy already beats most
    CD on Qwen2.5-VL.
- Attention and hidden-state interventions (PAI, SPIN, ICT, VTI, MemVR, ReVisiT): need forward hooks or eager attention.
  Memory is fine with max_pixels capped.
  - ReVisiT ships Qwen2.5-VL code and costs 1.0x.
  - VTI, Nullu and ICT steering vectors must be recomputed on merged weights.
- OPERA: 6-8x cost; portability to Qwen2.5-VL is contested (SAKED vs ResDec). Do not run.
- Detector-based (HALC, Woodpecker): GDINO tags already exist for KTVIC test. Measured: nothing to remove (2d).
- Self-verification (LURE, VOLCANO, Woodpecker-style VQA):
  - LURE's revisor rewrites short captions into long ones (CIDEr 138 -> 0 in MOCHa).
  - Our model's POPE yes-recall of 71-82% means many true mentions would be vetoed.
- Vietnamese specifics:
  - Token-level methods are language-agnostic.
  - Mention-level methods need spans. rescap.chair.objects_in gives them but covers only the 113-class lexicon (about 42% of
    noun mentions, K2). Multi-syllable classes need span-to-token alignment for rollback.
  - NF4 and bf16 greedy differ on 55% of images (R6 F5), so every comparison must use the deployed quantisation.
- E2 probe:
  - AUROC 0.807 measures SALIENCE (named by references), not existence, on the zero-shot model's Stage-1 proposals.
  - Its edge over the class prior is +0.10.
  - On P1's own mentions the comparable label-free signal reaches 0.71-0.73 (2e), and R6 found the probe equal to the vote veto.

==========================================================================================================================
6. PROPOSALS (two at most; neither is a breakthrough)
==========================================================================================================================
A. FOPR -- F1-optimal precision repair, a salience-calibrated mention veto stacked on the chosen recall lever.
  Mechanism:
  - For each lexicon class the caption names, estimate q = P(named by the KTVIC annotators | image, class) from support among
    k same-model samples. Calibrate with isotonic regression on dev300, using KTVIC-train refs as labels (training labels,
    allowed). Optionally add the E2 probe and the class prior.
  - Veto iff q < F_hat/2, where F_hat is the system's dev F1. This is the F1-optimal decision rule (Lipton et al., 1402.1892).
    There is no tuned operating point.
  - Realise the veto by switching to the nearest candidate (CIDEr-D similarity) that lacks the class. If none exists, roll back
    and ban the class (bad_words_ids) and re-decode.
  - Apply after UPE-MBR, CC-ADD or fusion. Their recall-seeking edits lower KTVIC precision: UPE on fuse2 gives panel P -2.1
    and KTVIC CHAIR_i +1.6/+2.4 (R8 T3).
  Novel element vs closest prior:
  - TLC (2305.07021) uses a validation-tuned threshold on token confidence for existence.
  - SelfVal (2601.22451) validates existence with extra LVLM queries and selects or aggregates; on Qwen2.5-VL-7B it costs
    CHAIR-F1 -1.7 to -3.8.
  - VGD (2607.27823) uses tuned @rec operating points. SelfCheckGPT (2303.08896) does sample consistency on text.
  - New here:
    (i) the posterior is SALIENCE, not existence, justified by the two-panel measurement (56% of KTVIC false positives are real);
    (ii) a closed-form F/2 threshold with a falsifiable prediction, already confirmed: on precision-heavy P1 only about 3% of
        mentions fall below F/2 (F1-neutral), while recall-heavy outputs carry more such mass (F1-positive);
    (iii) evidence of matched style is required (2f).
  - It is the precision complement of UPE-MBR, not a replacement. Novelty: moderate-low.
  Evidence (CPU):
  - test frozen rule: P +1.8 [+1.0, +2.6], R -0.4, F1 +0.0;
  - fuse2-detailed repair: P +1.3, F1 +0.4 [+0.2, +0.7] (cross-system pool);
  - COCO: P +1.9 [+1.4, +2.4], F1 0.0;
  - realised-caption cost on dev (R8 T1 3b): KTVIC CIDEr -0.5 [-2.6, +1.6].
  Expected effect:
  - KTVIC P +1.5 to +3 on P1-type output and +3 to +5 after a recall lever; R -0.3 to -2; F1 0 to +0.5;
  - COCO P +1.5 to +2, F1 +/-0.3;
  - CIDEr -0.5 to -1.5.
  GPU: 0 extra if it reuses the UPE-MBR sample jobs (36_z dev, T 0.5/0.8; 50_f KTVIC test; 52_c UIT-ViIC). Otherwise about
  3.5 h on one 3090 (6 samples at ~0.6 s each: 558 + 300 KTVIC, 2,500 COCO).
  Kill (pre-register before any output):
  - on dev300 (36_z, T 0.8), FOPR on greedy AND on the UPE-MBR pick: KTVIC-ref F1 delta lower CI < -0.3, or dP < +1.5;
  - realised KTVIC CIDEr cost worse than -1.5;
  - on 52_c (UIT-ViIC, instance gold): F1 lower CI < -0.5;
  - FOPR minus a matched random veto (same count): dP CI includes 0.
B. VGD-vi -- instance-supervised mention verifier with rollback, for COCO precision only.
  Mechanism:
  - Fit an L1-logistic verifier on features read at Vietnamese COCO-80 mention spans: image-attention mass, sink mass, token
    entropy, layer-24 hidden state.
  - Labels are COCO-2014 instance presence on train2014 images (existing human labels; training only). Choose tau on a
    train2014 hold-out, never on test.
  - At decode, if risk > tau, roll back to the mention start, ban the class lemma and its coco80_vi synonyms, and continue.
  Novel element vs VGD (2607.27823; English; LLaVA and Qwen2-VL):
  - transfer to a Vietnamese 4-bit QLoRA captioner with multi-syllable span alignment;
  - an evaluation that separates lexical-scope false positives (bàn / ghế, 56-62%) from hallucinations.
  - Novelty: low.
  Expected effect:
  - COCO P +1.5 to +2.5 (oracle 95.9; non-furniture "neither" false positives are about 4% of mentions), R -0.5 to -1,
    F1 +/-0.5;
  - KTVIC P +0 to +0.5 (COCO-type false positives are 1.7% of mentions; GDINO veto +0.0);
  - CIDEr about -2 (VGD @rec90, NoCaps).
  GPU: about 2 h (features on about 2k train2014 images, 0.5 h; rollback decoding on 2,500 COCO + 558 KTVIC at 1.3x greedy,
  about 1.2 h).
  Kill: COCO instance dP < +1.5, F1 CI < 0, or KTVIC CIDEr worse than -1.
Not proposed:
- VCD, ICD or M3ID on P1 (expected about 0; measured on zero-shot).
- OPERA (cost, portability).
- Woodpecker, LURE or VOLCANO (style-destroying rewrite; no COCO-object errors to fix).
- GDINO veto (measured +0.0).
- Person-noun choice by annotator mode (greedy already picks the mode; gaming risk).
- Mention-order salience (AUROC 0.53-0.58).

==========================================================================================================================
7. CAVEATS
==========================================================================================================================
- Test two-panel ceilings come from published aggregates. "Unconfirmed" is an upper bound on hallucination, because the panels
  cover about 84% of nameable classes. The human-level ceiling assumes the human's unconfirmed 6% is real objects.
- The test veto rows use R8's "final reporting only" pools with a dev-frozen rule. Test curves are descriptive, not selections.
- The cross-system pools in 2f and 2g are ensembles of trained systems, not same-model samples. The same-model sample effect
  (2e) is smaller.
- The binormal simulation is a model: its AUROC-0.70 row (+2.3 P at -2 R) matches the measured veto only roughly.
- The COCO furniture analysis is diagnostic. Changing the matcher or dropping classes would be metric gaming.
- Literature numbers are not comparable across papers (tokens, prompts, CHAIR implementations). Compare rows only within one
  table.
