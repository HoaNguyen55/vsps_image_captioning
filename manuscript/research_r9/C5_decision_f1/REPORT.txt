1. SETUP AND VALIDITY
==========================================================================================================================
- Metric: scripts/object_prf.py, applied exactly.
  - P = sum|M & G| / sum|M| (micro);
  - R = mean over images with G != {} of |M & G| / |G| (macro);

  - KTVIC gold = union of objects_in over the 5 references (113-entry lexicon, exact classes);
  - COCO = score_coco_probe.vi_objects (matcher v2) against COCO-2014 instance classes only.

    - 6 samples at T=0.5 and 6 at T=0.8: n2 samples/p1dev_s42-dev300-short-T{0.5,0.8}.jsonl, the 36_z step-A outputs, md5
      d61cf31a... / bc47ae89...; copied read-only after the job finished. Only KTVIC-reference endpoints are computed here.
      The UPE pre-registration's panel endpoints were never touched, and its frozen T* rule is unaffected.
  - Test (ESTIMATES): p1_s43 and fuse2_s42, each greedy + 6 samples at T=0.8 (md5 = n2).
  - COCO: no single-model sample pool exists. The committee is the greedy captions of 9 systems on the 2,500 manifest images.
- Segmentation: RDRSegmenter + lower(), with the sibling caches. 2,166 + 626 new strings were segmented into
  cache/seg_c5.json (VnCoreNLP-1.2, JVM capped at 384 MB). Greedy dev CIDEr-D was recomputed as 144.3, which equals S0/T3.
- Calibrator:
  - L2 logistic regression on 6 features: vote share among samples v, greedy flag g, v*g, logit class prior (KTVIC train
    minus dev300 reference unions), v*prior, intercept;
  - target y = class in the union of the 5 references;

- Dev numbers are 5-fold cross-fitted over images. The calibrator, lam, the EUM threshold and the (lam_u, margin) of the
  selection rules are fitted on 4 folds and applied to the 5th.
- Test numbers use everything fitted on the matched dev pool (P1_dev, 6 samples, T=0.8), frozen. Nothing is chosen on test.
- Statistics: paired image bootstrap, B = 2000, 95% percentile CIs (T3's boot_prf / boot_mean_diff, imported).

==========================================================================================================================
2. THEORY: THE BAYES ACT FOR THIS METRIC (micro precision, macro recall)
==========================================================================================================================
- Plug-in rule (linearisation at the operating point, exact as N grows).
  - Adding class o to image i changes TP by y, sum|M| by 1, and R by y / (N |G_i|).
  - With F_P = 2R^2/(P+R)^2 and F_R = 2P^2/(P+R)^2, the expected gain has the sign of
        R^2 (q_o - P) / m  +  P^2 E[y_o / |G_i|],    m = mentions per image.
  - So the Bayes act is: include o iff
        q_o + kappa * E[y_o / |G_i|] >= P*,    kappa = P*^2 m / R*^2.
  - (P*, R*, m) are the EXPECTED operating point under the posterior, found by a fixed-point iteration (no labels).
  - E[y_o / |G_i|] is computed exactly: independent classes plus Poisson(lam) gold outside the pool, by Poisson-binomial
    convolution.
- Special cases:
  - If recall were micro, the rule collapses to q_o >= F*/2, which is Lipton et al.'s threshold. The per-image 1/|G| term
    is what the paper's MACRO recall adds: images with few gold classes get a lower threshold.
  - The exact expected-F maximisers are exact for ONE F computed over ONE label set: Chai 2005, Jansche 2007, Ye et al.
    2012 (DTA: a top-k set under independence) and Dembczynski et al. 2011 (GFM, no independence needed). Applied per
    image they optimise INSTANCE-averaged F1, not this corpus metric. Measured: the per-image exact rule is <= the corpus
    plug-in on all 5 pools, by 0.2-0.7 F1 (3c).
- What the theory predicts, and what was confirmed:
  - The F1-optimal operating point names ~3 classes per image (fixed point m = 2.9-3.4), against 1.76 (P1 greedy) and
    2.09 (fuse2). fuse2-detailed (3.15) already sits there.
  - At the optimum P* ~ 0.61-0.66: the optimum TRADES precision for recall. CONFIRMED (3c).
  - The macro term changes F1 by -0.3 to +0.3 (n.s.) but raises P by +1.1 to +2.3 (CI > 0) at equal F1 (p14). It is
    correct, and empirically neutral on F1.
  - UPE-MBR is an instance of the same decision restricted to ONE existing candidate per image. It uses a
    ratio-of-expectations EF1 and an uncalibrated union-transform posterior (q = 1 - (1 - v)^5, E|G| = 6). The theory
    predicts that the candidate restriction, not the posterior, binds; calibrating UPE's posterior should therefore not
    move the selection frontier much. CONFIRMED (3d).

==========================================================================================================================
4. CAN THIS LEVER REACH >= 90?
==========================================================================================================================
- KTVIC P: NO, at any useful recall. On the calibrated plan's PR curve, P >= 90 leaves R <= 26.2 (P1 test) or 22.0 (fuse2).
- KTVIC R: NO.
  - Recall is capped by pool coverage: 62-71% (6 samples), 74% (20 samples), 81% (30 systems).
  - KTVIC's own annotators reach R 46.9 (one caption) and 66.6 (two fused) [R4_01].
- KTVIC F1: NO.
  - Best calibrated plan: 64.9 (frozen) / 65.2 (in-sample). Pool oracle with P = 100: 79.0-83.1.
  - 90 needs AUROC >= ~0.99 together with coverage >= ~85%.
  - The independent OpenViIC panel as evidence predicts the KTVIC set at F1 45.2 (one caption) to 54.8 (>= 2 of 5) on dev
    (p7). Human annotators of KTVIC: 60.5 / 70.9.
- COCO F1: NO. The union of 5 human captions scores 79.5 against instances, the committee oracle 86.9, and the best decision
  75.4 (set) / 74.3 (realised, fresh images).
- Scoring >= 90 against these golds would need near-oracle knowledge of what the annotators NAMED. Inventory-style
  captions that list detector classes are metric gaming, and on KTVIC they would still fail: G-DINO tags are only 25%
  KTVIC-precise. They also destroy the caption: two-sentence captions already cost CIDEr 143.9 -> 23.9.

==========================================================================================================================
5. PROPOSALS
==========================================================================================================================
M1. PLAN-F1: metric-Bayes object planning, then faithful realisation (KTVIC; COCO arm via M2).
  Mechanism.
    (1) Posterior. The model's k = 12 samples (T 0.5 / 0.8) plus greedy give per-class vote share, greedy flag and
        train-reference class prior. Optional: the E2 layer-24 probe P(co) for each pooled class. A logistic calibrator
        fitted on dev300 (P1_dev pools, never test) predicts "class is in the union of the references".
    (2) Decision. The closed-form plug-in for micro-P / macro-R F1, with no labels at test:
        include o iff q_o + kappa E[y_o/|G|] >= P*, (P*, R*, m) the posterior's own fixed point, capped at 3 classes.
    (3) Realiser. A control-SFT adapter trained with P1's recipe on KTVIC train minus dev300, with the prompt suffix
        "Doi tuong: o1, o2, o3" set to objects_in(target), so it learns to realise a given list in one sentence. At test the
        list is the plan from (2).
  Novel element vs closest prior (ids in section 6):
    - Content selection is the Bayes act for the EVALUATION metric. It uses a posterior of being NAMED BY THE REFERENCE
      PANEL (union of describers), calibrated from the model's own samples. This contrasts with:
      - detector confidence or top-k tags: Fang CVPR'15 word detectors -> LM; Anderson EMNLP'17 CBS; Neural Baby Talk
        CVPR'18; Show-Control-Tell CVPR'19;
      - existence validation for precision: SelfVal 2601.22451 (arXiv, verified in R8); Woodpecker; LURE.
    - The plug-in rule for the mixed micro-P / macro-R metric generalises Lipton ECML-PKDD'14's F*/2 rule.
      Koyejo NeurIPS'14/'15 and Narasimhan NeurIPS'14 treat micro, macro and instance averaging separately.
      The exact expected-F maximisers (Jansche ACL'07, Ye ICML'12 DTA, Dembczynski NeurIPS'11 / ICML'13) are exact for
      one F over one label set. Applied per image they target instance-F, the wrong target here: per-image exact EF1 is
      below the corpus rule on all 5 pools, by 0.2-0.7 F1.
    - vs in-house UPE / CC-ADD: those select among candidates and are bounded by the selection frontier (CL ties UPE).
      The plan moves past that frontier: +2.6 to +5.2 F1 over UPE at plan level, CI > 0.
    - vs the nearest decoding-time precision/recall controllers: Manas et al. ICCV'25 (2508.11616) hand-set a w dial
      whose best value differs by benchmark; PatchGate (2608.21819) uses a heuristic logit push/pull.
      PLAN-F1 instead derives the operating point (about 3 classes, P* 0.61-0.66) from the metric and a calibrated
      posterior, with no labels at test.
  Expected effect:
    - KTVIC test (plan level = upper bound of the realised effect), from the fuse2 pool and the P1 pool:
      | plan | fuse2 pool P / R / F1 (dF1) | P1 pool P / R / F1 (dF1) |
      |---|---|---|
      | cap 3 | 70.6 / 56.8 / 63.0 (+4.9) | 71.1 / 53.9 / 61.3 (+6.8) |
      | uncapped | 65.5 / 64.3 / 64.9 (+6.8) | 66.5 / 59.6 / 62.9 (+8.3) |
    - With 12 instead of 6 samples: about +1-2 more (p5).
    - With a probe-level posterior (AUROC 0.82-0.86, if it adds to the votes): +2 to +5 more (p10). That is still below 75.
    - Realised = plan x fidelity. CommonGen-type fidelity of 95-97% would keep most of it. Unmeasured.
    - COCO: F1 <= +2 (75.4 set level).
  CIDEr cost:
    - unmeasured; budget -5;
    - cap 3 keeps the target in the reference distribution (3-class references: CIDEr-LOO 192 vs 182-187);
    - risk: realising wrong classes as fluent false statements (priced into the plan's P, not into CIDEr).
  GPU (one 3090):

    - 12 test samples from the same base: ~1.1 GPU-h (R6: 3.6 s/img per 6 samples). Dev already has 20 samples.
    - realisation decode, dev300 + test: ~0.4 GPU-h;
    - optional probe extraction: ~0.5 GPU-h.

  Kill (pre-register before any decode; all on dev300 with the train-minus-dev300 control adapter):
    K1 fidelity: realised captions name < 85% of the planned classes, or add > 0.3 unplanned classes per caption;
    K2 realised KTVIC dev object F1 gain over greedy < +3.0 or its CI includes 0, or gain over UPE-frozen < +1.0.
       (Plan level on dev T0.8 x6, cap 3: +3.7 over greedy and +2.1 over UPE; 12-20 samples add about 1-2.)
    K3 KTVIC dev CIDEr-D < greedy - 5, or BLEU-4 < greedy - 3.
    Test runs once, everything frozen. Report negative if test dF1 < +2 or CIDEr < greedy - 8.

M2. Sentence-level plan realisation for multi-sentence captions (the COCO arm; incremental).
  Mechanism: a committee posterior (vote counts over existing captions).
    - FILTER: drop a sentence of the detailed caption when every class it names has < 2 votes.
    - ADD: append a whole sentence from another member for each missing class with >= 2 votes, if all of that sentence's
      classes have >= 2 votes.
    Text edits are whole model sentences, so no generation is needed.
  Novel element:
    - Atomic Self-Consistency (2405.13131) merges sample sub-parts for long-form QA recall.
    - Conformal factuality (Mohri & Hashimoto ICML'24) filters claims for precision.
    - M2 uses ADD + DROP, set by the F1-optimal (recall-seeking) decision, measured against human instance truth. Low
      novelty.
  Measured effect:
    - COCO F1 +1.8 [+1.3, +2.3] on the main 2,500 (k picked there);
    - +0.9 [+0.5, +1.3] on FRESH images with the rule frozen (5 members);
    - P +1.3 to -0.1, R +0.6 to +3.1.
  KTVIC: not applicable to one-sentence captions. Two-sentence captions already cost ~120 CIDEr.
  CIDEr cost: not measurable on COCO (no Vietnamese references on those images).
  GPU: 0.
    - Calibrating on COCO train2014 (allowed) would need committee captions on train images (~4-5 GPU-h).
    - Adding the KTVIC-trained members on the other 2,500 images would need ~4.8 GPU-h of p1/fuse2 decodes.
    Neither is recommended for +1-2 F1.
  Kill / status: the rule was pre-stated in p18 and replicated on fresh images (+0.9 [+0.5, +1.3]), so it passes,
    narrowly. Keep it as an appendix analysis, not a headline method.
NOT PROPOSED:
  - Calibrated expected-F1 MBR / exact-EF1 selection as a stand-alone method: it ties UPE at matched CIDEr (3d).
  - Committee-of-adapters selection: n.s.
  - A retrieval prior: +0 to +1.
  - Count-only control: vote-blind counting loses precision (T3 3c).
  - Precision-targeted decisions: P >= 90 leaves R ~ 22-26.

6a. Decision theory for F-measures. These are the parts we take; the micro/macro closed form is the only new piece.
- Lewis SIGIR'95 (10.1145/215206.215366) and Chai SIGIR'05 (10.1145/1076034.1076144): the probability ranking principle
  and the exact expected F.
- Jansche ACL'07 (P07-1093): maximum-expected-utility decoding for F.
- Ye, Chai, Lee, Chieu ICML'12 (1206.4625): EUM and DTA are "asymptotically equivalent given large training and test
  sets". Reuters macro-F1 with LR, C >= 1: thresholding 36.6 -> DTA 39.9.
- Dembczynski et al. NIPS'11: the exact GFM algorithm.
- Dembczynski et al. ICML'13 (PMLR v28): plug-in EFP vs binary relevance, instance F1 Scene 55.73 -> 74.44 and Enron
  55.49 -> 61.04.
- Waegeman et al. JMLR'14 (1310.4849).
- Lipton, Elkan, Naryanaswamy ECML-PKDD'14 (1402.1892): with calibrated probabilities the optimal threshold is F*/2.
- Koyejo et al. NIPS'14 and NIPS'15, Narasimhan et al. NIPS'14: consistent plug-in rules for generalised and multilabel
  (micro/macro/instance) metrics.
- Wu & Zhou ICML'17 (1609.00288).
- Nowozin CVPR'14 (10.1109/CVPR.2014.77): Bayes-optimal decisions for IoU, a CV precedent for "decide for the metric".
- Our data agree with Ye'12: EUM ~= DTA (3c).
- New here: the closed form for the MIXED micro-P / macro-R metric, i.e. the E[y/|G_i|] term with its label-free fixed
  point, and its use for caption content selection. The per-image exact maximisers target instance-F and are
  0.2-0.7 F1 worse here.

6d. Select-then-realise and controlled decoding (the closest prior art).
- Fang et al. CVPR'15 (1411.4952). Word detectors are calibrated to held-out PRECISION and cut at one global threshold
  tau (value not reported), then an ME LM plus DMSM writes the caption. COCO c5: BLEU-4 29.1, CIDEr 91.2.
  -> Their selection rule is a precision threshold. Ours is the metric's Bayes act on a named-by-panel posterior.
- Anderson et al. EMNLP'17, CBS (1612.00576). Forcing the top-4 tagger tags raises held-out mention F1 0 -> 54.0 (DCC
  split, 8 classes; DCC 39.8, NOC 49.1). In-domain CIDEr falls 93.0 -> 86.3.
  -> Fixed k and uncalibrated ranks; F1 is only the evaluation. CBS is a candidate realiser for us.
- Neural Baby Talk CVPR'18 (1803.09845), Show-Control-Tell CVPR'19 (1811.10652), Hokamp & Liu ACL'17 (1704.07138) and
  NeuroLogic NAACL'21 (2010.12884) are realisation machinery, reused rather than claimed.
- Manas et al. ICCV'25 (2508.11616, MRGD). Separate object-precision and object-recall reward models, mixed by a user
  weight w. LLaVA-1.5-7B on COCO:
  | w | C_i | recall |
  |---|---|---|
  | greedy | 15.05 | 81.30 |
  | 1.0 | 4.53 | 76.04 |
  | 0.25 | 7.67 | 81.56 |
  AMBER coverage 49.3 -> 63.8 (w 0.25). The best w differs by benchmark (about 0.25 for COCO, 1.0 for AMBER).
  -> The nearest controller. It exposes a dial; PLAN-F1 computes the metric-optimal operating point from a calibrated
     posterior, without labels.
- PatchGate (2608.21819, preprint Aug 2026). An intrinsic object inventory pushes logits up or down. AMBER coverage
  49.4 -> 56.0, CHAIR 7.5 -> 6.6.
  -> A heuristic push/pull, with no F1 decision.
- Precision-only claim filtering:

  -> All pay recall by deletion. Our metric rewards recall: at the operating point dF1/dR >> dF1/dP.

==========================================================================================================================
7. CAVEATS
==========================================================================================================================
- The set-level numbers are PLANS, not captions. Their realised value needs M1's realiser.
  - Fidelity is the main unknown: the existing adapters ignore prompt lists.
  - Hallucinated plan classes become fluent false statements. They are counted in P, not in CIDEr or human preference.
- Test pools come from models trained on all KTVIC train, at T = 0.8 with 6 samples. The calibrator transferred from P1_dev
  (dev, T = 0.8) with ECE 0.03-0.06. Test numbers are ESTIMATES. Nothing was selected on test except where marked (COCO k).
- The dev T0.5/T0.8 pools are step-A outputs of the UPE pre-registration (D9). They are used here for KTVIC-reference
  endpoints only, after the job had finished. The frozen T* rule and the panel endpoints are untouched.
- Committees mix systems and pipelines. Their vote shares are uncalibrated, and no clean dev committee exists (every
  adapter except P1_dev trained on dev300).
- The COCO sentence rule's k was picked on the main images. Only the fresh-image replication is clean.
- Everything uses the 113-entry lexicon (it sees about 42% of noun mentions, K2) and exact class matching.
- Model samples, votes and the probe are inside the method only. They are never labels and never evaluation evidence.
- Licence probe. p7 reads OpenViIC captions at run time and prints numbers only. Of 21,535 panel strings, 3 occur verbatim
  in my files, all in cache/seg_c5.json. Each is a P1_dev T=0.5 sample that coincides with a generic panel sentence; no
  panel text was copied.
