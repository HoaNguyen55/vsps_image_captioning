R9 / C10_standards — Is "every object metric >= 90" what Q1/Q2 venues require?
2026-10-13. CPU only (one local run, <1 min, 23 MB RSS).
This report extends manuscript/research_wall/08 (protocols) and novel_direction/R2_01 (n-gram SOTA on Vietnamese sets)
and does not repeat them. Every paper below was confirmed with the arXiv API, Semantic Scholar or OpenAlex, and every
number was read from the paper's own table (the PDF text is in research_r9/C10_standards/dl/<arxiv id>/p.txt). Numbers
marked [derived] are my arithmetic: F1 = 2PR/(P+R), and P = 100 - CHAIR.

======================================================================================================================
0. BOTTOM LINE
======================================================================================================================
- No Q1/Q2 journal and no top conference uses an absolute bar such as ">= 90". Q1/Q2 is the SCImago/JCR quartile of
  the JOURNAL; it is not a property of the results. Papers get accepted for a gain over strong baselines under a
  standard protocol.
- No accepted captioning or hallucination paper found reports object precision, recall AND F1 all >= 90 for captions.
  - Highest caption object F1 found: 79.7 (Attention Reallocation, IJCV 2025, CHAIR-F1, LLaVA-1.5-13B).
  - Highest caption object recall found: 80.8 (MemVR, ICML 2025, CHAIR recall, ~100-word captions).
  - Only yes/no probes reach about 90: POPE F1 90.6-91.1 (InternVL2.5/3-8B) and AMBER-discriminative F1 90.9
    (RLAIF-V-12B).
- Our own measured ceilings decide it (new in this round):
  - COCO-2500 probe, instance-only gold: pooling ALL FIVE English human captions gives P 85.3 / R 74.3 / F1 79.5.
    37.2% of instance classes are named by none of the five annotators.
  - Five pooled Vietnamese UIT-ViIC annotators (COCO images): F1 71.3 (test) and 72.0 (val).
  - KTVIC, one held-out human: F1 60.5. Two humans fused: F1 70.9 (R4_01).
  - So ">= 90" asks a single caption to beat five humans combined, and on COCO to beat dedicated COCO-trained
    multi-label classifiers (best OF1 found: 86.8).
- What does get accepted:
  - CHAIR_i -1 to -2 points absolute against the best baseline;
  - object F1 +1.2 to +1.8;
  - recall +3 to +4 with CHAIR not worse;
  - POPE F1 +0.8 to +4;
  - in one CVPR 2026 case, +0.38 correctness.
- Statistics in accepted papers: 7 of 30 report multi-run statistics; 0 of 30 test the main comparison for
  significance. Our 3 seeds plus paired bootstrap already exceed the norm.

Readings:
(i) The human P-R frontier against instance labels runs (P, R) = (92.6, 56.6) -> (90.0, 66.0) -> (88.1, 70.1) ->
    (85.3, 74.3). Precision FALLS as humans name more classes: 7-15% of human-named classes have no instance label
    (label gaps or synonyms). "P >= 90 and R >= 90" lies outside the human frontier.
(ii) To get R >= 90, a caption must name classes that none of 5 humans named. That is inventory-style text, which is
     the metric-gaming case the brief already names.
(iii) Where our systems sit on this frontier:
     - COCO short: zero-shot 90.8/48.5/63.3 and fuse2 90.9/44.2/59.4. Precision is at the human level; recall is
       below one human (56.6).
     - COCO detailed: zero-shot 80.5/67.5/73.4. This beats one human on F1 but is about 9 P below the pooled-human
       curve at the same recall (interpolated: P about 89.3 at R 67.5).
     - KTVIC short: fuse2 F1 58.2 vs one human 60.5 (R4_01, delta -2.3, p = 0.050). Recall is at human level; the
       whole gap is precision (-12.4).

Pattern:
- Papers headline large RELATIVE cuts in hallucination rate (-13% to -81%).
- They keep recall and F1 "not worse": changes of +0.4 to +3.7, often negative (Less-is-More -2.8, DeCo -1.4/-3.9,
  OPA-DPO -3.7).
- Nobody chases absolute recall or F1 levels.

Statistics (30 accepted papers read in full, venue verified: 25 top-conference, 5 Q1-journal; HalluciDoctor, HSA-DPO and VTI were left out because their venues could not be confirmed):
- Multi-run statistics: 7 of 30.
  - mean +- sd: VCD (5 runs), HALC, SID (3 seeds), MemVR (3 seeds), Nullu, HALVA;
  - mean of 3 runs only: AGLA.
- Significance test of the MAIN comparison: 0 of 30. (EFUF's t-test is on an analysis; HALVA's "significant" is an
  adjusted-delta rule for general-task drops.)
- None of the 5 journal papers (Woodpecker SCIS, MHR TOMM, ARA TOMM, Attention Reallocation IJCV, CLVA PR) reports
  seeds. Attention Reallocation states a single fixed seed.
- Per 08: HB (Sci. Rep. 2026) reports a mean of 3 trials.
- CCA (NeurIPS 2024) answered [No] to the NeurIPS checklist item "Experiment Statistical Significance": "Error bars
  are not reported since training of LVLMs is computationally expensive."
- The ACL ARR checklist C3 asks for error bars or whether a result is a single run, and states: "Not answering
  positively to a question is not grounds for rejection." (aclrollingreview.org/responsibleNLPresearch)
- Our minimum detectable effect [derived from R4_01's CIs; 80% power, two-sided 0.05]: about 3 F1 on KTVIC-558
  (paired SE about 1.15) and about 1.3 points on COCO-2500 (dP CI half-width 0.95, so SE about 0.48). Gains below
  these will not separate from noise, whatever the venue accepts.

======================================================================================================================
5. RECOMMENDATION: HOW TO FRAME THE TARGETS
======================================================================================================================
R1. Drop ">= 90 on each metric" as the success criterion. No venue states such a bar, no accepted paper meets it,
    and our own human ceilings put it out of reach:
    - KTVIC: R >= 90 vs two fused humans at 66.6; F1 >= 90 vs 70.9; P >= 90 vs one human at 85.3.
    - COCO: F1 >= 90 vs five pooled humans at 79.5.
    Chasing it forces inventory-style captions, which reviewers will flag through CIDEr.

R2. Frame the targets against the HUMAN P-R FRONTIER at matched mentions per caption, for example:
    - KTVIC short:
      - parity: F1 not significantly below one held-out human, 60.5 (1v4, R4_01);
      - stretch: close the 12-point precision gap at R about 47.
    - KTVIC detailed: parity with two fused humans (F1 70.9 at 2.57 objects per caption).
    - COCO (instance gold): reach the pooled-human curve (P 90.0 at R 66; P 85.3 at R 74.3).
    State each target as "on or above the k-annotator frontier" (bin/human_union.py; human_parity.py for KTVIC). This
    is a claim reviewers can check. It is also new: CHAIR, AMBER and CompreCap report single human or model points,
    not a pooled-annotator frontier against instance labels.

R3. The PRIMARY claim should be the paired gain:
    - ΔF1 >= +2 over P1 AND over fuse2 (above our MDE of about 3 to be safe);
    - precision not lower beyond its CI;
    - CIDEr and BLEU reported alongside, with no CIDEr loss beyond its CI;
    - mean +- sd over 3 seeds, paired bootstrap, Holm correction across P, R, F1, CHAIR_i, CHAIR_s.
    Accepted Q1 work shows +1.2 to +1.8 F1 suffices when the protocol is standard. Our statistics already exceed the
    norm (0 of 30 papers test significance).

R4. Add STANDARD COMPARABLES in an appendix, so reviewers can place us on known scales:
    - COCO CHAIR_s / CHAIR_i / recall with the Rohrbach gold (instances plus references), at 64 and 512 tokens;
    - POPE F1, plus RePOPE;
    - AMBER generative and discriminative.
    Expected levels for a Qwen2.5-VL-7B-class model: ObjHal CHAIR_i about 9 at baseline (SENTINEL); POPE about 86-88 [estimate from LLaVA-1.5 about 86 and Qwen2-VL-7B 88.1].
    Cost [estimate from R5_02's measured speeds of 1.3-6.1 s per image]: about 3.5 GPU-h per system, about 10 GPU-h for
    zero-shot, P1 and fuse2.

R5. In the paper, write: "first object-level P/R/F1 and hallucination benchmark for Vietnamese captioning, with
    measured human ceilings". Then give every number together with its human ceiling, never as an absolute
    percentage. Name the gaming check: the CIDEr and length cost of each gain.

Proposal tally:
- Framing (R2 + R3): CIDEr cost 0; 0 GPU-h (the scripts exist).
