C7_resolution (R9, 2026-10-13): visual resolution, tiling, zoom and small or peripheral objects as a RECALL lever.
Scope: Qwen2.5-VL-7B (repo backbone), KTVIC object P/R/F1 (113-entry lexicon, reference gold), COCO object F1 (instance gold).
All pilots are CPU-only (nice 15, 1 thread, <200 MB RAM, <15 s each). Scripts and outputs are in this directory (section 9).

======================================================================================================================
0. BOTTOM LINE
======================================================================================================================
- Resolution is not what limits our recall.
  - COCO: our captions already see native resolution. The probe budget is 256-1024 tokens. The 640-px COCO images need a
    median of 345 tokens (max 529), so 0 of 2,500 images hit the cap. A larger token budget therefore changes nothing on
    COCO. Only upsampling (S², tiles, zoom) adds pixels per object.
  - KTVIC: 50% of test images are shrunk at the 512-token eval budget, but mildly (median linear scale 0.97). The 75 most
    shrunk images (<0.6x linear) have the same recall as the 290 native ones: fuse2-short +0.4 points [-5.5, +6.5].
- The model sees far more than it says. POPE-COCO (RePOPE labels, Vietnamese prompt, native resolution) confirms present
  objects at these rates: SMALL (<32² px) 67%, MEDIUM 88%, LARGE 96% for fuse2. Its short captions NAME 7%, 24% and 56% of
  them.
- Ceiling of ANY perception lever (resolution, tiling or zoom), holding P(named | seen) per size fixed:
  - COCO fuse2-short: R 44.2 -> 47.6 with PERFECT perception; F1 59.4 -> 62.5.
  - COCO zero-shot detailed: R 67.5 -> 77.6; F1 73.4 -> 79.0.
  - A realistic "one size class better" gain is +1.4 R (fuse2-short) and +5.2 R (zero-shot detailed).
- Literature: higher resolution or tiling moves POPE by -1.1 to +4.9 (typically +0.2 to +3.2), mostly confounded.
  No resolution paper reports CHAIR or caption object recall, and several report resolution hurting hallucination
  benchmarks. The only zoom method measured on free-form captions with an object metric (HALC, ICML 2024) cut CHAIR,
  but its object recall FELL (45.73 -> 44.73).
- >= 90 is NOT reachable through this lever on any metric (section 7).
  - COCO F1 90 needs R about 89 at P 90.9.
  - One human English caption scores F1 70.7 against the instance gold, and the union of 5 human captions scores 79.4.
  - Naming every class that has an instance of at least 32² px, and nothing else, scores F1 91.5. So 90 is an
    inventory target, not a captioning target.

- SFT at cap 2,048: about 1.6x compute, so about 5.8 GPU-h for a fuse2-size run.

- Not recommended: section 3b shows nothing to gain.

6. PROPOSALS
======================================================================================================================
P1. UGZ, uncertainty-gated zoom verification. It is the visual verifier of a recall-completion step and does no training.

Mechanism:
1. Take the existing short caption c_s and the same model's detailed caption c_d. Both are already computed for KTVIC test
   and COCO.
2. Candidates = objects(c_d) - objects(c_s).
3. For each candidate x, compute a one-token existence probe p_full = P("có" | full image at native resolution, "Trong ảnh
   có x không?"). Use one image prefill plus a cached-KV continuation per candidate (flagged model signal).
4. Only when p_full falls in an uncertain band [t_lo, t_hi], re-probe on zoomed tiles: 2x2 overlapping tiles, each
   upsampled 2x (or a ViCrop rel-att crop), and take p_zoom = max over tiles.
5. Accept x if the combined score is >= t. Fit t on dev only: KTVIC dev300 and 500 Karpathy-VAL COCO images, whose
   instance labels are legal because they are not test images.
6. Insert at most k = 2 accepted objects. On KTVIC, use the CC-ADD sample-selection rule from R8 (the closest-to-greedy
   sample that contains them). On COCO, use a fixed clause.

Novel element vs the closest prior:
- HALC 2403.00425 uses detector-defined focal views to contrast next-token distributions. It removes objects, and its
  recall fell from 45.73 to 44.73. UGZ uses no detector, zooms only where the model's own probe is uncertain, and ADDS
  objects.
- ViCrop 2502.17422 crops for one question in VQA. UGZ gates the crop per candidate object and calibrates the gate with the
  size-dependent perception curve measured in 2f.
- CAPTURE 2405.19092 runs exhaustive SAM crops with an OWLv2 filter and an LLM merge offline. UGZ uses selective zoom,
  verifies with the VLM itself and inserts at most k objects into a SHORT caption under a CIDEr guard.
- DyFo, ZoomEye and V* search for a queried target. R8 CC-ADD triggers on consensus and has no visual check.

Expected effect (evidence in 2g and 2j):
- COCO: the pool holds 0.77 true additions per caption (25.3 R points), so k <= 2 rarely binds there.
  - A probe verifier that accepts 75-85% of true candidates and passes 40-60% of the model's own false ones gives about
    R +19 to +21 and P -4 to -7, i.e. F1 about 72-75 from 59.4. The pool ceilings are 74.2 unverified and 80.1 with an
    oracle verifier.
  - Of that, the ZOOM share is at most about +1.5 R, about +1 F1. Almost all of the gain is verified completion, which is
    verbalization and should be coordinated with the MBR and CC-ADD agents.
- KTVIC: the pool is 64.9 unverified and 70.7 if verified against references. A presence verifier cannot know what the
  annotators chose to name, and CC-ADD-style insertion is limited by the sample pool. Expect F1 +1 to +3 (CC-ADD measured
  +1.1). The zoom share is <= 0.5 R.
- CIDEr cost: CC-ADD-style insertion measured KTVIC dev CIDEr-D -1.2 [-4.7, +1.7] in R8. Appending detailed-style text is
  ruinous (fuse2 s43 short CIDEr 145.1 vs detailed 22.7), so k must stay small.
  - Report caption length and CIDEr next to every F1.
  - Name the gaming risk: with k uncapped this becomes an object inventory.

GPU cost:
- Per image: 1 prefill, <= 10 cached one-token probes, and 4 tile prefills for about 30% of candidates. About 1.5-3 s/image.
- KTVIC dev300 + test: about 0.7 h. COCO val500 + test2500: about 2.5 h. Total about 3-4 GPU-h.

Kill criteria:
1. On COCO-val, UGZ minus the same pipeline WITHOUT zoom is < +0.5 F1: zoom is not the active ingredient. Keep only the
   verified completion and hand it to the verbalization agents.
2. On KTVIC dev300, the CIDEr-D drop is > 3, or the F1 gain is < +1: do not ship on KTVIC.
3. With zoom, the POPE small-object yes-rate on the same 500 images stays below the medium rate minus 10: zoom does not
   repair perception.

P2. The direct resolution control. It is the test a Q1 reviewer will ask for; no novelty is claimed.
(a) KTVIC: evaluate fuse2_s42 and p1_s43 at --max-vision-tokens 2048, which puts 92% of test images at native resolution.
    Run dev300 first, then test, in both modes. The R4 job 73 planned 1,024 and was never run.
(b) COCO: 2x test-time upsampling, S²-style without retraining, on fuse2-short and zero-shot detailed.
(c) POPE-COCO at 2x, to measure seen-rate by size directly.

- Predictions from this pilot:
  - KTVIC: dR within +/-1 (3b), dP within +/-1.
  - COCO: dR <= +1.4 (short) and <= +5.2 (zero-shot detailed), from 2g.
  - POPE small objects: yes-rate +0 to +15.
- Cost: (a) about 1.3 GPU-h, (b) about 2-3 GPU-h, (c) about 1 GPU-h.
- Decision:
  - If KTVIC dev dR < +1 and COCO-val dR < +1.5, report resolution as a negative control and close the lever.
  - If KTVIC dev dR >= +2, re-open: SFT at 2,048 tokens, about 6 GPU-h, after measuring memory.

======================================================================================================================
7. CAN THIS LEVER REACH >= 90?
======================================================================================================================
- KTVIC P: NO. Resolution does not act on reference-gold precision. The false mentions are mostly real objects that the
  annotators did not name, and one human caption reaches only P 85.3. In the literature, resolution moves POPE by -1.1 to
  +4.9.
- KTVIC R: NO. Resolution-addressable recall is at most 7.1 points (every small-like miss), and more likely at most 1.8
  (never named), on top of R 47.1. No resolution effect is detectable (3b).
- KTVIC F1: NO.
- COCO F1: NO.
  - Perfect perception gives F1 62.5 (fuse2-short) or 79.0 (zero-shot detailed).
  - Recovering every small miss gives 70.9 or 79.2.
  - One human caption is F1 70.7.
  - F1 90 requires naming every class with a >= 32² instance (oracle 91.5): an inventory.

======================================================================================================================
8. CAVEATS
======================================================================================================================
- Seen-rates come from the 500 POPE images, not the 2,500 manifest images. The adapters' yes-bias inflates them. The
  ceiling assumes P(named | seen) does not depend on how the object became visible; zoom crops could change salience.
- Human vs model compares English references (Rohrbach synonyms) with Vietnamese captions (coco80_vi v2): different
  matchers.
- KTVIC has no boxes. The strata are confounded by content. The small-like class list is a hand tag (listed in 3c). The
  unions include many low-precision systems, and the chance correction over-corrects.
- Grounding-DINO tags and probe answers are model outputs and are flagged. No AI label is used as evidence of truth. All
  gold labels are existing human labels: COCO instances, KTVIC references and RePOPE.
- The literature numbers are mostly VQA-style existence (POPE, HallusionBench), not caption recall, and several gains are
  confounded (resampler, ViT tuning).
