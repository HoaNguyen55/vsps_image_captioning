A separately written caption panel of the same images names more than half of the objects that KTVIC counts against a fusion system.

None of these effects is about which system describes the image better, and, under our committed protocol, the appended-period and segmentation effects are each larger than the reported gap between our controlled system and the published GRIT score. KTVIC remains useful, but only with a stated protocol: segmenter, CIDEr variant, scale, punctuation policy and matcher, reported with every score, and object comparisons made at matched count or against a second gold. The protocol card and the evaluation kit released with this paper make that reporting cheap, and their scripts run on any Vietnamese dataset a reader holds.

The supplement records, with unchanged verdicts, the pre-registered training and decoding tests run with the same system: none produced a confirmed gain on the test set.
