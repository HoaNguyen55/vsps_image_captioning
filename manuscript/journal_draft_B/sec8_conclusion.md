None of these effects is about which system describes the image better, and, under our committed protocol, the appended-period and segmentation effects are each larger than the reported gap between our controlled system and the published GRIT score. KTVIC remains useful, but only with a stated protocol: segmenter, CIDEr variant, scale, punctuation policy and matcher, reported with every score, and object comparisons made at matched count or against a second gold. The protocol card and the evaluation kit released with this paper make that reporting cheap, and their scripts run on any Vietnamese dataset a reader holds. Under a description-only pre-registration, we ran the kit on 3 external systems from 3 sources (descriptive scores, no ranking); it reported every protocol column for each.
