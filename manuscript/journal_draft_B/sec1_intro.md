Public Vietnamese image-captioning benchmarks include UIT-ViIC (Lam et al., 2020), KTVIC (Pham et al., 2024) and UIT-OpenViIC (Bui et al., 2026), with further sets for narrower domains (Section 2). Systems are compared on them mainly by CIDEr. On KTVIC a fine-tuned vision–language model (VLM) now scores far above the reported transformer baseline. Our response-only fine-tune of Qwen2.5-VL-7B reaches a word-level CIDEr-D of 148.23 (SD 0.38 over three seeds)<!-- src: .session_backup/2026-10-15/paperA_keys/cider_3seed_mean.json :: mean ; sd_sample_ddof1 -->; GRIT is reported at 136.0<!-- src: literature: FAIR 2026 Table I (GRIT as reported for KTVIC); protocol not matched -->. Gaps of this size invite a leaderboard reading. This paper asks a prior question: what does a KTVIC CIDEr score measure, and how much of a difference between two systems is about the image?

We answer with an audit of the dataset, run with a controlled system as the measuring instrument, and we release the audit as an evaluation kit. Four properties of KTVIC change the answer.

1. **The five references of an image come from one writer.** The KTVIC annotation interface asks one annotator to enter all five captions of an image, with three annotators in total (Pham et al., 2024, §3.2, Fig. 5). The references are therefore not independent draws. The public code of the KTVIC baselines uses the PTB tokenizer; which pipeline produced the published KTVIC scores we could not verify (Section 3.3).<!-- CC-088 B-1: headline rewritten as a pipeline finding; R2 N-M7 note kept: "Any system that writes ordinary Vietnamese sentences pays this cost" stays removed. -->
3. **Object scores depend on an undocumented matcher.** Tone-mark normalisation, classifier nouns such as *cây* and role nouns change which objects a caption "mentions". We document three matcher versions. Only v1 and v2 are attested; v3 is a model-drafted rule set without human sign-off, so we report it as sensitivity only and do not release it.
4. **The test images are shared with UIT-OpenViIC, which gives a second caption panel.** Of the 558 KTVIC test images, 401 (71.86%) also appear in the UIT-OpenViIC training split, and 555 appear somewhere in UIT-OpenViIC, with captions written separately from KTVIC's<!-- src: .session_backup/2026-10-13/r10_cpu/r10_a1_a3_spotchecks_v2.json :: A_32_leakage_disclosure.splits.ktvic_test_558.in_openviic_train ; in_openviic_train_pct ; total_in_openviic_panel -->. **Contributions.**

- *A dataset-validity audit of KTVIC* (Section 3): single-writer references, the punctuation convention and what it costs with and without PTB-style punctuation removal, on KTVIC and across corpora, segmentation sensitivity, matcher versions, and image overlap with UIT-OpenViIC and COCO, with a second caption panel on the same images (Section 5).
- *A protocol card* (Section 4.2) that names segmenter, CIDEr variant, scale, punctuation handling and matcher for every score, applied to a controlled system whose committed scores a second machine reproduces byte for byte.

**Relation to earlier work.** A conference version (Nguyen et al., 2026b) introduced VSPS, which builds supervision for detailed Vietnamese captioning from verified propositions. It reported the P1 gold-SFT control on KTVIC (seed 42) as a baseline, and it already noted that KTVIC CHAIR values are upper bounds because the gold comes from the references, and that P1's precision comes with short, reference-style captions (FAIR §IV-C, §IV-E). This paper quantifies both points; none of its primary results appear there.<!-- R2 N-m8; checked in the IEEE-stamped FAIR PDF (pdftotext), 2026-10-21. --> The overlap is the KTVIC/COCO setup, the P1 training recipe and the P1 seed-42 score, which we reuse as a reference point. A companion paper (under review) studies when object-level hallucination scores support a comparison between systems: how the completeness of the gold and the number of mentioned objects drive them, on English and Vietnamese sets including XM3600 and UIT-ViIC. It reports no primary KTVIC number, but it uses the OpenViIC captions of KTVIC test images as one of its caption corpora, and it scores outputs of this paper's KTVIC-trained systems on XM3600 and of its ten UIT-ViIC systems, for its own claims about gold completeness.<!-- R2 N-M4: "uses KTVIC only as a motivating example" corrected (companion journal_draft/sec11_limitations_ethics.md:39; supplementary.md:262; its Table 5.1 OpenViIC panel row). --> Table 7.1 lists what each paper claims and which data it uses; no number is a primary result in both.

**What we do not claim.** We claim no state of the art on KTVIC: we report no published Vietnamese system's score under our protocol as a comparison: the released outputs of two KTVIC baselines enter only the rank correlation of Section 3.4 and the punctuation audit of Section 3.3; GRIT's 136.0 comes from a different protocol. We make no claim about human judgments of caption quality, because no new human annotation was collected. Model-generated labels and votes serve only as method components, never as evidence.
