# 1 Introduction

Vietnamese image captioning has three public benchmarks: UIT-ViIC (Lam et al., 2020), KTVIC (Pham et al., 2024) and UIT-OpenViIC (Bui et al., Signal Processing: Image Communication 140:117430, 2026). Systems are compared on them mainly by CIDEr. On KTVIC a fine-tuned vision–language model (VLM) now scores far above the reported transformer baseline. Our response-only fine-tune of Qwen2.5-VL-7B reaches a word-level CIDEr-D of 148.23 (SD 0.38 over three seeds)<!-- src: .session_backup/2026-10-15/paperA_keys/cider_3seed_mean.json :: mean ; sd_sample_ddof1 -->; GRIT is reported at 136.0<!-- src: literature: FAIR 2026 Table I (GRIT as reported for KTVIC); protocol not matched -->. Gaps of this size invite a leaderboard reading. This paper asks a prior question: what does a KTVIC CIDEr score measure, and how much of a difference between two systems is about the image?

We answer with a controlled system used as a measuring instrument and an audit of the dataset it is measured on. Four properties of KTVIC change the answer.

1. **The five references of an image come from one writer.** The KTVIC annotation interface asks one annotator to enter all five captions of an image, with three annotators in total (Pham et al., 2024, §3.2, Fig. 5). The references are therefore not independent draws. Of the objects named by exactly two references, a share of 0.7102 [0.6696, 0.7518] sit in adjacent slots, against 0.40 if the order were exchangeable<!-- src: .session_backup/2026-10-14/r11_verify/c3_n2_exchangeability_all_datasets.json :: k5_exchangeability_audit.ktvic_test_objects_in.adjacent_share ; adjacent_share_ci95 ; adjacent_share_null -->.
2. **The references contain no sentence-final punctuation.** None of the 2,790 test references ends with a period<!-- src: .session_backup/2026-10-14/r11_verify/c1_b1_b6_reproduction.json :: B5_L5_cider_punct_and_uitviic_chair.ktvic_period_audit.test_refs_total ; test_refs_ending_period -->. Appending a single "." to every output of our best system lowers CIDEr-D by 35.17 points (−23.66%)<!-- src: .session_backup/2026-10-14/r11_verify/c8_l5_d1_d3a_cpu_audit.json :: l5_d1_punct_idf_law|ktvic_test_k1_to_k5_curves|p1assist_s42-short|first_k|k=5|d_abs ; d_rel_pct -->. Any system that writes ordinary Vietnamese sentences pays this cost.
3. **Object scores depend on an undocumented matcher.** Tone-mark normalisation, classifier nouns such as *cây* and role nouns change which objects a caption "mentions". We release three matcher versions. Only v1 and v2 are attested; v3 is a model-drafted rule set without human sign-off, so we report it as sensitivity only.
4. **Two caption panels disagree.** About two thirds of the KTVIC test images also appear in UIT-OpenViIC with independent captions (401 of 558 in its training split)<!-- src: .session_backup/2026-10-13/r10_cpu/r10_a1_a3_spotchecks_v2.json :: A_32_leakage_disclosure.splits.ktvic_test_558.in_openviic_train -->. Scored against that second panel (aggregates only), some differences between systems shrink or vanish.

**Contributions.**

- *A controlled system with a protocol card* (Section 4): three seeds, a committed scorer that a second machine reproduces byte for byte, and a full statement of segmenter, CIDEr variant, scale and punctuation handling.
- *A pre-registered supervision ladder* (Sections 4.3, 5.2): predictions frozen before any ladder output existed, reported run by run. Run 55 misses its precision interval and fails its own equivalence test. Pooled over six landed runs, the frontier residual is equivalent to zero within ±1.0 point. Three runs are pending.
- *A dataset-validity audit of KTVIC* (Section 3): single-writer references, the punctuation artifact and its link to cross-corpus CIDEr drops, segmentation sensitivity, matcher versions, and image overlap with UIT-OpenViIC and COCO.
- *Transfer and a second caption panel* (Section 5): cross-dataset transfer to UIT-ViIC, an in-domain replication, and a comparison of KTVIC references with an independent caption panel on the same images.
- *Pre-registered negatives* (Section 6): a text-kNN prior, an object-consensus reranker (UPE-MBR), CIDEr-only RL, and further training and decoding levers fail their registered or count-controlled tests. We report them because each would look like a gain under raw scores.

**Relation to earlier work.** A conference version (FAIR 2026) introduced VSPS, which builds supervision for detailed Vietnamese captioning from verified propositions. It reported the P1 gold-SFT control on KTVIC (seed 42) as a baseline. None of this paper's primary results appear there. The overlap is the KTVIC/COCO setup, the P1 training recipe and the P1 seed-42 score, which we reuse as a reference point (`new_vs_FAIR.md`). A companion paper (under review) develops the general theory of object-count confounding in hallucination metrics on English benchmarks. It uses KTVIC only as a motivating example and reports none of the numbers that are primary here.

**What we do not claim.** We claim no state of the art on KTVIC: no published Vietnamese system has been rescored under our protocol, and GRIT's 136.0 comes from a different protocol. We make no claim about human judgments of caption quality, because no new human annotation was collected. Model-generated labels and votes serve only as method components, never as evidence.
