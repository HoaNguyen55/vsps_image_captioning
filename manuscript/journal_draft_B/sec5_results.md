Two readings follow.

- **CIDEr and object F1 rank systems differently.** fuse2 names more objects and has higher object F1 than P1-R, but lower CIDEr-D. In detailed mode its CIDEr-D collapses to 22.4 while its object F1 is the highest in the table. Part of the collapse is CIDEr-D's length penalty and the period that closes fuse2's detailed targets (Section 4.1); the rest is that CIDEr rewards matching the short, unpunctuated reference template rather than describing more of the image. We did not separate the parts.<!-- R2 N-M7 -->

## 5.3 Two caption panels disagree

The 555 KTVIC test images that also have UIT-OpenViIC captions let us score the same outputs against two caption panels written separately: KTVIC's single-writer references and OpenViIC's references. Their independence is not established: OpenViIC's caption-entry protocol is not documented, and the companion paper finds its full-test adjacency above the exchangeable null.
