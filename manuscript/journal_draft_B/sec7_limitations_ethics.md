## 7.1 Limitations

**One model family.** Every result in the main text comes from Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct with one QLoRA recipe. The registered second backbone (Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct) stopped at its zero-shot guard, and the 3B fallback's outputs are not verified and not reported in this version (Supplementary S3). The audit findings about the references do not depend on the model, but the system scores do.

**Method-side tests.** The supervision ladder, the leaderboard, the leakage test and the levers are reported only in the supplement; most ladder and lever runs have one seed (S2, S6). The single pre-registered test of the final system returned FAIL: −0.39 [−2.44, +1.67] CIDEr-D against its members' mean (Section 5.1, S7).<!-- src: data/results/b_f1/b_f1_run.json (sha256 998e4f33) :: primary.D_MBR_x100_mean_lo_hi[0..2] ; primary.verdict --><!-- CC-145 J6 (2026-10-28): condensed; the removed sentences (seed-noise caveat of one to three precision points, ladder frontier-invariance NOT MET, four uncomputed WiSE-FT reporting items) are in Supplement S2.2 / S6.6 and the supplement section map. --><!-- src: .session_backup/2026-10-18/ladder_check/final/stdout.txt :: lines 1-10 (rule: a run is inside when its line has no OUT; 6 of 10) --><!-- moved from main: PD-20 verbatim sentence now in supplementary_B.md S2.2; src: manuscript/prereg/2026-10-12_frontier_invariance_V4.txt:233-236 (rule; per-run clause l.235, pooled clause l.236) ; .session_backup/2026-10-18/ladder_check/final/stdout.txt :: line 11 (95% CI) ; lines 1-10 (per-run points; |point| <= 1.0 on 8 of 10) ; line 1 (p1ep4_s42-short −1.59) ; line 3 (cons_s42-short +1.44) ; manuscript/verification/paperA/LADDER_CHECK_final.json :: rows[0].items.C2.evidence (−0.007) -->

**No human judgments.** We collected no new human annotation. The OpenViIC panel is a second set of captions, not ground truth. The within-writer leave-one-out figures are not a human ceiling. Model-generated signals (sample votes, kNN priors, model-drafted matcher rules) serve only inside methods and never as evidence.

**Earlier human ratings.** The prior FAIR-2026 ratings (2 raters, 150 captions) exceeded our pre-set agreement gate (identical scores on 71–87% of captions per scale, against a threshold of 50%), so, as fixed in advance, no criterion analysis was run. The gate is a stop rule fixed before any statistic was computed; it is not a verdict on the ratings. These ratings are evidence for no claim in this paper.<!-- R2 N-m5: stop-rule clarification added; the PD-21b verbatim sentence is kept. -->

**The two-panel training study is development-only.** Sections 5.4 and 5.5 use the 187 dev300 images with an OpenViIC training-split copy and one backbone; the reverse direction is a pilot with two seeds and three sizes, and the two directions train on overlapping but not identical images. The result stays at the development-exploration tier: KTVIC test is closed for this paper under our one-look rule, and a confirmation needs a newly registered single look on data that no adapter has been tuned or read on. The intervals resample images with seed fixed effects, so they do not include seed-to-seed variance, and two reverse seeds cannot estimate it. The forward KTVIC-side slope was predictable from the training-set-size result when the two-panel rule was written. Sections 5.4–5.5 and Supplement S17 list the further limits: the post hoc masked and matched rows, the length and score-level confounds, near-twins excluded by hash only, the exact-caption matches, the 41 training images with an OpenViIC-test copy, the post hoc choice of object matcher, and the weak equivalence bound. The panels differ in writers, guidelines and caption length, and their independence is not established. Two explanations of the gap, homogeneous references and models learning KTVIC's three annotators, are equally live and untested.<!-- CC-163 W5 disclosure list; sources in sec5_results.md §5.4-5.5 and number_manifest_B.tsv --><!-- MERGE-SCOPED (PI-1029n approved B+D; was CONDITIONAL CC-163 W10; submission gated at 98, CC-166): remove with Sections 5.4-5.5 if the PI answers NO to B+D; revert text: delete this paragraph -->

**Matcher v3 is unattested.** Its rules were drafted with AI assistance and not reviewed by a person. No conclusion in this paper depends on it, and its rules are not in the kit.

**Exploratory and post hoc analyses.** In the main text, the PTB-style punctuation-removal row of Section 3.3 was added after an internal review and is post hoc, the ranking concordance of Section 5.3 is post hoc and descriptive, the masked re-read of Section 5.4 and the matched-design rows of Supplement S17 (summarised in Section 5.5) are post hoc and descriptive (development data)<!-- MERGE-SCOPED (PI-1029n approved B+D; was CONDITIONAL CC-163 W10; submission gated at 98, CC-166): drop this clause with Sections 5.4-5.5 -->, and the v3 matcher results are sensitivity analyses. The supplement labels each of its tests by registration status (S1). Our registrations are pre-specified and internally time-stamped (commits and file hashes in our repository); they were not deposited with an external registry.<!-- BD item 14 (R2): 'pre-specified (internally time-stamped)' -->

**Comparators.** We make no ranking claim. No published Vietnamese system is scored under our protocol as a comparison; the two KTVIC baselines and the three external systems enter only descriptively (Section 1), and HDUCap and ViG could not be obtained in full text (Section 2).<!-- CC-145 J6 (2026-10-28): condensed; same content as sec1 "What we do not claim". -->

**External systems.** The external-system run (Section 3.7; Supplementary S9, Table S9.1) covers three systems, one per dataset, all from checkpoints, because none of the ten published output files passed the structure gate. We decoded them ourselves with each author's documented settings, in our environment, so the run shows that the kit scores outputs it did not produce; it does not show use of the kit by an outside group, nor that our decodes reproduce the authors' outputs. Two systems are reported as aggregates only by licence, and the run supports no claim about the quality of any system.<!-- src: PREREG 2026-10-28_b2_external §0, §7.1-7.2, §9 ; scores.json e1a6e0d8 :: rows.K5.empty (57), rows.K5.n (231) ; gate.json 5519b26d (0/10) ; prov.json :: transformers --><!-- CC-145 J6 (2026-10-28): the four bullets condensed; the full list (incl. **People in images.** Captions describe everyday scenes with people. No identity recognition is performed.
