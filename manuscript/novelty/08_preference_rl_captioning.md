# 08 — Preference optimization and RL for faithful captions / LVLM outputs (2024–2026)

Agent 08. Focus: overlap with **C1** (verification turned into a training signal; offline-only verification, single-pass deployment, plain SFT loss), with secondary notes on C2/C4. Also covers how to position VSPS's SFT-only choice and its DPO negative result (v9 §IV-9: hedge saturation, CJK leakage, repetition under 4-bit QLoRA DPO).

Verification method. Every arXiv ID below was resolved through the arXiv export API (title, first author, date, comment field). Venues and DOIs were cross-checked with the Semantic Scholar batch API and, where noted, the publisher or proceedings page. "Scopus" marks venues that are normally Scopus-indexed (CVPR/ICCV via IEEE Xplore, AAAI, ACL/EMNLP, NeurIPS, ICLR, ACM MM, IJCAI). I did not check Scopus entries per paper.

Main finding. The idea of turning verifier outputs into a training signal is **crowded**. RLAIF-V, HSA-DPO, FGAIF, SENTINEL, AVES-DPO, OSCAR, CCCaption, ClaimDiff-RL, VCap and VES-RFT all do it, and all of them keep the deployed model single-pass. **VSPS should not claim C1 as "verification → training signal" or as "offline verification + single-pass inference".** What stays distinctive in this literature is the combination of:
- (a) **construction**: verdicts build the SFT *target text* compositionally from typed propositions, not a preference pair or a reward scalar;
- (b) an explicit **UNCERTAIN** state that is **neutralized**, not penalized or ranked;
- (c) a **standard SFT loss only**;
- (d) a reported **negative result** for DPO in a low-resource-language, 4-bit setting.

None of the papers below has (b). Most use a binary hallucinated/non-hallucinated label, or a severity score at most.

## Highest-overlap risks
1. **RLAIF-V** (CVPR 2025): claims are split and turned into yes/no questions for an open verifier, which builds training data, and the result is a single-pass model. A reviewer can say "VSPS = RLAIF-V with SFT". The counter is three states plus neutralization plus compositional target rendering, and that DPO fails in this setting (§IV-9).
2. **CCCaption** (CVPR 2026) and **ClaimDiff-RL** (arXiv 2026): decomposed or claim-level verification against the image becomes a captioning reward, with separate hallucination and omission signals, matching VSPS's precision/recall story. The counter is no online verifier or reward model, plain SFT, and UNCERTAIN handled explicitly instead of being penalized.
3. **OSCAR** (IJCAI 2026): the same motivation (supervision beyond what the model perceives, and verification beating generation) and the same yes/no self-verification. It argues that *offline* supervision is the problem, which directly challenges VSPS's offline design. VSPS must argue that verdict-gated offline targets *are* the remedy for the mismatch OSCAR describes.
4. **HSA-DPO** (AAAI 2025) and **AVES-DPO** (ACL 2026): sentence-level or consensus verification is followed by rewriting into clean text. These are the closest to "verified clean targets"; the only difference is that the targets feed DPO. The counter is three states versus binary or severity labels, and neutralization versus free rewriting.
5. **SEED** (ACM MM 2025): uses the same deployment argument, "purge hallucinations offline, distill into a single-pass model, no inference-time tools". So VSPS should not present "single-pass deployment" as a contribution in itself.

## Suggested novelty statement (topic 08)
> Preference- and reward-based methods (RLAIF-V, HSA-DPO, CCCaption, ClaimDiff-RL) already turn fine-grained verification into a training signal. They do so through ranked pairs or scalar rewards over a binary hallucinated/correct judgment. VSPS instead uses three-state proposition verdicts to *compose* the SFT targets themselves: refuted content is dropped, under-evidenced content is neutralized to its verifiable part instead of penalized, and training uses the standard SFT loss with no preference pairs, reward model or online verifier. We also report that a three-tier DPO stage built from the same verdicts degrades a 4-bit Vietnamese captioner through hedge saturation and language leakage. This is consistent with known DPO failure modes for minimal-edit and off-policy pairs (Smaug, OPA-DPO, mDPO), and it supports SFT-level encoding of caution (cf. nSFT).
