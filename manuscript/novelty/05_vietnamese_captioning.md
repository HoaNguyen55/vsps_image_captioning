Agent 05. Search date: 2026-10-10. Sources searched: web search (English and Vietnamese: "chú thích ảnh tiếng Việt mô hình ngôn ngữ thị giác", "ảo giác mô hình thị giác ngôn ngữ tiếng Việt"), the arXiv API (`vietnamese AND hallucination`, `vietnamese AND captioning`, `vietnamese AND vision-language`, `vietnamese AND multimodal AND large language`), OpenAlex (5 queries, works since 2023), the Semantic Scholar API (abstracts), Crossref, and arXiv HTML full text (grepped for hallucination, verification, filtering and voting).

Scope note: `Q2_LITERATURE.md` §1D already lists UIT-ViIC, UIT-OpenViIC, KTVIC, Vintern-1B, LaVy, Vi-VLM/Vistral-V, ViTextVQA, OpenViVQA, VMMU, ViHallu, Qu et al. (MHR) and CLAIM. This file re-checks only what matters for overlap and adds about 12 works that were not in that scan.

Scopus: Elsevier journals and the Springer series CCIS, LNNS and LNICST are Scopus-indexed. IEEE KSE, RIVF and MAPR proceedings are normally Scopus-indexed, but I did not check each paper. I could not confirm Scopus indexing for the national FAIR proceedings or the VNU and HaUI journals.

## Bottom line

**No Vietnamese work found does any of the following:**
- (a) measures object hallucination (CHAIR, POPE or similar) in Vietnamese captions;
- (b) uses a Vietnamese object lexicon or a gender-attribution metric;
- (c) verifies Vietnamese captions at the proposition level;
- (d) uses verification to build SFT data for Vietnamese captioning.

**C5 novelty holds.** The only Vietnamese hallucination benchmark is ViHallu, which is text-only. The two multilingual LVLM hallucination papers (MHR, CLAIM) do not include Vietnamese (0 mentions in their full HTML). Neither does SHROOM-Visions 2026, which covers ZH, EN, FR and IT.

**C1 is the closest Vietnamese overlap, at medium.** AutoViVQA (2026) and ViEduVQA (KSE 2025) both verify or filter MLLM-generated Vietnamese training data before training. Both work on VQA, at sample level, with keep/drop decisions only.

## Highest-overlap risks

1. **AutoViVQA (Springer CCIS 2026; arXiv:2603.09689). C1 MEDIUM.** It verifies generated Vietnamese training data automatically, with ensemble majority voting and a grounding score. Contrast it on four points: VQA vs. detailed captions, whole-sample keep/drop vs. three-state per-proposition verdicts, a multi-model rubric vs. dual affirmative/negated sampled queries, and discard vs. neutralize.
2. **Med-StepBench (IJCAI 2026). C5 low–medium wording risk.** It is a Vietnamese and English VLM hallucination benchmark, though medical. Do not claim "first Vietnamese VLM hallucination study" without the qualifier "general-domain image captioning".
3. **ViG (IEEE MAPR 2026). C5 low–medium.** It is the current KTVIC/UIT-ViIC model and calls itself "grounded". Reviewers may ask for it as a baseline. Note that it reports only n-gram metrics.
4. **ViEduVQA (IEEE KSE 2025). C1 low–medium.** MLLM-generated Vietnamese supervision with an emphasis on quality, but without a verifier.
5. **ViHallu (arXiv 2026). C5/C2 low.** It is the only Vietnamese hallucination benchmark, and its three-class labels could be confused with VSPS's three states. Clarify that it is text-only and uses a different taxonomy.

## Suggested novelty statement (Vietnamese topic)

> Existing Vietnamese captioning work (UIT-ViIC, VieCap4H, UIT-OpenViIC, KTVIC, HDUCap, ViG) reports only n-gram metrics. Vietnamese MLLMs (LaVy, Vintern, Vistral-V) acknowledge hallucination but do not measure it. The only Vietnamese hallucination benchmark (ViHallu) is text-only, and multilingual LVLM hallucination studies (MHR, CLAIM, CCHall, SHROOM-Visions) do not include Vietnamese. To our knowledge, VSPS is the first work to measure and reduce object and gender hallucination in general-domain detailed Vietnamese image captioning. Unlike sample-level filtering of synthetic Vietnamese VQA data (AutoViVQA), it verifies supervision proposition by proposition with a three-state verdict and repairs uncertain content instead of discarding it.
