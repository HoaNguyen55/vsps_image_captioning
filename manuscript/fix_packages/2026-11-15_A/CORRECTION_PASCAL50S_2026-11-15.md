# Correction record to the PASCAL-50S registration, section 1.4: the statement on written confirmation

Date of this record: 2026-11-15 (UTC).

Registration: `manuscript/prereg/2026-10-28_pascal50s/PREREG.md`, sha256 `1448ab13fcb509b4f6dff1e15344809f529478f6606df8880498427153c2db3b`, committed in the run repository at `353437f`. This record does not edit that file; the file keeps this sha256.

Route: ruling CC-407 (revised text), Ruling 2 and item W6, and ruling CC-412 (carried item CC-407 W6): a disclosed correction record, in place of an edit of the registration.

## 1. What the registration says

- Section 1.4, line 109: "**Written confirmation from the dataset authors was obtained (2026-10-28; on file with the corresponding author).**"
- Section 1.4, line 113, in the disclosure sentence written for the data-availability statement of Paper A: "written confirmation from the dataset authors was obtained (2026-10-28; on file with the corresponding author)".

## 2. What the record is

- Decisions LOG, line PI-1026az (2026-10-28). The investigator's typed words say that he contacted the dataset authors privately and that they accepted. The words "written confirmation obtained" stand in the coordinator's part of that line, not in the investigator's words.
- Decisions LOG, line PI-1110o (2026-11-10). The investigator states that the acceptance of the dataset authors has been confirmed and that no document will be filed in the repository. The same line fixes that the papers state the agreement as a fact and that the words "in writing", the date 2026-10-28 and "on file with the corresponding author" are not written, because their form and date are known only to the investigator.
- A search of the project files on 2026-11-15 (file names and LOG lines; ruling CC-407, revised text) found no saved reply of the dataset authors.

The record therefore supports this much: one of the authors of Paper A contacted the dataset authors, and he states that they agreed to the use. Three parts of the registration sentence are not supported by a record in the project files:

1. the word "written";
2. 2026-10-28 as the date of a confirmation (it is the date of the investigator's statement in the LOG);
3. "on file with the corresponding author".

This record does not say that the confirmation was not written. It says that the project record does not show its form, its date or where it is kept.

## 3. What the paper prints

The data-availability statement of Paper A (`declarations.md`) prints: "the dataset authors were contacted by one of us and agreed to this use". It prints the agreement as a fact, without "in writing", without a date and without "on file". The disclosure sentence of line 113 of the registration is therefore not the sentence the paper prints; the printed sentence claims less.

## 4. What does not change

- No prediction, analysis rule, verdict rule or branch text of the registration is affected, and no result file.
- The licence gate of section 1.4 was closed for the run on the investigator's statement. The withdrawal clause of section 1.4 stands as registered.
- `PREREG.md` stays byte-unchanged.

## 5. If a reply is saved later

Ruling CC-407 (revised text), Ruling 2 (b), lists what restores the registered wording: a saved copy of the reply of the dataset authors, kept outside every committed file, and one record line giving the role of the sender, the channel, the date, the use accepted and the sha256 of the copy. If that is done, a further record placed beside this one states it, and this record stays as written.
