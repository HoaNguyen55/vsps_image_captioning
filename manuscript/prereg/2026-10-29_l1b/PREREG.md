## 1. Question and claim

L1-a (decide.json fe915642, PROCEED, scope Qwen-family LMs) found that after chance correction, 13.5–21.9% of
wrong stratum answers are diacritic-only. The fix ceiling is mark-blind EM minus contained EM on full dev: 4.2
(qwen25vl_7b), 4.6 (qwen3vl_8b), 7.2 (internvl3_8b) and 4.0 (qwen25vl_3b) points.

The question for L1-b is whether a **training-free, glyph-verified re-rank over the closed orthographic candidate
set** (PORTFOLIO L1 "Fix") recovers a useful part of that ceiling. The test is EM on dev half E, against human gold.
The re-rank uses the backbone's own likelihood given the real image. A second question is whether the gain comes from
the image rather than the language prior. The grey-image score of the same model is the text-only baseline.

This stage tests PORTFOLIO kill clause (b), adapted (§6). It also tests the fix part of RAISE (≥ +2 EM on 3
backbones). A pass means "go to the tier-2 prereg". It never means a paper claim.

## 4. Method (fixed now)

### 4.1 First pass

For each E item, the first pass is the backbone's greedy answer: the L1-a img arm, or the L1-b decode for the non-Qwen
model, with the identical prompt (sha 24e65010).

### 4.2 Closed candidate set (rule-generated, no lexicon, no gold)

- **Syllables.** The answer is NFC-normalised. Syllables are l1a's letter/digit runs (`l1a.TOK`).
- **Variants per syllable:**
  - every vowel-mark choice on each vowel letter: a ∈ {a, â, ă}, e ∈ {e, ê}, o ∈ {o, ô, ơ}, u ∈ {u, ư}; i and y stay
    as they are;
  - × the 6 tones;
  - a syllable ending in p, t, c or ch takes only sắc or nặng.
- **Rendering:**
  - one fixed tone placement: the old style, which is the gold's majority (L1-a §2.5: hòa, thủy). qu- and gi- count
    as consonants. On ươ the tone goes on ơ.
  - The syllable's own letter case is kept.
  - One form is kept per l1a.sig signature, and the syllable's own signature is removed.
- **Not changed:** đ ↔ d is never changed. It is a base letter, as in L1-a §5.1. Syllables with a digit or with no
  vowel get no variants.
- **Caps:**
  - a syllable with more than 108 variants gets none (none occur in practice);
  - at most 256 candidates per answer, taken by whole syllables in order. Later syllables are then left unchanged,
    and decide.json counts the answers affected (`n_truncated_answers`, CC-168 W14).
- **Candidate answers:** each candidate is the first-pass answer with **one** syllable replaced. Punctuation and
  spacing are kept.
- **Measured on the L1-a preds (CPU):** a mean of about 60 candidates per answer (median 46–50, p90 about 140).
  About 16% of answers have none (numbers and codes). 52–77 of 4,585 answers reach the 256 cap.

### 4.3 Scores (GPU, M2)

For each E item and each candidate c (index 0 is the first pass), M2 computes two numbers:
- **lp_img(c)** = Σ log p(c tokens | real image, prompt). This is teacher-forced, through the model's own chat
  template with the generation prompt. The answer is tokenised without special tokens, and EOS is not scored.
- **lp_grey(c):** the same with L1-a's grey 512 × 512 image (sha c6bf3833).

The image+prompt prefix is computed once per item. The candidates are then scored in right-padded batches on a copy of
the prefix KV cache (batch sizes in l1b_registry.json).

### 4.4 Parity gate (pre-fixed; no silent fallback)

- **Check:** on the first 4 E items, the first 8 candidates are also scored by one full forward pass without a cache.
- **Pass:** max |cached − full| ≤ 0.25 nats on the summed log-likelihood.
- **Failure:** `rerank` writes PARITY_FAIL and exits 3. The backbone is NOT_ASSESSABLE for this run, and the job
  moves on to the next backbone. It is never re-run another way inside the run.
- **Code-fix re-run (CC-168 W6):** the parity check is the only on-GPU test of Qwen M-RoPE (rope_deltas on a
  batch-repeated cache), Gemma3's token_type_ids and sliding cache, and Idefics3. A code fix and a re-run of the
  failed backbone are allowed **only** through a judged amendment written **before** `score` runs. Parity numbers
  carry no gold, so the one look is not spent.
- **CPU selftest:** on a tiny random LLaVA, the cached and full scores agree to < 1e-4 nats (§8).

### 4.5 Selection rule per syllable (CPU, laptop)

For syllable k, the gain of variant v over the first pass is:

    gain_M(v) = [lp_img(v) − lp_img(0)] − α · [lp_grey(v) − lp_grey(0)]      (method M)
    gain_T(v) = lp_grey(v) − lp_grey(0)                                      (text-only baseline T)

- Per syllable, independently, the variant with the largest gain is applied if that gain is > τ. Otherwise the
  syllable stays as it is.
- α = 0 is plain image-conditioned re-ranking. α > 0 also subtracts the language-prior part of the gain (the
  contrastive form).
- T is the **same model with the image removed**. It is the structured text-prior baseline at zero extra cost.

**Known biases (disclosed before any output; CC-168 W9).** All three push toward "no change", that is, toward KILL.
- The log-likelihood is a sum, not normalised by length, so variants with fewer tokens are favoured. T shares the
  bias, so the image margin compares like with like, and α = 1 cancels it.
- The first pass keeps its own tone placement, while the candidates use the old style. This is a likelihood
  nuisance only, because EM is placement-free.
- Syllables are chosen independently and the choices are applied together, but the joint answer is never scored
  (§9).

## 5. Grid, metric, one look

### 5.1 Grid (pre-declared, finite)

- M: α ∈ {0, 0.5, 1} × τ ∈ {0, 0.5, 1, 2} nats, 12 configs.
- T: τ ∈ {0, 0.5, 1, 2}, 4 configs.
- Every config is computed from the same saved numbers. No GPU step depends on a config.

### 5.2 Metric (deterministic, human gold, l1a.classify unchanged)

- **Primary:** ΔEM = contained EM(config) − contained EM(first pass) in points, on **all E items**. Number answers
  are included, so harm on any item counts.
- **CI:** 95% percentile cluster bootstrap over images (B = 2,000, `default_rng(0)`), paired. One index matrix is used
  for both arms and all backbones (`l1a.boot_diff`).
- **Flips:** flips_good counts wrong → correct items, and flips_bad counts correct → wrong items.

### 5.3 Selection (one fixed rule; one config for all backbones)

- **M\***: the M config with the largest mean ΔEM over the backbones in use. Ties go to the smaller α, then the
  larger τ.
- **T\***: the same rule within T.
- The outcome (§6) is computed on M\* and T\*. **The selected M\* and every grid number are optimistic, because they
  are selected and evaluated on E.** That is why the tier-2 rehearsal on C exists.

### 5.4 Descriptive rows (never gating)

- the strict-view ΔEM;
- the diac answers in the E stratum before and after, and the relative reduction;
- the **oracle ceiling**: EM if every diac item whose differing syllables have the gold variant in the candidate set
  were fixed. Gold is used as an oracle, never as a selection input;
- the mark-blind ceiling on E;
- the mean number of candidates and the count of answers with none;
- the full grid per backbone;
- **cross-fit ΔEM:** select on E1 and evaluate on E2, and the reverse. This is the less optimistic number that the
  tier-2 P(PASS) prediction will use.

### 5.5 One look

- `l1b.py score` runs once on the laptop CPU.
- **It refuses:**
  - if decide.json exists;
  - if PREREG.md, the split or the registry differ from their pins;
  - if any rerank file's sidecar or provenance differs: split sha, prereg sha, and l1b.py and job shas against
    the pulled code_at_start.sha256 (W4b), plus the candidate sha per item. A missing sidecar makes that backbone
    NOT_ASSESSABLE ("incomplete") only when the pulled CAP_STOP.json exists; otherwise `score` refuses (W4a).
- **Output:** `.session_backup/2026-10-29/l1b/decide.json`, holding ids, counts and numbers only.
- **One look per stage.** The M2 job runs each GPU step once. A finished step is skipped on re-run, and an
  interrupted rerank resumes from its fsynced items under the same run key only. Scoring happens once.

## 6. Pre-fixed outcome (exactly one; clauses in this order)

**Constants:**
- SESOI = **+1.0 EM point** (PORTFOLIO kill clause (b)). For scale, this is about 15–25% of the per-backbone fix
  ceiling.
- STRONG = **+2.0** (the RAISE fix threshold).
- Image margin: ΔEM(M\*) − ΔEM(T\*) ≥ **0.5**.
- Harm guard: flips_bad ≤ **0.25 × flips_good**.

A backbone is *in use* if it is assessable. That means the Qwen three plus the first assessable non-Qwen model.

1. **NO_VERDICT:** fewer than 3 backbones in use, or no non-Qwen backbone in use. The case goes back to the judge.
2. **KILL:** ΔEM < 1.0 **or** CI_lo ≤ 0 on ≥ 2 backbones in use.
   - The fix part of L1 fails, and L1-P1 loses its "fix" leg.
   - The judge decides between a diagnosis-only paper (observe + explain, demoted) and stopping.
   - Pre-fixed: KILL is not reopened by changing α, τ or the candidate rule on E.
3. **GO_STRONG:** ΔEM ≥ 2.0 with CI_lo > 0 and the harm guard holding on ≥ 3 backbones, including the non-Qwen one,
   and the image margin holding on ≥ 3 of them, including the non-Qwen one.
4. **GO:** the same with ≥ 1.0.
5. **PRIOR_ONLY:** clause 3 or 4 holds except the image margin. The gain is a language-prior effect, not glyph
   verification. The "image-verified" claim is dropped, and the judge rules on demotion.
6. **INCONCLUSIVE:** anything else. It goes back to the judge. At most **one** further explore round on E is allowed,
   only through a written amendment before its run, and never on C.

**Next step on GO or GO_STRONG:** a tier-2 prereg on C with M\* frozen (α, τ, candidate rule, backbones). Its success
criterion and its predicted P(PASS) ≥ 98% are set from the **cross-fit** numbers, not the selected E numbers.

**Power note (AI estimate, before any output; corrected per CC-168 W7).** The paired SE of ΔEM is driven by
correctness flips, not by changed answers. At the SESOI with the harm guard binding (about 29 good and 7 bad flips),
SE ≈ √36 / 2,210 ≈ 0.3 points, inflated mildly by image clustering (about 3 QA per image). A true +1.0 then clears
CI_lo > 0 with high probability, so the cost of the "or" clause is small.

**KILL wording (CC-168 W8).** PORTFOLIO kill clause (b) reads "< +1.0 EM with CI_lo ≤ 0". Clause 2 uses "**or**".
This is an explicit tightening, recorded under CC-168.

**Tier-2 note (CC-168 W10).** Here the image margin is a point estimate. The tier-2 prereg must put a CI on
ΔEM(M\*) − ΔEM(T\*).
