## For the fidelity read: ambiguities in the English and the reading taken

1. **Item 2, "number".** This can mean a count ("three people" when there are two) or a visible numeral (digits on a sign). The Vietnamese "số lượng" takes the count reading. "con số" would take the numeral reading. A bare "số" was not used, because "một số" means "some".
2. **Item 2, "object".** The English does not say whether animals or scenery count. "vật" is as broad as plain-English "object". It is not narrowed to "đồ vật" (man-made items) and not widened to "sự vật".
3. **Item 2, "names … that is not in the image".** For an object that is present but described wrongly (for example, the wrong colour or a wrong count), the English counts it through the "colour" and "number" entries. The Vietnamese keeps the same literal frame ("nhắc đến … không có trong ảnh") and adds no rule for partly wrong mentions.
4. **Item 2, "worse".** Worse than what? The reading taken is: worse than the other caption on screen. "kém hơn" is a comparative with an implied other caption. The English is silent on what to do when both captions contain such an error, and so is the Vietnamese. Item 5's "equally bad" is the nearest guidance, and it is kept as written.
5. **Item 3, "among correct captions".** With two captions, this is a choice between two correct ones. The English is silent on how to compare two captions that both contain an error. The Vietnamese keeps the condition ("trong các chú thích đúng") and adds nothing.
6. **Item 3, "states more of the image's main content".** The reading is "more main content", not "more words" or "more detail". "nhiều nội dung chính … hơn" says nothing about length.
7. **Item 4, "spelling of tone marks".** This can mean (a) where the mark is placed ("hoà" vs "hòa") or (b) a wrong or missing mark. "cách viết dấu thanh" (the way tone marks are written) keeps the English breadth. It does not narrow to "cách đặt dấu" (placement only) and does not widen to "thiếu dấu" (missing marks). Whether a wrong mark that changes the word is a correctness error under item 2 is left open, as in the English.
8. **Item 4, "spacing".** This could mean spaces between words or spaces around punctuation. "khoảng cách giữa các chữ" names the first. The second is covered by "dấu câu" (punctuation) in the same item.
9. **Item 5, "differ only in wording".** The reading is: the same content in different words. "cách dùng từ" (choice of words) takes that reading. The broader "cách diễn đạt" (phrasing) was not used.
10. **Item 5, "the image or both captions cannot be understood".** The scope is read as (the image cannot be understood) OR (both captions cannot be understood). It is not "the image or both captions, together". "không hiểu được" stands in each part to fix this scope, and "cả hai ... đều" fixes "both captions" as "neither caption" (CC-364 F1).
11. **Item 5 against plan §1.** The §1 gloss of X reads "the image cannot be seen well enough, or neither caption can be understood". Item 5 says the image "cannot be understood". The Vietnamese follows item 5 ("không hiểu được ảnh"), not §1 ("không nhìn rõ ảnh").
12. **Item 6, "1 A".** The reading is: key 1 gives answer A (the button "Chú thích A tốt hơn"). It is rendered "1 chọn A", with the short label A rather than the full button name, as in the English.
13. **Time sentence, "per screen" and "per rater".** The reading is one screen = one pair (plan §3.2, one question per screen). "about 2 hours per rater" is read as per person's whole session, practice included. The sentence comes from §2.4, not §4. Plan §4 items 1 to 6 do not contain it.
