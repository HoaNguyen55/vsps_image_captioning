## 5.4 How much do five references leave unnamed?

Treating each reference as a sampling occasion, a pooled Chao2-type estimator gives an asymptotic upper bound on the coverage of the reference gold under (A1): on COCO-5000 (development data), five references cover at most 0.866 (95% CI 0.854–0.876)<!-- src: data/results/a_m1/a_m1_result.json (sha256 5687a66b) :: sets.coco5000.descriptive.U.E1.C_hat_5.{point,ci95} --> of the nameable (image, class) pairs (Chao, 1987; Supplementary S1.M, S5).
