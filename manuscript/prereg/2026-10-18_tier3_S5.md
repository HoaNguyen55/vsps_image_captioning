## 6. Analysis

- Points: x = log2(N) for N = 867, 1734, 3469 training images.
- Metrics: dev300 CIDEr-D x100 (mean of rl_report.dev_scores per-image values), object F1 vs ktvic5 (object_prf.scores, exact classes) and CHAIR_i of each model.
- Fit: ordinary least squares y = a + b x over the three points, separately for CIDEr-D (b), F1 and CHAIR_i.
- 95% CI: paired percentile bootstrap over dev images (the same resampled images for all three models; corpus metrics recomputed, slope refitted); B = 2000, numpy default_rng(0). It covers dev-image sampling only; one training seed per size, so seed variance is not in the CI.
