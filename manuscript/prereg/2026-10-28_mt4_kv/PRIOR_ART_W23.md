It completes the W23 re-search list (SENTINEL, KnowAda, SEED, OSCAR, LURE and HalluciDoctor are in Ken's K14-1 table, `manuscript/ken/K14_PRIOR_ART_MT4_2026-10-27.md` §1 rows 1–18; STIC and ReCaption had 0 hits there, CC-153 M8).

**K2, as compared here.** The backbone (Qwen3-VL-8B) writes zero-shot detailed Vietnamese captions for COCO images that carry human instance gold. A deterministic, LLM-free editor (matcher v1) deletes only the clauses whose COCO-80 mention the gold marks absent and keeps everything else. The student is then trained by plain QLoRA SFT on the edited captions (1:0 mix, no preference step). The prior method this follows is the verified-proposition supervision of Nguyen et al. (FAIR 2026), which K2 cites as prior work.

**Verdict.** Neither paper does gold-only deterministic editing of the backbone's own captions followed by plain SFT. The gap survives, narrowed. K2 cannot claim to be the first to fine-tune on edited captions (ReCaption, HalluciDoctor, Image Textualization, OPA-DPO) or the first to self-train on its own COCO descriptions (STIC). The 80% pivot rule (UPGRADE_PLAN §6) and PI Q3 of CC-153 are therefore not triggered.

**Narrowed gap claim (setting novelty, not method novelty; K14-1's sentence).** We know of no prior work that removes clauses from a backbone's own detailed captions by a deterministic rule against human COCO instance gold, trains the same backbone on them by plain SFT, and shows fewer hallucinations than its zero-shot output at matched coverage, with an unedited self-training control, in Vietnamese.
