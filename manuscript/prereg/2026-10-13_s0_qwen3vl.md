## 1. Proposal and decision rule (verbatim from C8's report, Section 5, and operationalised in `scripts/s0_screen.py`)

```
SHARED GATE S0: zero-shot object-truth screen. Inference only. Pre-register before any output.
  Runs:
  - Code: coco_probe.py and pope_vi.py, with only the model loader swapped.
  - Settings: bf16, greedy, 60 / 160 new tokens, the evaluate.PROMPTS Vietnamese prompts, 256-1,024 vision tokens
    (InternVL: <= 4 tiles + thumbnail).
  - Data:
    - COCO probe short on all 2,500 images;
    - COCO probe detailed on a fixed 1,000-image subset: random.Random(0).sample(sorted(cocoid), 1000);
    - POPE-vi, Vietnamese only, 5,127 questions.
  Comparison:
  - Against the EXISTING Qwen2.5-VL-7B zero-shot outputs on the same images (no rerun).
  - Scoring: object_prf.py COCO block (instances-only gold, matcher v2) plus pilot_coco_ceilings.py (absent share and
    never-named share); paired image bootstrap, B = 5000.
  Guards:
  - Report the share of captions that are Vietnamese and the CJK leakage rate.
  - Report the same deltas on the probe images that lie in COCO val2017: about 300 of the 2,500 expected, not counted
    here.
    - train2017 = train2014 + val2014 - val2017, so about 88 % of Karpathy-test images are expected to be in COCO-2017
      train, which the newer backbones' grounding data (COCO, Objects365) likely covers.
    - If a pass vanishes on the val2017 subset (CI includes 0 and the point estimate is below half the full-set point),
      call it contamination-driven.
  PASS (per backbone):
  - COCO detailed dF1 >= +2.0 with CI lower bound > 0, OR COCO short dF1 >= +1.5 with CI lower bound > 0;
  - AND the absent share does not rise;
  - AND mentions per caption stay within +-15 % of Qwen2.5-VL-7B. Otherwise judge on the absent and never-named shares
    only, and call the F1 change style-confounded.
  KILL the lever if no backbone passes. Then keep Qwen2.5-VL-7B and use the better candidate only as the
  second-backbone replication (C8-B's fallback).
```

### Operational reading (`scripts/s0_screen.py verdict`)

All paired deltas are `candidate - baseline` (`qwen3vl8b_zs - zeroshot`) on the same images using `object_prf.paired`
(`B = 5000`, `seed = 0`, 95% percentile CI):
- Primary gold (`instances_only`): COCO-2014 instance classes (`instances_val2014.json`).
- Union gold (`instances_plus_refs`): COCO-2014 instance classes union the 5 English Karpathy reference captions mapped
  with Rohrbach's `chair_synonyms.txt` (`object_prf.coco_gold`). Because a mention is "absent" iff it is in neither the
  instances nor any reference, `absent_share (%) = 100 - P_union`, so `d(absent_share) = -dP_union`.
- Salient instance gold (`salient_instances`): instance classes that at least one of the 5 English reference captions
  also names (`inst[i] & ref[i]`), measuring recovery of human-salient misses.

Per-mode pass rule (`decide_mode` in `scripts/s0_screen.py`):
1. Let `ratio = mentions_per_caption(cand) / mentions_per_caption(base)`.
2. **In-band** (`0.85 <= ratio <= 1.15`): the mode passes iff
   - `dF1(instances_only)` point estimate `>= +1.5` (`short`, 2,500 images) or `>= +2.0` (`detailed`, 1,000 images), AND
   - `dF1(instances_only)` 95% CI lower bound `> 0`, AND
   - the absent share is not significantly higher: `dP(instances_plus_refs)` 95% CI upper bound `>= 0` (equivalently,
     `d(absent_share)` 95% CI lower bound `<= 0`).
3. **Outside the ratio band** (`ratio < 0.85` or `ratio > 1.15`): the mode is flagged `style_confounded = True` and
   passes iff
   - `dP(instances_plus_refs)` 95% CI lower bound `> 0` (significantly lower absent share), AND
   - `dR(salient_instances)` 95% CI lower bound `> 0` (significantly higher salient recall).
4. **Overall S0 outcome**: `PASS` if `short` passes OR `detailed` passes; otherwise `KILL`.
5. **Qualifiers** (reported alongside the verdict, not kills):
   - `contamination_driven`: on the `val2017` subset (`data/coco_probe/probe_val2017_ids.txt`: 295 images in `short`,
     121 in `detailed`), `dF1(instances_only)` 95% CI includes `0` (`ci_lo <= 0 <= ci_hi`) AND its point estimate is
     `< 0.5 * dF1_full`.
   - `language_driven`: the same test on the subset of images where both baseline and candidate captions are Vietnamese
     (`rescap.vlm.mock.looks_vietnamese`) and free of CJK characters (`not abs_halluc.CJK.search`).
6. **POPE-vi and RePOPE-vi**: descriptive only (3-split mean F1, P, R, Acc, yes-ratio and per-split deltas under
   original POPE and corrected RePOPE labels).

## 3. Contamination caveat (88%), subsets and baseline (`Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct`) values

Contamination caveat:
- Because `train2017 = train2014 + val2014 - val2017`, 4,407 of the 5,000 Karpathy-test (`val2014`) images (88.14%)
  belong to `COCO-2017 train`, and only 593 (11.86%) remain in `COCO val2017` (verified from the `val2017.zip` central
  directory, `s0/val2017_names.txt`, md5 `afddd69f0d4ba0b7994752de5a5e36dc`).
- Newer backbones trained on post-2017 grounding mixes (`COCO`, `Objects365`, `RefCOCO`) can have seen up to 88.2% of
  the probe images during pre-training or stage-2 grounding.
- Of the 2,500 COCO probe images (`data/coco_probe/manifest.json`), **295 (11.80%)** lie in `val2017`
  (`data/coco_probe/probe_val2017_ids.txt`, sha256
  `73f8698901f08e7b1bd53dd2842e77683af41e4e9e19276c3e8e6efba5273453`).
- Of the 1,000 detailed-screen subset images (`data/coco_probe/manifest_s0_detailed1000.json`, sampled by
  `random.Random(0).sample(sorted(manifest["images"], key=lambda x: x["cocoid"]), 1000)` and sorted by `cocoid`, sha256
  `9870bc9871a54b24315ed880ac00943e21a51ba11f1b613cbad23fcbd650cee6`), **121 (12.10%)** lie in `val2017`.

Statistical precision, Minimum Detectable Effect (MDE) on `val2017`, and primary screening role:
- On the full sets (`B = 5,000` bootstrap), baseline `zeroshot` instances-only `F1` has 95% CI `[62.00, 64.50]`
  (half-width `±1.25`, `SE = 0.65`) on `short` (`N = 2,500`) and `[72.72, 75.55]` (half-width `±1.41`, `SE = 0.73`) on
  `detailed` (`N = 1,000`), providing sufficient statistical power for the `+1.5` (`short`) and `+2.0` (`detailed`) bars.
- On the uncontaminated `COCO val2017` subset, however, the sample sizes are `n = 295` (`short`) and `n = 121`
  (`detailed`): baseline `F1` 95% CIs widen to `[61.29, 68.50]` (half-width **`±3.61`**, `SE = 1.87`) on `short` and
  `[69.36, 77.40]` (half-width **`±4.02`**, `SE = 2.03`) on `detailed`. At 80% power (`alpha = 0.05`, two-sided paired
  test with cross-model correlation `r in [0.5, 0.7]`), the Minimum Detectable Effect (`MDE`) on `val2017` alone is
  **`+4.05` to `+5.24` F1** on `short` (`n = 295`) and **`+4.40` to `+5.68` F1** on `detailed` (`n = 121`), which exceeds
  the `+1` to `+3` F1 gain expected from a backbone swap.
- Consequently, `S0` is **not** interpreted as a standalone proof of uncontaminated visual superiority when `val2017` is
  inconclusive; its primary scientific roles are:
  1. **Degradation and pathology detection** before spending SFT GPU hours: verifying Vietnamese language compliance
     (`looks_vietnamese`: baseline `98.52%` short, `100.00%` detailed), CJK character leakage (`1.96%` short, `5.00%`
     detailed), absent-object hallucination share (`5.41%` short, `15.70%` detailed), and verbosity shifts;
  2. **Gate filter for G1**: any positive second-backbone claim requires passing `G1` on held-out Vietnamese KTVIC
     `dev300` (`n = 300`, non-COCO images) plus the 2,500-image probe after controlled SFT.

Baseline `POPE-vi` (`zeroshot-vi.jsonl`, 5,127 questions on 500 `val2014` images, 0 unparsed):
