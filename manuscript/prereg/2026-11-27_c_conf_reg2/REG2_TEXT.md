## 7. Statistics

### 7.1 Per-image score

Stripped CIDEr-D × 100 from kit B (`protocol_pilot.py` `6aee4282…`, `punctuation_audit.py` `a7e80240…`), RDRSegmenter of VnCoreNLP 1.2 (jar sha256 `9e2811cdbc2ddfc71d04be5dc36e185c88dcd1ad4d5d69e4ff2e1369dccf7793`), lower-cased, document frequencies over the scored set's own references. Scored sets: the carve's 2,000; OV-test's 1,941; OV-test's 2,001; the 1,929 if it runs. Each has its own document frequencies.

**Scoring parity gate.** Before any C-CONF run is scored, the scorer must reproduce the stored per-image stripped CIDEr-D of the batch-size-1 Qwen2.5-VL-7B `ret` run on OV-dev-B within 1e-4. A mismatch stops scoring. Scoring runs on the test host, a rented host or the investigator's own RTX 3090 machine, never on the laptop (the investigator's rule of 2026-11-11; PI-1127a); the environment freeze and the Java version are written into the run record.

### 7.2 Estimands

As v1.1 §7.2, per backbone m and set, with R = 3 and D = 2:
- τ_any = mean_i [s_i(`rand`) − s_i(`zs`)];
- τ_sel = mean_i [s_i(`ret`) − s_i(`rand`)];
- τ_marg = mean_i [s_i(`perm`) − s_i(`rand`)];
- **τ_pair = mean_i [s_i(`ret`) − s_i(`perm`)]**;
- for `rand` and `perm`, s_i is the mean over that image's draws or permutations;
- per image, τ_sel = τ_marg + τ_pair exactly.

New in this registration, for the two backbones of the first look only:
- Δ_batch(m) = mean_i {[s_i(`ret`) − s_i(`rand` d0)] at batch size 1 − [s_i(`ret`) − s_i(`rand` d0)] in the first look's stored ledger}, on the 1,941, both sides scored with the same references and document frequencies.

No estimand is pooled over backbones.

### 7.3 Interval

Paired image bootstrap of the per-image differences: B = 2,000, `numpy.random.default_rng(0).integers(0, n, (B, n))`, one index matrix per scored set shared by every interval on that set, percentile bounds. p-like statistic: (1 + #{b : bootstrap mean_b ≤ 0}) / (B + 1); floor 1/2,001. The interval includes the variance of the per-image random draws (v1.1 §7.3).

### 7.4 Labels and the smallest effect of interest

SESOI = **+2.0** stripped CIDEr-D points, the registered value of the paper. Labels exactly as v1.1 §7.4:

| label | rule |
|---|---|
| DETECTED | θ̂ ≥ +2.0 and p ≤ α_j, with α_j the Holm step-down threshold at family-wise one-sided 0.025 over the m claims of the row's family (a row outside every family has m = 1) |
| REVERSED | the 95% upper bound < 0 |

### 7.6 The rehearsal rule (fixed before any carve decode)

For each claim H_pair(m), on OV-carve (2,000 images):

1. **Carve PASS:** τ_pair(m) is DETECTED with the claims whose backbone completed block R as the Holm family (three if all completed). Item 2 keeps the strictest three-claim step in every case.
2. **Share of replicates:** B = 2,000 resamples of size 1,941 drawn with replacement from the 2,000 carve images, `numpy.random.default_rng(1).integers(0, 2000, (2000, 1941))`, one matrix for all claims. With SE_m = SD of the carve's per-image differences of claim m (ddof 1) divided by √1,941, replicate b passes for claim m when mean_b ≥ +2.0 and mean_b / SE_m ≥ 2.394 (= Φ⁻¹(1 − 0.025/3)). P_m = the share of passing replicates. Required: **P_m ≥ 0.98**. The joint share over the three claims is printed.
3. **Margin for the drop from development to test:** the carve point of τ_pair(m) ≥ **+3.65** (= 2.0 + 1.65; 1.65 is the largest drop from OV-dev-B to OV-test among the paper's registered per-backbone `ret` − `rand` rows without deletion, +5.36 to +3.71 for InternVL3-8B; Qwen2.5-VL-7B's is 1.34). Provenance: the value 1.65 was taken from first-look test rows of OV-test (06 Tables 7 and 9 against v1.1 l.35); it is fixed and disclosed, and it is not an estimate made on rehearsal data. It is one constant taken from two backbones and is used for all three; it says nothing measured about Aya-Vision-8B.

**Entry.** H_pair(m) enters the test family if and only if 1, 2 and 3 hold. **The test look takes place only if** H_pair(Qwen2.5-VL-7B) entered, the positive control is DETECTED on the carve (§8.1; a positive control that is not complete counts as NOT DETECTED), and the token of §6.4 exists. Otherwise OV-test is not touched, and the carve rows are reported as development rows with the sentence "the rehearsal did not reach its bar; OV-test was not looked at a second time".

The decision file prints, per claim: the carve contrast with its interval and p, carve PASS, SE_m, P_m, the margin check, the status (TEST FAMILY / CARVE PASS, NO MARGIN / CARVE FAIL / NOT RUN), and the family. It also prints every secondary of §7.7 on the carve as a development row, with no rule.

## 8. Controls of the procedure (same decodes; CPU)

### 8.1 Positive control

LMCap's generator never sees the image. τ_sel(LMCap) = `lmcap_ri` − `lmcap_rand` must be DETECTED (m = 1), on the carve and on OV-test. τ_pair and τ_marg of LMCap are printed with labels. Every row carries "re-implementation of the LMCap recipe by this study; not the authors' code; not a statement about the published numbers".

### 8.2 Placebo rows

Per backbone and set: mean of s_i(`rand` d1) − s_i(`rand` d2), and mean of s_i(`perm` p1) − s_i(`perm` p2), with intervals. A row is **flagged** when |θ̂| ≥ 2.0 and its 95% interval excludes 0 (v1.1 §7.7a). A flag is not a STOP. A flag changes no label, verdict or sentence; the flagged rows are printed with the flag; a judge read may add wording only. The share of the image-bootstrap variance of the draw-averaged τ_sel that the random draw accounts for is printed (v1.1 §7.7a).

### 8.3 What the controls show

The positive control shows that the arms, the scoring path and the label rule return DETECTED on this set when a large retrieval-specific effect must be present; it says nothing about sensitivity near +2.0. The placebo rows are the only check of how the rule behaves when no effect is present. Neither re-validates a row of F1.

### 10.2 Compute caps

With r_b the batch-size-1 seconds per caption of backbone b read from D1 on that host (model load excluded) and L_b the load seconds: P = Σ_b [N_b r_b + n_runs_b L_b] / 3600. **Hard cap per session = min(30, 1.5 P) GPU-hours**, written to the LOG after D1 and before the first registered step. If 1.5 P exceeds 30, the session is not started as planned: in the rehearsal session this is a STOP before the first step and a return to the judge; in the test session the cap is computed and logged before T0 (§6.5), and the drop order below is applied then, before any OV-test byte is staged. No backbone is run in part: a set that is not complete for an arm gives no row for that arm, and a backbone with an incomplete arm leaves the family as NOT RUN. **Drop order under a binding cap:** LMCap is never dropped; under a binding cap Aya-Vision-8B is dropped first, then InternVL3-8B; a positive control that is not complete counts as NOT DETECTED (rehearsal: no look; test: §7.12). Qwen2.5-VL-7B is never dropped either: without it there is no look (§7.6). On one host the order of steps is therefore R.0, R.1, R.4, R.2, R.3 (and T.0, T0, T.1, T.4, T.2, T.3), so that the control is complete before the backbones that may be dropped start; on two hosts LMCap runs first on its host. Every step runs under `timeout` for the time left; a timeout is a CAP STOP and is final for that step.
