- Train: job 35_a with only R and --seed changed: train_stage2.py --stage sft --data gold_p1dev --epochs 2 --seed <s> --loss-mask assistant (set_seed before the model is built) for s in 43, 44; the last checkpoint (end of training) only.
- Decode: evaluate.py --model qwen2.5-vl-7b --adapter <run> --split dev_data.json --prompt short (NCS_DATASET=ktvic_dev, its defaults) for p1dev_s43, p1dev_s44 and p1dev_soup3_s42s43s44.
- MBR: per image, the medoid of the three members' greedy captions: argmax over a of the sum over b != a of rl_reward.CiderD(c_a, [c_b]), document frequencies from the references of the 3469 train-minus-dev300 images and rl_reward.Segmenter word segmentation (UPE-MBR's selection side, so the selection reads no dev reference); ties go to the lower seed.
- Scoring: MBR captions are scored with evaluate.py --predictions; every system's per-image CIDEr-D comes from rl_report.dev_scores, which asserts that their mean equals evaluate.py's corpus value within 1e-6.

## 6. Analysis

- for v in {soup, MBR}: D_v = mean over dev images of [CIDEr_i(v) - (1/3) * sum over the three members of CIDEr_i(member)], word-level CIDEr-D x100.
- 95% CI: paired percentile bootstrap over dev images, B = 2000, numpy default_rng(0).
- ΔCHAIR_i: CHAIR_i(v) minus the mean of the three members' CHAIR_i, CHAIR-vi against the 5 dev references, in points (x100).
- Reported: BLEU-4, object P/R/F1 (ktvic5, exact classes), mentions per caption and CHAIR_i of every system, and as deltas vs the member mean; panel F1 on the 296 panel images (descriptive); the members' spread of dev CIDEr-D; share of images on which all three members agree.

## 8. Gate, kill rules and consequences

- Eligibility: variant v is eligible iff D_v >= +1.0 and dCHAIR_i(v) <= +1.0 points (point estimates).
- **Gate:** PASS iff at least one variant is eligible; the carried variant is the eligible one with the larger D_v (tie: the soup, a single model); KILL otherwise. It is a go/no-go gate, not a claim (PD-9 Q1).
- K1: D_soup < +1.0 and D_MBR < +1.0. K2: every variant with D_v >= +1.0 has dCHAIR_i > +1.0 points.
- PASS: the carried variant is the S1 component of the single final stacked-system prereg (PD-9 Q4); a confirmatory S1 number comes only from that registration's one test look (PD-9 Q5).
- KILL: S1 is reported as a pre-registered negative; the test-pool proxy stays labelled exploratory.
- Not in this job: no test decode: R14-4's test evaluation of the p1assist soup and its K3 (test F1 drop) belong to the final prereg.
- Test-look ledger: the R14-4 proxy +3.05 [+1.03, +5.11] (MBR over the three p1assist seeds' test captions minus their mean, syllable-level CIDEr-D; an exploratory test look (PD-9 Q5), never used to choose the variant).
