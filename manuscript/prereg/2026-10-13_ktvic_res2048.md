## 1. Purpose and hypothesis (frozen)

All main KTVIC runs cap Qwen2.5-VL's visual token budget at `--max-vision-tokens 512` (`max_pixels = 512 * 28 * 28`). On
KTVIC test (558 images), 52% of images are downscaled at the 512-token cap (mean 417 vision tokens/image), whereas
`--max-vision-tokens 2048` places 92% of KTVIC test images at native resolution (mean 720 vision tokens/image, $+73\%$).

The R9 CPU audit (C7 section 3b) found no recall deficit on the 75 most-downscaled KTVIC test images (`<0.6x` linear
scale) relative to the 290 native-resolution images (`fuse2-short` $+0.4 [-5.5, +6.5]$, `p1-short` $+2.5 [-3.4, +8.8]$),
and showed that KTVIC recall losses are dominated by salience ($k=1$ single-annotator classes carry 22.8 of 52.9 lost
recall points) and lexical choice rather than small-object resolution.

This control tests that null hypothesis directly at inference time by raising `--max-vision-tokens` from `512` to `2048`
with **zero edits** to `scripts/evaluate.py`. No novelty is claimed.

## 3. Runs and binding decision rule (frozen, D10 (iv)(c))

1. **Decision run (`dev300`)**:
   - Evaluate `p1dev_s42` on `dev_data.json` (300 images, `NCS_DATASET=ktvic_dev`) in `short` mode with
     `--max-vision-tokens 2048`, paired against the existing 512-token run `rl/p1dev_s42-dev300-short.preds.json`.
   - **Decision rule (`D10 (iv)(c)`)**: Re-open the resolution lever (e.g., SFT at 2,048 tokens) **only if**
     $\Delta R = R_{2048} - R_{512} \ge +2.0$ percentage points on `dev300`. Otherwise (`dR < +2.0`), close the
     resolution lever as a confirmed null.
2. **Report-only controls (`KTVIC test`, 558 images)**:
   - Evaluate `p1assist_s42` and `fuse2_s42` on `test_data.json` in `short` and `detailed` modes with
     `--max-vision-tokens 2048`, paired against their existing 512-token runs (`B=5000` paired bootstrap on exact and
     hypernym-credited object P/R/F1, plus word-level CIDEr-D and CHAIR).
   - **Pre-registered prediction (C7 section 6 P2)**: $\Delta R \in [-1.0, +1.0]$ and $\Delta P \in [-1.0, +1.0]$
     percentage points.
   - **Binding reporting condition (`D10 (iv)(c)`)**: The main manuscript tables stay at `512` vision tokens whatever
     the result; the `2048`-token numbers are reported in the resolution control / ablation table.
