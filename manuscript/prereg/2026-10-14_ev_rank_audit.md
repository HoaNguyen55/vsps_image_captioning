1. **Count-Matched vs Count-Divergent Regimes**: Across all `79` systems (`v2`), raw reference object `F1` (`ref_F1`) correlates strongly with `panel_F1` ($\rho = +0.6789$, $\tau_b = +0.5411$, concordance `77.05%` overall and `75.73%` on pairs with $|\Delta M| \ge 0.25$, dropping to `81.39%` on count-matched pairs $|\Delta M| < 0.25$).
2. **Precision & Count-Controlled Residual Behavior**: Raw reference precision (`ref_P_100_minus_CHAIRi`) achieves $\rho = +0.9718$ (`93.77%` concordance) with `panel_P`, whereas the count-controlled frontier residual (`delta_P_front`) removes the mention-count trend (`|dM| >= 0.25` concordance `78.65%` vs `panel_P`), achieving its highest concordance with `panel_P` on count-matched pairs (`|dM| < 0.25`: `75.66%`) and within `short` mode (`84.20%`).
3. **Matched-Count `(v, g)` Controls**: Excess precision over `(v, g)` tie-avg (`excess_P_vg_tieavg`) and `POOL-VOTE` (`excess_P_poolvote`) closely track `delta_P_front` ($\rho = +0.9460$ and `+0.8969` vs `panel_P` overall; `88.40%` and `86.05%` pairwise concordance with `panel_P` in `short` mode), confirming that count-controlled residuals measure **within-count precision quality** rather than unconditional coverage.
