# External review: Manuscript C

## Scores (1–5)

- **Novelty: 2/5.** The controlled Vietnamese setting and paired English arm are useful, but the paper does not introduce a new method. Prior image-captioning ICL work already varies retrieval and caption assignment, and retrieval-robustness work already compares relevant and random retrieved captions ([Li et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.08021); [Li et al.](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.503/)).
- **Correctness: 3/5.** Methods, preregistration, deviations and mixed outcomes are unusually transparent. The main inference is conditional on a single random-context realization, and pooled results obscure strong backbone heterogeneity.
- **Contribution: 2/5.** It is a useful controlled empirical study and negative-result record, but the generalizable scientific advance is narrow and depends on two model families and reference-based metrics.
- **Clarity: 3/5.** The core comparison and scope limits are stated clearly; the long disclosures, many labels and dense tables make the central message hard to navigate.

## Recommendation

**TALLIP: Major revision.** The study is potentially publishable as a carefully scoped empirical paper after the inferential and claim-scope issues below are resolved. **Neurocomputing Q1 readiness: not ready.** A venue-level Q1 case would need a broader and more generalizable contribution, stronger model coverage, and evidence that supports more than one model-specific result; polishing alone will not supply that.

## Major issues

1. **Uncertainty in the random control is not represented.** `rand` draws one seeded set of examples per query; the paired image bootstrap then holds those draws fixed. Thus the intervals quantify image-sampling uncertainty conditional on this particular random-context assignment, not variability across plausible random example selections. Clarify this estimand and narrow claims accordingly, or evaluate multiple independently drawn random controls and incorporate that variation. [05_preregistration_and_analysis.md:19,75–77](../journal_draft_C/05_preregistration_and_analysis.md) [03_method.md:22–29](../journal_draft_C/03_method.md)
2. **The pooled headline is not a stable “two-model” effect.** The equal-weight pooled estimand passes while Qwen carries the gain and InternVL3 is near zero on OpenViIC and significantly negative on COCO. Equal weighting is a registered choice, but with just two backbones it is not evidence of a typical MLLM effect. Make model-conditional results primary in the interpretation, explain the pooling rationale, and avoid broad claims that retrieval works across backbones. [05_preregistration_and_analysis.md:19,25–28](../journal_draft_C/05_preregistration_and_analysis.md) [06_results.md:27–45](../journal_draft_C/06_results.md) [08_english_comparison.md:46–60](../journal_draft_C/08_english_comparison.md)
3. **Object-precision claims do not isolate retrieval from in-domain examples.** P2 compares `ret` with zero-shot, although the paper’s key causal contrast is `ret` versus equal-k `rand`. P2 can support a practical comparison of the full retrieval prompt against zero-shot, but cannot establish that selecting similar examples has no precision cost relative to showing arbitrary in-domain captions. State this distinction consistently and, if the latter claim is intended, analyze/register `ret` versus `rand` on the same precision outcome. [05_preregistration_and_analysis.md:27–33,49–54](../journal_draft_C/05_preregistration_and_analysis.md) [06_results.md:45](../journal_draft_C/06_results.md)
4. **The novelty case is too incremental for the Q1 retarget as written.** The paper itself concedes that random controls, retrieval ICL and preregistration are not new; its remaining advance is a language/dataset/model application plus paired reporting. Better distinguish the insight from prior English image-captioning configuration studies, and state what knowledge the Vietnamese/English comparison adds beyond replication. Current coverage and two-backbone evidence are unlikely to sustain a Q1 contribution without a sharper generalizable finding. [02_related_work.md:44–49](../journal_draft_C/02_related_work.md)

## Minor issues

1. **Release claim is not actionable for readers.** The conclusion says code and decision artifacts are released, yet the anonymous draft gives no repository or archive link/DOI. Provide an accessible anonymized artifact or qualify the availability statement. [10_conclusion.md:18–23](../journal_draft_C/10_conclusion.md)
2. **Make companion-study dependencies self-contained.** The object matcher, verifier protocol and count-matching details rely on companion studies “under review”; include enough operational detail or stable supplement references for independent reproduction. [03_method.md:54–69,91–97](../journal_draft_C/03_method.md)
3. **Reduce repetition and front-load the result.** The extensive disclosure record is valuable, but repeated registration and scope reminders across Results, English comparison, Limitations and Conclusion dilute the main narrative. Move procedural detail to the supplement while preserving material deviations in the main text. [06_results.md:3–6](../journal_draft_C/06_results.md) [07_disclosures.md:1–76](../journal_draft_C/07_disclosures.md)
4. **Clarify the title’s scope.** “Vietnamese and English” may imply broad language comparison, but English is a frozen-configuration transfer study on COCO/NoCaps with no English tuning or object-precision prediction. Signal this narrower design in the abstract/title framing. [00_abstract.md:1–7](../journal_draft_C/00_abstract.md) [08_english_comparison.md:7–18](../journal_draft_C/08_english_comparison.md)
