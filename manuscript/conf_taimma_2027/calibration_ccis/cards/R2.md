## Paper card

**Problem and stated limitation of prior work.** Image captioners describe what is visible and miss names, dates, event context and significance, which matter for news photographs. Retrieval-augmented captioners are cited (a kNN-memory transformer, EXTRA, EVCap, a diffusion-guided retriever). The stated limitation is general: earlier retrieval treats an article as one block of text. No specific failure of a cited method is shown.

**Main claim.** A retrieval module that uses the internal structure of news articles, followed by a three-stage captioning pipeline with a vision-language model (VLM) and an LLM, gives context-rich news captions; the system placed fifth in a public challenge on event-enriched captioning, with an overall score of 0.2824 on the private test set.

**Method / resource.** Retrieval: each article is split into headline, lead, body sections and image captions, encoded and summed with component weights; images inside articles get CLIP ViT-B/32 features modulated by a small learned network over their position and surrounding text. Three similarities between query image and article are combined with learned weights: image-to-text content, best image-to-image match, and a position-weighted image score. Scores are adjusted by a 7-day temporal cluster term (weight 0.2) and by PageRank on a citation graph (weight 0.1). Captioning: (1) a VLM writes a structured description of the image; (2) the description is embedded with a text-only model and the three most similar sentences of the top-1 article are kept with up to two neighbours each; (3) an LLM writes the caption from the image, the description and these sentences. The VLM and the LLM are not named; prompts are not printed.

**Theorem, bound, identification result or provable property.** None.

**Data and splits (sizes).** OpenEvents V1, the challenge dataset: more than 200,000 news articles and 400,000 images from two English-language news outlets, 2011–2022. A public and a private test set are used; their sizes are not given. Weights were tuned on "a held-out subset" of unstated size.

**Models and model families.** CLIP ViT-B/32 with added projection layers; the M3 text-embedding model; one unnamed VLM and one unnamed LLM.

**Baselines and their years.** Internal variants only: CLIP-only retrieval, plus structured content, plus image-to-image; and captioning from the image alone. Four higher-ranked challenge entries are quoted from the leaderboard. None of the cited retrieval-augmented captioners (2022, 2023, 2024) is run.

**Key numbers (as printed).** Retrieval on the public test set (mAP / R@1 / R@10): CLIP only 0.11 / 0.08 / 0.10; plus structured content 0.15 / 0.12 / 0.16; plus image-to-image 0.95 / 0.942 / 0.985; full 0.97 / 0.956 / 0.991. Captioning on the public test set: CIDEr 0.039 without retrieval and 0.123 with the pipeline; CLIPScore 0.890 without and 0.883 with. Private test set: overall 0.2824, mAP 0.708, R@1 0.663, R@10 0.801, CLIPScore 0.783, CIDEr 0.081; the first-ranked entry has overall 0.5501, mAP 0.991, CIDEr 0.210.

**Ablations, seeds, statistical tests, confidence intervals.** One additive retrieval ablation (four rows) and one two-row captioning comparison. The temporal and citation refinement and the three captioning stages are not ablated separately. Seeds, repeated runs, statistical tests and confidence intervals: not reported; the text nevertheless says "significantly outperforms".

**Failure analysis.** None systematic. The authors suggest, without a test, that the top entries used more fine-tuning or augmentation, and attribute the low CIDEr to wording differences. The fall in retrieval from the public test set (mAP 0.97) to the private one (0.708) is not discussed. Four qualitative examples, all favourable.

**Code/data release and licence statements.** Source code is stated to be public in a repository. No licence is stated.

**Positioning against closest prior work.** Prose only, two short paragraphs; no comparison table and no head-to-head experiment.

**Out-of-domain or cross-language checks.** None: one dataset, English news, one challenge.

**Stated limitations.** No limitations section. Future work: other document types, more relevance signals, faster generation.
