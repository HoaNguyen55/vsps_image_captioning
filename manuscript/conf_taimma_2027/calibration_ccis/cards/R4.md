## Paper card

**Problem and stated limitation of prior work.** Captions for figures in scientific papers are generated either as image-to-text or as summarisation of the paragraphs that mention the figure. The paper states that each view uses incomplete information (the first lacks domain context, the second misses visual trends), and that training captions taken from arXiv are often poor, citing an earlier finding that over 50% are unhelpful to domain experts.

**Main claim.** A pipeline in which several LLMs filter the training data, write candidate captions, and select and edit the best one produces captions that human judges rank above author-written captions when captions may be long, and above two other challenge entries.

**Method / resource.** (1) Quality filter: GPT-4o scores 3,000 training captions from 1 to 6; a LLaVA model fine-tuned on these scores labels the whole training set (Kendall's tau with GPT-4o 0.5502 on 200 samples); captions scored 5 or 6 are kept. (2) Candidates: GPT-4o prompted with ten randomly chosen top-scored example captions of the same subject and its own description of the figure; LLaMA-3-8B and Yi-1.5-9B fine-tuned on paragraphs, mentions, OCR text and a figure description from MiniCPM-V; Pegasus fine-tuned on paragraphs and OCR text. (3) Judgement: GPT-4o picks one of the four candidates and edits it under a word limit (50 words "long", 30 words "short").

**Theorem, bound, identification result or provable property.** None.

**Data and splits (sizes).** SciCap (360,340 train / 47,639 validation / 47,639 test) and SciCap+ (394,005 train), de-duplicated by paper; after rule-based and quality filtering, 135,935 training samples. In the 3,000-caption sample 48.58% scored 5 and 27.11% scored 6.

**Models and model families.** GPT-4o (closed); LLaVA on LLaMA-3-8B; MiniCPM-V 2.5; LLaMA-3-8B-Instruct; Yi-1.5-9B-Chat; Pegasus-large.

**Baselines and their years.** The pipeline's own fine-tuned components; two other challenge entries (both 2024); author-written captions. No published figure-captioning system from outside the challenge is re-run.

**Key numbers (as printed).** Human study 1 (three computer-vision experts, 91 figures, best and worst choice): the long variant is chosen best most often (counts only in a figure); Fleiss' kappa 0.154 for best and 0.382 for worst. In the selection step the GPT-4o candidate was chosen in 89.38% of cases (LLaMA 6.17%, Yi 4.23%, Pegasus 0.19%). Human study 2 (run by the challenge organisers; three judges; 200 figures pre-selected by caption length and an LLM quality score; mean rank, lower is better): long track 1.27, against 2.84 for author captions and 2.82 and 3.08 for the other entries; short track 1.74, against 1.52 for author captions (authors better) and 3.56 and 3.18; judges' Kendall's tau 0.3589 (long) and 0.1100 (short). LLM-scored quality on 200 test samples (scale 1–6): GPT-4o alone 5.390, with post-edit 5.405, multi-LLM 5.430, both 5.440; filtering and figure descriptions raise LLaMA-3-8B from 4.612 to 5.030. ROUGE-L 0.257 (long) and 0.310 (short) against 0.418 for Pegasus.

**Ablations, seeds, statistical tests, confidence intervals.** Two component ablations, scored by an LLM judge of the same family as the main generator and selector. Seeds, repeated runs, statistical tests and confidence intervals: not reported; the multi-LLM gain over GPT-4o alone is 0.05 points on 200 samples, without a test.

**Failure analysis.** A discussion of low rater agreement and of the disagreement between n-gram metrics and human preference. No analysis of factual errors in generated captions.

**Code/data release and licence statements.** Code is stated to be public in a repository; prompts are in an appendix; the datasets are public. No licence is stated.

**Positioning against closest prior work.** Prose; no point-by-point table. The challenge ranking is the only comparison with other groups.

**Out-of-domain or cross-language checks.** None: arXiv figures only, English only; the expert study covers computer-vision figures only.

**Stated limitations.** Cost and latency of running several models; reliance on a closed model; evaluation limited to arXiv papers.
