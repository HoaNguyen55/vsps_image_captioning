## Paper card

**Problem and stated limitation of prior work.** Visual question answering (VQA) resources for Vietnamese are few. The paper states that ViVQA and OpenViVQA are too small to train current multimodal models, that their questions are mostly object-centred or text-centred, that multi-step, causal and culturally grounded reasoning is under-represented, that manual annotation is costly, and that naive generation with a language model adds hallucinations.

**Main claim.** A fully automatic pipeline, in which a large language model (LLM) writes questions and answers under a reasoning-level schema and an ensemble of models filters them, yields a larger and more varied Vietnamese VQA dataset, and models trained on it improve "consistently" (one model reaches about three times the F1 and eight times the CIDEr it had before training on the new data).

**Method / resource.** Images come from MS COCO; Vietnamese captions and conversations for them come from the VISTA corpus. Gemini 2.5 Flash is prompted with the merged captions of an image and a target reasoning level, and writes one question and five short answers grounded in the captions. Five levels (recognition, spatial/relational, compositional, commonsense/causal, text-in-image) have target shares 0.05, 0.24, 0.40, 0.24, 0.05; nine question categories are mapped to levels. Quality control: sanity checks removed 167 of 60,000 samples; an ensemble of "2n + 1" unnamed models scores 18 quality dimensions; each is binarised at the dataset median, the models vote by majority, and a sample is kept if it passes at least 9 of 18; about 37,000 remain; under-sampling then balances the categories.

**Theorem, bound, identification result or provable property.** None.

**Data and splits (sizes).** 19,411 images, 37,077 questions, 185,385 answers (five per question). The split is given twice and differently: 80% train / 20% validation in the dataset description, and 8:1:1 train / validation / test in the experiments. Whether images are disjoint across splits is not stated.

**Models and model families.** Vietnamese vision-language models: Vintern (base and fine-tuned), ViT5 with ViT, BARTPhoBEiT. General models: GPT-5, LLaMA 3.2, Gemini 2.0 Flash, Gemini 2.5 Flash (the dataset's generator is also a tested model). Which models were trained on the new data is clear only for Vintern.

**Baselines and their years.** Vintern-1B (2024), BARTPhoBEiT (2023). Dataset comparison: VQA v2 (2017), OpenViVQA (2023), ViCLEVR (2023), ViOCRVQA (2024), ViTextVQA (2025). No model is trained on an existing Vietnamese VQA dataset for comparison, and the unfiltered generated data is not used as a control.

**Key numbers (test set, as printed).** Vintern base → fine-tuned: accuracy 0.0012 → 0.1301, F1 0.1755 → 0.5376, CIDEr 0.0854 → 0.7284. BARTPhoBEiT: BLEU 0.4329, CIDEr 1.8896 (highest). GPT-5: F1 0.5089. Gemini 2.5 Flash: precision 0.2443, recall 0.7666, F1 0.2475. Highest accuracy of any model: 0.1301.

**Ablations, seeds, statistical tests, confidence intervals.** No ablation of the schema, the filter or the 9-of-18 rule. Seeds, repeated runs, statistical tests and confidence intervals: not reported. Human check: 1,000 sampled question-answer pairs rated by three annotators on fluency, correctness against the image and fit of the reasoning level; Krippendorff's alpha 0.72; the share judged acceptable is given only as "the majority".

**Failure analysis.** Four residual error types from the human check (under-specified questions, generic answers, awkward phrasing, hallucinated attributes), together "fewer than 6%" of inspected samples. No analysis of model errors by level or category.

**Code/data release and licence statements.** Not stated in the text read: no repository link and no licence for the new dataset.

**Positioning against closest prior work.** One table compares the new dataset with five datasets on eight descriptive criteria. No experimental comparison.

**Out-of-domain or cross-language checks.** None. Models trained on the new data are not tested on another Vietnamese VQA benchmark.

**Stated limitations.** COCO images limit Vietnamese-specific scenes; biases of the generating models may remain; standard Vietnamese only.
