3. RISKS
  - P1 may show raw F1 tracks humans as well as the residual -> the paper becomes "a validity audit", Q2 tier.
  - TMLR's no-extension/no-reuse rule vs FAIR 2026: one FAIR table reused = desk-reject risk.
  - Q1 reviewers (TACL/IJCV) weigh breadth; one own backbone + small UIT-ViIC (231) may cap at major revision.
  - Licence refusal (PD-2 Q4) removes the OpenViIC panel (C3 of the paper).
  Would change my view: positive P1 (-> TACL first, ~35-40% Q1); N9 showing training methods survive count
  control (-> stronger constructive story).
