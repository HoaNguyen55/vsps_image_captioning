1. EVIDENCE
1a. MODEL AND PROPOSITION (all algebra mine; cited pieces marked)
Model. Object o in image i has naming propensity pi_o = P(one reference names o). Unlike Chen et al. 2015 §4.2 (arXiv 1504.00325,
  read: one p per WORD, "we ignore image level saliency", subjects independent given o; H_p = pk/(pk+(1-p)^k), H_r = p), pi varies
  within the image (Spain & Perona's salience). (A1) the k references are exchangeable and independent given the image. Then
  P(o in G_k) = c_k(pi) = 1-(1-pi)^k and Gbar(k) = U(k) = sum_o E c_k(pi_o) is the incidence species-accumulation curve.
  Systems of one frontier family at count M: mention m is in the nameable population w.p. s_m (else hallucinated or
  guideline-unnameable) with propensity law F_m. TP_k(M) = sum_{m<=M} s_m g_k(m), g_k(m) = E_{F_m} c_k(pi).
  (A2) salience ordering: F_m falls in m in the likelihood-ratio order. (A3) the family shares s_m and F_m (G2's H4 invariance;
  prefix-nesting is NOT needed, consistent with G3 3f).
PROPOSITION. Let b_k(M) = dlnTP_k/dlnM = p_marg/P (G2/G3 identity).
 (a) b_k(M) is nondecreasing in k. b_1 = M s_M E_{F_M}[pi] / sum_m s_m E_{F_m}[pi]; b_inf = M s_M / sum_m s_m =: 1-D (system-only decay).
 (b) Gbar(k) is increasing and concave in k.
 (c) micro-F1 = 2TP/(M+Gbar) rises with M iff p_marg > F1/2 [Lipton et al. 2014, Thm 1 proof / Cor. 1: CITE, not new]
     iff b_k/(1-b_k) > M/Gbar(k). With constant b, F1 has a unique maximum at M*(k) = b_k Gbar(k)/(1-b_k) (L1's closed form).
 (d) SINGLE CROSSING. Lambda_k(M) = logit b_k(M) + ln Gbar(k) - ln M is nondecreasing in k, so the sign of F1's verbosity reward
     changes at most once as references are added, and only from - to +; M*(k) is nondecreasing.
 Closed form (deterministic salience pi_m = pi_1 m^-theta, s_m ~ m^-D): b_k(M) ~ 1 - D - theta*phi_k(pi_M), where
     phi_k(pi) = k pi (1-pi)^(k-1) / (1-(1-pi)^k) is the elasticity of c_k (phi_1 = 1, phi_k falls to 0). theta = b_inf - b_1 is the
     reference-incompleteness part of the exponent deficit; D is the system part (G3's b_sys = b_pool - D is this split).
Proof sketch. (a) 1/b_k = sum_m [s_m/(M s_M)] g_k(m)/g_k(M). d/dk ln c_k(pi) = h(x)/k with x = (1-pi)^k and h(x) = -x ln x/(1-x),
  increasing because h' ~ x-1-ln x >= 0; so c_k'/c_k falls in pi for k' > k (c is reverse-rule RR2 in (k,pi)). Under (A2) the
  symmetrised double integral over x<y of [c_k'(x)c_k(y) - c_k(x)c_k'(y)][f_M(x)f_m(y) - f_M(y)f_m(x)] is >= 0 (Karlin's
  composition), so g_k(m)/g_k(M) falls in k for m < M and 1/b_k falls. c_inf = 1 for pi > 0 gives b_inf.
  (b) each c_k(pi) is increasing and concave in k. (c) dlnF1/dlnM = b - M/(M+Gbar); at M*, d2 = -b(1-b)/M*^2 < 0.
  (d) logit b_k rises by (a), ln Gbar(k) by (b). Self-checked numerically (h4_check.py _check: RR2 on a grid, k = 1..10).
Single-writer references (KTVIC §3.2: one writer types all 5 captions) violate (A1) twice:
 (i) positive dependence. For associated indicators P(o not in G_k) >= (1-pi)^k (Esary, Proschan & Walkup 1967), so coverage and
     Gbar(k) shrink. For a lag-1 chain with autocorrelation rho, c_k = 1-(1-pi)(1-(1-rho)pi)^(k-1) ~ k_eff*pi for small pi, with
     k_eff = 1+(k-1)(1-rho): k captions of one writer act as k_eff < k annotators, b(k) ~ b_ind(k_eff) rises more slowly and the
     crossing k moves right. The chain kernel is still RR2 (checked), so (a)-(d) hold in k_eff.
 (ii) slot non-stationarity (pi differs by caption slot, e.g. a "headline" first caption). It breaks exchangeability even at k=1.
 Under (A1), every k-subset design (first-k, random-k, fixed slots) has the same expected b(k), Gbar(k) and F1 slope.
TESTABLE COROLLARIES
 C-1 completeness law: on fixed outputs b(1) <= ... <= b(5) <= b(inf), and instance+refs gold (which also credits present but
     never-named objects) sits at or above the reference limit. A gold-uniform (SCAR-type) thinning leaves b unchanged.
 C-2 single crossing: across k the F1-on-lnM slope changes sign at most once, - to +, and sign(slope) = sign(Lambda_k(M0)) at the
     family centre M0. Two channels push the same way: logit b (salience-dependent missingness; zero under SCAR) and ln Gbar.
 C-3 exchangeability signature: exchangeable refs give design-invariant b, Gbar, slope; single-writer refs give lag-1 pairs <
     lag>=2 pairs (Gbar, b), contiguous first-k < random-k, a later crossing, and slot-1 effects at k=1.

 MT   Freitag, Grangier & Caswell, arXiv 2004.06063, EMNLP 2020 [V abs]. Paraphrased references raise correlation;
      "multi-reference BLEU does not improve the correlation for high quality output". Nothing on length or a sign.
      BLEU brevity penalty with "closest reference length" (Papineni et al. 2002) is more lenient with more refs [S]; Post,

 SUMM Sun, Shapira, Dagan & Nenkova, "How to Compare Summarizers without Target Length?", NeuralGen 2019, ACL W19-2303 [V
      anthology listing + abstract]. ROUGE F1 varies non-linearly with summary length; they normalise by a random system of
      the SAME length. This is the closest precedent for C1 (count-matched control) and must be cited. Single reference
      (CNN/DM), so no k-dependence. Our M* = b*Gbar/(1-b) predicts their finding: long references (large Gbar) put M* above
      system lengths.
 IR   Buckley & Voorhees SIGIR 2004, doi 10.1145/1008992.1009000 (bpref; unjudged = nonrelevant biases measures) [L2];
      Lu, Moffat & Culpepper, Inf. Retr. J. 19(4):416-445, 2016 [S, author page]: recall-based metrics are more volatile
      across evaluation and pool depth than utility metrics; Webber, Moffat & Zobel EVIA 2010 [S]. Structurally the closest
      analogue: pool depth = reference completeness, retrieval depth = verbosity, and unjudged-as-nonrelevant penalises deep
      retrieval under shallow pools. I found no IR result stating that the sign of a depth/cutoff preference flips with pool depth.
 MLL/PU  Jain, Prabhu & Varma KDD 2016 doi 10.1145/2939672.2939756 (propensity-scored metrics; missing labels bias toward
      head labels) [L1 Crossref]. PU evaluation review arXiv 2206.02423 [S]: F is estimable only under SCAR (labelling
      independent of the instance). Our b-channel IS the non-SCAR part. Under SCAR, b is invariant and M* only scales with the
      label frequency c (M*_obs = c*M*_true). So "observed F's optimal threshold moves with missingness" is known in spirit,

 ECO  Incidence rarefaction/accumulation U(k) (Colwell et al. 2012 J. Plant Ecol.; Chao et al. 2014 Ecol. Monogr.
