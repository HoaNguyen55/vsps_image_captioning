3. RISKS
- All dev numbers are from 300 images, CI half-width ~1.15 F1; the kNN effect may shrink on test (winner's curse: the
  primary was pre-declared, but I examined 3 kNN variants).
- Text-kNN conditions on the model's own captions: if the captions are wrong, retrieval reinforces them; also reads
  KTVIC train refs (single-writer style), so the panel gain may not replicate -> watch L.
- Probe trained on ref-union labels learns typicality; its train-item candidate set differs from pool candidates.
- Test pools have 7 votes, not 13: votes are noisier, which favours any extra evidence (would inflate test gains).
What would change my view: text-kNN fails P1 on test (then drop retrieval; P(EV-RANK) -> 10%); probe in-cell AUROC
>= 0.68 on dev (then promote the probe to a method and to M3's critic).
