0. BOTTOM LINE
- YES, human-judgment validation can go in the paper, but as a MEASUREMENT-VALIDITY section, not as proof that the count-
  controlled residual or N1 "is what humans want". Of 5 frozen predictions: P3 held on THumB; P1a held twice; P1b, P2c, P5
  FAILED; P2 kill rule fired.
- What humans DO support: (i) object precision agrees with expert precision far better under complete gold (THumB pairwise
  0.736 -> 0.903 from 1-ref to inst+refs gold); (ii) experts do not penalise "pseudo-FPs" (beta -0.09 vs -1.16 for real FPs);
  (iii) matched-count pairs are where object metrics agree most with humans (0.85 T / 0.95 C vs 0.77 / 0.84 count-differing).
- What humans do NOT support: the residual z beats raw CHAIR for overall quality (it is WORSE, -19 / -8 points), and
  "complete-gold F1's verbosity reward is right" (humans are count-neutral; full-gold F1 prefers the longer caption 83% of the time).
- Caveat that limits everything: short COCO captions (M = 1-2 classes), English only, pre-LVLM systems.

3. RISKS, AND WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY JUDGEMENT
 - Regime mismatch: THumB/Composite captions name 1-2 COCO classes; E1's methods name ~4-8 at 512 tokens. Humans being count-
   neutral here says nothing about long captions. G3 (or LongCap-Arena/CapArena) is required before any claim about LVLMs.
 - Composite = 1 crowd rater per HIT and 2015 template systems; P3/P4 failures there may be noise. THumB has 4 annotators but
   only 16 confounded pairs.
 - Licences: THumB and Composite have no licence file; use aggregates only and cite (consistent with PD-2 #7).
 - "full" gold = COCO instances U 4 refs is itself incomplete (80 classes); human agreement 0.90 is an upper bound for this vocab.
 - Would change my view: G3 showing matched-count object precision tracks 14-VLM human factuality better than raw -> upgrade the
   residual/two-column claim to "human-supported in the LVLM regime".

5. DISAGREEMENTS (with our R10/R11/PD-3 line and Gemini's D16)
 - D16's two-column standard (raw F1 + count-controlled residual) should NOT be sold as "closer to human judgment": in both
   human sets the residual z agrees with overall human preference LESS than raw CHAIR (-19 / -8 pts). Justify the residual as
   a decomposition/attribution tool (what changed: count vs choice), not as a better quality score. Confidence 80%.
 - N1 should be framed as a property of the METRIC (sign flips with gold; replicated here: F1's long-caption preference 0.41 ->
   0.83 THumB, 0.54 -> 0.73 Composite), not as "sparse gold misjudges what humans prefer": humans are count-neutral and sit
   nearer the 1-ref value. Do not write "complete gold recovers human verbosity preferences". Confidence 80%.
 - ADD to C2: gold completeness is the dominant driver of human validity of object precision (0.74 -> 0.90). This is the most
   paper-ready human-backed number we have; it supports the two-gold design (C3) and argues for instance/panel gold. 75%.
 - Expected bar effect: human validation as a supporting section ~ Q2 +5, Q1 +3; it cannot carry the paper. G3 is the one
   check that could lift Q1 materially. Revised estimate after this round: Q2 60-68%, Q1 18-25%. Confidence 65%.
