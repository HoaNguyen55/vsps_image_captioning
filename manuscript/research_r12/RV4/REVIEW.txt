== WHAT THE PAPER CAN SAFELY SAY ABOUT N1 ==
 - On COCO-2500 (L1, predictions written before the run), the F1-vs-log M slope across systems goes from negative under
   one reference to positive under five references and more positive under instance labels. This is the only
   confirmatory evidence.
 - On UIT-ViIC test (231 images, 10 systems; exploratory, not pre-registered), the slope rises with gold completeness
   in every system group and both lexicons. Paired differences exclude 0: COCO-80 lexicon, all 10 systems: +20.7
   [16.4, 25.0] from 1 to 5 references and +32.7 [28.0, 37.3] from 5 references to instance gold.
 - On UIT-ViIC the slope is strongly positive under COCO instance gold (e.g. +37.5 [34.4, 40.2], all 10 systems), and
   it is negative under a single reference when zero-shot systems are included (-15.9 [-20.6, -11.0]).
 - The paper must NOT claim that the 1-ref -> 5-ref sign flip replicates on UIT-ViIC. With the paper's own matcher the
   5-ref slope stays negative (-6.3 [-8.4, -4.0]). Under COCO-80 it is positive but its CI touches 0 (+4.8
   [-0.2, +9.7]). Under COCO-80 without the zero-shot systems the 1-ref slope is not significant (-5.1 [-13.7, +4.0]).
 - Safe wording: "The verbosity reward of object F1 depends on gold completeness. It increases monotonically from
   single-reference to instance-level gold (pre-registered on COCO; replicated in direction, post hoc, on UIT-ViIC). The
   sign at five references depends on the matcher." No XM3600 claim. Do not use the numbers -9.94/+12.14/+22.87.
