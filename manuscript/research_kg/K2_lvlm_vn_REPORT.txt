======================================================================================================================
0. BOTTOM LINE
======================================================================================================================
- A KG is not a metric lever for KTVIC short captions. Every published case where a graph- or KG-structured term was added
  to an n-gram reward, or where a graph or KG was added to a strong captioner, moved CIDEr/BLEU-4/METEOR/ROUGE-L by about
  1 point:
  - SPIDEr: C +0.5, B4 -1.1.
  - MGRL-RSCC: C +0.56, B4 +1.02.
  - RA-T kNN memory after SCST: C +1.1 to +1.4.
  - MERGE's KG part alone: C -0.3.
  Entity and cultural KG-RAG cannot lift reference metrics, because KTVIC references carry no entities.
- Label-free CPU pilot on KTVIC (B1's best-of-7 protocol: pick on refs A, score on refs B and on the independent OpenViIC
  panel). Adding an object/noun F1 term to the CIDEr-D reward gives:
  - CIDEr on held-out refs: -2.9 to +2.4 over 48 settings. Every 95% CI covers 0 (two touch it).
  - BLEU-4: at most +-0.6. ROUGE-L: -0.5 to +0.9.
  - CHAIR_i against the panel: about -1 point with the repo's own 113-entry lexicon.
  The Wikidata versions (KG synonyms plus IsA credit over a 211-noun vocabulary) do no better than the hand lexicon, also
  on noun-level hallucination: -0.9 to +0.2.
- Graph uses compatible with CN-RLOO and no new labels, all low novelty:
  - a reference-derived object/IsA term as a hallucination guard;
  - a detector-evidence object penalty (model-generated, so the user may veto it);
  - kNN retrieval of KTVIC train captions. It opens a second copy channel that CN-RLOO's mask does not cover.
  None of these reaches the +8 CIDEr bar or the -4 CHAIR_i Pareto bar.
- Vietnamese KG resources, measured on the 200 most frequent KTVIC train nouns (image-weighted share in brackets):
  - Wikidata vi label/alias: 81.0% [87.2%]. IsA (P279) parent: 72.5%. Parent with a vi label: 53.0% [60.7%]. vi synonyms:
    43.5%. All three: 30.0%.
  - Automatic sense linking is right for 84-88% of terms (COCO-80 check).
  - The uncurated IsA closure is 72% generic sinks: product, part, goods.
  - ConceptNet 5.7 Vietnamese: 171,987 edges, ZERO IsA and ZERO commonsense relations. Its commonsense edges are reachable
    only through an English pivot, and the two pivots (ConceptNet vs Wikidata) agree on the English sense for 74% of nouns.
  - OMW Wiktionary-derived Vietnamese wordnet: 53.5% [59.4%].
  - Kaikki/Wiktionary: glosses for 96.0% [98.8%], hypernyms for 1 noun.
  - VietSGG: 0.3% of noun mentions.
  - The repo's CHAIR lexicon sees only 42% of noun mentions in KTVIC test references.
- Scoop check: 47 logged searches. No paper puts a KG, scene graph or cultural knowledge into Vietnamese caption
  generation, evaluation or RL rewards.
  - Closest: ViG (MAPR 2026; relation + "local-cultural" memories on KTVIC) and an unreadable FAIR 2025 KG-captioning paper.
  - Blocked claims: "first SCST/RL for Vietnamese captioning" (ObjectAoA 2023, VieCap4H 2022, OpenViIC baselines) and
    "first Vietnamese SG data" (VietSGG).
- Judgement: keep KG out of the method headline. At most, keep a lexicon object term (a hallucination guard checked on the
  OpenViIC panel and on COCO instance labels) and a measured resource-coverage paragraph. Making Wikidata IsA safe would
  need hand curation, which counts as annotation under the R7 rule.

2. PART 1 - KG AND GRAPH USES WITH LVLMs, 2024-2026 (numbers as reported: baseline -> +graph)
======================================================================================================================
Notation: C = CIDEr(-D), B4 = BLEU-4, M = METEOR, RL = ROUGE-L, CHs/CHi = CHAIR_s/CHAIR_i.
"Needs labels" means human labels that do not exist for Vietnamese.

2.1 Base fact: what CIDEr RL itself does to hallucination (decides whether a graph guard is needed)
- Rohrbach et al., Object Hallucination in Image Captioning, EMNLP 2018 (1809.02156). CE -> SCST(CIDEr), COCO Karpathy:
  - TopDown: C 107.6->117.2, CHs 8.4->13.6, CHi 6.1->8.8.
  - TopDown-BB: C 113.7->120.6, CHs 8.3->10.4.
  - FC: C 95.8->103.9, CHs 15.4->14.4.
  - Authors: "CIDEr does not penalize object hallucination sufficiently".
- Dai et al., Plausible May Not Be Faithful, EACL 2023 (2210.07688). Strong VLP models without -> with SCST:
  - OFA-L: C 140.5->149.5, B4 41.7->43.8, CHi 4.7->4.2, CHs 8.9->8.1.
  - VinVL-B: C 129.3->140.4, CHi 5.3->5.7, CHs 10.0->10.9.
  - NoCaps out-of-domain (VinVL-B): CHi 12.1->17.4, CHs 21.2->32.1.
=> At P1's strength (C about 145) CIDEr RL is roughly CHAIR-neutral in domain; the risk is out of domain (the UIT-ViIC and
   COCO replications). B1's oracle picks lowered the reference-derived KTVIC CHi from 17.7 to 10.9.

2.2 KG- or graph-structured REWARDS for RL
- SPIDEr, Liu et al., ICCV 2017 (1612.00370; DOI 10.1109/ICCV.2017.100). Reward 0.5 SPICE (scene-graph tuples) + 0.5 CIDEr.
  COCO C5 test server:
  - MLE: C 94.7, M 25.1, RL 53.1, B4 29.4.
  - PG-CIDEr: 99.5 / 24.9 / 54.8 / 33.3.
  - PG-SPIDEr: 100.0 / 25.1 / 54.4 / 32.2.
  - Graph term on top of CIDEr: C +0.5, B4 -1.1, M +0.2, RL -0.4.
  - Humans prefer PG-SPIDEr (+4 points vs PG-BCMR, p=0.014).
  - PG-SPICE alone gives ungrammatical repetitions.
  - Needs an English SG parser.
- MGRL-RSCC, Wang et al., arXiv 2609.26166 (Aug 2026; IEEE-Trans template, preprint). Remote-sensing change captioning.
  The structural reward includes a rule-based (head, relation, tail) triplet F1 against the 5 references, from a hand tag
  lexicon. No new labels: this is the closest analogue of a Vietnamese lexicon/KG term. LEVIR-CC, Table IV, single run:
  - CE: C 136.25, B4 62.98, M 39.42, RL 74.34.
  - + metric reward: 138.08 / 63.54 / 39.85 / 74.58.
  - + metric + structural/KG: 138.64 / 64.56 / 40.23 / 74.95.
  - Structural/KG term on top of the metric reward: C +0.56, B4 +1.02, M +0.38, RL +0.37.
  - In its own top-k ablation, k=4 vs k=3 moves C by -3.89, so +0.56 is inside the run-to-run spread.
- RadGraph reward, Delbrouck et al., Findings EMNLP 2022 (2210.12186). Entity/relation graph-F1 reward only. NLL -> RL:
  - MIMIC-CXR: B4 10.5->11.4, RL 25.3->26.5, F1-CheXbert 44.8->62.2, RG_E 23.0->37.1.
  - Open-i: B4 11.4->13.9.
  - Graph reward: factual metrics +14 to +17, n-gram metrics about +1.
  - Needs an extractor trained on radiologist labels.
- SC-Captioner, ICCV 2025 (2508.06125). Set-difference reward over objects/attributes/relations from the English FACTUAL
  parser; trained on 6.5K expert-revised COCO captions (new labels in their setting).
  - Qwen2-VL-7B, DOCCI500: B4 40.92->43.70 (44.88 with self-correction), M 22.04->23.35.
  - COCO-LN500: B4 34.59->35.29.
  - LLaVA-1.5: B4 33.81->32.72.
  - No CIDEr or CHAIR reported.
- FoCUS, EMNLP 2026 Main (2609.00709). GRPO with scene-graph component rewards on 280 CompreCap images with human dense
  SGs (needs labels).
  - Qwen2.5-VL-3B: SCoPE 22.61->31.74, CompreCap 47.08->50.82.
  - General DOCCI captioning, InternVL3-2B: C 11.10->9.08, RL 21.96->21.18. An SG reward without a CIDEr term leaves
    CIDEr flat or lower.
- CheXPO-v2 (2512.17213, preprint). GRPO; KG-consistency (Jaccard over Disease-Relation-Anatomy triplets) added to an
  outcome reward. MIMIC-CXR-VQA accuracy: SFT 0.752, outcome reward 0.805, + KG 0.828. Needs a domain NER/RE model.
- R1-SGG (2504.13617, preprint). Graph recall rewards for SG generation. VG150 AP@50 6.07->19.47. Needs SG labels.
- ESREAL, ECCV 2024 (2403.16167). Unsupervised hallucination penalties typed by object, attribute and relation (via
  reconstruction plus grounding) in PPO. LLaVA, Stanford Paragraph:
  - CHs 0.64->0.43 (-32.8%), CHi 0.18->0.12, C 4.52->9.10.
  - Without the holistic reward the model hacks by shortening: C 7.85->3.83.
- FGAIF (TMLR 2025 per PDF header; 2404.05046). Object/attribute/relation AI-feedback rewards.
  - LLaVA-7B: CHi 9.1->3.9, CHs 22.0->6.2.
  - Caption length 216->60 tokens: a length hack.
- MOCHa, EMNLP 2024, pp. 22680-22698, DOI 10.18653/v1/2024.emnlp-main.1263. Not a graph method; it is the trade-off evidence at
  P1-like strength. NLI fidelity + BERTScore adequacy, PPO:
  - BLIP-L: C 138.4->139.6, B4 41.5->41.9, CHi 2.3->2.1.
  - BLIP-2: C 144.3->144.3, CHi 1.7->1.4.

2.4 KG-RAG and retrieval-augmented captioning
- RA-T, ACM TOMM 2024 (DOI 10.1145/3663667; 2405.13127). The only paper found that combines a kNN caption memory with
  CIDEr SCST.
  - CE stage: C 121.6->123.7/124.5.
  - After SCST, from a baseline of 135.3: C 136.4/136.5 (COCO index), 136.7 (CC3M index); B4 39.7->40.1-41.0;
    M 29.6->29.6-30.0; RL 59.4->59.4-59.8.
  - The retrieval gain halves after RL.
- EVCap, CVPR 2024 (2311.15879). Ablation, memory vs none:
  - COCO C 134.4->140.1, NoCaps val 108.8->119.3.
  - CE only, no CHAIR.
- Retrieval robustness, ACL 2024 (2406.02265). SmallCap copies "majority tokens" of the retrieved captions; random
  retrieval costs up to 50% of CIDEr.
- NES, AAAI 2026 (2511.08909; venue per the OJS listing seen in search results).
  - 25.1% (NEF) / 23.4% (CLIP-Det) of hallucinated entities come from retrieved text.
  - Flickr->COCO: C 87.5, CHs 12.1, CHi 8.0 with the filter.
- MERGE, AAAI 2026 (2511.21002). Entity KB + KG for news captioning.
  - GoodNews: C 87.70->94.54 (+6.84).
  - Ablation: the KG-subgraph module alone gives 85.79, -0.3 vs 86.08; the gain comes from entity matching against

- MsRAG, IJCAI 2025. Object-level RAG: Qwen2-VL Kale C 16.8->13.3 (lower); user study 2.8->5.5/10.
- RAVENEA, ICLR 2026 (2505.14462). The "+11%" is RegionScore 57.5->68.8: does the caption name the country.
  - Kendall tau with human judgements on cultural captioning: CIDEr -0.316 (ns), RegionScore 0.442.
  - 8 countries, no Vietnam.
- Re3Cap, EMNLP 2026 Main (2608.21305). Retrieval as a reasoning signal inside GRPO; QA-based metrics only.
  - Qwen2.5-VL-7B relations QA: 44.15->46.02.
  - No CIDEr or CHAIR. It already occupies "retrieval inside caption RL".
- Multilingual retrieval captioning, XM3600 Vietnamese CIDEr:
  - PAELLA (Findings NAACL 2024): 38.0; mBLIP mT0-XL 39.2.
  - ConCap (COLM 2025; outside the venue list): no retrieval 31.8 -> concepts + captions 45.1.
  - Large retrieval gains appear only on weak bases (30-45 CIDEr).
- Graph RAG for VLMs (mKG-RAG SIGIR 2026, MMGraphRAG, MG2-RAG, KG-ViP) is VQA-only. AKGMA (AAAI 2026): OJS page
  unreachable, no numbers.

======================================================================================================================
3. LABEL-FREE CPU PILOT: does a lexicon/KG object term in the CIDEr-D reward change the one-step policy improvement?
======================================================================================================================
Protocol: B1's best-of-7 proxy for one RL step.
- Candidates: greedy + 6 samples (T=0.8) per KTVIC test image, for P1 s43 and fuse2 s42.
- Pick = argmax over candidates of CIDEr-D_A + lambda * 100 * F1_A, with A = refs{0,1,2}.
- Scored on B = refs{3,4} (and on the swapped split A{2,3,4} -> B{0,1}) and on the UIT-OpenViIC panel (555 images,
  independent annotators, never used in the selection).
- Scorer: the repo's RDRSegmenter path + pycocoevalcap. Self-check reproduces stored values: P1 s43 greedy CIDEr-D 143.9,
  mean sample 114.9. The CIDEr-only pick reproduces B1: +15.0 / +5.7 / +10.2 / +10.8 over greedy.
- lambda grid 0.25 / 0.5 / 1 / 2, all reported; none was chosen. lambda=1 gives a perfect-F1 caption +100 reward, about
  2 within-group SDs of CIDEr.
- Terms, all computed against refs A, no labels:
  - lex: CHAIR's own extractor (113-entry lexicon plus 27 hand hypernyms).
  - kgsyn: VnCoreNLP common nouns in the 211-term Wikidata-linked vocabulary, synonyms merged by shared item.
  - kgisa: kgsyn plus uncurated Wikidata P279+ credit for vaguer mentions.

Results: change vs the CIDEr-only pick, 95% paired bootstrap CI over images (B=2000). Full table in pilot/pilot_table.txt.
- CIDEr on held-out refs, all 48 settings (12 rules x 2 policies x 2 splits): between -2.9 and +2.4.
  - Every CI covers 0. Two touch it: kgsyn@0.25 and kgisa@0.25 (identical picks) on fuse2 A012->B34, +0.6 [0.0, 1.2].
  - lex@2.0 tends negative: -1.9 [-4.3, 0.3] and -2.9 [-7.1, 1.9] on P1.
- BLEU-4 on held-out refs: within +-0.6. ROUGE-L: -0.5 to +0.9 (the maximum is kgsyn@2.0, fuse2 A234).
- CHAIR_i (113-lexicon) vs held-out refs B, lex@1.0: -1.0, -1.5, -1.7, -0.5. CIs exclude 0 in 3 of 4 settings.
- CHAIR_i vs the independent panel:
  | rule | P1 A012 | P1 A234 | fuse2 A012 | fuse2 A234 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | lex@1.0 | 16.7->15.5 [-2.2,-0.2] | 16.7->15.5 [-2.3,-0.2] | 19.0->18.4 [-1.4,0.3] | 20.1->19.3 [-1.8,0.1] |
  | kgsyn@1.0 | 16.2 [-1.5,0.5] | 16.0 [-1.5,0.2] | 18.8 [-0.9,0.4] | 20.0 [-0.7,0.6] |
  | kgisa@1.0 | 16.1 [-1.7,0.4] | 15.9 [-1.7,0.1] | 18.5 [-1.4,0.3] | 19.5 [-1.5,0.3] |
- Noun-level hallucination vs the panel over the wider 211-noun vocabulary (plain string match, no KG relations):
  - all terms: -0.9 to +0.2;
  - KG terms are not better than lex. Only 2 of 16 KG CIs exclude 0: kgsyn@0.5 -0.6 and kgisa@0.5 -0.9, both P1 A234.
- Mentions per caption rise slightly with lambda: 1.76 -> 1.82, because the F1 term rewards recall.

Reading: a reference-derived object term buys about 1 point of CHAIR_i on an independent panel at about 0 CIDEr. Wikidata
synonyms and IsA add nothing measurable over the existing hand lexicon.
Caveats: one RL step, test images, 6 samples per image, single SFT seeds. Real RL takes many steps and can amplify both
the effect and the hacking; the uncurated IsA sinks in section 4.3 make kgisa hackable by generic words.

4.3 Linking precision and IsA quality (the part that decides usability)
- Automatic sense linking: pick the linked item that has a P279 parent and the most sitelinks. No human choice.
  - On the 224 COCO-80 terms: 118 found; 99 linked to an item whose English label/alias is in that COCO class's synonym
    list (83.9% of found); about 5 more are near-hyponyms (birthday cake, flush toilet, northern giraffe), so roughly
    88% acceptable. 61/80 COCO classes have at least one correctly linked Vietnamese term.
  - Real errors: con chó -> a ruthenium chemical compound; bóng đá/rổ/chuyền -> the sport, not the ball; balo and vali ->
    places; ván trượt tuyết -> ski; đĩa bay -> flying saucer; nón -> cone; kính -> glass (material); áo -> Austria
    (Q40); phương tiện -> "upaya" (Q581473).
- Wikidata IsA vs the repo's 27 hand HYPERNYMS pairs:
  - Both ends linkable: 19. P279* reproduces 11/19.
  - 7 of the 8 failures are linking errors: the 6 vehicle pairs (phương tiện -> "upaya") and áo -> Austria. The 8th,
    cặp đôi -> người, is not an IsA relation in Wikidata's model (a couple is a group).
  - Diagnostic with hand-picked senses (vehicle Q42889, furniture Q14745, shirt -> clothing Q11460): 9/9 re-tested pairs
    reach their parent (6 vehicle pairs, the 2 furniture pairs whose parent đồ đạc has no item, and áo as shirt).
  - 6 child terms have no item at all (bé trai, bé gái, đôi bạn, nam thanh niên, bạn trẻ, cô gái trẻ).
  - So the edges mostly exist; sense linking of short, generic Vietnamese words is the bottleneck.
- Uncurated transitive IsA (P279+) inside the 211-term linked vocabulary:
  - 241 distinct pairs; 174 (72%) end in three generic sinks: sản phẩm "product" (61), phần "part" (59), hàng hoá "goods"
    (54).
  - Absurd chains: chó, mèo, cây ⊂ phần; cờ ⊂ ảnh. phụ nữ ⊂ mái: Wikidata's "female" sense, while captions use mái for
    "roof".
  - An IsA-credit reward would therefore pay generic market words ("hàng hoá", "sản phẩm" are frequent KTVIC nouns).
  - Curating a safe-parent list or fixing senses by hand is annotation, which the R7 rule forbids, so the KG would have to
    be used uncurated.
- ConceptNet 5.7 Vietnamese, from the dump (the API returned 502 again):
  - 171,987 edges with a Vietnamese node, 44,996 Vietnamese terms.
  - Relations: RelatedTo 71,618; ExternalURL 46,183; Synonym 35,660 (only 1,550 vi-vi); HasContext 7,185;
    SymbolOf 4,542 (emoji); EtymologicallyRelatedTo 2,537; DerivedFrom 2,335; EtymologicallyDerivedFrom 1,393;
    FormOf 433; Antonym 96.
  - IsA = 0. AtLocation/UsedFor/CapableOf/PartOf/HasA = 0.
  - Sources: Wiktionary en 72,393 / fr 56,787 / de 713; DBpedia 37,552; emoji 4,542.
  - It is a translation dictionary. Commonsense requires an English pivot whose translations are noisy (người -> {an, body,
    he, her, him, human_being}; quầy -> {cabinet, counter, display, goods, kiosk, low}).
- Vietnamese WordNets:
  - OMW has no Vietnamese wordnet in its main collection, only the Wiktionary-derived wn-wikt-vie and wn-cldr-vie (431
    synsets of language and country names).
  - The `wn` package index has none.
  - The large Vietnamese WordNets in the literature have no release: GWC 2016 (32,413 synsets); viet.wordnet.vn (40,788
    synsets; unreachable 2026-10-12); Lam & Kalita 2022.

4.7 Usability verdict
- Wikidata is the only usable IsA backbone (CC0, about 80% label coverage), but under no-annotation it carries 12-16% sense
  errors and IsA chains dominated by generic sinks.
- OMW-wikt is the cleanest automatic IsA (PWN), at 59% coverage.
- ConceptNet gives commonsense only through a noisy English pivot.
- Kaikki is a good vi->en dictionary with no IsA.
- VietSGG is off-domain.
- None of these improved the pilot's metrics beyond the repo's 113-entry hand lexicon.

6. JUDGEMENT
======================================================================================================================
1. For the metrics-first goal (KTVIC CIDEr/B4/METEOR/ROUGE-L up, CHAIR_i down), a KG adds about 0 to the n-gram metrics,
   and at most about 1 point of CHAIR_i on top of CN-RLOO.
   - Literature: graph terms move n-gram metrics by 1 point or less (SPIDEr, MGRL-RSCC, RA-T, MERGE).
   - Our pilot: C -2.9..+2.4 (ns); panel CHAIR_i about -1 for the hand lexicon; no extra gain from Wikidata.
   - That is far from the +8 CIDEr bar and the -4 CHAIR_i Pareto bar. The CIDEr lever remains CN-RLOO itself.
2. The one graph use worth keeping is a small reference-derived object term (the hand lexicon, optionally with the 27 hand
   hypernyms) inside CN-RLOO, as a hallucination guard.
   - Report it as an ablation, not as novelty (SPIDEr 2017 and MGRL-RSCC 2026 are prior art).
   - Verify it where the reward cannot leak: COCO instance labels on UIT-ViIC and COCO Karpathy, and the OpenViIC panel.
     KTVIC CHAIR shares its gold with the reward (Goodhart).
   - CIDEr RL's documented hallucination risk is out of domain (VinVL NoCaps CHs +10.9), which is exactly the
     replication setting.
3. Do not put Wikidata IsA credit, ConceptNet or KG-RAG into the reward or prompt.
   - Uncurated: 12-16% wrong senses, 72% sink IsA pairs, 0 Vietnamese commonsense edges.
   - Curated: annotation, which is forbidden.
   - Measured: no gain over the hand lexicon.
4. Novelty for a Q1 journal will not come from "KG + captioning". The 2026 literature already holds KG-structured rewards
   (CheXPO-v2, MGRL-RSCC, FoCUS), graph metrics (PoSh, CAPTURE, DCScore) and graph RAG (MERGE, mKG-RAG). The Vietnamese
   gap is real but needs resources (parser, gold SGs, human ratings) that the R7 constraints exclude.
5. Two small, label-free, citable additions for the paper:
   (a) A resource-coverage paragraph with the numbers in 4.2-4.3. It justifies why no KG is used, and that the hand lexicon
       is not a straw man.
   (b) A CHAIR-coverage caveat: CHAIR_i sees 42% of noun mentions. Report noun-level hallucination against the OpenViIC
       panel as a broader, still label-free check (pilot/noun_hall.py).
   Neither changes the headline.
