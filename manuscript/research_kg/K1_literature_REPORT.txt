K1_literature: the quantitative evidence base for "knowledge graphs improve image captioning"
=============================================================================================
Date 2026-10-12. Agent K1_literature, round R7. English. Builds on research_wall/11 and kg_plan/{00,06,12,17,R3_01};
those files mapped WHO did WHAT (2019-2026) but carried almost no baseline-vs-+KG numbers. This report supplies the
numbers, the delta-vs-baseline-strength analysis, a KTVIC reference-content census with oracle bounds, an updated trend
count, and the post-2026-10-10 check. Every number below was read from a paper's own table (PDF full text) or computed
here on CPU from repo files; anything else is under "Unverified" (section 8).

0. BOTTOM LINE
--------------
(a) KG / scene-graph (SG) / concept gains shrink as the baseline gets stronger. Across 25 within-paper COCO ablations,
    Spearman rho(baseline CIDEr, delta) = -0.42 (n=25); for baselines >= 115 rho = -0.71 (n=21, approx. p < 0.001, rows not
    independent). Median delta: +6.5 CIDEr at baseline 115-125, +3.0 at 125-130, +1.4 at 130-140, +0.9 at >= 140 (one clean
    point: VinVL-L, 140.0 -> 140.9). Within single papers the same component loses most of its gain as the baseline
    improves (VinVL: +5.6 -> +3.0 -> +2.8 -> +0.9; TOMM'24 retrieval: +2.9 -> +1.4; TSG: +3.6 -> +2.2; Lee+'19: +1.1 -> +0.6),
    and proper controls shrink reported gains (VC R-CNN +8.2 -> +3.0; VSUA +5.8 -> +4.9; GCN-LSTM +8.6 -> ~+1.8 against a
    properly trained baseline). No published paper gives a clean
    KG ablation for a 7B instruction-tuned LVLM on generic short captions with n-gram metrics. The closest 7B evidence is the
    repo's own: Grounding-DINO tags in the Qwen2.5-VL-7B prompt, +1.1 CIDEr (P1 s43) and +2.5 (fuse2 s42) at 143.9, single
    seed, inside the 2.5-3.8 MDE.
(b) KGs pay off where the REFERENCES contain knowledge: named entities (news: +2.9 to +14.5 CIDEr at baselines 47-109,
    entity-F1 +2.0 to +3.7, one -1.3), novel/rare objects (NoCaps out-domain +13.2 vs in-domain +1.0), knowledge keywords (KnowCap +20.9 vs COCO -1.2
    to +1.4). They do not pay off on relations (SPICE relation-F1 stays ~5 with or without SG; Milewski+'20) and they LOWER
    n-gram scores when the output becomes more specific or more cultural (RAVENEA cultural RAG: CIDEr 22.2 -> 8.5; specificity
    guidance: CIDEr 126.1 -> 78.6; CONCAP, Vietnamese: cultural lexicon -5.2 CIDEr). KTVIC references contain almost none of
    the knowledge-type content: 0 of 2,790 test references has a named entity (all text is lower-cased), strict cultural terms
    appear in 5.3% of references, test-reference tokens unseen in training are 0.37%, and the knowledge-type union (strict
    cultural + named entity + train-rare words) carries 4.5% of CIDEr's unigram IDF weight. An oracle that reads the test
    references and inserts every missed knowledge-type token into P1's captions adds only +3.1 to +3.5 CIDEr (+4.0 to +4.8 with
    context words) and LOWERS BLEU-4 by 0.4-0.7. KG-structured hypernym backoff: blanket -13.7 to -15.5 CIDEr; with a perfect
    (reference-reading) verifier +0.2 to +0.6.
(c) Rising (arXiv abstracts per year, 2023 -> 2026-to-date): KG/SG + hallucination 2 -> 24; SG-based caption evaluation 12 -> 17;
    KG/SG rewards for captioning 1 -> 5; retrieval-augmented captioning 6 -> 29 (mostly caption/document memories, not KGs).
    Saturated or declining: KG/commonsense injection (flat at 16/yr; share of captioning papers 2.9% in 2019 -> 1.3% in 2026),
    KG/SG papers that report CIDEr (1-3/yr since 2019), entity/news captioning (10 -> 4).
(d) Nothing published since 2026-10-10 changes the old verdicts (arXiv index to 2026-10-09 and Crossref to 2026-10-12 checked).
    One old statement needs correcting: RAVENEA's "+11% culture-informed captioning" is RegionScore; its CIDEr falls.
Judgement: KG cannot materially raise KTVIC CIDEr/BLEU-4/METEOR/ROUGE-L (section 7).

2.8 7B instruction-tuned LVLM evidence, consolidated
- No clean KG on/off ablation on a 7B instruction-tuned captioner for generic short captions with n-gram metrics exists.
- Repo, Qwen2.5-VL-7B SFT on KTVIC: detector tags in prompt +1.1 / +2.5 CIDEr at 143.9 (single seed).
- SC-Captioner (Qwen2-VL-7B, SG reward, detailed captions): B4 +2.8 DOCCI, +0.7 COCO-LN; only +0.6 over plain DPO.
- MERGE (Qwen2.5-VL-7B, news): strong absolute scores, KG contribution not isolated for this backbone.
- CulturalGround (Pangea-7B): XM100 gain is mostly output-language compliance.
- RAVENEA (16 VLMs incl. Qwen2.5-VL-7B): cultural retrieval lowers CIDEr on average.
- EVCap (frozen Vicuna-7B decoder: COCO 139.0): ablation only at 13B (+5.7), and memory coverage barely matters.

3. (a) DOES THE GAIN PERSIST AS BASELINES GET STRONGER?
--------------------------------------------------------
25 within-paper COCO-Karpathy ablations (sections 2.1-2.3; families A concept/commonsense/VG, B scene graph, C memory):

4. (b) WHICH REFERENCE CONTENT DO KGs HELP, AND IS IT IN KTVIC?
---------------------------------------------------------------
Literature, by content type:
- Named entities: large gains only where references are entity-dense: news (Zhao+ +6.0/+6.4 C, entity F1 +2.1/+3.7; MERGE KG
  stage +2.9..+14.5 C, NER-F1 +2.0/+2.3); the IMAGE-side KG sub-graph alone adds nothing (-0.1/0.0 C, Zhao+ Table 2).
- Rare / novel objects: NOC-REK out-domain +13.2 vs in-domain +1.0; EVCap NoCaps +10.5 vs COCO +5.7; KnowCap +20.9 on
  knowledge-keyword references vs -1.2..+1.4 on COCO.
- Objects/attributes in general (VG-derived semantics): +5.6 -> +0.9 as baselines strengthen (VinVL); hallucination falls
  (VC R-CNN CHAIR_i -1.6/-2.5; CGRL -9.0).
- Relations: SPICE relation-F1 ~5 for every model with or without SGs (Milewski+); SG node embeddings alone +0.3 C (TSG).
- Cultural concepts: retrieval raises cultural relevance but LOWERS n-grams (RAVENEA: RegionScore +11.3, CIDEr -13.7, CIDEr
  anti-correlated with human cultural judgement); CONCAP's cultural lexicons cost Vietnamese -5.2 CIDEr.
- Specificity: more specific captions lose n-gram credit (Kornblith+: -47.5 C, -15.2 B4).

7. JUDGEMENT: CAN A KG RAISE KTVIC n-GRAM METRICS?
--------------------------------------------------
No, not materially. P1 already sits at 145.8 CIDEr (3 seeds), where the literature's own ablations put the KG/SG/concept
gain at +1.4 (median, 130-140) to +0.9 (>= 140), and where the repo's own detector-tag injection gave +1.1/+2.5 on one seed.
KTVIC's references are short, lower-cased, template-heavy everyday descriptions with no named entities, few cultural terms
and almost no out-of-training vocabulary; the content KGs supply carries 4.5% of CIDEr's unigram weight. Even an oracle
that reads the test references and inserts every missed knowledge-type word adds only +3.1..+3.5 CIDEr (+4.0..+4.8 with
context) and lowers BLEU-4, and KG-structured backoff ranges from -14 (blanket) to +0.6 (perfect verifier). Where KGs make
captions more specific or more cultural, n-gram metrics fall (RAVENEA, Kornblith+, CONCAP-vi). A realistic KG gain on
KTVIC is therefore 0 to +2 CIDEr, inside seed noise (SD ~3.4, MDE 2.5-3.8) and far from the +8 bar; RL on CIDEr-D
(R6 B1: +5 to +9 expected) dominates on cost and effect. A KG is defensible only for a different target, lower CHAIR or
verified faithfulness (VC R-CNN CHAIR_i -1.6/-2.5; CGRL -9.0), measured on existing truth sources, not for n-gram metrics.
