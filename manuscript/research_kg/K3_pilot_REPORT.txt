Classification rule:
- Every scoring token gets one category by token-aligned longest match over phrase lists, in this priority order:
  named entity > culture > template > scene > object > attribute > relation/action > function.
- Unmatched tokens fall back to their VnCoreNLP POS: N/Nb -> object; V -> relation/action; A/M/Nu -> attribute;
  everything else -> function/template.
- A separate flag marks non-visual inference. It is an overlay, not one of the seven categories.

The lists (lists.py, documented there):
- named entity / encyclopedic: 96 phrases (places, people, brands, encyclopedic labels);
- culture: 74 (includes all 13 top KTVIC terms from kg_plan/09);
- template: 26 (the "ở trong bức ảnh / xuất hiện / khung cảnh" framing);
- scene/location: 119;
- objects: 180 (rescap NOUN_CLASSIFIER minus chair._NOT_OBJECTS, plus nap.COMPOUNDS);
- attributes: 59 (rescap COLORS, colour modifiers, materials, patterns, gender/age);
- spatial relations: rescap SPATIAL_RELATIONS + 31;
- function words: rescap NUMBER_MARKERS + ~80;

The KTVIC test references are lowercase (POS "Np" finds nothing), so named entities come from the gazetteer only.

The KG is the repo's own lexical graph (409 surfaces):
- synonyms: rescap canonical()/SYNONYMS, nap.CANON, nap.COMPOUNDS, and the 2 NEAR pairs nap.json admits by TRAIN
  phi >= 0.9;
- hypernyms: rescap HYPERNYMS + nap.PERSON_FIX + nap.PARENTS, plus culture term -> generic head
  (lists.CULTURE_GENERIC).
Mentions are found as in chair.objects_in (syllable longest match, colour phrases masked). Edits are surface
rewrites, re-segmented with RDRSegmenter and scored as written. 'phụ nữ' and 'đàn ông' are written with 'người',
as in 99.8% of train mentions.

The edits:
- a1 (NOT an oracle): each mention -> its train top name, ManyNames-style. Votes come from the captions of the train
  images that name the mention. Candidates are synonyms or hypernyms attested in >= 3 train captions.
- a2 ORACLE: per image, the candidate-name combination with the highest CIDEr-D against the test references.
- b ORACLE: each mention CHAIR flags -> its nearest KG ancestor that is in CHAIR's gold set. Left alone if none.
- c ORACLE: a culture term used by >= 1 reference (c2: >= 2) and absent from the caption replaces the caption's
  generic word for it.
- d ORACLE: c, then b, then a2.

==========================================================================================================
5. JUDGEMENT: realistic metric headroom from a KG on KTVIC
==========================================================================================================
- Content: what a KG could add that the image does not show is about 3% of reference tokens and about 5% of
  reference CIDEr mass. It accounts for about 1 of the 32 CIDEr points between P1 and a human annotator. There are
  no proper names to link in the test set.
- Oracle ceiling, same references: +6.2 CIDEr, +1.7 BLEU-4, +1.0 ROUGE-L, -4.7 CHAIR_i (P1 s42; seeds 43/44:
  +5.1/+5.2 and -4.4/-4.7). This is a ceiling for any KG method that rewrites names, backs off, or inserts culture
  terms, and it is inflated by selecting on the scoring references.
- Transferable part (decisions on disjoint references): CIDEr about 0 (from -2.5 to 0.0 across ops and seeds);
  CHAIR_i -1.8 to -2.2 from backoff, but only with a perfect gate.
- With today's gates (train statistics, sample agreement): CIDEr +0.35 at best, down to -2.6; CHAIR_i unchanged.
- Expected KG contribution on KTVIC:
    CIDEr/BLEU-4/ROUGE-L: 0, with an upper plausible bound of about +1 CIDEr;
    CHAIR_i: 0 to -2, contingent on a verifier far better than sample self-consistency.
  CHAIR here uses reference-derived gold (an upper bound on hallucination), and R5-01 showed that reference
  precision measures typicality.
- Consequence for the paper's METRICS-FIRST goal: put GPU time into RL on CIDEr-D (B1) and MR5 (B2), not a KG. A KG
  remains defensible only as an analysis or evaluation instrument: naming-aware precision (NAP-lite exists), the
  culture split in section 3, or the held-out-oracle protocol used here. It is not a defensible metric method.
  Culture terms are a real content gap (kg_plan/09: P1 cultural recall 42.6%), but KTVIC's own references do not
  reward closing it.

==========================================================================================================
6. CAVEATS
==========================================================================================================
- The lists are hand-built and incomplete; the per-type audit is in out/type_categories.tsv. Moving a few types
  would shift the shares by fractions of a point. It would not change the conclusion: even K_broad in 'touch' mode
  is under 10% of reference mass, and the edit bounds do not depend on the taxonomy.
- Edits are rule-based surface substitutions, and some read badly (e.g. 'ghế nhựa' -> 'đồ đạc nhựa'). They are
  scored as written, which favours the oracles: a real system would also have to produce fluent text.
- The a2 oracle searches up to 256 variants per caption. Above that, each mention keeps its 3 most frequent train
  candidates.
- In held-out rows CHAIR is computed against 2-3 references, so its base is higher than the 5-reference value. Only
  the deltas are comparable.
- The RL analysis uses test samples from SFT policies (B1's method), not train rollouts. p1-gold (s42) has no T=0.8
  samples, so the u rows and section 4 use p1_s43 and fuse2_s42.
